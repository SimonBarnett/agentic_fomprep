What can I check if 365mail server is showing SMTP connection failed?
 
Problem: When trying to work with a 365 mail server, the connection fails and the test email is not sent. You may receive "SMTP connection failed" error, and chilkatlog
errors.
Office Management > Mail > Mail Options for Other Servers > Mail Options for Other Servers
Things to check:
1. Are the mail server details still correct in the mail servers form?
Office Management > Mail > Mail Options for Other Servers > Mail Servers
2. Is the specified port correct, and allowed through the firewall? (Define 25 for local, 465 SSL, 587 TLS)
3. Are the users in 365 still configured for sending SMTP? 
Enter the management interface using the admin account for the link: https://admin.microsoft.com/Adminportal#/usersSelect the relevant user and click on
manage email apps
In the next window, make sure Authenticated SMTP is checked.2
