Supplier quality maintenance 
What it’s for: 
This module allows to manage all the quality documents for each supplier, including expiry 
dates, and to drive supplier approval based on the fulfilment of such documents. 
Re-approval reminders can also be sent from the system when a document is about to expire. 
What included: 
1. A new menu on vendors for quality documents maintenance 
2. Two new standalone forms:   
a. Quality class, which includes a sub form for quality documents for the class 
b. Quality documents: name and description for available documents. 
3. New sub form on vendors for quality documents. The data will be populated when assigning 
a quality class for the vendor, but can be amended. 
4. This form has two sub forms for attachments and remarks. If the document is ticked for 
attachment or remark, the lucking of it will result the vendor to be unqualified. 
5. There are two added fields on suppliers:  
a. Send quality reminder (if ticked the supplier will get an email as well)  
b. Quality approved – this is a hidden field that is ticked by the system if one of the 
documents has expired or lucks an attachment or remark (when flagged to). If this 
field is ticked, orders cannot be authorised. 
6. New procedure to send reminders for the set group (hard coded in the procedure), and to 
tick vendor as unqualified if one of the documents has expired. 
Part quality maintenance (patch 5301) 
What it’s for: 
This patch allows to manage all the quality documents for each part (expiry dates are not 
included), and to drive part approval based on the fulfilment of such documents. 
What included: 
1. Addition of sub form to ‘Quality class’ to maintain the attached quality documents for parts 
having this class. 
2. New sub form on parts for quality documents. The data will be populated when assigning a 
quality class for the part, but can be amended.
