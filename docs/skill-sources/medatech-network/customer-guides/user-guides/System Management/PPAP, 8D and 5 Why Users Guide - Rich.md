1. Description 
 
The PPAP (Production Part Approval Process) module allows you to control all relevant PPAP data for parts/processes that have been requested by external and internal 
customers rather than having disparate spreadsheets and databases. As it is integrated within the Priority database you have access to link customer and vendor return 
data and full access to the customer and vendor database and their relevant contact information. The main external and internal PPAP forms include the following 
sublevels to record relevant data: 
 
Requirements 
Characteristics 
Control Plan 
DFMEA (Design Failure Mode Effect Analysis) 
PFMEA (Process Failure Mode Effect Analysis) 
ISIR (Initial Sample Inspection Report) 
AAR (Appearance Approval Report) 
Test results (Performance, Material and Dimensional) 
PSW (Part Submission Warrant) 
 
Each of the above has associated reports which can be configured to meet your individual requirements. 
 
The PPAP form has a BPM Flow chart allowing you to manage the PPAP process by defining statuses and appropriate business rules. 
 
The PPAP module also provides you with the ability to record 8D and 5 WHY information. The 8D form allows you to record internal and external 8D information. You can 
record the 8 stages from choosing the team, describing the problem, containment, root cause and corrective actions through to the prevention implementation and final 
recognition of the teams efforts in resolving the problem/concern. The inbuilt history of changes records changes made to key information allowing you to keep a track of 
what has been modified, by who and when.

3. 5 WHY 
 
The 5 WHY form and its sublevels allow you to record failure modes, areas of detection and measures put in place to resolve the concerns via the 5 WHY process. It enables 
you to explore the cause and effect relationships underlying a particular problem. You also have the inbuilt ability to add information to an Ishikawa fishbone diagram as an 
attachment to the 5 WHY form. Information recorded in the 5 WHY form and its sublevels can be viewed via the 5 WHY report. As with all Priority reports you can email 
them directly from the system.

The external PPAP form allows you to link Customer information, including contacts, customer sites, customer part numbers, customer Engineering approval, customer 
quality approval, customer site approval. The Team involved with the PPAP can be specified and by using the task functionality and business rule features you can create 
specific tasks for the whole team or individuals which will appear in their respective calendars, thus allowing you to track actions that have been created for the PPAP. PPAP 
is version controlled allowing you to make necessary changes and create new revisions as required. The New Revision direct activation will create the next sequential 
revision number and all details will be copied to the new revision. The process of the PPAP can be specified and recorded, i.e. Made In-House or Supplied and relevant 
planned start and end dates for the PPAP can be set as required. The facilities tab allows you to record the Laboratory/Inspection facility that will be used on the PPAP for 
Performance, Dimensional and Material testing purposes and these will be shown on the relevant Test Results reports. If you have the situation where you are preparing 
PPAP information and you do not need to record or provide information on all of the functional areas of the PPAP form then via the Sub-levels Allowed tab you can select 
the sublevels that you want to use per PPAP. If the sublevel is not flagged you cannot enter any data. In addition to this, it is important to point out that all fields and forms 
in Priority can be controlled for Read only or full write access per user or group of users right down to individual form columns. You can of course also remove entire access 
to any area of Priority as required.  
 
The sublevels of the PPAP form allow you to enter relevant data as applicable.

4.7. DFMEA 
The DFMEA and its sublevel DFMEA Items/Functions forms allow you to view and record core aspects of the DFMEA process. You can record a prioritised list of potential 
failure modes and effects whilst also recording recommended actions and target actions dates. You can record severity codes per effect with automatic risk and action 
priority number calculations (RPN). The DFMEA forms allow you to objectively evaluate functional requirements and design alternatives whilst identifying and minimising 
potential failure modes and their effects early in the design process. The DFEMA direct activation allows you to view in a report format the data that you have entered.

4.11. PTR 
The Performance test results form allows you to record itemised performance test results that have been carried out for the PPAP, including the facility to record test
