# Financials II part 1exercises

- **Source:** `Financials II part 1exercises.docx`

Financials II part 1

Exercises – In your demo company

Review your company data and fill out any missing data.

Review your document numeration settings. Note that any changes you decide to make will only affect documents that you have not yet made in the system until the fiscal year changes.

Review how your financial documents are set up.  Set  up your sales invoice to check the order of dates. Set a pre date limit of 30 days.

Read through the financial constants to understand what each one does. Modify your MaxReversing constant to be 15.

Create a new currency in the currencies form.

Make a new GL account for customer discount in the chart of accounts and then in the default accounts form change the default customer discount account to be the one you have created.

Create a new payment terms for month end + 50 days and assign this to a customer.
