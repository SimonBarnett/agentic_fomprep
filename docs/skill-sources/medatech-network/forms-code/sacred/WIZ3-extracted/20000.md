# Service Call Wizard

 

In Priority , you can record several different kinds of service calls: regular calls, follow-up calls and routine periodic calls. You can base a service call on a sales order for replacement parts and/or work hours; you can also open a service call directly from an e-mail message. 
 
## What do you want to do? 

 

 Open the call 
 

 Prepare a price quote (optional) 
 

 Schedule the call 
 

 Report service and close the call 
 

 Revise a closed call 
 

 Prepare an invoice for the call 
 

 Print a service call 
 

 Cancel a service call 
 
## See also:

 

 Viewing service call data 
 

 Maintaining FAQs for service calls 
 
## Note:

 

If repairs of defective parts returned by customers are performed in a company laboratory, run the Serial Numbers Wizard to assist you in defining a repairs warehouse (to hold laboratory inventory), recording the receipt of serialized parts for repair and returning them to the customer.
