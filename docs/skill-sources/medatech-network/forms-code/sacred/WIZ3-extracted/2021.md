# Vendor Parts and Prices Wizard

 

 Priority allows you to maintain parts and part prices per vendor, to rank vendors in order of preference, and to assign quality levels to parts and vendors so as to ensure the quality of purchased parts. 
 

Vendor part lists are useful for several reasons, such as: 
 

 ·        to maintain vendor or manufacturer part numbers and link them to internal part numbers 
 

 ·        to record the vendor's unit of measure for a given part when it differs from the part's buy/sell unit 
 

 ·        to define vendor rankings per part 
 

 ·        to prepare a vendor price list automatically 
 

 ·        to prepare requests for price quotations automatically from purchase requisitions. 
 

Vendor prices can be maintained in the part catalogue, with a single purchase price assigned to each part, or in an unlimited number of vendor price lists. 
 

Finally, preferred vendors, vendor rankings and quality levels help determine the right vendor from which to buy any given part.
