# Customer Surveys Wizard

 

Use customer surveys to track customer satisfaction with your service. Specifically, create one or more questionnaires and record customer responses. Then, use various reports to help you analyze survey results. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Define categories for questions in a questionnaire 
 

 Create a questionnaire 
 

 Send requests to customer contacts to fill out a survey 
 

 Record customer responses to a survey 
 

 Analyze the results of a survey
