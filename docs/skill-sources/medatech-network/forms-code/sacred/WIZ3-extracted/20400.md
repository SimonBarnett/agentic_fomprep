# Sales Reps Wizard

 

Sales representatives are agents who are compensated based on their performance. They work either for your company or for another company that sells your products. Priority provides a system for keeping track of sales reps' sales and commissions. 
 

Commissions due to external sales reps are recorded in sales rep commission documents, which can be created automatically from customer invoices or receipts recorded by the rep. 
 
## Note:

 

You will need to open vendor accounts for all external sales reps, in order to allow them to bill your company for commissions due. If you do not have the permissions required to open vendor accounts, request them from your system manager. Otherwise, ask the user responsible for vendors to open the accounts for you.
