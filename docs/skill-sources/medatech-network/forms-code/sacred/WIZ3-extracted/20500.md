# Cash Flow Wizard

 

Tracking cash flow involves categorizing withdrawals and deposits into and out of your bank accounts into different types of expenses (e.g., electricity, telephone, gasoline) and/or income (e.g., consulting, sales). You can then display the transactions for each cash flow category in cash flow reports. 
 

 Cash flow forecast reports display expected cash intake and outflow over a designated time period. The reports are based on financial transactions, already recorded in the system, whose due dates fall within the specified future period. For example, expected receipts from customers are based on unpaid invoices, while forecasted payments to vendors are based on unpaid vendor invoices.
