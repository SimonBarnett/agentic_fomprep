# Set Up Credit Card Accounts Wizard

 

You need to set up accounts for all of the credit card companies you work with, including the companies from which customers issue you payment, so that you can record credit card transactions in Priority . 
 

For each credit card company recorded in the system, you must define a GL account, at least one means of payment and payment receipt dates. 
 

Once you have set up a credit card company, you can record its credit card vouchers and perform reconciliations against its GL account. 
 
## Note:

 

You can also record credit card accounts after you begin working (e.g., you decide later to begin accepting customer payments made through a particular company). 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Define a GL account for a credit card company 
 

 Define means of payment for a credit card company 
 

 Set up a credit card account 
 

 Define payment receipt dates
