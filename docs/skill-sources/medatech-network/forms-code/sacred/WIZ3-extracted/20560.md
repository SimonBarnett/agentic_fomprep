# Set Up Bank Accounts Wizard

 

You need to set up bank accounts so that you can record cash transactions in Priority. 
 
## Before you can set up bank accounts, you need to:

 

 ·        record a list of the banks (and bank branches) in which your company, your vendors and your customers maintain accounts 
 

 ·        define GL accounts for each of your company's bank accounts. 
 
## You then need to record:

 

 ·        your company's bank accounts 
 

 ·        bank accounts of vendors that you pay by bank transfer 
 

 ·        bank accounts of customers that pay you by bank transfer. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        If you do not yet have information about a particular customer or vendor, you can complete that information later, when a bank transfer is made. 
 

 ·        Once you have recorded your company's bank accounts, you need to run the Opening Balances Wizard to record opening balances for each account. (If you are using the dual-currency package, this should include accounts for incoming and outgoing postdated checks, which are recorded differently than routine account balances.) 
 

 ·        If you work with subsidiaries and want to link a bank account to a subsidiary, link the account to a branch office that is linked to the subsidiary.
