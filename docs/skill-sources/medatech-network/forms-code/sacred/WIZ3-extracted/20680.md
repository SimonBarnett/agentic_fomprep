# Customer Credit Wizard

 

The customer credit utility provides up-to-date and detailed data about open orders, unbilled documents , unbilled reported project hours, pending sales invoices and unpaid bills. You can define credit limits for customers in a single company and/or a multi-company setting . You can also define a customer for credit that is shared by several customers in order to consolidate credit calculations for these customers. Based on this information, you can limit the business activity of customers who exceed their credit limits. 
 
## Note to dual-currency users: 

 

 Priority also calculates liability, which is defined as credit + any unredeemed postdated checks. Unless otherwise stated, any information recorded in this wizard for credit is also relevant to liability. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Set up credit definitions 
 

 Define a customer for credit 
 

 Define customer credit limits 
 

 View a customer ' s credit while recording a document or working with the customer ' s project 
 

 View the credit of one or more customers
