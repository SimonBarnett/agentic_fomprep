# Bonuses Wizard

 

In Priority , you can define a part as a bonus, to be awarded to a customer upon the purchase of another part. Examples. 
 

There are two types of bonuses: regular and special. 
 

 ·        A regular bonus is given to all customers (i.e., that are eligible to receive them), for free or at a reduced price, in exchange for purchasing a given part. 
 

 ·        A special bonus is only given to a specific customer.
 Note: If the customer in question is a representative customer, then the bonus is given all customers in that group. 
 

 ·        Both kinds of bonuses can be defined either for a single part or a part family. That is, a regular or special bonus can be given either in exchange for the purchase of a given quantity of a single part, or in exchange for the purchase of a total quantity of any parts belonging to a given part family. 
 
## Whether you are defining a regular or a special bonus, you need to indicate:

 

 ·        The minimum quantity of the part that must be ordered so as to receive the bonus item 
 

 ·        The time period in which the bonus offer is valid 
 

 ·        The quantity of the bonus item being offered and its price (if it is not free)
