# Special Offers Wizard

 

 Priority 's Special Offers module allows you to promote the sales of a given part or part family, by offering incentives to purchase a designated amount of goods within a defined period. Target sales can be measured in either quantity or price, and the incentive can take the form of a price reduction for a designated quantity of a given part, a fixed monetary credit or a credit based on a percentage of the total purchase of target parts. 
 

As opposed to the Bonuses module, which presents the customer with an immediate reward at the time of purchase, special offers are earned through an accumulation of invoices (for a single customer or for a shared billing customer for offers) over the defined offer period, and are only awarded at the conclusion of that period.
