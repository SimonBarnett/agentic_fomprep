# Withholding Tax Wizard

 

At the time of payment, income tax regulations often require companies which use a double-entry accounting system to withhold tax from vendors. 
 

In addition, your own company may be required to have tax withheld when receiving payment from customers. For details, run the Customer Receipts Wizard . 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Set up withholding tax 
 

 Record a payment and withhold tax 
 

 Record a manual journal entry for payment with tax withheld 
 

 Prepare a monthly report of withheld tax 
 

 Prepare an annual report of withheld tax 
 

 Verify or revise vendor expiration dates for withholding tax authorization
