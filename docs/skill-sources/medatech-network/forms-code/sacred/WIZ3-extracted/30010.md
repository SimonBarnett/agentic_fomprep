# Tax Prepayment Wizard

 

Income tax authorities often require companies to prepay portions of their income tax, frequently in monthly installments. Priority enables you to calculate monthly prepayment installments based on a percentage of monthly revenue: 
 

 Percentage of Monthly Revenue - Tax Withheld by Customers = Monthly Payment
