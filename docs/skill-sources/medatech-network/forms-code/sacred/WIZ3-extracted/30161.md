# Price Adjustment Wizard 

 

When a sales or purchase order is linked to a foreign currency, price index or currency basket, prices in the corresponding invoice or shipping/receiving document are adjusted automatically as the exchange rate or index fluctuates. Example . 
 

In addition, when an invoice in a given currency is paid for in a different currency, the payment amount is automatically converted to the payment currency, based on the exchange rate in effect on either the invoice or the payment date (in keeping with a system-wide definition). 
 

Blanket orders (to the customer and from the vendor) can also be linked to a foreign currency, price index or currency basket. Such linkage is then copied to the order. 
 

Finally, payment installments for a service contract can be adjusted for the payment date. That is, the amount of any payment made against the contract can be linked to a price index, foreign currency or currency basket.
