# Commission Sales Wizard

 

Commission sales are a type of transaction in which your company brokers a sale. In a commission sale, the vendor delivers the goods directly to the customer, without the goods passing through your company/factory warehouses at all. In return for your service, the vendor pays you a commission. The Commission Sales module provided by Priority allows you to record all transactions pertaining to commission sales, from price quotes to payments of commission.
