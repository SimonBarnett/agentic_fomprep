# Past Balances Wizard

 

A past balance calculates and "locks" inventory balances in all warehouses for any given date in the past. That is, Once a past balance has been calculated, you cannot record or revise inventory transactions dated prior to the past balance date. To make such retroactive changes, you must first delete any past balance(s) dated on or after the transaction date in question. 
 
## Past balances should be calculated periodically (e.g., every quarter) for the following reasons:

 

 ·        Periodic calculation of past balances is essential for costing, inventory counts and many inventory reports. Explanation . 
 

 ·        Calculating the past balance prevents retroactive revisions of inventory balances. This is particularly important when filing financial reports. 
 

 ·        When calculating past balances, inventory balances are recalculated and current inventory balances are corrected. Explanation .
