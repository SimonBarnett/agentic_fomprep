# Prepaid Expenses/Deferred Income Wizard

 

In Priority , you can record prepaid expenses and deferred income in the following ways: 
 

 ·        Defining distribution of expenses/income in the invoice (e.g., in a vendor invoice for a magazine subscription). OR 
 

 ·        When there is no invoice, recording their distribution directly in the entry journal. 
 

Regardless of the method used, the end result is a journal entry made up of multiple items, each with a different due date. The Transfer PrepaidExp/DeferredInc program takes these due dates into account when transferring expenses/income that have come due to P&L accounts.
