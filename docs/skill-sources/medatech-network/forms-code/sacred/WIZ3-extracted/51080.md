# Working With Subcontractors Wizard

 

If part of the production process takes place outside of your company at a subcontractor, Priority allows you to track inventory at the subcontractor and take subcontracted work into account when tracking production. You can prepare automatic issues of raw materials required by the subcontractor and accurately calculate floor inventory, including the subcontractor's. 
 

When you subcontract a part that is modeled for production in the factory (either sometimes or always), or has not been modeled for a specific subcontractor, you have two options: 
 

 ·        You can let MRP decide which subcontractor to use, or whether production should take place in your factory. 
 

 ·        You can manually adapt the work order for production by the subcontractor that actually performs it. 
 

Finally, you can model production of a part whose entire manufacturing process is performed by a turnkey subcontractor, even when the part is sometimes produced in the factory.
