# Material Requirements Planning (MRP) for Distributors Wizard

 

 MRP for Distributors calculates the projected demand for parts on the basis of open sales orders and forecast/stock orders , and prepares an issues plan according to the required quantities and the due dates of the ordered items. Afterwards, purchase planning creates purchase requisitions based on this issues plan. 
 

MRP also handles parts assembled in the warehouse from purchased components. Demands for a given part can arise from two sources: 
 

 ·        Orders for the part itself (direct demand) 
 

 ·        Orders for the part`s parent (indirect demand) 
 

When MRP encounters an order for an assembled part, it calculates the demand for each of its child parts, according to the order quantity and the defined parent/child ratios.
