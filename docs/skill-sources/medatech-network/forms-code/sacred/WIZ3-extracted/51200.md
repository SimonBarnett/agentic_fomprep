# Purchase Planning Wizard

 

Preparing purchase requisitions is the first step in the purchasing process. A requisition is prepared for each part that needs to be purchased, and it consists of the required quantities of the part and the date on which they are required. All purchase requisitions – whether recorded manually or automatically – are grouped together within purchase demand documents. They serve as the basis for vendor price quotes (RFQs) and purchase orders. 
 

The Purchase Planning program uses the issues plan prepared either by MRP or by the Production Planning program, as well as additional data, to calculate projected balances. You can use the projected balances reports as well as other purchase planning reports as reference sources to open purchase requisitions manually. Alternatively, you can set the program to open requisitions automatically, based on the calculated projected balances. 
 

You can also run purchase planning as part of the MRP program.
