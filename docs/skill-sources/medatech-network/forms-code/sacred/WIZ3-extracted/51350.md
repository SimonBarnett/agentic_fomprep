# Outgoing Shipping Vouchers Wizard

 

Outgoing shipping vouchers are used to distribute the expenses incurred by shipping goods to your customers among the sold goods, in order to establish their total cost. This allows you to analyze the profitability of your sales and review your pricing strategy. 
 

Sold goods and their shipping expenses are included in a shipping voucher by attaching the invoices in which they are billed – customer invoices for the goods, and purchase invoices for the expenses. When the voucher is closed, the expenses are automatically distributed among the sold goods, according to the burden type defined for each part. 
 

The results of this process are displayed in customer invoice profitability reports. 
 

Furthermore, shipping vouchers enable you to plan the shipment of order items. When an order item is linked to an outgoing shipping voucher, shipping data for related items can be maintained in the shipping voucher.
