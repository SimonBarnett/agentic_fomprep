# Blanket Sales Orders Wizard

 

A blanket sales order is not an actual order, but a commitment on the part of your customer to purchase a given quantity or monetary value of goods over a defined period of time, usually in exchange for reduced prices or special payment conditions. It is actualized by recording regular sales orders that are linked to the blanket order and inherit the promised payment conditions that are defined in it. Each actual order automatically reduces the balance that the customer has committed to buy. The conditions defined in the blanket order remain in effect until the specified quantity or monetary value of goods has been sold or until its expiration date. 
 
## Note:

 

To open sales orders on the basis of a blanket order, run the Sales Order Wizard .
