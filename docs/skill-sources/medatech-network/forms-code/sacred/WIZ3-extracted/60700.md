# Blanket Purchase Orders Wizard

 

A blanket purchase order is not an actual order, but rather a commitment to purchase a given quantity of goods from a given vendor over a defined period of time, usually in exchange for reduced prices or special payment conditions. It is actualized by placing regular purchase orders that are linked to the blanket order and inherit the promised payment conditions that are defined in it. Each actual order automatically reduces the balance of goods that the company has committed to buy. The conditions defined in the blanket order remain in effect until the specified quantity of goods has been purchased or until its expiration date. 
 
## Note:

 

To open purchase orders on the basis of a blanket order, run the Purchase Order Wizard .
