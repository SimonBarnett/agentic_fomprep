# Customer Shipment/Return Wizard

 

 Priority provides you with various ways to record the shipment of goods to customers and their occasional returns: 
 

 ·        You can record customer shipments in the Customer Shipments form, and returns in the Customer Returns form. 
 

 ·        You can record both shipments and returns in the Customer Shipments form, designating negative quantities for returned items. 
 

 ·        You can record a bill of lading in the Customer Shipments form, when the shipment is of packed crates. 
 

 ·        You can record shipments and returns in the Sales Invoices form. In this case the shipping document is also an invoice. 
 

Use a shipping document (rather than a sales invoice) when several shipments are billed in a single (multi-shipment) invoice, or when the goods are sent to one address and the invoice to another (e.g., to the billing customer). 
 

Use a sales invoice when goods and invoices are sent together to the same address.
