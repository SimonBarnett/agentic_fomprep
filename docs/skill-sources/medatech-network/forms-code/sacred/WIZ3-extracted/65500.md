# Opening Balances Wizard

 

When you first start to work with Priority , you need to transfer account balances from your previous system into the new one. Assuming your start date is the first of January , you need to record balances for December 31 of the previous year and then transfer them to the new year. 
 
## This entails the following steps:

 

 ·        Opening the previous and current fiscal years. 
 

 ·        Opening all periods in the previous year and the first period in the current year. 
 

 ·        Recording balances for December 31 of the previous year. 
 

 ·        Transferring balances to January 1 of the current year. 
 
## Note:

 

Click if fiscal periods are already opened.
