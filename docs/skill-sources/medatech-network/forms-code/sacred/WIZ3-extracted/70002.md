# CRM and Sales Main Wizard

 
## Introduction

 

 Priority provides you with the flexibility to create a working procedure for CRM and sales that conforms to your unique operating methods. For example: 
 

1) Start by defining working parameters for a staff of sales representatives. 
 

2) Your sales reps interview potential customers, open files for them and record their significant details in a customer profile. 
 

3) Your company conducts a marketing event, tracking the participation of your potential customers and opening new ones. 
 

4) In response to requests for price quotations, you define price lists appropriate to your various potential customers, produce price quotes based on these lists and send them out. 
 

5) Potential customers that place an order can now be regarded as regular customers. You convert each one to regular customer status and open a corresponding A/R account. Next you set the default payment terms for your new customers, and assign them specific price lists and discounts. Alternatively, you can attach each new customer to a customer group that is entitled to defined prices and discounts. 
 

6) You prepare invoices and ship them with the ordered goods to their respective customers. 
 

7) As your customer base grows, you plan new marketing campaigns, activities and events that focus on your developing market niche. 
 
## Note:

 

Use various BPM tools to track the progress of sales documents, streamline the sales process and increase the flow of information between employees handling these documents. 
 
## Using this wizard

 

The following sections outline various aspects of sales and customer relations management. 
 

Click the hypertext links to open specialized wizards designed to assist you in the performance of specific CRM and sales tasks. Each wizard features an introductory page providing a brief overview of the subject, followed by detailed instructions. Most of these wizards are also accessible from the main Priority CRM and Sales menus and their sub-menus. 
 
## Customer Management

 

 Priority provides a full array of tools enabling you to manage your customers . Open records for regular customers (including customer data, sales contacts, part lists, special prices and discounts, marketing data, etc.), customers who are also vendors(for which you can open a special kind of ledger account), walk-in customers (one-time or infrequent customers, who share a common ledger account) and temporary customers with the potential to become regular customers. Potential customers are easily converted into regular customers by means of an auxiliary program. You can further optimize customer management by creating a representative customer whose price and discount terms are shared by a group of customers. You can also define secured customers and hide them from unauthorized users. 
 

The customer credit utility allows you to define credit limits and view detailed data regarding credit status. Customers who have exceeded their credit limit can be restricted from further transactions, with warnings issued to users who try to process them. These warnings can be converted into error messages for specific groups of users by means of the Privilege Explorer . 
 
## Customer Relations Management (CRM)

 

A number of tools are provided with which to maintain customer relations . 
 

You can define an initial survey to be filled in after your initial contact with a new customer. This survey, which takes the form of a telemarketing questionnaire, contains pertinent questions that you choose to assist you in classifying the customer for future marketing activities. 
 

You can keep track of customer contacts, including new leads. Moreover, you can create a network of relations between your customers and their contacts, allowing them to participate with you in multi-company deals. 
 

The system is equipped with a bi-lateral interface with Microsoft Outlook, which allows you to synchronize contacts, calendar appointments, customer tasks and the like. 
 

The central task utility allows you to keep track of all your customer tasks. For example, you can see all tasks opened for a specific customer, or assigned to a specific user; all price quotations sent out in a given month or all telemarketing calls; all service calls, sales orders or warehouse assemblies opened for a specific customer. 
 

Track and analyze your marketing efforts in marketing campaigns: group specific marketing tasks within the framework of a campaign, designate campaign milestones, and open sales opportunities for each customer in the campaign. You can analyze the progress of the campaign as a whole, or the status of a specific customer within the campaign. 
 

Create monthly sales/order targets for the year on the basis of past data, and view compliance with targets and/or sales forecasts for your sales representatives on the basis of their actual and estimated future performance during a given sales period. Record commission agreements establishing the percentage of commission to be awarded to each sales rep for meeting and exceeding their targets. 
 

You are also provided with a log in which to record the details of marketing events and activities held by your company, and track your customers' participation. You can use this data to plan future activities that focus on certain customer types. 
 

Among the advanced tools at your disposal is a Customer Work Area, in which you can perform numerous marketing tasks involving categories and groups of customers. 
 
## Pricing

 

Prices for line items in sales transactions are retrieved from among a variety of sources, according to an established price hierarchy . You can create a variety of price lists, discounts and bonuses for parts and part families, and assign them to customers. Advanced tools are provided that calculate order profitability . You can use this data to prepare and print out price quotations , including quotes based on product bundles . 
 
## Sales

 

Open sales orders for your customers, either manually, or based on a price quotation or blanket order . You may itemize your orders on the basis of defined product bundles. In the dual-currency package, order prices can be linked to a foreign currency , price index or currency basket. 
 

You can record and view data regarding your company's sales representatives . Furthermore, you can document all stages of a commission sale (e.g., price quotes, commission orders, reports of delivery, credit notes). The system also provides tools for branch management : you can associate users and customers with specific branch offices, create journal entry codes specific to a certain branch, and view sales and inventory levels per branch. You can also define which users are permitted to view documents recorded for a given sales rep or branch. 
 

Define sales forecasts for your sales reps for the coming year, and track their compliance by recording projected revenues for open sales opportunities. Record a commission agreement for each sales rep, defining commission percentages based on performance. 
 
## Shipping

 

Document the shipping process with the appropriate shipping (and return) documents . 
 

If your company commands its own delivery resources, you can plan, view and update detailed delivery schedules based on sales orders, according to defined delivery dates and routes. 
 
## Invoicing

 

 Priority provides a full complement of billing options . Use a sales invoice to both debit the sale and record the supply of goods to the customer, or a multi-shipment invoice to debit sales independently of the shipping documents. Over-the-counter invoices are useful for transactions that involve immediate payment, and pro forma invoices serve as temporary bills until payment is finalized and/or the goods have been received. 
 

Invoices can also be prepared for projects, for service calls and for service contracts. 
 

Finally, you can send a credit memo to a customer who has returned goods or was overcharged. 
 
## Executive Reports

 

A broad spectrum of reports are available to help you analyze sales and marketing data. 
 

 ·        Once calculated for a given period, sales order information can be viewed from a variety of perspectives (e.g., per part, customer, sales rep, branch). You can also obtain varied levels of detail — from a period total down to actual order details — and view a wealth of data hierarchies (e.g., total orders per month, total monthly orders per branch, total monthly orders per branch customer). BI reports allow you to compare data from different years, periods and companies in various graphic formats. You can also determine the exact cross-section to be displayed in the data cube, by drilling down within categories and accessing only the specific information that you want to see. 
 

An easy-to-use sales order report generator allows you to create an unlimited number of reports utilizing the above information.
