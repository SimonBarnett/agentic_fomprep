# Purchase Invoices and Memos Wizard

 

 Priority provides you with various ways to record purchase invoices and credit due from vendors: 
 

 ·        Vendor invoices – received with purchased goods; serve both as billing invoices and as goods receiving vouchers (GRV). 
 

 ·        Multi-GRV invoices – bill your company for goods received in multiple shipments, each documented in a separate GRV. 
 

 ·        Expense invoices – bill your company for non-inventory expenses, such as storage or technical support. 
 

 ·        Debit memos – sent to a vendor to request credit for returned goods or overcharging. 
 

 ·        Credit memos – received from the vendor; record credit awarded as compensation for returned goods or overcharging. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        For information on distributing shipping and other expenses among purchased parts, whether they are included in the same invoice or in separate invoices, run the Shipping Expenses Wizard . 
 

 ·        If you are working with the Fashion module and you need to itemize an invoice or memo manually, run the Fashion Wizard to learn how to record style assortments. 
 

 ·        Dual-currency users: Prices are adjusted in invoices/memos when the original order is linked to a foreign currency, price index or currency basket. User-defined parameters determine the date for which linkage is calculated, whether linked amounts are added to part prices or displayed on a separate line in the invoice, and whether a drop in the exchange rate or index should lower invoice prices or leave them as is. For details, run the Price Adjustment Wizard .
