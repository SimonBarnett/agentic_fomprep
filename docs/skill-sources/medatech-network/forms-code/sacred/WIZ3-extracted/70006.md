# Executive Reports Wizard

 

The Executive Reports Module provides a broad view of your company's activities, in areas such as: 
 

 ·        Sales – orders, invoices, shipments, commission sales, sales opportunities, sales forecasts, sales rep commissions and compliance with sales/order targets 
 

 ·        Purchasing – purchase orders, purchase invoices 
 

 ·        Inventory – warehouse tasks and inventory value analysis 
 

 ·        Account Transactions – budgets, journal entries, profit and cost centers 
 

 ·        Projects – human resource management in projects, project hour reporting, profitability analysis 
 

 ·        Customer Service – service call analysis. 
 

 ·        Production – production report and production cost analysis 
 

 ·        Payroll – work hour reporting analysis 
 

The module offers Dashboards that display data in a variety of cross-sections, report generators for creating custom executive reports, and powerful BI reports that allow you to compare data from different years, periods and companies. 
 

Once calculated for a given period, information can be viewed from a variety of perspectives (e.g., per part, customer, sales rep, branch, project) and you can obtain varied levels of detail.
