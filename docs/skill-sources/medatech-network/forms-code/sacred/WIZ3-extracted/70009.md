# CRM Wizard

 

 Priority provides a variety of powerful tools to assist you in identifying and targeting potential customers, tracking sales opportunities and managing relations with existing customers and their contacts. 
 

An extensive CRM (Customer Relations Management) tasking utility is particularly useful for executing and tracking your marketing activities and other interactions with customers (and vendors). For example, you can: 
 

 ·        maintain an appointment calendar, based on tasks with distinct start and end times 
 

 ·        view at a glance all tasks for a specific customer (or vendor), or those assigned to a certain user 
 

 ·        view all price quotations sent out or all telemarketing phone calls made in a given month 
 

 ·        keep track of service calls, sales orders and warehouse assemblies opened for the customer 
 

 ·        generate reminders of approaching tasks or appointments 
 

 ·        plan and manage marketing campaigns 
 

 ·        generate sales/order targets and define commission agreements for sales reps who meet their targets.
