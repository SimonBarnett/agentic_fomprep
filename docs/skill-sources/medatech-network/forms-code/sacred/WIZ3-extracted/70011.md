# Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) Wizard

 

 Priority allows you to calculate COGS in one of two ways: 
 

 ·        Perpetual Cost of Goods 
 

 ·        Periodic Cost of Goods. 
 
## Note: 

 

Periodic COGS is meant for for users of the Commercial package only. 
 
## Perpetual COGS

 

When you work with perpetual cost of goods sold, the Post Cost of Goods Sold program updates inventory and COGS accounts, creating journal entries for all inventory transactions recorded in the system. Entry sums are calculated in keeping with the standard costs of the inventory transaction. 
 

When perpetual COGS entries are recorded, purchase invoices debit a balance-sheet inventory account; that is, the purchase is not considered an expense until a customer invoice is recorded. When the customer invoice is finalized, an entry is automatically recorded that credits the inventory account (reduction of the asset value) and debits the COGS account (increase of the cost of goods sold). 
 

After the Post Cost of Goods Sold program is run, the balance of the inventory accounts reflects the inventory value and the cost of goods sold for each date within the fiscal period. 
 

Click to work with perpetual COGS . 
 
## Periodic COGS

 

Working with periodic COGS is similar to maintaining periodic inventory values in the ledger. It is meant for enterprises using the Commercial package. 
 

In each period, the cost of goods sold is calculated as: 
 

 ·        Opening inventory value plus purchases in period minus closing inventory value. 
 

The logic is as follows: Sales in a given period are taken from available stock in the beginning of the period plus purchases that were made during the period. Not all stock is necessarily sold, so at the end of the period a balance remains that should not be included in the cost of goods sold. The cost of goods sold is therefore calculated as the sum of the opening balance and purchases minus the closing balance. 
 

Click to work with Periodic COGS .
