# Customer Invoices and Credit Memos Wizard

 

 Priority offers various ways to bill customers and to record credit due to them. 
 
## There are several types of invoices available:

 

 ·        Multi-shipment invoices 
 

 ·        Sales invoices 
 

 ·        Over-the-counter invoices 
 

 ·        Holding invoices 
 

 ·        Pro forma invoices (for cash-based accounting) 
 

 ·        Export invoices (for dual-currency users) 
 
## There are two options for recording credit due:

 

 ·        Record a separate credit memo 
 

 ·        Record credit in an invoice
