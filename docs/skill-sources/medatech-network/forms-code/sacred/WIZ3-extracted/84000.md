# Customer Service Main Wizard

 
## Introduction

 

 Priority 's customer service module provides you with the tools you need to efficiently manage customer service processes. For example, you can: 
 

1)  Keep track of parts by serial number. 
 

2)  Open and manage service calls, recording the customer's complaint, details of the problem and the resolution. Work hours, traveling time and parts required for the service call are also recorded, enabling automatic calculation of the cost of the call and the charge to the customer. 
 

3)  Record service contracts for customers, specifying part details, type of service provided, warranties, payments, and the like. 
 

4)  Track customer satisfaction via surveys and analyze results. 
 

5)  View reports tracking and analyzing service data. 
 
## Note:

 

Use various BPM tools to track the progress of service calls and contracts, streamline the customer service process and increase the flow of information between employees handling these documents. 
 
## Using this wizard

 

The following sections outline various aspects of the service and maintenance module. 
 

Click the hypertext links to open specialized wizards designed to assist you in the performance of specific service-related tasks. Each wizard features an introductory page providing a brief overview of the subject, followed by detailed instructions. Most of these wizards are also accessible from the main Priority Customer Service menu and its sub-menus. 
 
## Customer Service Definitions

 

Begin by recording a number of definitions to be used when working with the customer service module. 
 
## Serial Numbers

 

 Priority allows you to assign serial numbers to parts in order to keep track of individual items. In the catalogue of parts with serial numbers, you can view all data relating to a specific serialized part, including its serialized component parts, audit trail, maintenance history (at the customer site or in the laboratory), warranty and service history. 
 
## Warranties

 

 Warranty periods for serialized parts are determined automatically by the warranty code assigned to the part. You can assign different types of warranties, and change or extend the expiration date with or without charging the customer. Service terms can be set out in a customer service contract once the warranty expires. 
 
## Service Contracts

 

 Service contracts determine which services (labor, replacement parts and so on) are offered for a particular part, which are exempt from payment and which are billable. Charges for service calls are determined by the service terms defined in the service contract. Priority offers programs which automatically renew contracts, calculate payment installments, prepare invoices and more. In the dual-currency package, contract payments can be linked to foreign currencies or a price index. 
 
## Service Calls

 

 Service calls are a tool used to manage customer service, enabling you to track parts requiring servicing, schedule technician visits, record details of malfunctions and their resolution, and report labor and use of replacement parts. When the handling of the service call has been completed, an invoice for the call can be prepared automatically. 
 
## Service Quality and Service Reports

 

Use customer surveys to keep track of customer satisfaction with your service. Then, use various reports to help you analyze results. 
 

Among the reports provided to help you track customer service, you can find: 
 

 ·        Service call reports: Analysis of malfunctions, labor and replacement parts usage; scheduling of technicians and various summary reports. 
 

 ·        Reports for serialized parts: Audit trail of maintenance and repairs; warranty and component information. 
 

 ·        Service contract reports: Contract details, payments, profitability, projected costs and expected income. 
 

Furthermore, Priority provides easy-to-use report generators that allow you to create an unlimited number of custom reports on serialized parts, service calls and servicing reports.
