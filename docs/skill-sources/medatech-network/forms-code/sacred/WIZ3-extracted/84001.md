# Human Resources Main Wizard

 
## Introduction

 

 Priority 's human resources module provides you with the tools you need to handle all aspects of personnel management in your company. For example, you can: 
 

1)  Process jobs in your company and plot the organizational structure.  
 

2)  Create new job openings and manage recruitment of job candidates. 
 

3) Manage candidates applying for positions, and track their progress throughout the hiring process. 
 

4) Record the qualifications required for a job and identify those candidates who possess them. 
 

5)  Open personnel files for new employees and record personal and professional details. 
 

6)  View reports tracking human resource data in your company and analyze that information. 
 
## Note:

 

Use various BPM tools to track the position of candidates and employees in the company, maintain courses and facilitate personnel management. 
 
## Using this wizard

 

The following sections outline various aspects of the human resource module. 
 

Click the hypertext links to open specialized wizards designed to assist you in the performance of personnel-related tasks. Each wizard features an introductory page providing a brief overview of the subject, followed by detailed instructions. Most of these wizards are also accessible from the Human Resources menu and its sub-menus. 
 
## Human Resources Definitions

 

Begin by recording a number of definitions to be used when working with the human resources module. 
 
## Jobs

 

 Priority allows you to process a single job at a time, or several openings simultaneously if you are seeking to fill multiple openings for the same position. You can also compare applicants for a job, record the method of recruitment, and fill the position with the chosen applicant. Moreover, you can plot your company's organizational structure and display the hierarchy graphically. 
 
## Candidates

 

You can use Priority to manage the recruitment of candidates for open positions, whether they are external applicants or existing employees applying for a new position in the company. You can document all aspects of the candidate consideration and selection process, track the hiring process, and easily convert a hired job candidate to an employee. 
 
## Employees

 

You must open a personnel file for every employee in your company. How you do so depends on how far along you are in working with Priority : 
 

If you have just begun working with Priority and are recording employees in the system, use the Open Personnel File Wizard , which includes instructions for setting up and beginning to work with the system. 
 

If you are already working with the system on a regular basis, and are opening a new personnel record or managing existing records, use the Employee Wizard . 
 

Either way, the personnel file is the central tool by which human resources are managed. Use the personnel file and its sub-level forms to record personal information, professional details, and the employee's current department and position. 
 
## Courses

 

You can track the educational progress of employees by recording courses of study attended –both internal courses offered to company personnel and external courses. 
 
## Reports

 

Use reports in the Human Resources menu to view a summary and/or breakdown of employee data. You can also generate customized reports on employee data.
