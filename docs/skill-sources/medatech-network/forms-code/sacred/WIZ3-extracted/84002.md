# Basic Finance Main Wizard

 
## Introduction

 

This wizard describes basic financial processes for Sales , Purchasing and Accounting as they are handled in Priority . 
 

Click the hypertext links to open specialized wizards designed to assist you in the performance of specific tasks related to the above-mentioned processes. Each wizard features an introductory page providing a brief overview of the subject, followed by detailed instructions. Most of these wizards are also accessible from the main Priority Financials menu and its sub-menus. 
 

Use this wizard only after the basic system set up has been completed and the basic parameters of the financial system, including customers and vendors, have been defined. If you have not already done so, click to run the Getting Started Main Wizard . 
 
## Note: 

 

Financial documents, such as invoices and receipts, are used to record sales and purchasing transactions. Once final, the financial document automatically generates a corresponding journal entry, which can be posted automatically or manually. Such attributes of financial documents must be defined for all document types before you begin working in the Accounts Receivable and the Accounts Payable modules. You can also define which users are allowed access to documents recorded for a given branch or sales rep, or for secured customers and vendors. 
 
## Sales

 

The first stage in the financial processing of sales is issuing an invoice to a customer. Priority offers various billing options . You can use a sales invoice to bill the customer for a sale and record the shipment of goods. You can record multi-shipment invoices to bill customers independent of a shipment (or to bill customers for non-inventory items such as work hours) and to manage advance payments received from customers. Finally, you can use over-the-counter invoices to record immediate payment for a sale, and pro forma invoices to send temporary invoices to the customer before the sale is final, or until the goods are received by the customer. You can also send the customer a credit memo for returned goods or over-charging. 
 

 Priority allows you to document payments from customers in a receipt which can be linked to one or more invoices. In the receipt you can specify method of payment (cash, check, bank transfer or credit card). The system will automatically create a journal entry once the receipt is finalized, and will reconcile it with the journal entry for the invoice. 
 

You can manage cashiers and track financial transactions. You can also make deposits , transferring funds from a particular cashier to a bank account, credit company or other cashier. And you can refund customers by check, bank transfer or credit voucher. 
 

If the bank or credit card company returns a customer`s payment, you can open a return payment document to record the details, including additional charges on the return if appropriate, and to debit the A/R account. You can also calculate financial charges on the missed payments, and include them in the invoice. 
 

By default, the system automatically flags any customers with return payments as restricted , for which you will not be able to record any transactions (e.g., orders, shipments, invoices). Customers can also be restricted if they exceed their specified credit limit . Aging reports can assist you in tracking data pertaining to outstanding customer debt and the open transactions on which it is based. 
 
## Purchasing

 

 Priority provides you with a number of ways to record purchase invoices . When goods are received with an invoice, you can record the charge and receipt of goods in the vendor invoice. When the invoice is received independently of the goods, record a multi-GRV invoice instead (and link it to the appropriate GRV). You can open a debit memo to request credit for returned goods or overcharging, or a credit memo to record credit from a vendor. Use a multi-GRV invoice to record advance payments to vendors for a large purchase order with multiple shipments. 
 

You can record vendor payments by check or bank transfer . In both of these instances, you can open each payment document individually or prepare a batch for many vendors. 
 

Shipping and other expenses can be recorded in shipping vouchers and distributed among part costs. In the dual-currency package, costs related to imports and bonded warehouses can be tracked as well. 
 

 Note: In the dual-currency package, it is important to set up withholding tax definitions for each vendor prior to payment. 
 
## Accounting

 
## Journal Entries

 

Journal entries are a primary accounting tool whereby financial transactions are recorded in the system. While most financial transactions are recorded automatically, you can also record simple and complex journal entries manually . Priority allows you to organize a batch of journal entries recorded manually by a particular user. Similarly, using complex journal entries, you can record prepaid expenses and deferred income , where distribution of payments is recorded in the invoice or directly in the journal entry. 
 

 Note: You can block user access to sensitive entries by defining secured accounts. 
 
## Taxes

 

Using Priority you can automatically track taxes . The tax code assigned in any given financial document determines the tax account appearing in the journal entry. 
 

 For dual-currency users: If your company is required to prepay portions of their income tax , Priority allows you to calculate prepayment installments. 
 
## Executive Reports

 

 ·        Priority provides you with summary reports to calculate and view sales, purchasing and accounting data easily and effectively. You can perform a variety of analyses by varying the data cross-sections that are retrieved, the manner in which they are sorted and their levels of hierarchy. BI reports allow you to compare data from different years, periods and companies in various graphic formats. You can also determine the exact cross-section of data to be displayed in the data cube, by drilling down within categories and accessing only the specific data that you want to see. 
 

 ·        Furthermore, Priority provides easy-to-use report generators that allow you to create an unlimited number of custom reports for sales, purchasing and accounting data.
