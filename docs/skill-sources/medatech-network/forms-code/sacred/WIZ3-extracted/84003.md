# Advanced Finance Main Wizard

 
## Introduction

 

This wizard describes various advanced financial processes as they are handled in Priority . 
 

Click the hypertext links to open specialized wizards designed to assist you in the performance of specific tasks related to these processes. Each wizard features an introductory page providing a brief overview of the subject, followed by detailed instructions. Most of these wizards are also accessible from the main Priority Financials menu and its sub-menus. 
 
## Reconciliations

 

 Priority enables automatic and manual account reconciliation , where journal entries for the same account (e.g., A/R or A/P account; prepaid expenses account) are reconciled with each other, as well as bank reconciliation , where entries in your bank statement are reconciled against recorded journal entries. You can define additional reconciliation codes for both types of reconciliation, as necessary. 
 
## Ledgers and Financial Statements

 

The Balance Sheet , Profit and Loss and Trial Balance reports are all predefined in the system. Priority provides a default hierarchy of headings and items for your balance sheet and P&L reports; trial balance items are created when accounts are set up automatically. In both instances, you can add new items and change their order of appearance in your financial statements. You must link a balance sheet or P&L item to every account defined in the system. 
 

 Priority provides an easy-to-use report generator that allows you to create custom financial statements . Additional report generators can be used to create custom reports for account ledgers and financial transactions . 
 
## Cash Flow

 

A number of reports in Priority can be used to generate and analyze current and forecasted cash flow for your company. By assigning cash flow codes to relevant journal entries, you can track withdrawals and deposits into and out of your bank accounts by expense or income type. 
 
## Costing

 

 Priority calculates part costs, transaction costs and inventory values, taking into account materials, labor and other costs, both for manufacturing settings and for resale businesses (purchases and assemblies) . By performing cost analysis and updating costing data on a regular basis, you will have accurate information for determining profitability and pricing. 
 
## Budgets

 

 Priority allows you to track the flow of expenses (and income) in your company, and to compare the budget appropriations to actual figures, by managing budgets . Priority automatically debits budget items at every stage of the purchasing (or sales) transaction; these charges progress automatically from one stage to the next (e.g., from purchase requisition to order to receipt of goods). As a budget item can be assigned to any financial transaction, you can compare budget appropriations with actual expenses/income at any stage of the purchasing or sales process. 
 
## Cost Allocation

 

 Priority ’s cost allocation module allows you to define different portions of your enterprise as profit and cost centers in order to analyze revenues, direct costs and indirect costs, and to compare profitability and expenses. 
 

Any sales or purchasing transaction (from the sales order to the customer invoice, and from the purchase requisition to the purchase invoice) can be linked to a profit or cost center. You can define up to five sets of profit/cost centers within your company in order to analyze profitability on different levels. 
 
## COGS

 

The COGS (Cost of Goods Sold) program records journal entries for inventory transactions that have occurred over a given period, crediting and debiting special pre-defined GL accounts. Priority offers a choice between two methods: Perpetual Cost of Goods based on standard costs (with adjustment transactions for variance) and Periodic Cost of Goods, based on actual costs. Both methods are based on costs calculated by the costing program. A variety of reports aid in analyzing COGS data. 
 

When you work with COGS, inventory accounts always paint an accurate picture of inventory valuations and of the cost of goods sold, and financial statements reflect the true financial status of company assets and company profit. 
 
## Fixed Assets

 

Using Priority , you can easily manage and track depreciation of fixed assets in your company: record the purchase and sale of assets, calculate depreciation for tax authorities and record it in the ledger, calculate separate depreciation for accounting purposes, record the sale/scrapping of an asset and run various fixed asset reports. A report generator also allows you to create your own fixed asset reports . 
 
## Bank Loans

 

Use Priority ’s loans module to handle all aspects of bank loans : record trial loans in the system (to experiment with interest rates and amortization schedules), calculate and make payments of principal and interest and record journal entries for loan payments. View loan data in a variety of reports, or use the loan report generator to create your own custom reports. 
 
## Exchange Rate Adjustments

 

All accounts in Priority can be maintained in up to three different currencies: the local currency, the second currency (for dual-currency users) and the account currency (if different from the first two). When fluctuations in exchange rates between currencies result in variance between the account balance in the local (or second) currency and the balance in the account currency, you can run one of the exchange rate adjustment programs to create adjustment entries in the entry journal. 
 
## Indexing (dual-currency users only)

 

 Priority allows you to run financial statements with account data linked to the price index . Use the programs available in the system to calculate and record indexed opening balances for a given fiscal year, to record journal entries with indexed values and to run indexed financial statements and reports.
