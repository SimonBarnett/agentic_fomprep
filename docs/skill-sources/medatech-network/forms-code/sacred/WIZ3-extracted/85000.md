# Demand Forecasts Wizard

 

 Priority allows you to plan your annual sales cycle using demand forecasts and forecast orders. The demand forecast reflects expected part demands in each month of the year. On its own, it is solely informative. Thus, if you want MRP (material requirements planning) to take forecasted quantities into account, you need to also open forecast orders. MRP treats each forecast order item as a monthly demand for inventory, and will deduct such expected inventory from the demand generated by actual sales orders. In this way, you can produce in advance the goods that you expect to sell to customers each month. 
 

You can create one or more forecasts for any given year. For instance, you can create a different forecast for each customer or group of customers. You can thereby record anticipated ordered quantities throughout the year. 
 

A demand forecast for a current or future month can be revised at any stage, even after forecast orders have been opened. After revising a forecast, you will have to rerun the Prepare Forecast Order program, thereby canceling the existing forecast order and replacing it with an updated version that reflects the changes made. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        For users of the MRP for Manufacturers module: You can also use the Demand Forecast facility to plan your production cycles and generate inventory, by opening forecasts and designating desired monthly quantities of manufactured goods, and opening forecast orders based on these forecasts. MRP will take these forecast orders into account and generate work orders for them.
