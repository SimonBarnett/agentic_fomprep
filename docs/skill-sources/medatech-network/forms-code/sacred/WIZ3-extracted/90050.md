# Converting Accounts Wizard

 

 Priority offers the option of defining a GL account so that it also functions as an A/R account. This allows you, for instance, to record invoices for employees who purchase goods in your warehouse outlet (where the amount of the invoice is deducted from the employee's salary). Along similar lines, you can define a GL account to function also as an A/P account. 
 

Finally, you can convert an A/R or A/P account into a GL account. This is useful when you want to fix accounts recorded in the past in the wrong Chart of Accounts (e.g., an employee account was recorded in the Chart of Accounts Receivable or Payable). 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Define a GL account to serve also as an A/R account 
 

 Define a GL account to serve also as an A/P account 
 

 Convert an A/R account into a GL account 
 

 Convert an A/P account into a GL account
