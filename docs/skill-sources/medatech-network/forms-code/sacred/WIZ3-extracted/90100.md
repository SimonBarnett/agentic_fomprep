# Finance Charges Wizard

 

 Priority allows you to charge customers interest on late payments. Finance charges are calculated automatically in accordance with parameters defined per customer. When the amount owed is lower than the ceiling defined for a given customer, regular rates are calculated. When the amount owed exceeds that ceiling, penalty rates are calculated. These values may be revised as needed (e.g., in accordance with changes in bank interest rates). 
 

Any invoice for finance charges is recorded in the Multi-Shipment Invoices form, for which a special finance charge journal entry is then recorded. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Set up finance charges 
 

 Update finance charge rates 
 

 Bill a customer for finance charges 
 

 View finance charge calculations for customers
