# Advance Payments Wizard

 

The advance payment mechanism is useful when working with large orders involving multiple shipments and invoices. It entails: 
 

 ·        Deduction of advance payments received from customers (for a sales order) from invoices billing the shipments of the ordered goods. 
 

 ·        Deduction of advance payments made to vendors (for a purchase order) from purchase invoices billing receipts of the ordered goods. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Record advance payments from customers 
 

 Record advance payments to vendors 
 

 View the advance payment balance of a given order
