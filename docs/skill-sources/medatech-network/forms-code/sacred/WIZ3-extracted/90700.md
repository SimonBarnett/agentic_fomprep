# Part Revisions, BOM Revisions & ECOs Wizard

 

 Priority allows you to document and track engineering changes in the manufacture of processed parts. You can create codes representing the reasons for and implications of such engineering changes, and assign the appropriate codes to each Engineering Change Order, or ECO. 
 

Engineering changes often necessitate the creation of new BOM revisions or even part revisions. This is documented by linking an ECO number to either type of revision. You can even open a new BOM revision from within the Engineering Change Orders (ECO) form. 
 

Maintaining revisions enables you to work with the most up-to-date BOM while keeping a record of the changes made to it throughout its history. However, the decision to maintain BOM revisions for a part can only be made once and is irreversible. Each BOM revision can differ from its predecessor in its child parts, parent/child ratios and operations . It can also contain changes to child part designations and customization restrictions . At any given point there is only one valid and active revision of the BOM that defines the part’s components to the system for planning and inventory purposes. 
 

You can also maintain part revisions, in tandem with or independently of BOM revisions. You can specify a revision number for both the raw material purchased from a vendor and the part manufactured from it. Part revisions appear in virtually every inventory transaction document.
