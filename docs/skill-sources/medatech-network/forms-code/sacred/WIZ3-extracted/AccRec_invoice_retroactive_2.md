# To revise invoice amounts and due dates:

 

 1 .  For each unreconciled entry item, enter the Split Amount sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  As necessary, split each entry amount into separate sums and/or change the due date(s).
 Tip : Whenever you record a due date, the balance of the entry is automatically filled in, in the Sum column, for your convenience. Change this as necessary. 
 

 3 .  The changes you have made are still temporary. To actually update the reconciliation data, return to the upper-level form and select Update Reconciliation Amounts from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 

 4 .  Retrieve all records in this form again to view results. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to undo the update of reconciliation data if you are not satisfied with results. 
 

 ·        Click to reconcile any of the installments with a receipt . 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen .
