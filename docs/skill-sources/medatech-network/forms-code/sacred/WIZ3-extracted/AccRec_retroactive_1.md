# Retroactive Reconciliation of Invoices With Receipts

 

If you work with the “By Invoice” reconciliation method (i.e., tracking of partial payments), you can retroactively reconcile customer invoices with receipts directly within the entry journal or ledger, rather than using the Reconciliation Worksheet. 
 

Specifically, you can access an entry for an invoice and break down the payment amount into separate installments, in preparation for reconciliation with receipts. Example. 
 

Furthermore, you can access an entry for a receipt and flag the invoices being paid. You can even record partial payments against specific invoices and amounts still unreconciled, just as you can do when recording a new customer receipt. Example. 
 
## Note:

 

There are two possible work methods: You can access all unreconciled items in a given A/R account and deal with each one in turn. Or you can work on specific entries in the entry journal. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Break down the payment amount in an invoice entry into separate installments 
 

 Retroactively reconcile a receipt with invoices 
 

 Revise updates to reconciliation data
