# To record installments for an invoice entry:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts Receivable form and retrieve the customer's account. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Unreconciled Transactions sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Move to the entry item for the invoice. 
 Note : You are currently working within the ledger. Click if you prefer to work in the entry journal .
