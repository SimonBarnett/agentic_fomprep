# Closing a Shipping Voucher

 

After you have attached all of the necessary purchase invoices and expense invoices to the shipping voucher, and recorded the entry of the imported goods into the bonded warehouse, you should close the voucher. 
 

Each Release from Bonded Warehouse document that you link to a shipping voucher uses the data recorded in the voucher (in addition to the customs invoice) to calculate the proportion of expenses to be assigned to the released goods. Closing the shipping voucher can prevent any miscalculation of these expenses. 
 
## To close a shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the desired voucher. 
 

 2 .  Select Close Shipping Voucher from the list of Direct Activations . The document status will change to a final status and the Closed column (in the References tab) will be flagged automatically.
