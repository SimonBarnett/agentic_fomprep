# Working With Shipping Vouchers for Imported Goods in a Bonded Warehouse

 

When imported goods are stored in a bonded warehouse , shipping vouchers are used to document the incoming shipment and record the expenses incurred by the goods prior to their entry into the warehouse (e.g., shipping expenses). All further expenses (e.g., storage charges, customs duties) are recorded when the goods are released (fully or partially) into inventory. 
 

The first thing to do after your order has been confirmed is to open a shipping voucher for the incoming goods. As you begin to receive invoices for the goods and their shipping expenses, you can attach them to the shipping voucher, which is itemized automatically according to the invoice items. You may also add expenses to the shipping voucher manually. Example. 
 

When the goods themselves are received, record their arrival in a GRV (or vendor invoice, when the goods arrive together with their invoices). 
 

Once you have received all of the necessary invoices and attached them to the voucher, you can run a program that automatically itemizes the voucher with the contents of the bonded warehouse, based on the purchased parts in the attached invoices. At this point it is recommended to close the shipping voucher. If you should then receive an additional invoice (or discover additional expenses that need to be recorded manually), you will need to reopen the voucher, add the new expenses and close it again.
