# Itemizing the Imported Goods in the Shipping Voucher

 

When you have received all of the relevant invoices for expenses and customs and attached them to the shipping voucher, you are ready to itemize the voucher with the contents of the bonded warehouse. 
 
## To itemize the imported goods:

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the desired shipping voucher. 
 

 2 .  Click the Bonded Warehouse tab and specify the Bonded Warehouse into which the goods were received. 
 

 3 .  Select Auto Record Bonded Ware Contents from the voucher's list of Direct Activation s . This will itemize in the Contents of the Bonded Warehouse sub-level form all the purchased goods (but not expenses) billed in the invoices that are attached to the voucher.
 Note: Though both shipped goods and their expenses are added to the shipping voucher in the same manner, the system identifies expense items and does not assign them to the bonded warehouse. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Contents of the Bonded Warehouse sub-level form and record each item's Platform Number , as denoted by the customs authorities. 
 
## Note:

 

Click to close the shipping voucher.
