# Adding a Purchase or Expense Invoice to a Shipping Voucher 

 

Both shipped goods and the expenses related to their shipment are added to the shipping voucher in the same manner – by linking the invoice in which they are billed to the voucher. The costing program identifies the expense items in an expense invoice, and distributes them, together with any additional expenses recorded in the voucher, among the part items in the purchase invoices. 
 

You can link an invoice that contains both parts and expenses to the voucher; in this case, the costing program will only distribute those expenses among the parts listed in that invoice. You can also link individual invoice items to a shipping voucher. 
 
## To link an invoice to a shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·     Record and itemize the invoice. When you are done, click the References tab. 
 

 ·     If the invoice has already been recorded in the system, retrieve it in the Invoices in Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 2 .  Record the voucher number in the Shipping Voucher column.
 Tip: Press F6 to enter the Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the relevant shipping voucher. Press F8 to leave the form and automatically copy the voucher number. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To view all invoices in a particular shipping voucher, use the Purchase Invoices sub-level of the Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 ·        Click to manually distribute any items in an expense invoice among the purchased parts in the voucher , thereby overriding their defined burden types
