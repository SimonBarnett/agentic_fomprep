# Manually Recording Expenses in a Shipping Voucher 

 

You can record actual expenses in a shipping voucher if the expense item's exact cost is known in advance. You can also record projected expenses, i.e., those costs that are expected to become actual expenses over time, but the exact numbers are not yet available. 
 
## To record expenses in a shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the desired shipping voucher. 
 

 2 .  To record actual expenses, enter the Additional Expenses sub-level form and fill in the details of each expense.
 Note: This form also displays individual expense items linked to the shipping voucher from invoices. In this case, the Invoice and Ln (line) columns will be filled in (these columns will remain empty for expenses recorded manually). 
 

 3 .  To record projected expenses, enter the parallel Projected Expenses sub-level form and fill in the details of each expense. 
 
## Tip:

 

Click to distribute an expense in a different manner than its defined burden type.
