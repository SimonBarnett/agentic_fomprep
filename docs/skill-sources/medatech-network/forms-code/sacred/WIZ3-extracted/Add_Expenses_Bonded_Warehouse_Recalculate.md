# Recording Additional Costs After Some Goods Have Been Released

 

Occasionally an expense invoice will be received after the shipping voucher is closed and there has been a partial release of goods from the bonded warehouse. In such a case, you must reopen the shipping voucher, add the new expenses and recalculate the distribution of costs in each Release from Bonded Warehouse document attached to the voucher. 
 

 Note: 
 

If, in such a case, the costing program was run after the release was recorded, you must rerun costing after the release documents have been updated; otherwise, the new expenses will not be taken into account. 
 
## To record new expenses in the shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the desired voucher. 
 

 2 .  Select Reopen Shipping Voucher from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .  Add the new expenses to the shipping voucher.
 Tip: Click to select the appropriate instructions. 
 

 4 .  Select Close Shipping Voucher from the list of Direct Activations .
