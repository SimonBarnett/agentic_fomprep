# Releasing Goods from a Bonded Warehouse

 

As mentioned previously, the costing program does not relate to goods stored in a bonded warehouse as inventory. Consequently, it does not take into account the data recorded in a shipping voucher until it is linked to a Release from Bonded Warehouse document. 
 

This document must include the total sum of expenses owed for the parts to be released, composed of the following: 
 

 ·        Expenses incurred by the importation process prior to the entry of parts into the bonded warehouse (i.e., those expenses recorded in the shipping voucher). 
 

 ·        Customs duties recorded in the customs invoice. 
 

 ·        Additional expenses incurred after entry into the bonded warehouse (e.g., storage charges, handling charges). 
 
## To record a release document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Release from Bonded Warehouse form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the General tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the Date . The Document number is assigned automatically. 
 

 3 .  Specify the Shipping Voucher recorded for the imported goods. The relevant Bonded Warehouse and Vendor appear automatically.
