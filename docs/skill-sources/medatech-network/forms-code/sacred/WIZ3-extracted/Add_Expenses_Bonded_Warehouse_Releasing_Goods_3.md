 9 .  Enter the Released Items sub-level form. The form automatically displays the current balance of goods in the bonded warehouse (line items that have already been released in a previous document appear with a quantity of 0 ). 
 

 10 .  For each item to be released from the warehouse, do one of the following: 
 

 ·     If the full quantity of parts on a given line is to be released, flag the Approve column. The Quantity column will be updated automatically with the amount in the Balance to Release column (if the quantity is specified in crates, the Packing Crates (No.) column will be updated with the quantity in the Crates to Release column). 
 

 ·     Otherwise, specify the amount of parts to be released in the Quantity (or Packing Crates (No.) ) column. 
 

 Note : If you are allowed to record partially packed crates (i.e., the IPartialPack constant is set to 1 in the Logistic Constants form), you can designate the number and type of packing crates and then revise the released quantity accordingly. Once you do, however, any revision to the number or type of packing crates will have no effect on the released quantity. 
 

 11 .  Enter the Import Costs per Line Item sub-level form and specify the value of the item (either in your local currency or a designated foreign currency) as it appears in the customs manifest. 
 

 12 .  The default percentage of surcharge for releasing goods from the warehouse appears automatically in the Extra Costs (%) column (the actual amount appears in the Extra Costs column), but may be revised. Alternatively, if you know the exact amount, delete the percentage and record the sum in the Extra Costs column.
