Shipping Expenses - Releasing Goods from a Bonded Warehouse.4 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 13 .  All expenses related to the shipment of these imported goods prior to their entry into the bonded warehouse are calculated automatically on the basis of the shipping voucher and appear in the General Import Costs column, where they may be revised. 
 Tip: If you revise this column and wish to return to the calculated value, flag the Recalculate column, and refresh the screen ( Ctrl+F11 ). The new value appears in the General Import Costs column, and the Recalculate column is cleared for future use. 
 

 14 .  When all items, quantities and costs have been correctly recorded, return to the Release from Bonded Warehouse form and select Record Import Costs per Ln Item from the list of Direct Activations . This program distributes the customs duties (recorded in the designated Customs Invoice ) among the released items, proportional to their recorded value.
 Note: To view the amount of customs duty calculated for each item, re-enter the Released Items and its sub-level Import Costs per Line Item . The value appears in the Customs (Amount) column and may be revised. 
 

 15 .  In the Release from Bonded Warehouse form, click the General tab and change the Status to Final .
