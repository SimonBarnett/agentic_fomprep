# Linking a Single Expense Invoice to a Single Purchase Invoice

 

An invoice for expenses is recorded in the same manner as a regular purchase invoice. Such an invoice bills expense items (i.e., non-inventory parts with burden types). In order to distribute these expenses among parts recorded in a purchase invoice, you need to link the two invoices. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You cannot link an expense invoice to a purchase invoice if the former is final. In such a case you need to cancel the expense invoice and record a new one. 
 

 ·        For assistance in recording an expense invoice, run the Purchase Invoices Wizard . 
 
## To link an expense invoice to a purchase invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Multi-GRV Invoices form and record or retrieve the desired expense invoice. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  In the Ship Exp Inv Code column, select the code for the appropriate purchase invoice type. 
 

 4 .  In the Shipping Exp Invoice column, specify the number of the purchase invoice. 
 
## Tip:

 

To have a record of these expenses in the purchase invoice itself, open the purchase invoice and record the details of the expense invoice to which it is linked in the Invoice/Memo - Remarks sub-level form.
