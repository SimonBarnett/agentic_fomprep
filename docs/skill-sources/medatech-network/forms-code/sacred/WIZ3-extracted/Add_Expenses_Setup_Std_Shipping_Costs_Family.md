Shipping Expenses - Setup: Standard Shipping Costs for Part Families 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To define standard shipping costs for all parts newly assigned to a given part family:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Families form and retrieve the desired part family. 
 

 2 .  Record a standard Air Cost (%) , Sea Cost (%) and/or Land Cost (%) , as appropriate, as a percentage of the unit price of all parts in the family. 
 

 3 .  Select the appropriate default cost type in the Shipping Cost Typ e column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        The costs defined in this form appear as the default for parts that are newly assigned to this family, but may be revised. 
 

 ·        The costing program will not take into account standard shipping costs defined for the part family, but rather the costs defined for parts (in the Financial Parameters for Parts form) or for vendor parts (in the Shipping Costs per Part form).
