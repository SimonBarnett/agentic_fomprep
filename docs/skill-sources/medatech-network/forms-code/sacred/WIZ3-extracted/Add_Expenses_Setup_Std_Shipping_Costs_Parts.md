Shipping Expenses - Setup: Standard Shipping Costs for Parts 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To define standard shipping costs for a part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Parameters for Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Record a standard Air Cost (%) , Sea Cost (%) and/or Land Cost (%) , as appropriate, as a percentage of the part's unit price. 
 

 3 .  Select the appropriate default cost type in the Shipping Cost Type column. This value will appear as the default for this part in any purchase document that you open (unless a different type of shipping cost was defined for the vendor part), but may be revised. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        The costing program will not take standard shipping costs into account unless the Shipping Cost Type column is filled in. 
 

 ·        The shipping costs in this form are filled in automatically when the part is newly assigned to a part family, but may be revised.
