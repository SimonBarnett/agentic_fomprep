Shipping Expenses - Setup: Canceling Standard Shipping Costs for Vendor Parts 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To prevent the calculation of standard shipping costs for parts purchased from a given vendor:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Parameters for Vendors form and retrieve the desired vendor. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Shipping Costs per Part sub-level form and move to the desired part. 
 

 3 .  Select N in the Shipping Cost Type column.
