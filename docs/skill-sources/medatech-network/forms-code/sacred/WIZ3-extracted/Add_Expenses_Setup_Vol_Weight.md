# To define weight and volume for purchased parts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Parameters for Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Click the Shipping Expenses tab. Specify the appropriate values in the Weight , Unit and Volume columns. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        The predefined unit of volume is the cubic meter (cu). This unit applies to all parts in the system; thus it should not be changed once you have begun working with volume. If you are working with volume for the first time and want to change the volume unit, revise its name in the Part Units form. 
 

 ·        Click to perform additional setups .
