# Recording a Customs Invoice and Linking it to a Shipping Voucher

 

A customs invoice is an expense invoice in which your customs agent bills you for customs duties, as well as any additional expenses incurred during transport (e.g., local handling charges). The latter items are taxed in the usual manner (i.e., as a given percentage of the total sum of each part). The customs duties, on the other hand, are calculated according to the country or zone from which the goods have been imported. It is thus expedient to flag the customs duties as non-taxable, and manually record the amount of tax due for the customs duties. The designated amount of VAT is automatically transferred to the VAT from Customs Duty account, and is not taken into account by the costing program. 
 
## To record a customs invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Vendor Invoices or Multi-GRV Invoices form and record the expenses for which you are being billed.
 Tip: For assistance, run the Purchase Invoices Wizard . 
 

 2 .  In the Vendor column, specify the customs agent (usually the shipping company).
 Tip : Click if the agent is not yet defined as a vendor. 
 

 3 .  Specify the Date and Invoice Number appearing on the original invoice. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form and record the Customs Manifest No. 
 

 5 .  Enter the parallel Invoice Items sub-level form and record the expenses billed in the invoice. 
 

 6 .  Use the predefined Customs part to record the customs duties. The Taxable column will remain unflagged. 
 

 7 .  Use the predefined Tax part to record the taxes due on the customs duties. The Taxable column will remain unflagged. 
 

 8 .  Return to the upper-level form.
