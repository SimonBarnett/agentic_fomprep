Shipping Expenses - Attaching a Customs Invoice to a Shipping Voucher 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To attach the customs invoice to the shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Click the References tab and press F6 from the Shipping Voucher column. The Shipping Vouchers form will open. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the relevant shipping voucher. Click the Shipment tab and record the Customs Man. # as it appears in the customs invoice (for reference purposes only). 
 

 3 .  Press F8 to leave the Shipping Vouchers form. The voucher number will be copied automatically to the Shipping Voucher column. 
 

 4 .  Select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to manually distribute any expense items in this invoice among the purchased parts in the voucher , thereby overriding their defined burden types. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the shipping vouchers menu .
