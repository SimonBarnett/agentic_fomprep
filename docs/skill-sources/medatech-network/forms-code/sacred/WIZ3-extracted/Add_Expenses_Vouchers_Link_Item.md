# Adding an Item from an Invoice to a Shipping Voucher

 

When individual items in an expense invoice are for expenses incurred by several different purchased parts that have been included in different shipping vouchers, you can assign each expense item to the appropriate voucher. You can also distribute such expenses manually, thus overriding their defined burden types. 
 

When individual items in a purchase invoice are included in different shipping vouchers, you can assign each purchased item to the appropriate voucher. 
 
## To add an individual item:

 

 1 .  Enter the Invoices in Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the desired invoice.
 Tip: Retrieve by Invoice Type and Vendor Number . 
 

 2 .  Enter the Invoice Items sub-level form. Each invoice item appears on a separate line. 
 

 3 .  Move to the item you want to include in the voucher, and record the voucher number in the Shipping Voucher column.
 Tips: 
 

 ·     Press F6 to access the Shipping Vouchers form and then retrieve the voucher. Press F8 to exit the form and copy the voucher number into the Invoice Items form. 
 

 ·     Click to distribute this expense in a different manner than its defined burden type. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To view all expense items included in a shipping voucher, use the Additional Expenses form, sub-level of the Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 ·        To view all purchased items included in a shipping voucher, use the Additional Parts form, sub-level of the Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 ·        You can also use the Invoices in Shipping Vouchers form to link an entire invoice to a shipping voucher.
