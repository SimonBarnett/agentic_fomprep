Shipping Expenses - Adding Invoices to a Shipping Voucher 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To link an invoice to a shipping voucher while recording the invoice:

 

 1 .  Record and itemize the invoice. When you are done, click the References tab. 
 

 2 .  Select P in the Ship. Voucher Type column. 
 

 3 .  Select the desired voucher from the Choose list of the Shipping Voucher column. 
 
## To link an existing invoice to a shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Invoices in Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the desired invoice. 
 

 2 .  Record the voucher number in the Shipping Voucher column.
 Tip: Press F6 to enter the Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the relevant shipping voucher. Press F8 to leave the form and automatically copy the voucher number. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        If you are using the dual-currency package, enter the Invoice Items sub-level of the Invoices in Shipping Vouchers form. For each part, the Country of Origin is taken automatically from the same column in the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level of the original invoice (or, if that column is empty, from the Country column in the Vendors form). If the part was shipped from a different country than that recorded for the invoice or vendor, specify the country from which it was shipped. This will affect the customs estimate. 
 

 ·        To view all invoices in a particular shipping voucher, use the Purchase Invoices sub-level of the Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 ·        To view the purchase order items billed in all invoices included in the shipping voucher, use the Purchase Order Items in Voucher sub-level of the Shipping Vouchers form. To update the status and/or due date of all open purchase items in these invoices, select Update Purchase Order Items from the list of Direct Activations of the Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 ·        Click to manually distribute any items in an expense invoice among the purchased parts in the voucher , thereby overriding their defined burden types 
 

 ·        Dual-currency users: Click to add a customs invoice to the voucher .
