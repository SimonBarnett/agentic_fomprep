# To open a shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 2 .  Record the date in the Date Opened column. The Voucher Number appears automatically.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 3 .  Select the Currency of the voucher. If the voucher includes invoices with different currencies, specify the main currency, and the currency of each invoice will appear automatically in the itemized form. 
 

 4 .  If you want to base the shipping voucher on a purchase order, specify the Order in the References tab. The details of the order (e.g., Vendor Number ) will appear in the appropriate columns, but may be revised. 
 

 5 .   Dual-currency users: In the References tab, select your customs agent in the Customs Agent – Code column. 
 Tip: If you have not defined a customs agent, click to do so . 
 

 6 .  Click the Shipment tab and record the shipping details. Details that are as yet unavailable can be added later. 
 

 7 .  Enter the Attachments sub-level form and attach any relevant documents to the shipping voucher. 
 

 8 .  Change the Status of the shipping voucher.
 Tip: Later you can review the voucher's history of changing statuses in the History of Statuses sub-level form. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can use the checklist in the Shipping Voucher Checklist form, a sub-level of Vendors to check the desired order of tasks for this vendor. 
 

 ·        Click to add an invoice to the shipping voucher.
