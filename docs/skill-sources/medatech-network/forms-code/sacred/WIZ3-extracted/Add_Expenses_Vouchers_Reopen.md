# Adding Expenses to a Closed Shipping Voucher

 

You may receive a late invoice for an expense related to goods listed in a closed shipping voucher. In such a case, you may reopen the voucher in order to attach the invoice. 
 
## Note:

 

If the costing program was run after the voucher was closed, you will need to run it again after updating and closing the voucher; otherwise, the new data will not be taken into account. 
 
## To reopen a voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the desired voucher. 
 

 2 .  Select Reopen Shipping Voucher from the list of Direct Activations . The check mark will be removed from the Closed column (in the References tab). 
 

 3 .  Enter the Purchase Invoices sub-level form and attach the new invoice. 
 

 4 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·     Return to the upper-level form and select Close Shipping Voucher from the list of Direct Activations . A check mark will appear in the Closed column. 
 

 ·     If you intend to leave the voucher open after attaching the new invoice (e.g., you have been informed of further invoices that are on their way), change the Status of the voucher to what it was prior to closing.
