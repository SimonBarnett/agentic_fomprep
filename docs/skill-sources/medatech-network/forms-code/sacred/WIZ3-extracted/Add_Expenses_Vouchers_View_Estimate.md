# Viewing Customs Estimates in a Shipping Voucher

 

 Priority shipping vouchers feature a program that provides an estimate of the customs duties to be paid for the imported goods, based on a defined percentage of the part price. You can compare this estimate to the actual customs duties recorded in the customs agent's invoice. 
 
## Before you begin:

 

 ·        Click to set up customs rates if you have not already done so . 
 

 ·        Enter the Vendors form and make sure that the vendor's country is recorded (in the Address & Phone tab). 
 
## To calculate the customs estimate:

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the desired voucher. 
 

 2 .  Select Calculate Expense Burdening from the list of Direct Activations .
 Note: This program also calculates the distribution of expenses among the purchased parts in the voucher. 
 
## To view the customs estimate:

 

 ·        Enter the Customs Estimates sub-level form. The customs estimate for each part appears in the Customs Amt column (in your local currency). 
 Tip: If no value appears in the Country of Origin column, and the part comes from a country that does not belong to the 'Rest of the World' category (i.e., the country belongs to the EU or NAFTA or has a separate trade agreement), you can record it manually in the Invoice Items form (sub-level of Purchase Invoices , itself a sub-level of Shipping Vouchers ). Then return to the voucher and rerun the Calculate Expense Burdening program.
