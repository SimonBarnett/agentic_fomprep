# Viewing the Advance Payment Balance

 

In the original order (sales or purchase), you can view the balance of the advance payment, that is, the amount that still has to be deducted from future invoices for these ordered goods. 
 
## To view the advance payment balance:

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Enter the Sales Orders form. 
 

 ·      Enter the Purchase Orders form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the order for which the advance payment was made. 
 

 3 .  Click the Payment Info tab. The balance appears in the Advance Payment Balance column.
