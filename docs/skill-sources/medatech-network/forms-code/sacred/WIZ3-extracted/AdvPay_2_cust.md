# Advance Payments from Customers

 

When you receive an advance payment from a customer, you need to: 
 

 ·        Record, for the sales order in question, the percentage to deduct (against the advance payment) from any future invoice for this order 
 

 ·        Record an invoice for the payment received. 
 

As a result, the designated percentage to be deducted will be applied to any multi-shipment invoice for a shipment involving those ordered goods, until the entire advance has been used up. Example. 
 
## Note:

 

Advance payments can only be deducted from multi-shipment invoices (and not sales invoices or over-the-counter invoices). Thus, any related shipment must be recorded in the Customer Shipments form and billed in the Multi-Shipment Invoices form. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Record the percentage to deduct from future invoices for a given sales order 
 

 Record an invoice for the advance payment itself 
 

 Record an invoice for a shipment of the ordered goods (which deducts part of the advance payment)
