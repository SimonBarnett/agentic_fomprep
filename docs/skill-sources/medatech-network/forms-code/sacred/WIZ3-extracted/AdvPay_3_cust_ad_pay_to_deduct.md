Advance Payments - Recording the Deduction Percentage for a Customer 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record the percentage to deduct from each multi-shipment invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders form and retrieve the sales order in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Payment Info tab. In the % Advance Payment to Deduct column, indicate the percentage of the total invoice amount to be automatically deducted (against the advance) from any invoice billing a shipment of goods in this order. 
 

 3 .  To allow an invoice to be recorded against this order for the advance payment, change the order status (in the Follow-up tab) to a status that allows for shipment.
