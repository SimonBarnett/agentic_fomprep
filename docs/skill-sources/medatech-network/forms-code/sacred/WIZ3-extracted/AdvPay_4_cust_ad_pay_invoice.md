Advance Payments - Recording an Invoice for an Order Payment 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record an invoice for the advance payment received for an order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Multi-Shipment Invoices form.
 Note to dual-currency users: If this is a foreign customer, use the Export Invoices form instead. 
 

 2 .  Record the number of the customer that made the payment. The customer name appears automatically.
 Note: A temporary Invoice Number (prefixed with a T ) is filled in automatically when you leave the field. It will be replaced by a permanent number when the invoice is finalized. 
 

 3 .  Today's date appears automatically; revise it if necessary. 
 

 4 .  Click the References tab. In the Order column, indicate the number of the sales order for which the advance payment was received.
 Note: The order must have a status that allows for shipment to the customer. 
 

 5 .  Click the Financial Terms tab. Check mark the Advance Payment column. 
 

 6 .  Enter the Invoice Items sub-level form. The predefined Prepay part will be filled in automatically with a quantity of 1. 
 

 7 .  In the Unit Price column, indicate the amount of the advance payment. 
 

 8 .  Return to the upper-level form and select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activation s .
