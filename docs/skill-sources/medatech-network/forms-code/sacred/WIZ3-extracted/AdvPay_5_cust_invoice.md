# Invoicing a Shipment of the Ordered Goods (deducting part of the advance payment)

 

When an advance payment has been recorded for a sales order, the first invoice recorded against a shipment of these ordered goods will deduct a portion of that advance payment (based on the percentage of the invoice total specified in the sales order) from the amount owing. This deduction will appear as a separate line item recorded automatically in the invoice. Each new invoice for a shipment of these same ordered goods will deduct an additional part of the advance payment, until the balance reaches 0. Example. 
 

The invoices for the shipments may be recorded automatically (e.g., by Direct Activation from within the Customer Shipments form or manually (in the Multi-Shipment Invoices form). There are two restrictions on how the invoice must be prepared: 
 

 ·        It must be a multi-shipment invoice. 
 

 ·        It must be based on a single shipment that is linked to the original order.
