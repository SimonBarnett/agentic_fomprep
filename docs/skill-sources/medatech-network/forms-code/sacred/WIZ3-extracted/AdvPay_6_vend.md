# Advance Payments to Vendors

 

When you make an advance payment to a vendor, you need to: 
 

 ·        Record, for the purchase order in question, the percentage to deduct (against the advance payment) from any future invoice for this order 
 

 ·        Record a purchase invoice for the advance payment. 
 

As a result, the designated percentage to be deducted will be applied to any multi-GRV invoice for a receipt involving those ordered goods, until the entire advance has been used up. Example. 
 
## Note:

 

Advance payments can only be deducted from multi-GRV invoices (and not vendor invoices). Thus, any related receipt of goods must be recorded in the Goods Receiving Vouchers form and billed in the Multi-GRV Invoices form. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Record the percentage to deduct from future invoices for a given purchase order 
 

 Record an invoice for the advance payment itself 
 

 Record an invoice for a receipt of the ordered goods (which deducts part of the advance payment)
