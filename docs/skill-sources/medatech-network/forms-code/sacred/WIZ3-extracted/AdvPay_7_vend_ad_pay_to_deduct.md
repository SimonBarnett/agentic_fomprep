Advance Payments - Recording the Deduction Percentage for a Vendor 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record the percentage to deduct from each multi-GRV invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Orders form and retrieve the purchase order in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Payment Info tab. In the % Advance Payment to Deduct column, indicate the percentage of the invoice amount to be automatically deducted (against the advance) from any multi-GRV invoice billing a shipment of goods in this order. 
 

 3 .  To allow an invoice to be recorded against this order for the advance payment, change the order status (in the Authorization & Follow-up tab) to a status that allows for receipt into inventory.
