Advance Payments - Recording an Invoice for a Purchase Payment 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record a purchase invoice for the advance payment received for an order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Multi-GRV Invoices form. 
 

 2 .  Record the number of the vendor to which payment has been made. The vendor name appears automatically.
 Note: A temporary Internal Invoice (prefixed with a T ) is filled in automatically when you leave the field. It will be replaced by a permanent number when the invoice is finalized. 
 

 3 .  Today's date appears automatically; revise it if necessary. 
 

 4 .  Specify the vendor's Invoice Number (if available). 
 

 5 .  Click the References tab. In the Order Number column, indicate the internal number of the purchase order for which the advance payment was made.
 Note: The order must have a status that allows for receipt into inventory. 
 

 6 .  Click the Payment Info tab. Check mark the Advance Payment column. 
 

 7 .  Enter the Invoice Items sub-level form. The predefined Prepay part will be filled in automatically with a quantity of 1. 
 

 8 .  In the Unit Price column, indicate the amount of the advance payment. 
 

 9 .  Return to the upper-level form and select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activation s .
