# Recording an Invoice for the Receipt of Ordered Goods (deducting part of the advance payment)

 

When an advance payment has been recorded for a purchase order, the first multi-GRV invoice recorded against a receipt of these ordered goods will deduct a portion of that advance payment (based on the percentage of the invoice total specified in the purchase order) from the amount owing. This deduction will appear as a separate line item recorded automatically in the invoice. Each new invoice for a receipt of these same ordered goods will deduct an additional part of the advance payment, until the balance reaches 0. Example. 
 

The invoices for the receipts of goods are recorded in the Multi-GRV Invoices form. There are two restrictions on how the invoice must be prepared: 
 

 ·        It must be a multi-GRV invoice. 
 

 ·        It must be based on a single receipt that is linked to the original order.
