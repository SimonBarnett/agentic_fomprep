# Opening A/R and A/P Accounts for a Specific Subsidiary

 

When the IdentCustAcc constant is set to its default value 1 , a new A/R or A/P account is created automatically in the main company when you open a new customer or vendor record. When you assign the customer/vendor to a branch, the account is transferred to the subsidiary linked to that branch. All transactions are then conducted with that subsidiary. 
 
## Before you begin

 

 ·        Enter the Financial Constants form and make sure that the IdentCustAcc constant is set to 1. 
 
## To link a customer or vendor to a branch:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers or Vendors form and retrieve the desired customer or vendor.
 Tip: To open a new customer or vendor, run the Open Customers Wizard or the Open Vendors Wizard . 
 

 2 .  Click the Details tab and select the desired Branch . 
 
## Note:

 

A/P and A/R accounts opened in this manner do not bear the prefix or suffix defined for the subsidiary in the Subsidiaries form. You can add a prefix or suffix manually to the account number, provided that no journal entries have yet been posted for that account.
