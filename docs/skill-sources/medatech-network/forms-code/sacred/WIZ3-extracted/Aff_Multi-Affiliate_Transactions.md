# Defining Journal Entry Codes for Multi-Subsidiary Entries

 

You may encounter transactions that involve more than one subsidiary. For example, a customer billed for goods purchased from several different branches may make one payment that covers all due invoices. In this case, the A/R account recorded in the receipt is in a different subsidiary than that recorded in some of the invoices. 
 

 Priority allows you to flag specific journal entry codes to automatically include transfer accounts when posting the entry, so as to balance the accounts in each subsidiary involved. Example. It is advisable to flag in advance any entry codes that are likely to require such adjustments (e.g., receipts, payments). 
 
## Before you begin:

 

 ·        Enter the Chart of Accounts form and open a transfer account for each subsidiary.
 Tip: For assistance, run the Open GL Accounts Wizard .
