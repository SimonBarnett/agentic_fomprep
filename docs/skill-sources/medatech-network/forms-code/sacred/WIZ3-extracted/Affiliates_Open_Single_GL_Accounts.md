# To open single GL accounts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 2 .  For each account, fill in the Account No. and Account Description columns. 
 

 3 .   Click the Report tab and specify an Account Group , if necessary. 
 

 4 .   Fill in the Trial Bal Item and Bal Sheet/P&L Item columns. 
Note: The value 000 appears by default in the Trial Bal Item column, but may be revised. 
 

 5 .  Click the Subsidiary tab, and specify the Subsidiary Code to which to the account belongs. 
 
## Note:

 

If this is a bank account or cashier account, it is also recommended to assign the appropriate Branch in the Bank Accounts and Cashiers forms, respectively.
