Aging Receivables Wizard - Excluding Accounts from Aging Reports 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To exclude specific A/R accounts from aging reports:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts Receivable form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired account and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 3 .  Click the Report Definitions tab and flag the Not for Aging column.
