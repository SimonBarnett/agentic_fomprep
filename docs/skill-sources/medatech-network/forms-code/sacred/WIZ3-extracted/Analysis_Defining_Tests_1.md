# Defining Laboratory Tests

 

 Priority allows you to define laboratory tests on several levels: 
 

 1 .  For operations in the routing of a specific part 
 

 2 .  For operations in a routing 
 

 3 .  For operations 
 

 4 .  For parts 
 

 5 .  For base products 
 

 6 .  For part families 
 

These levels form a hierarchy of increasing specificity, by which tests defined on a higher level take precedence over those lower in the list. 
 

In inventory documents that do not take into account the last production operation performed on the part in question (e.g., Customer Shipments , Sampling , Warehouse Assembly , Customer Returns ), the system begins to search for required tests on the part level (Level 4).
