# To define a laboratory test for an operation in the routing of a specific part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Part Routing sub-level form and move to the operation in question. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Required Analyses sub-level form and click to continue . 
 
## To define a laboratory test for an operation in a routing:

 

 1 .  Enter the Routings form and retrieve the desired routing. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Operations in Routing sub-level form and move to the operation in question. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Required Analyses sub-level form and click to continue . 
 
## To define a laboratory test for an operation: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Operations form and retrieve the desired operation. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Required Analyses sub-level form and click to continue . 
 
## To define a laboratory test for a part: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Required Analyses sub-level form and click to continue . 
 
## To define a laboratory test for a base product:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Catalogue form and retrieve the desired base product. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Required Analyses sub-level form and click to continue . 
 
## To define a laboratory test for a part family:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Families form and retrieve the desired part family. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Required Analyses sub-level form and click to continue .
