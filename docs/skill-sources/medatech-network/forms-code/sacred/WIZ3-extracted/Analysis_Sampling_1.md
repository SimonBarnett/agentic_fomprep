# Recording a Sampling Document

 

Samples for testing are subtracted from the inventory balance of the lot/work order being tested (for the specified warehouse and status) and documented with their test results in a sampling document. 
 
## To open a sampling document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sampling form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the General tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the date the sample was taken. The document number will be filled in automatically. 
 

 3 .  The default warehouse and bin for sampling appear automatically as the warehouse/bin from which the sampled materials have been taken. Revise them if necessary.
 Tip: Click to change the default warehouse for future sampling documents. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Sampled Items sub-level form. 
 

 5 .  For each sampled part, specify the Part Number and Work Order/Lot that was sampled. 
 

 6 .  In the Quantity column, specify the amount sampled.
