# Assigning a Warehouse for the Reduction of Child Parts

 

By default, the reduction of child parts that results from production reports (or subcontractor GRVs) is performed in the warehouse specified in the report. If there are materials in a different plant floor warehouse, you can assign that warehouse to the production warehouse for automatic reduction of child parts issued in bulk, and save yourself the step of issuing from the materials warehouse to the production warehouse. 
 
## To assign the warehouse:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouses form and select the Type F warehouse in which the goods in question are manufactured. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Backflush From Other Floors sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Specify the Type F warehouse from which the child parts that are issued in bulk will automatically be reduced. 
 

 4 .  Repeat the above steps for every part in every warehouse where this situation may arise.
