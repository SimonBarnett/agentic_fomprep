## V. Check the child part’s inventory balance in the last calculation of past balances:

 

Enter the Past Balances form and retrieve the part’s inventory balance as it was calculated in the last past balance.
 Tip: Use the Date , Warehouse and Part Number columns to retrieve the balance. 
 

 ·        If the balance is negative: Click to resolve the negative balance. 
 

 ·        If the balance is not negative: Click to continue.
