# Production Reporting and Receipt of Parts from Subcontractors

 

Production reports are records of operations executed upon a specific quantity of parent parts (usually as part of a work order). When an operation is executed by a subcontractor, production is indicated by the receipt into inventory of the processed parts (in a GRV). 
 

For each production report or subcontractor GRV, the Backflush program records two types of automatic transactions: 
 

 ·        Entry into inventory of the parent parts, in the reported operation. 
 

 ·        Removal from inventory of the child parts required for the operation. Example. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        If the parts are received from the subcontractor into a regular warehouse (rather than onto the floor), inventory balances of the parent are updated immediately; however, child parts are not reduced until the Backflush program is run.
