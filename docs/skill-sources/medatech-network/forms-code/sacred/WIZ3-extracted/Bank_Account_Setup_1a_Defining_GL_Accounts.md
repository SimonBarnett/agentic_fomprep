# Opening GL Accounts for Company Bank Accounts

 

You need to open a GL account for every bank account maintained by the company. 
 
## Dual-currency users only: 

 

You should also open accounts for incoming and outgoing postdated checks, in order to maximize the efficiency of cash flow reports and bank reconciliations. The deposit of a customer's postdated check into one of the company's bank accounts automatically debits the incoming checks account, and payment by means of a postdated check automatically credits the outgoing checks account. When the checks' payment dates arrive, the Cash Postdated Checks program transfers the amounts to the regular bank account. 
 
## Note:

 

For banks in which your company maintains parallel accounts in different currencies, you need to open a separate record for each currency. That is, you have to define several GL accounts for each bank account, where the bank account code, currency and GL account number are different for each one.
