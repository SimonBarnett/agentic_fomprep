# To open a GL account for a company bank account:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 2 .  For each bank account, fill in the Account No. and Account Description columns. 
 

 3 .  Click the Report tab and fill in the Trial Bal Item and Bal Sheet/P&L Item columns. 
 Note: The Trial Bal Item has a default value of 000 . You can change it now or before you run your first trial balance report. 
 

 4 .  To make it easier to identify the account, you should fill in the Account No. – Previous System column in the Previous System tab. 
 
## Note:

 

Click to record the bank account itself.
