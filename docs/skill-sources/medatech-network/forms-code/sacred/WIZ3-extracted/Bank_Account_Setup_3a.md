Set Up Bank Accounts - Recording Your Company's Bank Accounts 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record a company bank account:

 

 1 .  Enter the Bank Accounts form. 
 

 2 .  Record a Bank Account Code . You should use the account number of the matching GL account you created. 
 

 3 .  Record the details of the bank account in the Account Description, Bank, Branch and Account columns. 
 

 4 .  Record the GL Account you assigned to this bank account. 
 

 5 .  Flag the Deposit Lump Sum column if you want deposits from multiple checks to be recorded in the ledger as one debit from the bank account. 
 

 6 .  If the bank account is used by a particular branch, specify the Branch Code . 
 

 7 .   Dual-currency users: 
 

 ·      Record the appropriate accounts in the Postdated Chk Acc IN and Postdated Chk Acc OUT columns. 
 

 ·      Flag the Post dated as 1 Sum columns if you want deposits from multiple postdated checks to be recorded in the ledger as one debit from the bank account. 
 

 ·      If you want this bank's details to be printed on export invoices as a target account for the receipt of payments, flag the Payments from Abroad column. 
 

 ·      If this is a European bank account, specify the BIC - Bank ID Code and the IBAN - Account No. , which are used to identify the account during bank transfers to other European countries. 
 

 8 .  Enter the Additional Information sub-level form and record the bank's address.
