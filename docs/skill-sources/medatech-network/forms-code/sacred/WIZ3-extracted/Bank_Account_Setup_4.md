- 
 
 
 
# To record a vendor bank account:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts Payable form and retrieve the desired A/P account. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Bank Account Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Record the vendor's Bank Account number. 
 

 4 .  Choose the vendor's Bank Code. 
 

 5 .  Record the Branch Number and its address.
