- 
 
 
 
# To record a customer bank account: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the desired A/R account. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Bank Account Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Record the customer's Bank Account number. 
 

 4 .  Choose the customer's Bank Code . 
 

 5 .  Record the Branch Number and its address.
