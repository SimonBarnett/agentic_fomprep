# To review bank transfers:

 

 1 .  Enter the Payments by Bank Transfer form and retrieve all prepared payment documents by their payment date.
 Note: To assign handling of a document to another employee, specify his/her username in the Assigned to column. If you have done so for all prepared documents, you can exit the form and ignore the following instructions. 
 

 2 .  For each document, check that all data in that form and in the following sub-level forms is correct. Make changes where needed. 
 

 ·      Payment Details – this affects the amount of the bank transfer and the journal entry for the payment. 
 

 ·      Flag Invoices for Payment – the invoices that will be reconciled with the payment. 
 

 ·      Bank Transfer - Add'l Details – the address that will appear on the printout of the bank transfer; the Form 1099 category for this payment. 
 

 ·      Note to dual-currency users: If IBAN and BIC have been assigned to the vendor's bank account, these will be copied automatically into the upper-level form (in the Vendor ' s Bank Account and BIC - Bank ID Code columns of the Vendor ' s Bank tab, respectively), but may be revised.
