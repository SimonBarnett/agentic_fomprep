# Payments Not Applied to Invoices

 
## To prepare a payment without reference to an invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Payment Details form (sub-level of the Payments by Bank Transfer form). 
 

 2 .  Record the amount of payment in the Sum column. 
 

 3 .  Record any reference numbers. 
 

 4 .  Return to the upper-level form. 
 

 5 .  If the bank transfer needs to be checked before notification is sent to the bank, assign it the Checking status (in the References tab). In the Assigned to column, specify the username of the person who is responsible for checking the document. The transfer cannot be finalized until this status is changed.
 Tip: You can define work flow rules so that the assigned user changes automatically when the status is assigned. 
 

 6 .  Move to the parallel Bank Transfer - Add'l Details sub-level form and make sure the Form 1099 category is correct.
