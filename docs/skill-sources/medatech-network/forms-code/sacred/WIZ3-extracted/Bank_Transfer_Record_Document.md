# To record a payment document: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Payments by Bank Transfer form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the References tab). 
 

 2 .  Designate the Vendor ' s Account that is to receive the payment and the code of the Paying Bank Account . The Branch linked to the vendor appears automatically in the References tab, but may be revised. 
 Note: You will not be able to designate the account of a vendor for which payments are blocked (i.e., whose status in the Financial Parameters for Vendors form does not allow for payment), or whose No Payment After date (in the same form) has expired. 
 

 3 . Dual-currency users: Click the Vendor ' s Bank tab. If IBAN and BIC have been assigned to the vendor's bank account, these will be copied automatically into the Vendor ' s Bank Account and BIC - Bank ID Code columns, respectively, but may be revised. 
 

 4 .  Set text appears in the Bank Transfer - Remarks sub-level form. You can revise or add to it, as desired. These remarks will appear on the printed notifications issued to the bank and to the vendor. 
 

 5 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Click to flag outstanding invoices for payment. 
 

 ·      Click to record a payment without reference to an invoice.
