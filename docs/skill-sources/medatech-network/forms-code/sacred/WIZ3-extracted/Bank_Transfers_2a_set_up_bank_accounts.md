# To define bank accounts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Bank Accounts form. 
 

 2 .  Make sure each of your bank accounts is listed and assigned a GL account. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Additional Information sub-level form and make sure that the full address of your bank branch is recorded. This will appear in the copy of the transfer printout that is sent to your bank.
