# Combining Installment Payments

 

When an invoice calls for several payment installments, each installment appears on a separate line in the Flag Invoices for Payment form, distinguished by its payment date. Only those installments that are due up until the designated date are automatically flagged for payment. Priority ignores any amounts due after this date. 
 
## If you decide to pay later installments now as well:

 

 1 .  Enter the Flag Invoices for Payment form and retrieve the installments in question. 
 

 2 .  Manually flag them for payment (in the Prepare Payment column).
