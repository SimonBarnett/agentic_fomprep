# Printing Notifications to the Vendor and Your Bank

 
## Before You Begin

 

The remarks recorded in a given transfer document appear on the notifications issued to the vendor and to your bank. 
 
## To check remarks:

 

 1 .  In the Payments by Bank Transfer form, retrieve the desired documents. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Bank Transfer - Remarks sub-level form. Make sure the text recorded there is correct. 
 
## To print notifications to the vendor and to your bank:

 

 1 .  Enter the Payments by Bank Transfer form, and retrieve the payment document in question. 
 

 2 .  Select Print Bank Transfer (Vend Copy) and Print Bank Transfers (Bank Copy) from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .   Dual-currency users: To print the vendor notification in English when working in another language, select Print BkTransf(Vend)-ForeignLang from the list of Direct Activations . To print the bank notification in English, select Print BkTransf(Bank)-ForeignLang from the list of Direct Activations .
