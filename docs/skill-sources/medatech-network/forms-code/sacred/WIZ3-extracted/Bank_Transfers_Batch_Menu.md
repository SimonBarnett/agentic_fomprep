# Preparing Bank Transfers in a Batch

 

When preparing bank transfers in a batch, you have to do the following: 
 

 ·        flag outstanding invoices, adjusting payment amounts where applicable 
 

 ·        prepare the payment documents 
 

 ·        review the prepared bank transfers 
 

 ·        finalize the bank transfer documents 
 

 ·        print notifications to vendors and to your bank. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Flag invoices and prepare the payment documents 
 

 Review the prepared bank transfers 
 

 Finalize the bank transfer documents 
 

 Print notifications to vendors and your bank
