- 
 
 
 
# To itemize the blanket order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Itemized Blanket Order sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Record the Part Number of the ordered item. 
 

 3 .  Make sure that the part description is suitable.
 Tip: You can change the description of any part that is flagged in the Change Desc. column of the Part Catalogue form. 
 

 4 .  In the Quantity column, specify the total quantity of this part that the customer committed to order within the context of this blanket sales order.
 Tip: To check part availability, use the Inventory for Part and the Part Availability sub-level forms. 
 

 5 .  If the Unit Price appears automatically, make sure it is correct. Otherwise, specify the price manually.
 Tip: The column Price Source displays the origin of the price specified in the order. To view all possible prices for this part, enter the Part Price Options sub-level form. 
 

 6 .  Make sure that the discount percentage is correct.
 Tip: To check the sources of the discount, select Discounts Applying to Item from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 7 .  Enter the parallel Blanket Sales Orders - Remarks sub-level form and check the remarks recorded there. Change them as needed. These remarks will appear in the order confirmation. 
 

 8 .  In the Follow-up tab, change the S tatus of the order and assign it to the next user responsible for handling it.
