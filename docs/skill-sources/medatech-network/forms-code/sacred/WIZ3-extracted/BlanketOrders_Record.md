# To record a quantity-based blanket order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Blanket Sales Orders form. 
 

 2 .  Record the Customer Number . The customer's name, today's date and an order number are filled in automatically, and the order is assigned the Draft status.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the order to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Follow-up tab). 
 

 3 .  Check the Contact column to make sure that the relevant order contact appears. Change it if necessary.
 Tip: To define new contacts for the customer or to assign a default contact for orders, use the Customer Contacts form, a sub-level of the Customers form. For more information, see the Open Customers Wizard . 
 

 4 .  In the Expiration Date column, specify the date until which the conditions of the blanket order apply. After this date, you can no longer base sales orders on the blanket order. 
 

 5 .  If the blanket order price is in a foreign currency, click the Price tab and make sure that the correct Currency appears. 
 

 6 .   D ual-currency users: If the order price is linked to a foreign currency or price index, specify the currency or index in the Linked Currency column, and fill in the various linkage parameters.
 Note: For more information, see the Price Adjustment Wizard . 
 

 7 .  Click the Payment Info tab and make sure that the T ax C ode (for dual-currency users: VAT Code ) and the P ayment T erms are correct. 
 

 8 .  If you want the sales orders based on this blanket order to be billed to a third party rather than the customer's usual billing customer, choose the desired Billing Customer . 
 

 9 .  Click the Follow-up tab. If you do not want all lines of the blanket order to be copied automatically into any sales order that is based on it, flag the Disable Auto Copy column. This option is useful when the customer is planning to purchase a different group of parts in each sales order. 
 

 10 .  Click the Add'l Details tab. If the goods in this blanket order are to be delivered to a site other than the customer's main address, choose the appropriate customer site in the Site column of this form. 
 Notes: 
 

 ·              You can add to the list of customer sites in the Customer Sites form, a sub-level of the Customers form. 
 

 ·              To specify a site that is not in the Choose list (e.g., in the case of a walk-in customer), record the shipping address manually in the Shipping Address sub-level form. 
 

 ·              The shipping address defined for the blanket order will be copied automatically into any sales orders that are subsequently placed and tied to the blanket order (in the Shipping Address sub-level of the Sales Orders form).
