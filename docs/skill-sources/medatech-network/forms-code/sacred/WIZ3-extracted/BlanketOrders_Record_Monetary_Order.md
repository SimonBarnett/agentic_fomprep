Blanket Sales Orders - Recording a Blanket Order Based on Monetary Value 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record a blanket order based on monetary value:

 

 1 .  Enter the Blanket Sales Orders form. 
 

 2 .  Record the Customer Number . The customer's name, today's date and an order number are filled in automatically, and the order is assigned the Draft status.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the order to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Follow-up tab). 
 

 3 .  Check the Contact column to make sure that the relevant order contact appears. Change it if necessary.
 Tip: To define new contacts for the customer or to assign a default contact for orders, use the Customer Contacts form, a sub-level of the Customers form. For more information, see the Open Customers Wizard . 
 

 4 .  In the Expiration Date column, specify the date until which the conditions of the blanket order apply. After this date, you can no longer base sales orders on the blanket order. 
 

 5 .  If the blanket order price is in a foreign currency, click the Price tab and make sure that the correct Currency appears. 
 

 6 .   Dual-currency users: If the order price is linked to a foreign currency or price index, specify the currency or index in the Linked Currency column, and fill in the various linkage parameters.
 Note: For more information, see the Price Adjustment Wizard . 
 

 7 .  Click the Payment Info tab and make sure that the Tax Code (for dual-currency users: VAT Code ) and the Payment Terms are correct. 
 

 8 .  If you want the sales orders based on this blanket order to be billed to a third party rather than the customer's usual billing customer, choose the desired Billing Customer . 
 

 9 .  Click the Follow-up tab. Flag the Disable Auto Copy column.
 Note: If you include specific parts in the blanket order (in addition to the set monetary value), you may want to copy all line items into any sales order based on this blanket order. In that case, leave the column unflagged. 
 

 10 .  Enter the Itemized Blanket Order sub-level form. 
 

 11 .  Specify the Part Number “000”. 
 

 12 .  Specify the Quantity “1”. 
 

 13 .  In the Unit Price column, specify the total monetary value of the blanket order. 
 

 14 .  Record additional lines with other part numbers, as desired, with their own quantity and price. Sales orders for these parts will not affect the monetary balance of part "000", but will rather affect the balance of these lines.
