Bonuses - General Bonuses: Defining Eligibility 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 To define customers as eligible for regular bonuses: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form, and retrieve the desired customer(s). 
 

 2 .  Click the Details tab and flag the Gets Reg. Bonus column.
 Tip : To flag all customers, use a macro.
