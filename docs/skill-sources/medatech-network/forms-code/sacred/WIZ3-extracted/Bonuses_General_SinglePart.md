# Defining a Regular Bonus for a Single Part

 

In order to work with regular bonuses, you need to: 
 

 ·        Define the bonus for each appropriate part 
 

 ·        Flag the customers who are eligible for all such bonuses 
 
## To define a regular bonus for a single part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Bonuses for Ordering Parts form. 
 

 2 .  Choose the part number that must be ordered to receive the bonus. 
 

 3 .  Record the start and end dates of the bonus offer.
 Note : You can define a number of bonuses for the same part in the same period of time, where the sole variable is the quantity of parts one must purchase in order to receive the bonus. This is known as a graded bonus (for example, if you order 10 units of a given part you are eligible to receive 2 units of the bonus part; if you order 20 units you are eligible to receive 5 units of the bonus part). 
 

 4 .  Record the minimum quantity of this part that customers must order to be eligible for the bonus. 
 

 5 .  Choose the part number of the bonus item, and record the quantity of parts offered as the bonus. 
 

 6 .  If the bonus item is not free, record its price and choose its currency. 
 
## Note:

 

If the bonus period for a single part overlaps the bonus period of the part family to which the item belongs, a warning message will appear to the effect that both bonuses apply simultaneously.
