# Defining a Special Bonus for a Part Family

 

A special bonus is a bonus granted to a single customer or to a group of customers linked to a representative customer. 
 
## To define a special bonus for a part family:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form, and retrieve the desired customer.
 Tip : To assign the same bonus to all customers linked to a given representative customer, retrieve the representative customer. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Special Family Bonuses sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Choose the part family from which the customer must order to receive the bonus.
 Tip: To define a new part family, use the Part Families form. 
 

 4 .  Record the start and end dates of the bonus offer.
 Note : You can define a number of bonuses for the same part family in the same period of time, where the sole variable is the quantity of parts one must purchase in order to receive the bonus. This is known as a graded bonus (for example, if you order 10 units from a given part family you are eligible to receive 2 units of the bonus part; if you order 20 units you are eligible to receive 5 units of the bonus part). 
 

 5 .  Record the minimum quantity of parts from this family that customers must order to be eligible for the bonus. 
 

 6 .  Choose the part number of the bonus item, and record the quantity of it being offered. 
 

 7 .  If the bonus item is not free, record its price and choose its currency. 
 
## Note:

 

If the bonus period for a part family overlaps the bonus period of a single part within that family, a warning message will appear to the effect that both bonuses apply simultaneously.
