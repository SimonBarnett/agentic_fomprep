# Defining a Special Bonus for a Single Part 

 

A special bonus is a bonus granted to a single customer or to a group of customers linked to a representative customer. 
 
## To define a special bonus for a single part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form, and retrieve the desired customer.
 Tip : To assign the same bonus to all customers linked to a given representative customer, retrieve the representative customer. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Special Part Bonuses sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Choose the part number that must be ordered to receive the bonus. 
 

 4 .  Record the start and end dates of the bonus offer.
 Note : You can define a number of bonuses for the same part in the same period of time, where the sole variable is the quantity of parts one must purchase in order to receive the bonus. This is known as a graded bonus (for example, if you order 10 units of a given part you are eligible to receive 2 units of the bonus part; if you order 20 units you are eligible to receive 5 units of the bonus part). 
 

 5 .  Record the minimum quantity of this part that the customer must order so as to be eligible for the bonus. 
 

 6 .  Choose the part number of the bonus item, and record the quantity of it being offered. 
 

 7 .  If the bonus item is not free, record its price and choose its currency. 
 
## Note:

 

If the bonus period for a single part overlaps the bonus period of the part family to which the item belongs, a warning message will appear to the effect that both bonuses apply simultaneously.
