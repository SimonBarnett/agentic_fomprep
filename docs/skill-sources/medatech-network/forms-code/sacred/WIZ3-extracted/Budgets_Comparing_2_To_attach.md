Budgets - Assigning a Budget Item to a Journal Entry 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To assign a budget item to a journal entry: 

 
## Tip: 

 

Run the Transactions w/out Budget Item report to view journal entries with no assigned budget items. 
 

 1 .  Enter the Entry Journal form and retrieve the desired journal entry. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Itemized Entry sub-level form, move to the desired line and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 3 .  Click the General tab, and specify the desired Budget Item .
 Tip: Use the Budget Explorer to select the budget item. That is, press Shift+F6 from within the Budget Item column.
