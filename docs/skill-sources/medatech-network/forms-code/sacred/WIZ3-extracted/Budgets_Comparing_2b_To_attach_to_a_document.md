Budgets - Attaching a Budget Item to a Document 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To attach a budget item to a document (e.g., a purchase order):

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Orders form and retrieve the desired order. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Order Items sub-level form and move to the desired line. 
 

 3 .  Choose the desired budget item.
 Tip: Use the Budget Explorer to select the budget item. That is, press Shift+F6 from within the Budget Item column.
