- 
 
 
 
# To designate a multi-year budget item:

 

 1 .  Enter the Budget Items form and retrieve the desired budget item. 
 Tip: Use the Budget Explorer to select the budget item. That is, press Shift+F6 from within the Budget Item column. 
 

 2 .  Move to Full-Record Display Mode and click the Definitions tab. Flag the Multi-Year Budget column.
 Tip: Leave the Budget Items form open. 
 
## To transfer budget balances from the previous year:

 

 ·        Run the Transfer Last Year's Balance program, choosing the fiscal year to which you want to transfer the budget balances, and specifying whether all balances are to be transferred or only those for items flagged as multi-year. 
 
## To view the results:

 

 1 .  Retrieve the desired budget item in the Budget Items form. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Appropriation per Budget Version sub-level form and move to the desired budget version (the version in effect for the fiscal year to which you have transferred budget balances). 
 

 3 .  The transferred balance appears in the Balance from Prev Yr column.
