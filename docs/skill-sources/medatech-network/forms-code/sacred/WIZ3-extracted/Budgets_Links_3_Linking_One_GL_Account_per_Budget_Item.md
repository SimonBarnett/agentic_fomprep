## To link a GL account to a budget item:

 

 1 .  Enter the Budget Items form and retrieve the desired budget item.
 Tip: Use the Budget Explorer to select the budget item. That is, press Shift+F6 from within the Budget Item column. 
 

 2 .  Move to Full-Record Display Mode and click the Definitions tab. In the Account No. column, choose the desired GL account. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and retrieve the desired GL account. 
 

 4 .  Move to Full-Record Display Mode and then click the Add'l Definitions tab. In the Default Budget column, choose the desired budget item.
