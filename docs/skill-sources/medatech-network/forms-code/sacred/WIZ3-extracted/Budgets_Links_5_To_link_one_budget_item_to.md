## To link one budget item to several GL accounts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and retrieve the desired GL accounts. 
 

 2 .  Move to Full-Record Display Mode and then click the Add'l Definitions tab. For each account, in the Default Budget column, choose the desired budget item.
 Tip: Press Shift+F6 to open the Budget Explorer and select the budget item.
