# To Link a GL account to a budget item via a profit/cost center:

 

 1 .  Enter the Budget for Profit/Cost Ctr+Acct form. 
 

 2 .  Record the Profit/Cost Center , Account No , and Budget Item in question. 
 

  
 

 Note s : 
 

 1 .  Alternatively, you can retrieve the profit/cost center from a particular group, using one of the forms in the Profit/Cost Centers-Aux. Forms menu (e.g., Profit & Cost Centers - Group 1 ), and define the GL account and budget item in the Budget for Profit/Cost Ctr+Acct sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Click to return to the main screen .
