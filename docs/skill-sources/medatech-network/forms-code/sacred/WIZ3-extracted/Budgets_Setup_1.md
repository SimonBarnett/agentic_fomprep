# Online Check of Budgetary Deviations

 

You can determine (by means of the BudCheck constant) whether or not an online check is performed to ensure that documented transactions do not exceed budgetary appropriations within the designated period. Deviation checks for sub-annual budgets (i.e., a month, quarter or half-year) also take into account the annual budget from the beginning of the year. The default value is 0 (no check). You can also authorize specific users to deviate from the budget; these users will receive a warning message (rather than an error message) when a transaction exceeds the budget. 
 
## Note:

 

Unless the constant has a value of 0 , you will later be able to define different checking methods for individual budget items. 
 
## To define how to check for general deviations from the budget:

 

 1 .  Move to the BudCheck constant. 
 

 2 .  Select the appropriate value from the Choose list. 
 
## To authorize specific users to deviate from the budget:

 

 1 .  Enter the Users Authorized for Deviation form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the names of any users who are authorized to deviate from the budget. 
 
## Tip:

 

To view a history of all changes to this form, select History of Auth'd Users-Deviatn from the list of Direct Activations.
