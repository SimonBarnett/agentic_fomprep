# Calculating Item Costs in Open Purchase Requisitions

 

You can determine whether the budget cost of open purchase requisitions is calculated according to each item’s catalogue cost, or according to its estimated cost in the purchase requisition. The default value is 0 (according to catalogue cost). 
 
## To budget purchase requisitions according to estimated cost:

 

 1 .  Move to the BudPRPrice constant. 
 

 2 .  Change the value to 1 .
