# Distributing Prepaid Expenses Over the Year

 

The BudgetPostDate constant determines how prepaid expenses are distributed over a budget. The entire sum can be allocated to the invoice date (the preset constant value of 0), or it can be divided into monthly sums apportioned over the year. 
 

 To divide prepaid expenses into monthly sums : 
 

 1 .  Move to the BudgetPostDate constant. 
 

 2 .  Change the value to 1 .
