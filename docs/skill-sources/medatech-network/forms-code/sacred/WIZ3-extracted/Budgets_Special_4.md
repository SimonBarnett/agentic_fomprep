# To perform an additional appropriation for a special budget:

 

 1 .  Enter the Special Budget Appropriations form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the Date . The Document Number appears automatically.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the appropriation to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 3 .  Select the Currency of the original appropriation. 
 

 4 .  Specify the additional appropriation’s Start Date and End Date (in the General tab). 
 

 5 .  In the Details column, record a brief remark regarding this budget appropriation. 
 

 6 .  Move to the Appropriations per Budget Item sub-level form to list the special budget items for which the current appropriation is to be used. 
 

 7 .  In the Budget Item column, select the desired item from the Choose list. The initial appropriation for this item appears in the Orig. Appropriation column. 
 

 8 .  Specify the additional sum in the Appropriated Amount , in the Currency of the original appropriation. The total revised appropriation appears automatically in the Revised Budget column. 
 

 9 .  Return to the upper-level form and change the Status of the appropriation to Pending Auth , to put the budget appropriation into effect or to begin the authorization process.
