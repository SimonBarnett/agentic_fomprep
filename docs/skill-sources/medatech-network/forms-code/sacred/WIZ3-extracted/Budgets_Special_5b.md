# To authorize a special budget appropriation:

 

 1 .  In the Special Budget Appropriations form, retrieve those special budget appropriations you are supposed to authorize.
 Tip: The username of the next person to sign appears in the Next Signature column. Consequently, you can click the Authorization tab and retrieve all appropriations for which your username appears in that column. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Special Budget Authorization sub-level form. The names of all users whose signatures are needed for authorization will appear. 
 

 3 .  Authorize next to your own name by flagging the Approve column. 
 

 4 .  Return to the upper-level form: 
 

 ·      If the appropriation is now fully authorized, the Status changes automatically to Authorized . 
 

 ·      If the appropriation is not yet fully authorized, the name of the next user who is supposed to authorize it appears in the Next Signature column (in the Authorize tab). This user will receive an e-mail message regarding this authorization. 
 

 5 .  In the Assigned to column, designate the next user who is to handle the appropriation (e.g., the next authorizer, the CFO). 
 

 Note: 
 

Click to return to the Special Budgets menu.
