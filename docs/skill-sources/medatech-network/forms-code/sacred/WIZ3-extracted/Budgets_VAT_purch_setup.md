# Adding VAT to Calculations of Purchase Budget Usage

 

When managing the purchase budget for a non-profit organization in the dual-currency system, you can use this constant to determine whether or not VAT is added to calculation of budget usage. The VAT amounts are taken from the organization’s purchase orders. 
 
## To add VAT to purchase budget usage:

 

 1 .  Move to the BudgetAddVAT constant. 
 

 2 .  Change the value of the constant to 1 . 
 
## Note:

 

The constant also determines whether VAT is added to the purchase price of fixed assets that are opened from within purchase invoices.
