# Authorizing the Budget Version

 

You can only authorize the budget version when your turn in the signature order arrives. 
 
## To authorize the budget version:

 

 1 .  Enter the Budget Versions form and move to the line for the desired budget version. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Budget Version Authorization sub-level form. The names of all version authorizers appear. 
 

 3 .  Sign next to your own name by flagging the Approve column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Once the version is fully approved, its status is automatically changed to Authorized . 
 

 ·        Use the Change Version in Effect/Auth'd form to revise an authorized budget version.
