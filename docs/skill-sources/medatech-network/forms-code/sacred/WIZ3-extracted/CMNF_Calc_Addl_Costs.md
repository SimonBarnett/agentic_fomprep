# Additional Costs

 

As with standard additional costs, actual additional costs consist of subcontractor costs, special costs and materials issued as supplies to the plant floor. 
 

 ·        Subcontractor Costs: When production takes place at a subcontractor, the receipt of a processed part is recorded in a GRV. The cost of the inventory transaction is calculated on the basis of the price appearing in the vendor (subcontractor) invoice linked to the GRV (including shipping costs, if any). If you have not yet been sent a receipt, the cost is calculated on the basis of the price recorded in the purchase order linked to the GRV. 
 

 ·        Special Costs: The actual cost of non-inventory parts (e.g., consulting services) charged to the relevant work order when they are ordered or received. 
 

 ·        Cost of Supplies: The cost of materials issued to the plant floor as supplies, charged to a given work order when they are issued to the plant floor. 
 

The same method is used to calculate each type of additional cost, as illustrated in the following example, which applies the method to subcontractor costs: 
 
## Step 1: Actual Cost Per Item

 

For each work order, all subcontractor costs are totaled, including those in the work orders for each child part. This total is then divided by the number of finished parts. 
 
## Step 2: Average Subcontractor Cost 

 

The average subcontractor cost for one part unit is determined by calculating the total subcontractor cost of all closed work orders for the part, and dividing it by the number of finished parts.
