# Rework Costs

 

Operations that are flagged in the Rework column in production reports involve the re-execution of one or more operations (or the execution of different operations that are not included in the work order routing) on at least part of a regular work order. 
 

The cost of such operations is calculated on the basis of the total direct and indirect machine and labor costs, and added to the work order cost as rework cost. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Costing does not take into account production reports for rework work orders , which usually include non-standard operations. 
 

 ·        Rework operations can be flagged as setups or breaks, but will then have no affect on costing or inventory.
