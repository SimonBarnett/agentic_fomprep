# Methods of Costing Calculation 

 

 Priority offers you a choice between two methods of calculating cost: Moving Average and FIFO . 
 

In the dual-currency package, the system automatically indexes the cost of all inventory transactions for both methods. You also have the option of choosing FIFO Adjusted , which uses the FIFO method and adjusts local-currency costs as follows: costs that were initially converted from the second currency are adjusted according to the exchange rate on the costing date, while costs that were initially recorded in the local currency are adjusted according to the index on the costing date. 
 
## The Moving Average Method 

 

In this method, the costing program creates an average cost and applies it to the entire part inventory. When a new quantity of the same part enters inventory with a different cost, the average cost is recalculated for the entire inventory of the part. Example . Consequently, parts leaving inventory do not affect the average part cost. 
 
## Note: 

 

Inventory entering or leaving warehouses flagged as not participating in costing does not affect part cost. However, if inventory is transferred from such a warehouse into a regular warehouse, this transaction is considered entry into inventory and does affect part cost.
