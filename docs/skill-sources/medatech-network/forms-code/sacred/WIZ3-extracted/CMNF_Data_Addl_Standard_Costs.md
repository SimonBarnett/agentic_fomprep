# Defining Additional Standard Costs 

 

There are three types of additional standard costs, which are added to the standard cost of each manufactured part: 
 

 ·        Subcontractors 
 

 ·        Non-standard costs 
 

 ·        Materials issued as supplies to the plant floor 
 
## To record additional standard costs:

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts - Std Costs form and retrieve the desired part(s).
 Tip: To retrieve all manufactured parts, use P in the Type (P/R/O) column. 
 

 2 .  For each part, record its additional standard costs in the appropriate columns ( Subcontract Costs , Non-std Costs and Supply Costs , respectively).
