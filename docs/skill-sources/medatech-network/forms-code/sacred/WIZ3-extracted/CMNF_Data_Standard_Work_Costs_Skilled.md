# To define or update the standard cost of skilled labor: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Skilled Labor - Std Costs form and retrieve the desired skill.
 Tip: If you are defining the cost of a new skill, make sure it's been defined in the Skills form. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Skilled Labor Costs sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  In the From Date and the To Date columns, record the dates that the cost of labor with this skill will be in effect. 
 Tip: Leave the To Date column blank to leave the cost in effect indefinitely. 
 

 4 .  In the Cost/Hour column, record/revise the average hourly cost of an employee with this skill. 
 

 5 .  In the Indirect Cost/Hour column, record/revise the total indirect costs incurred by one hour of skilled labor.
