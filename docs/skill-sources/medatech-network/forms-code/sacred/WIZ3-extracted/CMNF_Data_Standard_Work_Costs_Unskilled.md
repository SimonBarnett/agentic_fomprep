# To define standard costs for a new work cell:

 

 1 .  Enter the Departments -Std Costs form and retrieve the department to which the new work cell belongs. If labor and machinery costs have been defined for the department, they are assigned automatically to the new work cell. 
 

 2 .  If the new work cell incurs different costs from other work cells in the department, or does not belong to a department, enter the Work Cells - Std Costs form and retrieve the new work cell. 
 

 3 .  Record the direct and indirect costs of unskilled labor and machinery in the new work cell.
 Note: If costs have been defined for the department, you need only record those costs that differ from departmental costs. 
 
## To update standard costs in an existing work cell: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Work Cells - Std Costs form and retrieve the desired work cell. 
 

 2 .  Make the necessary revisions.
