# Standard part costs 

 

You need to choose between two methods of calculating the standard cost of an assembled part: 
 

 ·        Std Material Cost: Standard quantities of child parts (i.e., the quantities recorded in the standard BOM) are multiplied by their standard cost . 
 

 ·        Actual Material Cost: Standard quantities of child parts are multiplied by their actual cost if they are purchased (Type R ) parts, by their standard cost if they are manufactured or assembled. 
 
## Whether lot costs are taken into account

 

You need to decide whether inventory values of raw materials will be determined by the raw material lots in which they were received. Explanation . 
 
## Whether to calculate the cost of each work order for parts with Costing Option T :

 

 ·        No Costing for WkOrd: The costing program will not calculate the cost of work orders, nor include them in calculations of part costs. 
 

 ·        Costing for Wk Ords: Costing will calculate the cost of each work order, but will not include them in calculations of part costs.
