 5 .  Enter the Initial Product Costs form, and record the cost of each manufactured part (per costing unit). Dual-currency users can choose either the local- or second-currency Cost columns, according to their company's method of price management.
 Note: If you specify the cost of a part in both the local- and second-currency Cost columns, you thereby set the exchange rate for that part. That is, costing reports will display its local-currency costs according to the exchange rate derived from the values recorded here, and not according to the exchange rate in effect on the report date. 
 

 6 .  Record a past balance for the day of the initial inventory count, and run the Costing for Past Balance Date program for the same day. The actual costs of the raw materials will be taken from the inventory count if a cost was recorded; otherwise, the standard cost will be used. 
 

 7 .  Run the Update PartCost by Initial Costs program to update the actual cost of manufactured parts in the past balance, in keeping with the values recorded in Step 5 . 
 

Part costs and inventory values are now initialized. You can now run costing for a given date or at regular intervals, according to the frequency at which your company closes past balances.
