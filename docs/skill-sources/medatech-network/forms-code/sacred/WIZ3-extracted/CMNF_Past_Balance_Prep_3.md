# Checking the Work Orders

 
## Note:

 

You cannot make revisions to open work orders after a past balance has been calculated; make the necessary revisions beforehand. If a problem is discovered after the past balance was calculated, delete that past balance, make the necessary revisions, then calculate it again. 
 
## To check the work orders:

 

 1 .  Enter the Work Orders form and retrieve the work orders whose closing dates fall within the past balance period that you want to record. 
 

 2 .  Make sure that all production reports for these work orders are complete and accurate. 
 

 3 .  If you do not want a particular work order to be taken into account by costing, click the Inventory/Costing Data tab and flag the Not for Costing column. 
 

 4 .  Close the work orders for which production is fully completed and reported. 
 
## To check shipping vouchers: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the latest shipping vouchers . 
 

 2 .  Make sure that all completed vouchers have been closed, and that the details of all closed vouchers are correct.
 Note: The costing program only takes into account shipping vouchers closed since the previous costing run. Dual-currency users: If you store imported goods in bonded warehouses, make sure that their recorded import costs are correct.
