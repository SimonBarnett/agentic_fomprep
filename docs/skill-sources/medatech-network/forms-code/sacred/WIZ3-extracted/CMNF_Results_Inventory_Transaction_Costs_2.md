## To view the inventory transaction costs used to calculate the cost of a manufactured part: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Product Costing form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired part, by Part Number and Costing Date . 
 

 3 .  Enter the Product Transactions sub-level form. This form displays the inventory transactions taken into account by the costing program. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        The form reflects the program's method of calculating the cost of assembled parts only. The cost of manufactured parts is determined by closed work orders (as detailed in the Product Cost Components form), or by the bill of materials (that is, actual raw material costs and standard production costs). 
 

 ·        Dual-currency users: Click for information on indexing and price adjustment for inventory transactions . 
 

 ·        Click to view additional costing results.
