# Defining Standard Hourly Costs of Skilled Employees 

 
## Before you begin:

 

 ·        Make sure that the required skills (e.g., Production Engineer) are properly defined in the Skills form. 
 

 ·        Make sure that each skilled employee is linked to a skill in the Company-Specific Information sub-level of the Personnel File form. 
 
## To define the costs of skilled employees:

 

 1 .  Enter the Skilled Labor - Std Costs form and retrieve the desired skill. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Skilled Labor Costs sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  In the From Date and the To Date columns, record the dates that the cost of labor with this skill will be in effect. 
 Tip: Leave the To Date column blank to leave the cost in effect indefinitely. 
 

 4 .  In the Cost/Hour column, record the average hourly cost of an employee with this skill. 
 

 5 .  In the Indirect Cost/Hour column, record the total indirect costs incurred by one hour of skilled labor.
