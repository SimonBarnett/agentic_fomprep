# To define standard hourly costs of unskilled employees and machines:

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              To define standard costs for a work cell, enter the Work Cells - Std Costs form and retrieve the desired work cell. 
 

 ·              To define standard costs for all work cells in a department, enter the Departments -Std Costs form and retrieve the desired department. 
 

 2 .  Fill in the Machine Cost/Hour and Indir. Mach. Cost/Hour columns for the work cell/department. Explanation. 
 

 3 .   Fill in the Labor Cost/Hour and Indir. Labor Cost/Hour columns for the work cell/department. Explanation. 
 

 4 .  Specify the average cost of one setup hour in the Set-up Cost/Hour column. 
 

 5 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If you are working in the Work Cells - Std Costs form, specify the percentage of the total machine time required for setup in the Set-up Time(%) . This value, together with the standard mount and dismount times, determines the total setup time, which is then multiplied by the hourly setup cost to arrive at the operation's total setup cost. 
 

 ·              If you are working in the Departments -Std Costs form, enter the Work Cells - Std Costs form and use the Department column to retrieve all the work cells in the department. For each cell, specify the Set-up Time(%) .
