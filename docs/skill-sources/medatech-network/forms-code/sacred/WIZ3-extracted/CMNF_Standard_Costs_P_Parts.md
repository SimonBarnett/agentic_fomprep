# Recording Standard Costs of Processed Parts

 

Standard costs of processed (Type P ) parts are calculated by the costing program, on the basis of child part and production costs. These are the values that are used by the system in costing reports, and in calculations of inventory values. 
 

You can also define standard costs for Type P parts manually in the Part Catalogue or Parts - Std Costs form. Keep in mind that these values are not updated by the standard costs calculated during costing (unless the UpdStdCost constant is set to 1 ), and are not reflected in costing reports or inventory valuations.
