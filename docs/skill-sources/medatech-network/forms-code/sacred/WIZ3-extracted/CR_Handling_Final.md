# Revising a Final Receipt

 

Final receipts cannot be revised, with the exception of certain columns (e.g., Details ). You can also revise the details of any checks or credit card payments recorded in the receipt. 
 
## To revise a final receipt:

 

 1 .  Enter the Revision of Receipt Details form and retrieve the desired receipt. 
 

 2 .  Make the necessary revisions in this form and its sub-level, Invoices That Were Paid . 
 
## To revise check details:

 

 1 .  Enter the Revision of Checks form and retrieve the desired check. 
 Tip: For best results use the Document Number column (for the receipt number) together with the Document Code (REC). 
 

 2 .  Make the necessary revisions. 
 
## To revise credit card payment details:

 

 1 .  Enter the Revision of Credit Card Slips form and retrieve the desired voucher. 
 Tip: For best results use the Document Number column (for the receipt number) together with the Document Code (REC). 
 

 2 .  Make the necessary revisions.
