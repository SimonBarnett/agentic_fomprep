# Revising/Deleting a Pending Receipt

 

Pending receipts (i.e., receipts that have not yet been finalized) can be revised freely or deleted from the system. 
 
## To revise a pending receipt:

 

 1 .  Enter the Receipts form and retrieve the desired receipt. 
 

 2 .  Make the necessary changes. 
 
## Note:

 

Click to finalize the receipt . 
 
## To delete a single pending receipt:

 

 1 .  Enter the Receipts form and retrieve the desired receipt. 
 

 2 .  Select Delete Pending Receipt from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 

 To delete a group of pending receipts: 
 

 ·        Run the Delete Pending Receipts program. 
 
## Note: 

 

Click to return to the main screen.
