## To view payment details per cashier, bank or credit card company:

 

 ·        Run the Receipts by Cashier report. 
 
## Note: 

 

This report only includes final receipts and over-the-counter invoices. 
 
## To view all receipts to walk-in customers:

 

 1 .  Enter the Walk-in Customer Invs & Receipts form and retrieve the desired customer by name. This form displays all receipts, invoices and credit memos (both pending and final). 
 

 2 .  To view details of the receipt, press F6 in the Invoice/Receipt No. column.

Click to return to the main screen.
