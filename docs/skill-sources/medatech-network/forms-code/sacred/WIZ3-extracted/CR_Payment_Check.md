# To record payment by check:

 

 1 .  Enter the Check Payment (Details) sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  For each check, fill in the Check Date , Payment Amount and Check Number . 
 

 3 .  Record the details of the bank account against which the check is drawn. 
 

 4 .  Specify the Cash Flow Code , as needed. 
 

 5 .  Return to the upper-level form. 
 

 6 .  If the receipt needs to be checked before it is sent to the customer, assign it the Checking status (in the References tab). In the Assigned to column, specify the username of the person who is responsible for checking the document. The receipt cannot be finalized until this status is changed. 
 Tip: You can define work flow rules so that the assigned user changes automatically when the status is assigned. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        If a cashier has been defined for all checks received by your company, or received by this particular branch, the GL account for that cashier will appear in the journal entry that is recorded when the receipt is finalized. Cashiers are assigned in the Payment Means form and its sub-level, Cashier/Bk Acc/CredCd for Branch .
