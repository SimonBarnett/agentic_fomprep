# To record payment by credit card:

 

 1 .  Make sure that the Cashier column displays the number of the bank account to which payment will be transferred. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Other Forms of Payment (Details) sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  In the Payment Code column, choose the appropriate credit card company.
 Note: If a cashier or credit card account has been defined for all payments by this credit card received by your company, or received by this particular branch, the GL account for that cashier/credit card account will appear in the journal entry that is recorded when the receipt is finalized. Cashiers and credit card accounts are assigned in the Payment Means form and its sub-level, Cashier/Bk Acc/CredCd for Branch . 
 

 4 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If the computer is attached to a magnetic card terminal, run the credit card through the terminal. The last four digits of the credit card number will appear automatically.
 OR 
 

 ·              Record the last four digits of the credit card number manually in the BkAcct/Credit Cd No. column. 
 

 5 .  Record the Card ' s Expiration Date . 
 

 6 .  Specify the Payment Amount . 
 

 7 .  If the customer has default payment terms, the Payment Terms Code column is filled in automatically. Revise it as needed.
 Note: If payment terms have been defined specifically for this credit card in the Payment Terms for Credit Cards form (sub-level of the Payment Means form), you must select one of these terms from the Choose list. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        As of version 14, credit card data is protected in keeping with PCI security standards. 
 

 ·        Run Delete CreditCd Data in Receipts in any company where credit card data was recorded in the system prior to Version 14, so as to delete sensitive authentication data.
