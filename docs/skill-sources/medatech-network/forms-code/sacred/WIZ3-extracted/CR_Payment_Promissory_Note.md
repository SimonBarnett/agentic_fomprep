# To record payment by promissory note:

 

 1 .  Select Payment by Promissory Note from the list of Direct Activations to open one or more promissory notes for the receipt. 
 

 2 .  Specify the Total Sum , No. Payments and Date of 1st Payment for the promissory note(s) to be opened. 
 

 3 .  To view the promissory note(s), enter the Other Forms of Payment (Details) sub-level form (of the Receipts form).
 Tip: To print the promissory note(s), return to the upper-level form and select Print Promissory Notes from the list of Direct Activations. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        Depending on how receipts are set up, you may be required to finalize the receipt before you can leave the form. Explanation.
