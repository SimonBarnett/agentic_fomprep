# To record payment by bank transfer:

 

 1 .  Make sure that the Cashier column displays the number of the bank account to which payment will be transferred. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Other Forms of Payment (Details) sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  In the Payment Code column, choose Bank Transfer .
 Note: If a bank account has been defined for all bank transfers received by your company, or received by this particular branch, the GL account for that cashier will appear in the journal entry that is recorded when the receipt is finalized. Bank accounts are assigned in the Payment Means form and its sub-level, Cashier/Bk Acc/CredCd for Branch . 
 

 4 .  In the BkAcct/Credit Cd No. column, specify the number of the bank account from which the payment will be transferred. 
 

 5 .  Record the Payment Amount . 
 

 6 .  If the customer has default payment terms, the Payment Terms Code column is filled in automatically. Revise it as needed. 
 

 7 .  If the amount is split into several payments, record the amount of the first payment in the First Installment column. 
 

 8 .  Specify the Payment Date . 
 

 9 .  Specify the customer`s bank details in the Cust `s Bank Code and Cust `s Branch columns.
