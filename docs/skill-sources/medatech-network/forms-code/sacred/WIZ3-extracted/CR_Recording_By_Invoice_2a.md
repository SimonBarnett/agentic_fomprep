 4 .  If you have changed the invoice amount and the invoice balance is smaller than the value assigned to the MaxReconVar constant, you do not need to do anything more. The system will fully reconcile the receipt and invoice and transfer the variance to the default Reconciliation Adjustments account (defined in the Default Accounts form). However, if the remainder to be paid on the invoice exceeds the constant, you can still perform a full reconciliation (and write off the invoice balance). In such a case, specify the number of the Adjustment Account for the invoice in question. The system will fully reconcile the receipt with the invoices and transfer the difference to the specified account. (Otherwise, the balance to be paid will remain part of the customer`s debt, and the invoice will continue to appear in the Flag Invoices for Payment sub-level form.)
