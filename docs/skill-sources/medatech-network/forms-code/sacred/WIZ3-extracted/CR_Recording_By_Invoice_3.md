# Recording Receipt Details Manually

 

If you have received payment for goods that have not yet been invoiced, record the payment details manually. After an invoice has been recorded and finalized for these goods, you can reconcile it with the receipt retroactively. For details, see the Account Reconciliation Wizard . 
 
## To record receipt details:

 

 1 .  Enter the Invoice Amounts Being Paid sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Record the amount of payment in the Sum column. 
 

 3 .  Record a brief remark regarding the payment in the Details column (e.g., advance payment). 
 

 4 .  Check that the correct account appears in the Account No. column. 
 

 5 .  Designate the Profit/Cost Center to which this payment is attached, if desired. 
 

 6 .  In a multi-company setting, click to transfer a portion of one company `s receipts to another company. 
 

 7 .  Return to the upper-level form.
