# To record a receipt "First Invoice First":

 

 1 .  Enter the Receipts form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 2 .  Specify the Customer `s Account number. The Customer/Acct Name , Date and a temporary Receipt Number appear automatically. 
 

 3 .  In the Contact column, make sure that the appropriate customer contact appears.
 Tip: To define new contacts for the customer or to assign a default receipts contact, use the Customer Contacts form, a sub-level of the Customers form. For more details, run the Open Customers Wizard . 
 

 4 .  The default Cashier into which payment is received appears automatically, but may be revised. If payment is by credit card or bank transfer, select the bank account into which the payment is received.
 Tip: To change bank account definitions or define additional accounts, use the Bank Accounts form. 
 

 5 .  Click if the payment is for work on a project .
