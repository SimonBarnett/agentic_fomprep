# Recording a Receipt

 

The way in which you record customer payments is determined by your chosen reconciliation method (i.e., the value defined for the AutoRecon constant in the Financial Constants form, or the value defined for the customer account in the Chart of Accounts Receivable form. 
 

If you work with subsidiaries, a receipt may not credit the A/R account recorded in the invoice, but rather another customer account in a different subsidiary. Similarly, a receipt may cover payment of several invoices belonging to different subsidiaries. In order to reconcile such receipts with their invoices, you need to define transfer accounts for your subsidiaries and flag the journal entry code for receipts accordingly (run the Subsidiaries Wizard for detailed instructions). 
 
## Note:

 

If you work on a cash basis, you can prepare receipts on the basis of pro forma invoices. For detailed instructions, run the Cash-Based Accounting Wizard . 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Record a receipt when reconciling "By Invoice" 
 

 Record a receipt when reconciling by "First Invoice First"
