# To record a receipt for a walk-in customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Receipts form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 2 .  Record the Customer `s Account number. The Date and a temporary Receipt Number appear automatically. 
 

 3 .  Specify the name of the customer in the Customer/Acct Name column. 
 

 4 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              To link the receipt to a single order, click the References tab and specify the Order number. 
 

 ·              To link the receipt to multiple orders, enter the Invoice Amounts Being Paid . Record the order numbers and the amount paid by the customer for each one, and return to the upper-level form. 
 

 5 .  The default Cashier into which payment is received appears automatically, but may be revised. If payment is by credit card or bank transfer, select the bank account into which the payment is received.
 Tip: To change bank account definitions or define additional accounts, use the Bank Accounts form.
