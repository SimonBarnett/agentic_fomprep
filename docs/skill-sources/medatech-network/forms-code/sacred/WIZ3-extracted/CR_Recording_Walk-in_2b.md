 6 .  By default, the journal entry that is created when the receipt is finalized contains one line crediting the customer`s account with the receipt`s total sum. If you prefer to itemize the entry according to the lines of the receipt (e.g., so that each invoice amount appears on a separate line in the entry), check the box in the Copy to Journal Entry column (in the Check Invoice Data tab). 
 

 7 .  To itemize the receipt, enter the Invoice Amounts Being Paid sub-level form. Record the amounts paid in the receipt next to the sales orders for which they were received. 
 

 8 .  Enter the Receipt - Add'l Details sub-level form and check that the customer`s details are correct, as they will appear in the printed receipt.
