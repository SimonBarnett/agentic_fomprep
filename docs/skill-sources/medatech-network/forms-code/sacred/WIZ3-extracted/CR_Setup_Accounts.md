# To define banks and accounts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Banks form and check that the banks in which you and your customers have accounts are listed.
 Note: You can add to this list later. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Bank Accounts form and retrieve your bank accounts, into which bank transfers will be made. Make sure that the account numbers and bank codes are correct.
 D ual-currency users: Flag the Payments From Abroad column for all bank accounts in which foreign money will be deposited. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts Receivable form and retrieve your customers' accounts. Make sure that the information in this form and in its sub-level, Bank Account Details , are correct. 
 
## Tip: 

 

For help with recording accounts, run the Set Up Bank Accounts Wizard .
