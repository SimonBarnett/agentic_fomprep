# Setting Up Receipts

 

Before you begin working with receipts, you need to set up the following: 
 

 ·        The method of reconciling invoices with receipts 
 

 ·        A variance account to which small differences between reconciled amounts will be transferred 
 

 ·        Bank accounts for your company and your customers 
 

 ·        Company cashiers and means of payment 
 

 ·        General receipt attributes, such as automatic journal entry posting 
 

 ·        Business processes (BPM) for receipt – i.e., statuses 
 

 ·        Tracking of check dates 
 

 ·        Whether to check for credit card authorization before finalizing receipts for credit card payments 
 

 ·        Printing format for receipts 
 

In addition, dual-currency users need to define how payment sums are adjusted when the receipt currency differs from the invoice currency.
