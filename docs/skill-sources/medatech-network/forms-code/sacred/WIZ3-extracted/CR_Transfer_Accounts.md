# To transfer a portion of one company`s receipts to another company:

 

 1 .  Enter the Target Acct for Multi-Co Entries sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the target account in the Target for this Entry column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can only use this feature if the account in the Account No. column of the Invoice Amounts Being Paid form is defined as a transfer account, and if the Copy to Journal Entry column is flagged in the Receipts form. 
 

 ·        To define an account as a transfer account, use the Definitions for Transfer Account sub-level form of the Chart of Accounts form.
