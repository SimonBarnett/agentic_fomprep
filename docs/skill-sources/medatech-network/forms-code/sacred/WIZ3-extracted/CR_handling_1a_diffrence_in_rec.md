# Dealing With Variance

 

You will not be able to finalize a receipt whose amount differs, even slightly, from the total payment due. 
 
## If there is variance:

 

 1 .  Enter the Invoice Amounts Being Paid sub-level form and change the amount next to one of the invoices so that the payment due is identical to the receipt total (difference = 0). 
 

 2 .  Return to the upper-level form. 
 
## Note:

 

If the invoice balance is now smaller than the value assigned to the MaxReconVar constant, the system will fully reconcile the receipt and invoice and transfer the variance to the default Reconciliation Adjustments account (defined in the Default Accounts form).
