Job Applicants - Recording a Salary Offer 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record a salary offer made to the job applicant: 

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If this is an external candidate, enter the Job Candidates form. 
 

 ·              If this is a current employee, enter the Personnel File form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired applicant and enter the Salary Offers sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Record the date of the offer, and choose the number of the job for which the offer was made. 
 

 4 .  Choose the employee ID of the employee who made the offer. 
 

 5 .  Record the date by which the applicant agrees to accept or reject the offer. 
 

 6 .  If the applicant accepts the offer, in the Salary Offers form, flag the Accepted column. If the applicant rejects the offer, leave the Accepted column blank and choose the reason he/she gave for rejecting it. 
 

 7 .  If necessary, return to the upper-level form and change the applicant's status. 
 
## Note:

 

To record that a job was filled, click to run the Job Openings Wizard .
