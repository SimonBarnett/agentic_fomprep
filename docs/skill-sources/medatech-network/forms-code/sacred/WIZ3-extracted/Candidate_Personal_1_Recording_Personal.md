# Recording Personal Information

 

 Priority distinguishes between new candidates and current employees applying for a given job opening. While data about current employees is already recorded in the Personnel File , you need to record personal information about new candidates. You can record this information now or at any time during the interview process (e.g., if the candidate supplies the information during an interview). 
 
## To record general information about the candidate:

 

 1 .  Enter the Job Candidates form. 
 

 2 .  Record the candidate's name, social security number, title (e.g., Ms. , Dr. ) and gender. The status that is automatically assigned to new candidates appears in the Status column (e.g., Candidate ). 
 

 3 .  If you are working in a multi-company setting, choose the company in which the candidate is applying for a position. 
 

 4 .  Click the Address & Phone tab. Record the candidate's address and telephone numbers. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Personal Details sub-level form and fill in additional details regarding the candidate.
 Tip: You can attach a file containing the candidate's CV to the Resume File column, or use the parallel Attachments sub-level form. 
 

 6 .  If the candidate is being recruited for a specific job, enter the parallel Jobs Applied For sub-level form and do the following: 
 

 ·              Record today's date in the Date Opened column. 
 

 ·              Select the appropriate Job Number . 
 

 Note: The complete job recruitment process is discussed in the Job Openings Wizard .
