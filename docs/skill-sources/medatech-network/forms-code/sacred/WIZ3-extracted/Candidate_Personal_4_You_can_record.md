# Recording Additional Information

 

Use the following sub-levels of the Job Candidates form to record additional information about the candidate: 
 

 ·        Education in the Education sub-level form. 
 

 ·        References in the References sub-level form 
 

 ·        Previous work experience in the Professional Experience sub-level form 
 

 ·        Knowledge of foreign languages in the Foreign Languages sub-level form 
 

 ·        Medical tests undergone by the candidate in the Medical Tests sub-level form. You can also attach documents in this form's Medical Records sub-level form. 
 

 ·        Any additional information in the Remarks sub-level form.
