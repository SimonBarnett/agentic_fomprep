Job Applicants - Recording a Job Interview 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record a job interview:

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If this is an external candidate, enter the Job Candidates form 
 

 ·              If this is a current employee, enter the Personnel File form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired applicant and enter the Job Interviews sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Choose the employee who performed the interview, and record whether the applicant passed the interview. 
 

 4 .  Attach any new documents pertaining to the interview. 
 

 5 .  If desired, record a brief remark about the interview. You can record a more extensive summary in the Summary of Interview sub-level form. 
 

 6 .  Change the interview status (e.g., to Completed). 
 

 7 .  Record the date by which the company has agreed to respond to the applicant. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        Click to record the results of a test taken by the applicant. 
 

 ·        Click to record a salary offer made to the applicant.
