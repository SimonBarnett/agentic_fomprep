# To schedule a job interview: 

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If this is an external candidate, enter the Job Candidates form. 
 

 ·              If this is an employee of the company, enter the Personnel File form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired applicant and enter the Job Interviews sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Record the date set for the interview. 
 

 4 .  Choose the job number. 
 

 5 .  Choose an interview code. 
 

 6 .  Attach any documents related to the interview (e.g., work sample). 
 

 7 .  If you know how the candidate heard about the job, record this information. 
 

 8 .  Choose the interview status (e.g., Scheduled). 
 

 9 .  Choose the preferred method of contacting the candidate.
