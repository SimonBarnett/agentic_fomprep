# Assigning Cash Flow Codes to Types of Cash Intake

 

In order to assign a cash flow code to a type of cash intake, you must assign it to the appropriate journal entry code (receipt, returned check, over-the-counter invoice or deposit). 
 

 1 .  Enter the Journal Entry Codes form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the line for the desired journal entry code. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Entry Patterns sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Move to the line for TOCASH . 
 

 5 .  In the Cash Flow Code column, choose the desired code.
