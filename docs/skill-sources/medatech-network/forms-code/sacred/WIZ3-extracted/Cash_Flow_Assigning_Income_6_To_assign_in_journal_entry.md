- 
 
 
 
# To assign cash flow codes in a journal entry:

 

 1 .  Enter the Entry Journal form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired entry.
 Note: Click if the entry has been posted. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Itemized Entry sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Move to the line for the bank account or cashier and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 5 .  Click the General tab, and specify the desired Cash Flow Code . 
 
## Note: 

 

Click to return to the main screen.
