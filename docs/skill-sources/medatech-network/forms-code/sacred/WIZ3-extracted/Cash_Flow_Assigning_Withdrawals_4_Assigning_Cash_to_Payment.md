# Assigning Cash Flow Codes to Payment Types

 

In order to assign a cash flow code to a payment type, you must assign it to the journal entry code for that payment type (check payments or bank transfers). 
 

 1 .  Enter the Journal Entry Codes form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the line for Check Payment or Bank Transfer (whichever is appropriate). 
 

 3 .  Enter the Entry Patterns sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Move to the line for CASH . 
 

 5 .  In the Cash Flow Code column, choose the desired code.
