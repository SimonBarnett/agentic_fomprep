# Defining Opening Balances 

 

The Cash Flow Forecast report gives you the option of displaying either opening account balances as they are defined in the General Ledger, or opening balances defined manually by the user. 
 
## To record a user-defined opening balance:

 

 1 .  Enter the Bank Accounts form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired account. 
 

 3 .  In the Cash Flow Balance column, record the desired balance.
