# To view cash flow forecasts:

 

 1 .  Run the Cash Flow Forecast report, flagging the relevant runtime options: 
 

 ·      Provisional Entries – to include open PRs, open purchase orders, unbilled transactions and miscellaneous transactions recorded in the Cash Flow Forecast Sums form. 
 

 ·      Unreconc ' d Entries – to include payments or receipts not yet appearing on bank or credit card statements. 
 

 ·      With Payment Delays – to display the average number of days any payment is late. 
 

 ·      Show Past Dates – to include transactions that were entered in the journal prior to the report date, but were never completed. 
 

 Note: The report automatically includes all unpaid invoices that are due to be paid within the designated period, unless the relevant A/P or A/R account was manually omitted. 
 

 2 .  If you created provisional entries for the purposes of this report, run the Delete Provisional Entries program to delete them from the ledger. 
 
## Note:

 

In the report output, anything dated before the From Date you designated appears in the line for Pre-period . Anything between the From Date and To Date is broken down by the designated time period. Anything after the To Date appears in the line for Post-period .
