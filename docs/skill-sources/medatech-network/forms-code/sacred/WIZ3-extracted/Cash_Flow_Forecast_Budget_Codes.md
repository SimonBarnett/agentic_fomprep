# Assigning Cash Flow Codes to Budget Items

 

Assign cash flow codes to budget items when you intend to include unused sums appropriated to these items in cash flow forecasts. 
 
## To assign a cash flow code to a budget item:

 

 1 .  Enter the Budget Items form and retrieve the desired budget item. 
 

 2 .  Move to Full-Record Display Mode and click the Definitions tab. In the Cash Flow column, choose the desired code. 
 

 3 .  In the Bk Acc for Cash Flow column, specify the bank account for projected transactions assigned to this budget item.
