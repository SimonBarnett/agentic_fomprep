Cash Flow - Excluding A/R and A/R Accounts from Forecast 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To exclude specific A/R or A/P accounts from the cash flow forecast:

 

 1 .  Enter the relevant form: 
 

 ·      Chart of Accounts Receivable or 
 

 ·      Chart of Accounts Payable 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired account and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 3 .  Click the Additional Definitions tab and flag the Omit from Cash Flow column.
