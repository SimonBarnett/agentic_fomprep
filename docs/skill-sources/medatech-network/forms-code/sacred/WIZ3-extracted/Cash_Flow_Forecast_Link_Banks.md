# Linking Ledger Accounts to Bank Accounts

 

If you intend to run the cash flow forecast using the "Per Bank" runtime option, you need to link each A/P and A/R account to the appropriate bank account. You should first create a default bank account for all ledger accounts, then manually link specific ledger accounts to different bank accounts where necessary. 
 

An addition to A/P and A/R accounts, you can also link cashier accounts whose contents are always deposited in the same bank account. Consequently, forecasts will take into account receipts recorded for that cashier that have not yet been deposited in the bank. 
 
## To define a default bank account for ledger accounts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Bank Accounts form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired account and flag the Main Bank Account column. 
 
## To link a ledger account to a bank account for cash flow purposes:

 

 1 .  Enter the relevant form: 
 

 ·      Chart of Accounts 
 

 ·      Chart of Accounts Receivable 
 

 ·      Chart of Accounts Payable 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired account and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 3 .  Click the Add ' l Definitions tab and fill in the Bk Acc for Cash Flow column.
