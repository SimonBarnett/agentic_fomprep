# Defining Detailed Payment Installments for Purchase Orders

 

When provisional journal entries are created for purchase orders (in preparation for running the Cash Flow Forecast report), the expected payment for a given purchase order can be distributed in multiple installments. To do so, you must define detailed payment installments for the order in question. 
 
## To define payment installments:

 

 1 .  Open the Purchase Orders form and retrieve the order in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Payment Terms for Order sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  In the Date column, record the date payment is due. 
 

 4 .  In the relevant column, indicate either the payment amount or the percentage of the order total that is due on the designated date. 
 

 5 .  Record any pertinent remarks in the Details column, if desired.
