# Setting Up Ledger Accounts and Budget Items

 

Before running the cash flow forecast, you can assign cash flow codes to GL accounts and budget items, exclude specific A/R or A/P accounts from the report, and record different opening balances for specific bank accounts, when the current GL balance does not reflect the actual balance of the bank account. Example. 
 

If you intend to run forecasts using the "Per Bank" runtime option, you need to link each A/R and A/P account to a specific bank account. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Assign cash flow codes to GL accounts 
 

 Assign cash flow codes to budget items 
 

 Flag A/R and A/P accounts that should not participate in cash flow 
 

 Record opening balances 
 

 Link A/R and A/P accounts to bank accounts
