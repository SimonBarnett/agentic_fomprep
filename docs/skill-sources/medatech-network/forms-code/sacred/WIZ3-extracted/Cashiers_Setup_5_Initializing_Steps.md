# To initialize a cashier:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and retrieve the Opening Bal account. 
 

 2 .  Move to the Account No. column and copy the account number. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Receipts form. Paste the account number into the Customer ' s Account column. 
 

 4 .  If needed, change the cashier appearing in the Cashier column. 
 

 5 .  In the Cash Receipts column, record the amount of cash in the cashier. 
 

 6 .  Select Finalize Receipt from the list of Direct Activation s . The transactions are recorded in the general ledger and the contents of the cashier are recorded.
