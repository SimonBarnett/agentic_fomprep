# Combining Installment Payments

 

When an invoice calls for several payment installments, each installment appears on a separate line in the Flag Invoices for Payment form, distinguished by its payment date. Only those installments whose due date falls within the designated period are automatically flagged for payment. Priority ignores any amounts whose due date falls outside this period. 
 
## If you decide to pay later installments now as well:

 

 1 .  Enter the Flag Invoices for Payment form and retrieve the installments in question. 
 

 2 .  Manually flag them for payment (in the Prepare Payment column).
