# Installments and Partial Payments

 

One or more invoices for a given A/P account can be paid by aggregating the amounts due into multiple installment payments. These partial installment payments may be recorded automatically or manually. 
 
## To record installment payments automatically:

 

 1 .  Enter the Flag Invoices for Payment form and retrieve the invoice(s) in question. 
 

 2 .  Select Split Payment from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Payments will be applied to invoices on a FIFO basis, with the oldest invoice being paid first. 
 

 ·        All subsequent monthly payments will be recorded on the same day of the month as the first payment (designated during program input). 
 
## To record a partial payment manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Flag Invoices for Payment form and retrieve the invoice in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Payments for Invoice sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Designate the partial amount to be paid. The invoice will continue to be considered outstanding (and will appear in the Flag Invoices for Payment form) until full payment has been made. 
 
## Note: The next time that you prepare a payment for this invoice, any partial payments that have already been recorded will appear in the sub-level form, Prior Payments on Invoice .
