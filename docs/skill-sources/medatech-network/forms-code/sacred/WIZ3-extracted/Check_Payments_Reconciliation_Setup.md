# Setting the Reconciliation Method

 

The following methods of reconciling payments with invoices are available: 
 

 ·        Reconcile by "First Invoice First" – reconciliations are handled within each account ledger. 
 

 ·        Reconcile "By Invoice" – full tracking of partial payments. 
 
## To set the reconciliation method:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Constants form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the AutoRecon constant and change its value, as needed. 
 
## Note: 

 

This constant also affects reconciliation of customer receipts with invoices. For a full explanation of the reconciliation methods, run the Customer Receipts Wizard .
