# To remit checks to an address other than the vendor's main address:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Parameters for Vendors form and retrieve the vendor. 
 

 2 . In the Remit to Address (Checks) sub-level form, record the vendor name, contact and address you want to appear on checks to that vendor. 
 Note: This information is copied automatically to the Check Payments form and its sub-level, Check Payment - Add'l Details , but may be revised. 
 

 3 . In the Make Payable to column, record the name of the company or individual to whom checks should be made payable.
 Note: This name is copied automatically to the Vendor Name column of the Check Payments form, where it can be revised for an individual payment. Whatever ultimately appears in the Vendor Name column will appear on the body of the check, on the "Make Payable to" line.
