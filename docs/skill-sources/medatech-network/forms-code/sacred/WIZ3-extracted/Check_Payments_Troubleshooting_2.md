- 
 
 
 
# What should I do if I need to reprint a check?

 

 1 .  Note the number of the check. It will need to be voided. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Update Check Numbers form, and retrieve the problematic check 
 

 3 .  Delete its check number. 
 

 4 .  If desired, you can also change the Check Date . To do so, leave the line and return to it, then record the new date. 
 

 5 .  Rerun the Print Checks program. The next available check number will be assigned. 
 

 6 .  Enter the Check Payments form, and open a new payment document for the problematic check. 
 

 7 .  Fill in the correct date, vendor account, paying bank account and the original check number. Leave a payment amount of zero. 
 

 8 .  Select Void the Check from the list of Direct Activations .
