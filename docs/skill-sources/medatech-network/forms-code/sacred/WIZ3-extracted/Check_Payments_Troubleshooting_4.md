Troubleshooting Check Payments - Postponing a Payment 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# What should I do if I want to postpone the payment date?

 

 1 .  Enter the Entry Item Revisions form, and retrieve the relevant journal entry item (according to the check's Payment Number ). 
 

 2 .  Change the Due Date to the desired date.
