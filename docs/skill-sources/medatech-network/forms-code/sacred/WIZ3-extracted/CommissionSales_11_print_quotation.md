# Printing a Price Quote: Before You Begin

 
## To check remarks:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quote (Commission)-Remarks sub-level form. The remarks recorded there will appear on all printouts of the quote. Make sure they are correct. 
 

 2 .  If you intend to print quotes with item remarks , return to the upper-level form, and enter the Quoted Items (Commission Sales) form. 
 

 3 .  For each item, make sure that the remarks displayed in the Quoted Item (Commission)-Remarks sub-level form are correct. Explanation. Revise them if necessary. 
 Note: The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable). 
 
## To verify the vendor`s address:

 

 ·        Make sure the details of the vendor`s address are correctly defined in the Vendors form. 
 
## Verifying the Customer`s Addresses

 

Printouts of the quote display both the customer`s mailing and shipping addresses (the latter is determined by customer site). 
 
## To verify the customer`s mailing address:

 

 ·        Enter the Customers form and check that the customer`s main address is correct. 
 
## To verify the customer`s shipping address:

 

 ·        If the goods are to be delivered to a site other than the customer`s main address, make sure that site is correctly defined in the Shipping Address form (sub-level of the Price Quotes for Commiss'n Sales form).
