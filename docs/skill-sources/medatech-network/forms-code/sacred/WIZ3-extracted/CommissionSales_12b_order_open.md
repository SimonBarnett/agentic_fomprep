# To record a commission order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Commission Orders form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Follow-up tab). 
 

 2 .  Click the References tab. In the Price Quote column, indicate the number of the quote on which the order is based. Once you leave this column, the order will be itemized automatically on the basis of the price quote.
 Note: You can only base commission orders on active price quotes, with a status defined to allow orders to be opened. 
 

 3 .  Today`s date appears automatically as the order`s date. Revise it as necessary. 
 

 4 .  Fill in the Site , Shipment Mode and Sales Rep Number , if desired. 
 

 5 .  If the goods are to be delivered to a site other than the customer`s main address, specify the shipping address in one of the following ways: 
 

 ·      Designate a customer site in the Site column of this form (in the References tab). OR 
 

 ·      Record the shipping address manually in the Shipping Address sub-level form.
