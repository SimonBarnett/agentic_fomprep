# To record additional customer details:

 

 1 .  Enter the Comm. Order - Additional Data sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  If the customer has a billing customer, designate the billing customer`s number. 
 

 3 .  Specify relevant bank account information. 
 

 4 .  Indicate the terms of shipment (e.g., the shipping company), if desired. 
 

 5 .  Return to the upper-level form.
