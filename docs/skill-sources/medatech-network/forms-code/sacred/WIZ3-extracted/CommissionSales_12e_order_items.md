# To check order items:

 

 1 .  Enter the Commission Order Items sub-level form. This form lists the ordered items, as taken from the price quote. 
 

 2 .  If an item appears in more than one line (with different quantities), delete the extra lines. Example . 
 

 3 .  For each item in the commission order, ensure that the quantity, unit price and item discount are correct. 
 

 4 .  The Due Date is calculated automatically, based on the delivery time designated in the price quote. Revise it as necessary. 
 

 5 .  Specify the Requested Date of delivery. 
 

 6 .  Make sure the commission percentage for this item is correct. If no commission percentage appears, and both the vendor`s regular price list and the commission sales price list are recorded in the system, you can calculate the commission percentage and the commission for every item in the order. To do so, return to the upper-level form and select Calc. Commission by Vendor Price from the list of Direct Activations .
 Note: To view the commission for each ordered item, return to the Commission Order Items sub-level form. 
 

 7 .  After you have finished itemizing the order, return to the upper-level form and click the Follow-up tab. Adjust the Status of the order (e.g., to Completed ).
