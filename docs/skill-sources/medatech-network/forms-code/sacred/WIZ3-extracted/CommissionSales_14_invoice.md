# Recording a Vendor Invoice

 

You record a vendor invoice to indicate delivery of the ordered goods. The invoice is automatically itemized with the parts in the attached commission order(s). A line in an invoice which reports the full delivery of an ordered quantity closes the parallel line in the commission order. 
 

 To record a vendor invoice: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Vendor Invoices-Commission Sales form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the invoice to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the References tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the vendor number. 
 

 3 .  Specify the date of the invoice. 
 

 4 .  Record the number appearing on the vendor invoice. 
 

 5 .  If the invoice is for delivery of a single commission order, click the References tab and specify this order in the Commission Order column. Otherwise, click to continue.
