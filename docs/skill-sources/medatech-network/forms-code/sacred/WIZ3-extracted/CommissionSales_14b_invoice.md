# To report delivery of ordered items:

 

 1 .  Enter the Invoice Items (Commission Sales) sub-level form. All items in the specified commission order(s) appear. 
 

 2 .  For each item in the invoice, if the quantity delivered equals the balance of the order to be supplied ( Balance to Ship ), flag the Approve Quantity column. Otherwise, designate the supplied quantity in the Reported Delivery column. 
 

 3 .  Make sure unit prices and discounts (if any), are correct. 
 

 4 .  Ensure that the percentage of commission and the total commission are correct. 
 

 5 .  Return to the upper-level form and click the References tab. Adjust the Status of the invoice (e.g., to Final ).
