# Recording a Credit Note

 

A credit note lists the amounts of commission approved by the vendor. These amounts are based on vendor invoices. 
 
## To record a credit note: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Credit Notes form. 
 

 2 .  Designate the date of the credit note and the vendor number. 
 

 3 .  Designate the estimated pay date. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Credit Note Details sub-level form. 
 

 5 .  List all relevant vendor invoices.
 Note: Only invoices with a status flagged as Final and not flagged as Commission Paid will appear in the Choose list. 
 

 6 .  For each invoice, check the value in the Commission A mount column. This should display the balance of the commission approved for this invoice. Revise the amount, if necessary. 
 

 7 .  Return to the upper-level form. Check that the Total Amount column displays the balance of the commissions you are owed. If it does not, repeat step 6. 
 Tip: Leave the Credit Notes form open. 
 
## Note:

 

To view a list of credit notes, together with the corresponding commission orders and invoices, run the Commission and Invoices report.
