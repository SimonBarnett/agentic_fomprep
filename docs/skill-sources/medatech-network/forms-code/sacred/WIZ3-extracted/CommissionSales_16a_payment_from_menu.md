# Recording Payment of Commission

 
## Before You Begin

 

 ·        Make sure a journal entry debiting the vendor`s account was recorded in the system. 
 
## To record payment of commission:

 

 1 .  Enter the Credit Notes form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the credit note in question. 
 

 3 .  In the Payment Entry No. column, specify the number of the journal entry debiting the vendor`s account.
