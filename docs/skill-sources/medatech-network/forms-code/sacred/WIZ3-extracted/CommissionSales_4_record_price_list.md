# Recording a Vendor`s Commission Sales Price List

 

If you work with vendors on a regular basis, you can define a commission sales price list for each one. Consequently, when you fill in a price quote for commission sales and record the vendor number, the price will be taken from this price list automatically, although it may be revised. 
 
## To create a commission sales price list:

 

 1 .  Enter the Vendor Price Lists form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired vendor`s price lists. 
 

 3 .  If a vendor has more than one price list, move to the line for the one used in commission sales. 
 

 4 .  Flag the Commission Prices column. 
 
## Note: 

 

You can also create a new commission sales price list for the vendor. On how to create a vendor price list, see the Vendor Parts and Prices Wizard .
