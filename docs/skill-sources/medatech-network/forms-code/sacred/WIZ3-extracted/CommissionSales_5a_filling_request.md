# Itemizing the RFQ

 

 Note: Click to record an RFQ for a product bundle . 
 

 1 .  Enter the Quoted Items (Commission Sales) sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the quoted item, by the system part number or by the vendor/manufacturer`s number for this part. 
 

 3 .  Specify the delivery time, in days or weeks. Flag the Days or Weeks column accordingly. 
 

 4 .  If quoted price depends on quantity, designate the requested quantity.
 Note: To receive different price quotes for different quantities of the same part, record a new line for each quantity. 
 

 5 .  If the unit price appears automatically, delete it. 
 

 6 .  Specify the target price for this item, if desired. 
 

 7 .  Return to the upper-level form.
