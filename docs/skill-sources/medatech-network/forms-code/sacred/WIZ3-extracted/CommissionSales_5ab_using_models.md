# To itemize the RFQ in the case of a product bundle:

 

 1 .  Enter the Product Bundles sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  List the product bundles included in the current RFQ; for each bundle, record the quantity to which the price quote will refer. 
 

 3 .  Enter the parallel sub-level form, Quoted Items (Commission Sales) . It is itemized automatically according to the recorded product bundles and their quantities. 
 

 4 .  The Part Number of each default component for this bundle is filled in automatically. To change an item, press F6 and select the part in question from the Choose list.
 Note: The Choose list includes all parts defined as substitutes for the default component in this product bundle. 
 

 5 .  Specify the delivery time, in days or weeks. Flag the Days or Weeks column accordingly. 
 

 6 .  Make sure the part quantity is correct. 
 

 7 .  If a unit price appears automatically, copy it to the Target Price (first ensure that it is correct and revise when necessary. ) and delete it from the Unit Price column. 
 

 8 .  If a Discount% was defined for the component part, it will appear automatically. Revise as necessary. 
 

 9 .  Return to the upper-level form. 
 
## Note:

 

Click to add items that are not part of the product bundle .
