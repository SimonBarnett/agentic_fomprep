# Printing an RFQ: Before You Begin

 
## To check remarks:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quote (Commission)-Remarks sub-level form. The remarks recorded there will appear on all printouts of the RFQ. Make sure they are correct. 
 

 2 .  If you intend to print RFQs with item remarks , return to the upper-level form, and enter the Quoted Items (Commission Sales) form. 
 

 3 .  For each item, make sure that the remarks displayed in the Quoted Item (Commission)-Remarks sub-level form are correct. Revise them if necessary. 
 Note s : 
 

 ·              Any part remarks recorded in the part catalogue appear in this form automatically if Copy Part Remarks has been flagged for price quotes for commission sales (in the Copy Part Remarks to Document form). 
 

 ·              The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable). 
 
## To verify the vendor`s address:

 

 ·        Make sure the details of the vendor`s address are correctly defined in the Vendors form. 
 
## Verifying the Customer`s Addresses

 

Printouts of the RFQ display both the customer`s mailing and shipping addresses (the latter is determined by customer site). 
 
## To verify the customer`s mailing address:

 

 ·        Enter the Customers form and check that the customer`s main address is correct. 
 
## To verify the customer`s shipping address:

 

 ·        If the goods are to be delivered to a site other than the customer`s main address, make sure that site is correctly defined in the Shipping Address form (sub-level of the Price Quotes for Commiss'n Sales form).
