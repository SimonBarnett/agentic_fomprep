# Recording the Vendor`s Quoted Prices: Before You Begin

 
## Determining Your Commission

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Parameters for Vendors form and retrieve the vendor who supplies the ordered parts. 
 

 2 .  Indicate the percentage of commission this vendor pays you: 
 

 ·      If the vendor pays a fixed percentage of commission for all (or most) commission sales, ensure that this percentage appears in the Commission column. 
 

 ·      If the vendor pays a different percentage of commission for sales of different part families, enter the Order Commissions per Family sub-level form. For each relevant part family, specify the family code and the percentage of commission. 
 
## Determining How Commission is Calculated 

 

The commission owed by the vendor is automatically calculated in an order. 
 

Enter the Logistic Constants form to make sure the value of the SCommissType constant is in keeping with how commission should be calculated for an order.
