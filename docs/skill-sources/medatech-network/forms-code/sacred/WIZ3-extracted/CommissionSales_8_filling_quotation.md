# To record a price quote when there is no RFQ:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quotes for Commiss'n Sales form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Follow-up tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the customer for whom the price quote is intended. 
 

 3 .  Choose a customer contact, if desired. The printout of the quote will be addressed to that person.
 Tip: You can set up a default contact for all price quotes to this customer in the Price Quote Contact column of the Customer Contacts form (a sub-level of the Customers form). This default will then appear automatically in all subsequent quotes to this customer. 
 

 4 .  Specify the vendor who is to supply the ordered parts. 
 

 5 .  Today`s date appears automatically as the quote`s date. Revise it as necessary. 
 

 6 .  The expiration date of the price quote appears automatically. Revise it as necessary.
