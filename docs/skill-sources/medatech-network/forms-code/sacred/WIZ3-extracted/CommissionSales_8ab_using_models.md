# To itemize the price quote in the case of a product bundle:

 

 1 .  Enter the Product Bundles sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  List the product bundles included in the current price quote. 
 

 3 .  For each bundle, record the quantity to which the price quote refers. 
 

 4 .  Enter the parallel sub-level form, Quoted Items (Commission Sales) . It is itemized automatically according to the recorded product bundles and their quantities. 
 

 5 .  The Part Number of each default component for this bundle is filled in automatically. To change an item, press F6 and select the part in question from the Choose list.
 Note: The Choose list includes all parts defined as substitutes for the default component in this product bundle. 
 

 6 .  Specify the delivery time, in days or weeks. Flag the Days or Weeks column accordingly. 
 

 7 .  Make sure the part quantity is correct. 
 

 8 .  If a unit price and/or discount appear automatically, ensure that they are correct and revise when necessary. . 
 

 9 .  If a default commission percentage has not been defined for the vendor, and both the vendor`s regular price list and the commission sales price list are recorded in the system, you can calculate the commission percentage and the commission for every item in the price quote. To do so, return to the upper-level form and select Calc. Commission by Vendor Price from the list of Direct Activations .
 Note: To view the commission for each quoted item, return to the Quoted Items (Commission Sales) sub-level form. 
 

 10 .  Click to add items that are not part of the product bundle . 
 

 11 .  After you have finished itemizing the price quote, return to the upper-level form and click the Follow-up tab. Adjust the Status of the price quote (e.g., to Ready ).
