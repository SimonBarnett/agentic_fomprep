# Itemizing the Price Quote

 

 Note: Click to record a price quote for a product bundle . 
 

 1 .  Enter the Quoted Items (Commission Sales) sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the quoted item, by the system part number or by the vendor/manufacturer`s number for this part. 
 

 3 .  Indicate the customer`s number for this part, if desired. 
 

 4 .  Specify the delivery time, in days or weeks. Flag the Days or Weeks column accordingly. 
 

 5 .  Designate the quantity of the item.
 Note: To record different prices for different quantities of the same part, record a new line for each quantity. 
 

 6 .  If the unit price appears automatically, it has probably been taken from the vendor`s commission sales price list. Check it and revise if necessary. 
 

 7 .  Enter any discount offered by the vendor in the Discount% column. 
 

 8 .  If a default commission percentage has not been defined for the vendor, and both the vendor`s regular price list and the commission sales price list are recorded in the system, you can calculate the commission percentage and the commission for every item in the price quote. To do so, return to the upper-level form and select Calc. Commission by Vendor Price from the list of Direct Activations .
 Note: To view the commission for each quoted item, return to the Quoted Items (Commission Sales) sub-level form. 
 

 9 .  After you have finished itemizing the price quote, return to the upper-level form and click the Follow-up tab. Adjust the Status of the price quote (e.g., to Ready ).
