Commission Sales - Recording a Vendor Price Quote from an RFQ 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record a price quote based on the vendor`s reply: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quotes for Commiss'n Sales form, click the References tab and retrieve the RFQ on which this quote is based. 
 

 2 .  Specify the vendor`s reference (in the Vendor `s Ref. Column), as taken from the vendor`s price quote. 
 

 3 .  If you want this quote to serve as the basis of additional quotes (including prices), flag the Price Source column. 
 

 4 .  Revise the quote`s date and expiration date, if necessary. 
 

 5 .  Choose a customer contact, if desired. The printout of the quote will be addressed to that person.
 Tip: You can set up a default contact for all price quotes to this customer in the Price Quote Contact column of the Customer Contacts form (a sub-level of the Customers form). This default will then appear automatically in all subsequent quotes to this customer. 
 

 6 .  If the percentage of commission is the same for every quoted item, and this percentage differs from the vendor`s default commission, record it in the Commission % column (in the Vendor & Prices tab).
 Note: If a value is specified here, you cannot record a different percentage for individual items.
