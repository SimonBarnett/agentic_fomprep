# To itemize the price quote based on the vendor`s reply:

 

 1 .   Enter the Quoted Items (Commission Sales) sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  For each item, specify the Part Number and Unit Price . 
 

 3 .  Revise the Delivery Time , if necessary. 
 

 4 .  Check that the Quantity is correctly recorded. 
 

 5 .  Enter any discount offered by the vendor in the Discount% column. 
 

 6 .  If a default commission percentage has not been defined for the vendor, and both the vendor`s regular price list and the commission sales price list are recorded in the system, you can calculate the commission percentage and the commission for every item in the price quote. To do so, return to the upper-level form and select Calc. Commission by Vendor Price from the list of Direct Activations .
 Note: To view the commission for each quoted item, return to the Quoted Items (Commission Sales) sub-level form. 
 

 7 .  After you have finished itemizing the price quote, return to the upper-level form and click the Follow-up tab. Adjust the Status of the price quote (e.g., to Ready ).
