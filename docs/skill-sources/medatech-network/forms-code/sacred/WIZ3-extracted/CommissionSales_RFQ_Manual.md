# To record an RFQ manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quotes for Commiss'n Sales form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Follow-up tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the customer for which the price quote is intended. 
 

 3 .  Specify the vendor that is to supply the ordered parts. 
 

 4 .  Choose a vendor contact, if desired. The printout of the RFQ will be addressed to that person.
 Tip: You can set up a default contact for all RFQs to this vendor in the Vendor Quote Contact column of the Vendor Contacts form (a sub-level of the Vendors form). This default will then appear automatically in all subsequent RFQs to this vendor. 
 

 5 .  Today`s date appears automatically as the RFQ`s date. Revise it as necessary. 
 

 6 .  The expiration date of the price quote appears automatically. Revise it as necessary. 
 

 7 .  Click the References tab and choose the type of commission sale, if desired.
 Tip: To add a new sale type, use the Types of Commission Sales form. 
 

 8 .  If the goods are to be delivered to a site other than the customer`s main address, specify the shipping address in one of the following ways: 
 

 ·      Designate a customer site in the Site column of this form (in the References tab). OR 
 

 ·      Record the shipping address manually in the Shipping Address sub-level form.
