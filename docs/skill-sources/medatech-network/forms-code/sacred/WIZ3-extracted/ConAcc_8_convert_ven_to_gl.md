# Converting an A/P Account into a GL Account

 

To convert an A/P account into a GL account, you simply change its balance sheet item to the one that will be appropriate for the GL account. Once you leave the form, the account in question will no longer appear in the Chart of Accounts Payable. Rather, it will appear in the Chart of Accounts. 
 
## To convert an A/P account into a GL account:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts Payable form and retrieve the desired A/P account. 
 

 2 .  Click the Report Definitions tab. Change the Balance Sheet Item to the one you wish to be assigned to it in the Chart of Accounts. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and retrieve the account in question. Change the Trial Bal Item as necessary.
