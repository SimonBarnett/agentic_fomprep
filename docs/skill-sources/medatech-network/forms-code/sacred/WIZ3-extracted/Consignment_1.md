# To ship goods to a consignment warehouse:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Shipments form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the customer number.
 Note: The To Consignmt Warehs column is filled in automatically with the consignment warehouse defined for this customer. If a transit warehouse has also been defined, it appears instead, indicating that the goods are in transit. 
 

 3 .  In the General tab, the Sending Warehouse is filled in automatically, but may be revised. Specify a Sending Bin , if desired. 
 

 4 .  Click the Price tab and remove the check mark from the Billable column. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Shipped Items sub-level form. 
 

 6 .  For each item, specify the Part Number and Quantity .
 Notes: 
 

 ·      For help itemizing the shipment, run the Customer Shipment/Return Wizard . 
 

 ·      If the consignment warehouse serves as a virtual warehouse for numerous customers (i.e., the Inv. by Customer column is flagged in the Warehouses form), the part's To Status column is filled in automatically with the ID number of the receiving customer (to view all goods consigned to this customer, enter the Customer's Inventory sub-level form). Otherwise, the part retains its From Status , usually “Goods”. 
 

 7 .  Return to the upper-level form and change the status to Final (in the General tab).
