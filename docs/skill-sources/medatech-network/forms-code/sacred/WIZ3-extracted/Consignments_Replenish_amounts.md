# Defining Replenishment Quantities and Increments for Parts

 

In order to determine the quantity of a certain part to be ordered for a consignment warehouse, the Replenish Consignment Warehouses program first consults the minimum quantity of the part that can be ordered, together with its permitted increment. Example. 
 
## To define replenishment parameters for a part: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase/MRP Parameters for Part form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Click the Replenishment tab. 
 

 3 .  In the Minimum for Replenishment column, specify the smallest quantity (in buy/sell units) of this part that may be ordered at a time. 
 

 4 .  In the Increment for Replenishment column, specify the number of parts (in buy/sell units) by which the order quantity may be increased.
