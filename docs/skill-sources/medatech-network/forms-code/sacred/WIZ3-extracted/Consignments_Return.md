# To record the receipt of returned goods:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Returns form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the customer number.
 Note: The From Consignmt Warehs column is filled in automatically with the customer's consignment warehouse, or with its linked transit warehouse , if one has been defined. 
 

 3 .  In the General tab, the Receiving Warehouse is filled in automatically, but may be revised. Specify a Bin , if desired. 
 

 4 .  Click the Price tab and remove the check mark from the Billable column. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Returned Items sub-level form. 
 

 6 .  For each item, specify the Part Number and Quantity . 
 Tip: For help itemizing the return, run the Customer Shipment/Return Wizard . 
 

 7 .  The To Status column is automatically filled in with each part's original pre-shipment status, usually “Goods”. 
 

 8 .  Return to the upper-level form and change the status to Final (in the General tab).
