# Linking Financial Transactions to Profit/Cost Centers

 

Costs and revenues are linked to profit and cost centers each time a financial transaction is documented, in one of three ways: 
 

 ·        In the transaction document, one profit or cost center can be assigned to each item in the document's itemization form. The assigned center appears in all subsequent transaction documents and in the entry journal. Example. Up to five profit/cost groups can be assigned to invoice items. 
 

 ·        In the entry journal, one profit or cost center can be assigned in each of up to five profit/cost groups, to each entry item. 
 

 ·        You can set profit or cost centers to appear automatically in transaction documents by pre-assigning them to specific GL accounts, warehouses, accounting families, accounting families in specific warehouses, customer groups, projects and/or branches. Example. 
 

You can also require a profit/cost center to be assigned to transactions involving a particular account, or restrict the permitted profit/cost centers for an account to those attached to a specific tree. 
 

Once profit/cost centers have been assigned to transactions, you can calculate the allocation of indirect costs to profit centers, and view the results.
