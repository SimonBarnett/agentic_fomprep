# Linking Invoice Items to Multiple Profit/Cost Centers

 

When profit/cost centers are pre-assigned (e.g., per warehouse, customer group, project), they appear automatically in the line items of invoices. However, you can also assign them manually. 
 
## To link invoice items to multiple profit/cost centers manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the invoice (e.g., Sales Invoices ). 
 

 2 .  Enter the sub-level form in which the invoice is itemized (e.g., Invoice Items ). 
 

 3 .  For each line, specify the desired profit/cost centers, one per profit/cost group (e.g., 101 in the Profit/Cost Center column, 201 in the Profit/Cost Center 2 column).
 Note: If a specific profit/cost center tree has been defined for the account being credited/debited, you can only specify a profit/cost center from that tree in the first Profit/Cost Center column.
