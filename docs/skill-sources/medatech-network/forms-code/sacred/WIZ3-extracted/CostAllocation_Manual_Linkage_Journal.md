# Linking Journal Entry Items to Profit/Cost Centers

 

The profit/cost centers assigned in the invoice or other financial document are automatically transferred to the journal entry. However, you can also assign them manually. 
 
## If the entry has not yet been posted:

 

 1 .  Enter the Entry Journal form and retrieve the desired journal entry. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Itemized Entry sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  For each line, specify the desired profit/cost centers, one per profit/cost group (e.g., 101 in the Profit/Cost Center column, 201 in the Profit/Cost Center 2 column).
 Note: If a specific profit/cost center tree has been defined for the account being credited/debited, you can only specify a profit/cost center from that tree in the first Profit/Cost Center column. 
 
## If the entry has already been posted:

 

 1 .  Enter the Entry Item Revisions form and retrieve the desired journal entry item. 
 

 2 .  Specify the desired profit/cost centers, one per profit/cost group (e.g., 101 in the Profit/Cost Center column, 201 in the Profit/Cost Center 2 column).
 Note s : 
 

 ·      If a specific profit/cost center tree has been defined for the account being credited/debited, you can only specify a profit/cost center from that tree in the first Profit/Cost Center column. 
 

 ·      If the Bud NoChange constant is set to 1, you will not be able to change profit/cost centers in entries that fall in a closed fiscal period.
