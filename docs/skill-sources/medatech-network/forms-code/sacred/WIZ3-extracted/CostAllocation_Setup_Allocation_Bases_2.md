# To define bases of cost allocation:

 

 1 .  Enter the Bases of Cost Allocation form. The form displays ten predefined bases of allocation, five for weighted parameters and five for direct costs.
 Tip: You can add new bases of allocation when the defaults are used up. 
 

 2 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              To allocate a cost based on a weighted parameter, choose one of the first five codes and change its description to suit your needs. 
 

 ·              To allocate a cost based on direct or weighted direct costs, choose one of the second five codes and change its description to suit your needs. 
 

 3 .  Select the Group among whose profit centers the cost is to be distributed. 
 

 4 .  Flag the In Use column. 
 

 5 .  If you are using direct costs or weighted direct costs, do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Flag the By Direct Costs /Mnth column to distribute indirect costs in keeping with direct costs since the beginning of the month. 
 

 ·              Flag the By Direct Costs YTD column to distribute them in keeping with direct costs since the beginning of the year. 
 

 6 .  Enter the Cost Allocation Dates sub-level form, and do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If you want to define different versions of the current base of allocation, which will go into effect on different dates, open a line for each version, specify its starting Date , and enter the Allocations per Profit Center sub-level form. 
 

 ·              If you do not want to limit the lifespan of the current base of allocation, ignore the default date that appears automatically and then enter the Allocations per Profit Center sub-level form. 
 

 7 .  List the profit centers among which you want to allocate the indirect costs. 
 

 8 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If you are using a weighted parameter or weighted direct costs, list the weight for each profit center. The resulting percent appears automatically. 
 

 ·              If you are using direct costs, leave a 1 in the Weight column for each profit center. 
 
## Tip:

 

To view the breakdown of each cost allocation base among its profit centers, run the Bases of Cost Allocation report.
