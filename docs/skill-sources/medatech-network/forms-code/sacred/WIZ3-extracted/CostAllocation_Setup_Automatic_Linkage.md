# Setting Up Automatic Links to Profit and Cost Centers

 

When a profit/cost center is defined for one of the following entities, it appears automatically in the line items of invoices opened for that entity. If two different links are activated in the same invoice (for example, different profit centers are linked to the customer and to the warehouse recorded in the invoice), one of two things will occur: 
 

 ·        If the profit/cost centers belong to different groups, each center will appear in its respective group column. 
 

 ·        If the profit/cost centers belong to the same group, the center that appears is selected according to a set order of precedence . 
 
## To link a profit/cost center to a GL account:

 

 ·        Enter the Chart of Accounts form and record a default profit/cost center for one or more profit/cost groups. Click for more details. 
 Note: You can also link a profit/cost center to an account and budget item (e.g., in the Budget for Profit/Cost Ctr+Acct form). 
 
## To link a profit/cost center to an accounting family:

 

 1 .  Enter the Accounts for Families form and retrieve the desired Family . 
 

 2 .  Specify the Profit/Cost Center in the Income & Purchasing Accounts tab. 
 
## To link a profit/cost center to a warehouse:

 

 1 .  Enter the Accounts for Warehouses form and retrieve the desired Warehouse . 
 

 2 .  Specify the Profit/Cost Center in the Income & Purchasing Accounts tab. 
 
## To link a profit/cost center to an accounting family within a warehouse:

 

 1 .  Enter the Accounts for Warehouses form and retrieve the desired Warehouse . 
 

 2 .  Enter the Acc'ts for Families in Warehouse sub-level form and retrieve the desired Family . 
 

 3 .  Specify the Profit/Cost Center .
