 To link a profit center to a customer group: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Accounts for Customer Groups form and retrieve the desired customer group. 
 

 2 .  Specify the Profit Center . 
 
## To link a profit center to a project:

 

 1 .  Enter the Accounts for Projects form and retrieve the desired project. 
 

 2 .  Specify the Profit Center . 
 
## To link a profit/cost center to a branch:

 

 1 .  Enter the Branches form and retrieve the desired company branch. 
 

 2 .  Click the Financial Data tab and specify the Profit/Cost Center .
