# To view the inventory transaction costs used to calculate the actual cost of an assembled part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Product Costing form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired part, by Part Number and Costing Date . 
 

 3 .  Enter the Product Transactions sub-level form. This form displays the inventory transactions taken into account by the costing program. 
 
## To analyze the cost of receiving an assembled part, based on its components:

 

 1 .  Enter the Audit Trail form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the components of the assembled part by specifying "Assm Component" in the Inv. Transaction column, and the relevant assembly document in the Doc/Invoice Number column. 
 

 3 .  Calculate the total cost of the assembly by adding together the issue costs of its child parts.
