Costing for Businesses - Viewing Inventory Values 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To view inventory values for a specific part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Material Costing or Product Costing form, depending on the part type. 
 

 2 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If you want to view the part's inventory valuation in a certain run of the costing program, retrieve by Part Number and Costing Date . 
 

 ·              If you want to view the part's inventory valuation at different points in time, retrieve by Part Number only. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Materials Inventory Valuation or Product Inventory Valuation sub-level form. This form displays the inventory balances of the part and their values, as of the costing date. 
 
## Note: 

 

Standard inventory values appearing in this form are calculated for each costing run on the basis of child part and other costs. These values may differ from standard costs appearing in the Part Catalogue or Standard Costs form. 
 

For purchased parts, these forms display the costs defined by the user, which serve as the basis for the calculation of standard costs by the costing program. For assembled parts, they display the standard cost calculated by costing, provided that the UpdStdCost constant is set to 1 (otherwise, they display the standard cost that was recorded manually, which is irrelevant to costing calculations). 
 
## To view various cross-sections of inventory values:

 

 ·        Run the reports in the Inventory Valuation Reports menu.
