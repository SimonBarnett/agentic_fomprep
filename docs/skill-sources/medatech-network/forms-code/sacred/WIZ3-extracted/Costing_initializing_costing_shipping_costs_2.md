# To define standard shipping costs: 

 

 1 .  Do any of the following: 
 

 ·              To define per part family: Enter the Part Families form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 ·              To define per part: Enter the Financial Parameters for Parts form, retrieve the desired part and click the Shipping Expenses tab. 
 

 ·              To define per vendor part: Enter the Financial Parameters for Vendors form and retrieve the desired vendor; then, enter the Shipping Costs per Part sub-level form and move to the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Record a standard Air Cost (%) , Sea Cost (%) and/or Land Cost (%) , as appropriate, as a percentage of the part's unit price. 
 

 3 .  Select the appropriate default cost type in the Shipping Cost Type column. This value will appear as the default for this part in any purchase document that you open, but may be revised.
