# Warehouse Costing Definitions 

 

When defining warehouses, the Inventory Valued flag determines whether the warehouse contents are included in inventory valuations, and the Transaction Costing flag determines how the costing program relates to inventory transactions to and from the warehouse. 
 

If the Transaction Costing column is not flagged, the warehouse is treated like an external one. Transactions from the warehouse to a company warehouse are therefore considered receipts into inventory (as opposed to warehouse transfers), and transactions from the company to such a warehouse are considered shipments of outgoing inventory. Transactions from such a warehouse to a customer, or from a vendor to the warehouse, are not taken into account when calculating the part's average cost. 
 

 Notes: 
 

 ·        Costing calculates the cost of inventory transactions in any case; the Transaction Costing column determines whether these costs are used when calculating average part cost. 
 

 ·        You cannot flag a warehouse for participation in inventory valuations if it is not flagged for participation in transaction costing. 
 
## To determine the way costing will relate to your company's warehouses:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouses form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve all existing warehouses. 
 

 3 .  For each warehouse, make sure that the Inventory Valued and Transaction Costing columns are correctly flagged.
