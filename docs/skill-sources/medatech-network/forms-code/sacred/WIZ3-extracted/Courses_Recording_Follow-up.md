# Recording Follow-up Information

 

When the course is over, you can record which participants passed the course, the numbers of credits they earned, whether they provided feedback about the course and any additional remarks (e.g., the feedback itself). 
 
## To record follow-up information:

 

 1 .  Enter the Courses of Study form and retrieve the course in question. 
 

 2 .  In the Instruction Info tab, record the score given as a result of an evaluation of the course in the Evaluation Score column. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Participants in Course sub-level form and record information for any of the following columns: Passed , Feedback Received and/or Credits(Code) .
 Tip: To add new types of credits, use the Course Credits form. 
 

 4 .  Record any additional remarks in the parallel Remarks sub-level form.
