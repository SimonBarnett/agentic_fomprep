# Recording General Course Information

 

 1 .  Enter the Courses of Study form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the course to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. You can also change the course Status . 
 

 2 .  Record the name of the course. A course number is assigned automatically. Change it, if desired. 
 

 3 .  Today's date appears automatically in the From Date column. Revise it if necessary. 
 

 4 .  In the To Date column, record the date on which the course ends. 
 

 5 .  Choose the topic of the course in the Topic column. 
 Tip: To define a new course topic, use the Topics of Study form. 
 

 6 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If the course is given by your organization, click the Instruction Info tab and select the Instructor ' s Employee ID .
 Note: Only employees flagged as instructors in the Personnel File form can be chosen. 
 

 ·              If the course is external, click the Add ' l Details tab and record the instructor in the Training Ctr Teacher column.
