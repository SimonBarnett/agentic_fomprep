Courses of Study - Recording Course Participants 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record course participants:

 

 1 .  Enter the Participants in Course form (a sub-level of the Courses of Study form). 
 

 2 .  If the participant is from your organization, choose his or her employee ID. If the participant is from an external organization, record his or her name in the Name of Participant column and choose the company number. 
 

 3 .  If there is a contact for an external participant, choose that person in the Contact column.
 Note: You can add new contacts in the Customer Contacts form (a sub-level of the Customers form). 
 

 4 .  In the Degree/Certif. Code column, choose the highest diploma or academic degree held by the participant (outside the company), or the degree/diploma toward which he or she is taking this course. 
 Tip: You can record new degrees/certificates in the Degrees/Certificates form. 
 

 5 .  Record any additional information about the participant in the Details column. 
 

 6 .  If completion of the course automatically grants certification (internal to the company), in the Certification for Participant sub-level form, record the type and level of the certification.
 Tip: To add more certification codes, use the Certification form. For more levels of certifications, use the Certification Levels form. 
 
## Tips: 

 

 ·        Use the Participants in Course report to prepare a list of participants in a course. 
 

 ·        To view the courses attended by a given employee, use the Courses of Study form (a sub-level of the Personnel File form).
