# Defining Payment Means for a Credit Card Company

 

You must define at least one payment means for each credit card company. If you have two different credit cards from a given company or two different sets of payment receipt dates, define two payment means for it. 
 

Once you have defined a credit card as a payment means, you can define a set of payment terms applicable to that card. When recording a customer receipt entailing payment with that credit card, only one of the defined payment terms can be used. 
 
## To define a means of payment:

 

 1 .  Enter the Payment Means form. 
 

 2 .  Fill in the Payment Code and Means of Payment columns. Flag the Credit Card and Allow Negative Value columns. 
 

 3 .  You can set it up so that all payments received from the credit card company are directly deposited into the company's account (rather than to a cashier). To use this feature, choose the account in the Cash/Bank/Credit Cd column. Example. 
 
## To restrict the payment terms applicable to the credit card:

 

 1 .  Enter the Payment Terms for Credit Cards sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Select the applicable Payment Terms . 
 

 3 .  If there is more than one, add each on a separate line. 
 
## Note:

 

To allow unrestricted usage of payment terms, leave this form blank.
