# Recording Payment Receipt Dates

 

You need to record the dates in the month when the credit card company issues payment to you for credit card vouchers. 
 
## To record payment receipt dates:

 

 1 .  Enter the Payment Receipt Dates sub-level form.
 Note: If you defined more than one payment means for this credit card company, use the Payment Means form and its sub-level Paymt Receipt Dates for CreditCd instead. 
 

 2 .  The Day on Voucher column lists all the dates of the month. For each date, record the corresponding payment date in the Day of Payment column. Example. 
 

 3 .  If, for a given voucher date, payment is made in the following month and this is not obvious (that is, the payment date is a larger number than the voucher date), flag the Next Month column. Example.
