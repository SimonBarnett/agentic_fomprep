# To define a GL account for a credit card company:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 2 .  Fill in the Account No. and Account Description columns. 
 

 3 .  Click the Report tab and fill in Trial Bal Item and Bal Sheet/P&L Item columns as well. 
 Tip: Once you specify the account number, the Trial Bal Item column is filled in with 000 as a default. You can change this either now or before you run your first trial balance report. 
 
## Note:

 

For detailed information on setting up an account, see the Open GL Accounts Wizard .
