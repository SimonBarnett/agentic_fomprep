# Changing the Order of Currencies

 

After the predefined local currency (whose position is always 1 ), and the second currency (whose position must be 2 ), all other currencies are listed in alphabetical order. The order of the currencies in the Currencies form is reflected in the Itemized Rates sub-level form and in all currency Choose lists (e.g., in quotes, price lists, orders). If you work with other currencies besides the local one, you can position those currencies near the top of the list, for convenience. Example. 
 
## To change the order of currencies:

 

 1 .  Enter the Currencies form and move to the currency in question. 
 

 2 .  In the Pos column, assign a number to the currency. The lower the number, the higher the place on the list.
