# Viewing Exchange Rates

 
## To view the most recently recorded exchange rates:

 

 ·           Enter the Currencies form. The exchange rate appears in the Exchange Rate column. 
 
## Tips:

 

 ·           Enter the Past Exchange Rates sub-level form to view previously recorded rates. 
 

 ·           You may find the following reports helpful: 
 

 ·                  Daily Exchange Rates 
 

 ·                  Exchange Rates per Currency
