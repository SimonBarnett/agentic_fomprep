# To copy a batch of past exchange rates from an MS-Excel spreadsheet:

 

 1 .  Create an Excel spreadsheet that includes columns for the exchange rates and dates. 
 

 2 .  In the spreadsheet, select the exchange rate and date columns for the desired range of dates and press Ctrl+C to copy this data to the clipboard. 
 

 3 .  In Priority , enter the Currencies form. Move to the currency in question and enter the Exchange Rates Load Table sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Move to an empty line, place the cursor in the Date column and press Ctrl+V to paste the data from the clipboard. 
 

 5 .  Make sure the copied data appears in this form correctly, then return to the upper-level form and select Update Exch Rates by Load Table from the list of Direct Activations to load the copied exchange rates into the Past Exchange Rates sub-level form for this currency. 
 
## Note:

 

In a multi-company setting, repeat the procedure (starting with step 2) for each Priority company.
