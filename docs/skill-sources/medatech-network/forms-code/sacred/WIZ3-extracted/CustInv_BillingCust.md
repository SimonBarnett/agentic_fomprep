# When There is a Billing Customer

 

When there is a billing customer, you have several options: 
 

 ·        You can address the invoice/memo to the billing customer (so that that customer will appear on the printout) and debit/credit the account of the billing customer. 
 

 ·        You can address the invoice/memo to the regular customer and debit/credit the account of the billing customer. 
 

 ·        You can address the invoice/memo to the regular customer and debit/credit the account of the regular customer. 
 

 ·        You can address the invoice/memo to the regular customer and debit/credit the account of a third party. 
 
## To address the invoice/memo to and debit/credit the account of the billing customer:

 

 ·        Specify the billing customer's number when opening the invoice/memo. 
 
## To address the invoice/memo to the regular customer and debit/credit the account of the billing customer:

 

 ·        Specify the regular customer's number when opening the invoice/memo. 
 
## To address the invoice/memo to and debit/credit the account of the regular customer: 

 

 1 .  Specify the regular customer's number when opening the invoice/memo. 
 

 2 .  In the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form, designate the regular customer's account number in the A/R Acct to Charge . 
 
## To address the invoice/memo to the regular customer and debit/credit the account of the third party:

 

 1 .  Specify the regular customer's number when opening the invoice/memo. 
 

 2 .  In the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form, designate the third party's account number in the A/R Acct to Charge .
