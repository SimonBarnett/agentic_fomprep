# To record additional details:

 

 1 .  Return to the Over-the-Counter Invoices upper-level form. 
 

 2 .  Click the Price tab. The Total Before Discount , Overall Discount , Sales Tax (for dual-currency users: VAT ) and Amount Owing appear automatically, according to the itemized invoice. If necessary, revise the overall discount or the amount owing. 
 

 3 .  Click the Cash Receipts & Terms tab. Make sure that the Payment Balance is 0 .
 Note: You won't be able to finalize the invoice unless the amount of receipts equals the amount owing. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 5 .  Make sure that the Entry Code is correct. 
 

 6 .  If the invoice needs to be checked before it is presented to the customer, return to the upper-level form and assign it the Checking status (in the References tab). In the Assigned to column, specify the username of the person who is responsible for checking the document. The invoice cannot be finalized until this status is changed. 
 Tip: You can define work flow rules so that the assigned user changes automatically when the status is assigned. 
 

 7 .   For dual-currency users in Europe : Specify the Shipment Code , Delivery Terms and Nature of Transaction for the Intrastat report.
 Note: If this data differs for a specific line item, move to the item in the Invoice Items sub-level form and revise its details in the Invoice Data for Intrastat Rep sub-level.
