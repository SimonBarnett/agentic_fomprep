# Preparing an Over-the-Counter Invoice

 

An over-the-counter invoice records the removal of goods from inventory, bills the customer for those goods and records receipt of payment. You can itemize the invoice manually, or automatically on the basis of sales orders. 
 

After recording and checking the invoice, it should be finalized and printed. You can only finalize an invoice if the sum received is equal to the sum billed. Finalizing an invoice creates a journal entry. If necessary, you can perform certain revisions in final invoices or cancel them. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Record an invoice manually 
 

 Record an invoice based on a sales order 
 

 Finalize an invoice 
 

 View payments recorded in over-the-counter invoices 
 

 Print an invoice 
 

 Revise a final invoice 
 

 Cancel a final invoice 
 

 Delete a pending invoice
