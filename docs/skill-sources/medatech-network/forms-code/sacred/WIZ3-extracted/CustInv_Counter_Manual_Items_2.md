 Tip: To view information on usage of this budget item, enter the Budgeted vs. Actual sub-level form. 
 

 10 .  Check the Income Account . Revise it if necessary. Explanation. 
 

 11 .  The sales rep and the commission percentage due for the line item appear automatically. Revise them as necessary. 
 

 12 .  Enter the Invoice Item - Remarks sub-level form and check the remarks that appear there. Revise or add to them, as desired.
 Notes: 
 

 ·              Any part remarks recorded in the part catalogue appear in this form automatically if Copy Part Remarks has been flagged for over-the-counter invoices (in the Copy Part Remarks to Document form). 
 

 ·              The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable). 
 

 ·              To include these remarks in the printout of the invoice, select a suitable print format . 
 

 13 .  To record serial numbers for this part, move to the parallel Serial Numbers in Transaction or Auto Recording of Serial Nos. sub-level form.
 Tip: For more information on working with serialized parts, run the Serial Numbers Wizard .
