# To base an over-the-counter invoice on an order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Over-the-Counter Invoices form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 2 .  Specify the number of the customer to be billed. 
 Notes: 
 

 ·              Click if the customer who placed the order has a billing customer . 
 

 ·              In the case of a walk-in customer, record the walk-in customer number. Then revise the name in the Cust. Name column, and fill in the address and other details in the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 ·              The Cust. Name , Date and a temporary Invoice No. (prefixed with a T ) are filled in automatically when you leave the field. The invoice will receive a permanent number when it is finalized. 
 

 3 .  Check the Contact column to make sure that the relevant invoice contact appears. Change it if necessary.
 Tip: To define new contacts for the customer or to assign a default contact for invoices, use the Customer Contacts form, a sub-level of the Customers form. For more information, see the Open Customers Wizard . 
 

 4 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              To base the invoice on a single sales order, click the References tab and specify the order number. The invoice will be itemized by the chosen order, and the Details column will automatically display the order details, though they may be revised. 
 

 ·              To base the invoice on multiple sales orders, leave the Order column empty and enter the Orders for Invoice sub-level form. Record a line for each order you want to bill in the current invoice. Return to the upper-level form and record a remark in the Details column, as needed. 
 

 ·              To select individual order items, enter the Choose Order Items sub-level form and flag the Choose Item column for each desired item.
