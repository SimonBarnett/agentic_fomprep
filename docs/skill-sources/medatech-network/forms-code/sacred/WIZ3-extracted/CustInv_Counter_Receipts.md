# Recording Receipt of Payment

 

You can record payments received against this invoice in cash, by check, by promissory note or by credit card. 
 
## To record payments in cash:

 

 1 .  Return to the Over-the-Counter Invoices upper-level form. 
 

 2 .  Click the Cash Receipts & Terms tab, and specify the amount received in the Cash Payments column. 
 
## To record payments by check:

 

 ·        Enter the Check Details sub-level form and record the details of each check. 
 
## To record payments by promissory note:

 

 1 .  Return to the Over-the-Counter Invoices upper-level form. 
 

 2 .  Select Payment by Promissory Note from the list of Direct Activations. 
 

 3 .  In the program input, specify the relevant Total Sum , No. Payments and Date of 1st Payment . 
 

 4 .  Enter the Payment by Credit Card/Coupon sub-level (of the Over-the-Counter Invoices form) to view the details of the new promissory note(s).
 Tip: To print any promissory notes recorded for the invoice, return to the upper-level form and select Print Promissory Notes from the list of Direct Activations. 
 
## To record payments by credit card:

 

 ·        Enter the Payment by Credit Card/Coupon sub-level form and record the details of each credit card payment. 
 
## Note:

 

Each payment received updates the Payment Balance column in the upper-level form.
