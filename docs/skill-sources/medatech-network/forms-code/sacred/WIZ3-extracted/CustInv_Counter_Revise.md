# Revising a Final Over-the-Counter Invoice

 

Only certain columns can be revised in a final invoice (e.g., Payment Date , Status , Details ). In addition, you can record new remarks and update existing ones. Finally, you can revise the details of any received checks or credit card slips. 
 
## To revise a final invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Invoice Revisions form and retrieve the desired invoice.
 Tip: For best results, use the Invoice column together with the Invoice Type ( OVR ). 
 

 2 .  Make the necessary revisions in the updateable columns of this form and its sub-level, Invoice Items .
 Note: You can only change the invoice status to another status defined as final. 
 

 3 .  To record/update remarks for the invoice, use the parallel Invoice/Memo - Remarks sub-level form. 
 
## To revise check details:

 

 1 .  Enter the Revision of Checks form and retrieve the desired check. 
 Tip: For best results, use the Document Number column (for the invoice number) together with the Document Code ( OVR ). 
 

 2 .  Make the necessary revisions. 
 
## To revise credit card payment details:

 

 1 .  Enter the Revision of Credit Card Slips form and retrieve the desired voucher. 
 Tip: For best results, use the Document Number column (for the invoice number) together with the Document Code ( OVR ). 
 

 2 .  Make the necessary revisions.
