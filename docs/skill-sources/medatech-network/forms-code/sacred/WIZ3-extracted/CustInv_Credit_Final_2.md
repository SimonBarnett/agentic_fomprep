# Before you begin:

 

 1 .  Enter the Multi-Shipment Invoices form and retrieve the desired memo. 
 

 2 .  Click the References tab and check the memo's Status . You will only be able to finalize the memo if its current status allows for finalization (e.g., it is assigned the Approved status).
 Note: You must also be authorized to change the status of credit memos (and multi-shipment invoices) to Final . 
 
## To finalize the memo:

 

 ·        Select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To finalize and print the memo:

 

 ·        Select Finalize & Print Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To finalize a batch of memos:

 

 ·        Run the Finalize Invoices program. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·              If any of the memos were not finalized by the program, run the Finalize Invoices - Errors report to find out why. 
 

 ·              Click to return to the Credit Memos menu .
