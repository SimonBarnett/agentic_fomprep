# Deleting a Pending Export Credit Memo

 

Pending memos are credit memos that have not yet been finalized and can be deleted from the system. 
 
## To delete a pending credit memo:

 

 1 .  Enter the Export Invoices form and retrieve the desired memo. 
 

 2 .  Select Delete Pending Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations .
