# Before you begin:

 

 1 .  Enter the Export Invoices form and retrieve the desired memo. 
 

 2 .  Click the References tab and check the memo's Status . You will only be able to finalize the memo if its current status allows for finalization (e.g., it is assigned the Approved status).
 Note: You must also be authorized to change the status of export credit memos (and export invoices) to Final . 
 
## To finalize the memo:

 

 ·        Select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To finalize and print the memo:

 

 ·        Select Finalize & Print Invoice from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Note:

 

Click to return to the Credit Memos menu .
