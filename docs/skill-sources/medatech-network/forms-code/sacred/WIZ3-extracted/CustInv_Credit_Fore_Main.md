# Preparing Credit Memos for Foreign Customers (Export Credit Memos)

 

Credit memos for foreign customers are recorded in an Export Invoices form designated for credit. When goods are returned, the credit memo must be accompanied by a return document recorded in the Customer Returns form. 
 

After recording the credit memo, it should be finalized and printed. Finalizing a memo creates a journal entry. If necessary, you can perform certain revisions in final memos or cancel them. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Prepare a credit memo for overcharging 
 

 Prepare a credit memo for returned goods
