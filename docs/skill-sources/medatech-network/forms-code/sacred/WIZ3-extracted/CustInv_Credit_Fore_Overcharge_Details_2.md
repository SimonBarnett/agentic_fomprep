# To reconcile the credit memo with the original invoice:

 

 1 .  In the Code-Orig. Inv. column of the Invoice - Add'l Details form, specify the type of invoice for which credit is due. 
 

 2 .  Specify the number of the Original Invoice in which the returned goods were billed.
 Notes: 
 

 ·              If you are crediting the customer for the entire amount of the original invoice, the invoice and this memo will be reconciled automatically when the memo is finalized. 
 

 ·              If you are crediting the customer for part of the amount of the original invoice, the invoice, the memo and the receipt of payment will all be reconciled together.
