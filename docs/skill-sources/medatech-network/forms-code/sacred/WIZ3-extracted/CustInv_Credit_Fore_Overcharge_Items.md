# To itemize the credit memo:

 

 1 .  Enter the Invoice Items sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Record the Part Number of the credited item. 
 

 ·              Specify the Part Number of a non-inventory part (e.g., the predefined “000” part) and change its Part Description to “Overcharge.” 
 

 Note: Click to define a new general-purpose non-inventory part . 
 

 3 .  Record a Quantity of 1 . 
 

 4 .  Record the full amount of credit due in the Unit Price column. 
 

 5 .  Flag the Credit Only column. 
 

 6 .  If relevant, specify a Budget Item . 
 

 7 .  Click to link the line item to a project or project activity . 
 

 8 .  Check the Income Account . Revise it if necessary. Explanation.
