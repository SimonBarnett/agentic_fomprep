# To print an export credit memo:

 

 1 .  Enter the Export Invoices form and retrieve the desired memo. 
 

 2 .  Select Print Export Invoice from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To simultaneously finalize and print the memo, select Finalize & Print Invoice from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 ·        Click to set up sending credit memos as e-documents. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the Credit Memos menu .
