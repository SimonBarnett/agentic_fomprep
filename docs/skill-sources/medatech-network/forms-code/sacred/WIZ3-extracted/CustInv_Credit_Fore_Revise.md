# Revising a Final Export Credit Memo

 

Only certain columns can be revised in a final memo (e.g., Status , Details , Credit Only ). In addition, you can record new remarks and update existing ones. 
 
## To revise a final credit memo:

 

 1 .  Enter the Invoice Revisions form and retrieve the desired memo.
 Tip: For best results, use the Invoice column together with the Invoice Type ( CEX ). 
 

 2 .  Make the necessary revisions in the updateable columns of this form and its sub-level, Invoice Items .
 Note: You can only change the memo status to another status defined as final. 
 

 3 .  To record/update remarks for the memo, use the parallel Invoice/Memo - Remarks sub-level form.
