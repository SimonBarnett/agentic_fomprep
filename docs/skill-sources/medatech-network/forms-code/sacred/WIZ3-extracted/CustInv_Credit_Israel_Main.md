# Preparing Credit Memos for Local Customers

 

Credit memos are usually recorded in a Multi-Shipment Invoices form designated for credit. When goods are returned, the credit memo must be accompanied by a return document recorded in the Customer Returns form. 
 

After recording the credit memo, it should be finalized and printed. Finalizing a memo creates a journal entry. If necessary, you can perform certain revisions in final memos or cancel them. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        A different form is used to credit returns originally recorded in the Sales Invoices form. Click to prepare a credit memo in that form. 
 

 ·        Yet another form is used for foreign customers. Click to prepare credit memos for foreign customers .
