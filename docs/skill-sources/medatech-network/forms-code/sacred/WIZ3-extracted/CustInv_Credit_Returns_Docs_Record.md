# To record a credit memo for return documents:

 

 1 .  Enter the Multi-Shipment Invoices form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 2 .  Specify the number of the customer to be credited. 
 Notes: 
 

 ·              Click if the customer in the original document(s) has a billing customer . 
 

 ·              In the case of a walk-in customer, record the walk-in customer number. Then revise the name in the Cust. Name column, and fill in the address and other details in the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 ·              A temporary memo number (prefixed with a T ) is filled in automatically when you leave the field. It will be replaced by a permanent number when the memo is finalized. 
 

 3 .  Check the Contact column to make sure that the relevant contact appears. Change it if necessary.
 Tip: To define new contacts for the customer or to assign a default contact for invoices (which also affects credit memos), use the Customer Contacts form, a sub-level of the Customers form. For more information, see the Open Customers Wizard . 
 

 4 .  Today's date is filled in automatically as the memo date. Change it if necessary.
 Note: The memo date determines the reference date in the journal entry for the credit memo. 
 

 5 .  In the Debit/Credit column, change the D to a C .
