# To reconcile the credit with the original invoice:

 

 1 .  In the Code-Orig. Inv. column of the Invoice - Add'l Details form, specify the type of invoice (INV) for which you are crediting the customer. 
 

 2 .  Specify the number of the Original Invoice in which the returned goods were billed.
 Notes: 
 

 ·              If you are crediting the customer for the entire amount of the original invoice, an automatic reconciliation is performed between the two invoices. 
 

 ·              If you are crediting the customer for part of the amount of the original invoice, an automatic reconciliation is performed between the current invoice, the original invoice, and the receipt of payment. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can now treat the credit memo just like any other sales invoice. 
 

 ·        Depending on how sales invoices are set up, you may be required to finalize the memo before you can leave the form. Explanation.
