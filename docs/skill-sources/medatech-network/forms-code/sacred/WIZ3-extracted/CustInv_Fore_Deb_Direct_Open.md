# Preparing an Export Invoice for a Specific Document

 
## Before you begin:

 

Make sure that the customer for which you are about to prepare an export invoice has been defined as a foreign customer. Otherwise, a multi-shipment invoice will be prepared instead. 
 
## Note:

 

Click to check how the customer has been defined . 
 
## To prepare the invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the appropriate form for the document you wish to bill: 
 

 Customer Shipments 
 

 Service Calls 
 

 Service Contracts 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired document. 
 

 3 .  Select Prepare Invoice/Credit Memo from the list of Direct Activations .
