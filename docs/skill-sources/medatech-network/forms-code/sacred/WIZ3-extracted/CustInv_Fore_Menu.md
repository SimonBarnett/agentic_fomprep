# Preparing Export Invoices

 

Export invoices are used to bill foreign customers . Like multi-shipment invoices, they are generally prepared on the basis of documents , automatically or manually, and can combine credit (for returns) with charges (for shipment). They can also be recorded for non-inventory parts (e.g., services). Likewise, they have no effect on inventory levels. 
 

After recording and checking the invoice, it should be finalized and printed. Finalizing an invoice creates a journal entry. If necessary, you can perform certain revisions in final invoices or cancel them.
