# To record additional details:

 

 1 .  Return to the Multi-Shipment Invoices upper-level form. 
 

 2 .  Click the Price tab. The Total Before Discount , Overall Discount , Sales Tax (for dual-currency users: VAT ) and Amount Owing appear automatically, according to the itemized invoice/memo. If necessary, revise the overall discount or the amount owing (or due, in a credit memo). 
 

 3 .   Dual-currency users: Click if the customer will be paying by bank transfe r or credit card . 
 

 4 .  Enter the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 5 .  The payment terms appear automatically. To revise them, do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Select a different Payment Terms Code . 
 

 ·              Define payment installments for this particular invoice in the parallel Payment Installments sub-level form. These installments will override the customer's default payment terms. 
 

 Tip: To change the default payment terms for future transactions with this customer, enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and click the Customer tab.
