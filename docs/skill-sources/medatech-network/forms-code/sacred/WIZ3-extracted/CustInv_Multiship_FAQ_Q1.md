# Errors During Automatic Billing

 

The reason for the error depends on the type of document you are billing: 
 

 ·        Customer Shipments 
 

Is the shipment flagged as Final and Billable ? 
 

 ·        Customer Returns 
 

Is the return flagged as Final , and is there a check mark next to Credit Customer ? 
 

 ·        Service Calls 
 

Does the service call have a final status (e.g., Closed ) and is it flagged as Billable ? 
 

 ·        Service Contracts 
 

Is the shipment flagged as Billable , and have payments been recorded in the Payment Installments sub-level form? 
 

 ·        Reports of Project Hrs/Expenses 
 

Is the report flagged as Billable ? 
 

 ·        Projects 
 

Have payments been defined for the project (see the form and its sub-level Payments for Project )? 
 
## Note:

 

None of the documents mentioned above will generate an invoice for any line items flagged for Checking .
