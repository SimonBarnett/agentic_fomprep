# Preparing Multi-Shipment Invoices

 

Multi-shipment invoices are generally prepared on the basis of documents , automatically or manually. They can combine credit (for returns) with charges (for shipment). They can also be recorded for non-inventory parts (e.g., services). Unlike other types of invoices, they have no effect on inventory levels. 
 

After recording and checking the invoice, it should be finalized and printed. Finalizing an invoice creates a journal entry. If necessary, you can perform certain revisions in final invoices or cancel them. 
 
## Note:

 

If you work on a cash basis, you should prepare multi-shipment invoices on the basis of receipts. For detailed instructions, run the Cash-Based Accounting Wizard .
