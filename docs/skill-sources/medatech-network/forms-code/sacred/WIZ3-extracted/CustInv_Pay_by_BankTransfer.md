# Customer Invoices - Payment by Bank Transfer or Credit Card

 

If the customer will be paying by bank transfer or credit card, you can record the relevant details in the invoice. 
 
## To do so:

 

 1 .  Enter the Credit Card to Charge sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Fill in all relevant columns. If this is a foreign customer located in another European country, specify the customer's IBAN in the Bank Account column and the BIC in the BIC - Bank ID Code column.
