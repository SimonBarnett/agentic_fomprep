# Canceling a Final Sales Invoice

 

If errors are discovered in a final invoice, you can create a new invoice with negative values, which cancels the original one and creates a reversing journal entry. 
 
## To cancel a final invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Invoices form and retrieve the desired invoice. 
 

 2 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Select Cancel Document (Original Date) from the list of Direct Activations to create a cancellation invoice and reversing journal entry dated to the original invoice date. 
 

 ·              Select Cancel Document For Date from the list of Direct Activations to create a cancellation invoice and reversing journal entry for a designated date. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        In order to cancel a final invoice whose journal entry is still pending, you must post the entry before running the cancellation program. 
 

 ·        You must be authorized to change the status of sales invoices to Canceled . 
 
## To view cancellation results:

 

 ·        Every canceled invoice and the invoice that canceled it are flagged in the Canceled column of the Price tab. Both invoices will be for the same amount, with different signs (positive amounts in the original invoice and negative amounts in the cancellation invoice).
