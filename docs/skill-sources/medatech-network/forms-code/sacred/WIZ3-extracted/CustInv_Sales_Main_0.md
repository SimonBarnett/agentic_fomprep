# Preparing Sales Invoices

 

A sales invoice both records the removal of goods from inventory and bills the customer for those goods. 
 

You have several options for recording a sales invoice and linking it to the orders it fills (and thereby reducing the order balance): 
 

 ·        If each shipment fills one or more specific sales orders, you can have the system itemize the invoice automatically with all ordered parts. All you then need to do is approve (or revise) quantities. 
 

 ·        If each shipment partially fills a specific sales order, and you want to determine which of the ordered parts are being shipped and billed, you can designate the order but record the shipped parts manually. The system will fill in the proper order line number for each recorded item. 
 

 ·        If you are unaware of which orders are being filled by each shipment, you can record the invoice items manually and have the system fill in the proper order number and line number. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        Bills of lading are always itemized automatically, according to the packing crates they include. 
 

 ·        You can also record a sales invoice manually, without linking it to any order. 
 

 ·        Sales invoices can also be created automatically, when using the Delivery Scheduling module. For details, run the Delivery Scheduling Wizard . 
 

 ·        If the Negative Sum Counter option is flagged in the Financial Documents form, a sales invoice whose total amount is negative is numbered from the credit memo numeration template and prints out as a credit memo, with positive amounts. 
 

 ·        After recording and checking the invoice, it should be finalized and printed. Finalizing an invoice creates a journal entry. If necessary, you can perform certain revisions in final invoices or cancel them.
