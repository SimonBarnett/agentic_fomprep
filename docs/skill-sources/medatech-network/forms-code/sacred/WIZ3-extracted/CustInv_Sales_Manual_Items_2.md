 7 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If you are shipping packed goods, designate the Crate Type Code and the number of crates. The shipped quantity will be calculated automatically, based on crate capacity. 
 

 ·              Otherwise, specify the shipped quantity manually in the Quantity column. 
 

 Note : If you are allowed to record partially packed crates (i.e., the IPartialPack constant is set to 1 in the Logistic Constants form), you can designate the number and type of packing crates and then revise the shipped quantity accordingly. Once you do, however, any revision to the number or type of packing crates will have no effect on the shipped quantity. 
 

 8 .  Make sure that prices and discounts are correct.
 Note: If you set the price list for this invoice, prices will be taken from there but may be revised. 
 

 9 .  If you work with budgets, specify a Budget Item . 
 Tip: To view information on usage of this budget item, enter the Budgeted vs. Actual sub-level form. 
 

 10 .  Click to link this line item to a project or project activity . 
 

 11 .  Check the Income Account . Revise it if necessary. Explanation. 
 

 12 .  The sales rep and the commission percentage due for the line item appear automatically. Revise them as necessary. 
 

 13 .  Enter the Invoice Item - Remarks sub-level form and check the remarks that appear there. Revise or add to them, as desired.
 Notes: 
 

 ·              Any part remarks recorded in the part catalogue appear in this form automatically if Copy Part Remarks has been flagged for sales invoices (in the Copy Part Remarks to Document form). 
 

 ·              The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable). 
 

 ·              To include these remarks in the printout of the invoice, select a suitable print format . 
 

 14 .  To record serial numbers for this part, move to the parallel Serial Numbers in Transaction or Auto Recording of Serial Nos. sub-level form.
 Tip: For more information on working with serialized parts, run the Serial Numbers Wizard .
