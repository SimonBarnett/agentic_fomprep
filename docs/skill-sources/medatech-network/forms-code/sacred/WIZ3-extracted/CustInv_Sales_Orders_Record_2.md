 6 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              To base the invoice on one sales order, click the References tab and specify either the internal order number or the customer's purchase order number. Details are filled in automatically from the order, but may be revised. 
 

 ·              To base the invoice on multiple sales orders, leave the Order column blank, and enter the Orders for Invoice sub-level form. Record a line for each order billed by the current invoice. Return to the upper-level form and record a brief remark in the Details column, if desired. 
 

 ·              To select individual order items to be shipped and billed, enter the Choose Order Items sub-level form and flag the Choose Item column for each relevant item. 
 

 7 .  If the invoice has already been recorded externally by a sales rep, specify the document number in the Sales Rep Inv. No. column (in the References tab). Because the external document has already been issued, the Printed column in the Price tab is flagged. 
 

 8 .  Check the Sales Rep No. column to make sure that the correct sales rep appears. Change if necessary.
 Note: If the invoice is for several orders which have been made through more than one sales rep, designate the main one here. When itemizing the invoice, indicate the sales rep for each line item.
