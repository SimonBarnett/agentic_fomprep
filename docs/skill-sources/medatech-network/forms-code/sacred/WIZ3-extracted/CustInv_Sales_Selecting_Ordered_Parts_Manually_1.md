# Selecting Ordered Parts Manually

 

Before you begin, make sure the SAutoFindOrd constant is set to 2 or 4 (in the Logistic Constants form). 
 
## To base a sales invoice on an order: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Invoices form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 2 .  Specify the number of the customer to which the invoice is sent. 
 Notes: 
 

 ·              Click if the customer who placed the order has a billing customer . 
 

 ·              In the case of a walk-in customer, record the walk-in customer number. Then revise the name in the Cust. Name column, and fill in the address and other details in the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 ·              A temporary Invoice No. (prefixed with a T ) is filled in automatically when you leave the field. It will be replaced by a permanent number when the invoice is finalized.
