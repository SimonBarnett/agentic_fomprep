# To itemize the invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Invoice Items sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the Part Number of the item being shipped and billed. 
 

 3 .  Make sure that the Part Description is suitable.
 Tip: You can change the description of any part that is flagged in the Change Desc. column of the Part Catalogue form. 
 

 4 .  The Order and Line are filled in automatically. To revise them, first delete the line number. 
 

 5 .  Where applicable, specify the Work Order to which the part belongs.
 Tip: To check available inventory, use the Inventory for Part and the Available Inventory in Warehouse sub-level forms. 
 

 6 .  Make sure the Status , Warehouse and Bin are correct.
 Note : The balance of this part for the designated Work Order , Status , Warehouse and Bin is displayed in the Warehouse Balance column.
