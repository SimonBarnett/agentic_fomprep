# Checking for Credit Card Authorization

 

You can determine whether users will be able to finalize an over-the-counter invoice before a confirmation number is recorded for the corresponding credit card payment. 
 
## To determine whether to check for credit card authorization:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Constants form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the CreditCardOK constant and choose the desired value. 
 
## Note:

 

The constant also affects receipts that are paid via credit card.
