# Defining a Billing Customer

 

For each customer, you can define a billing customer, whose account is debited when an invoice is finalized (and credited when a credit memo is finalized). Once a billing customer is defined, you can record the order for the regular customer and the invoice for the billing customer. 
 
## To define the billing customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form, and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  Click the Customer tab and specify the billing customer in the Billing Cust. No. column. 
 

 Note : 
 

You can change the billing customer to a third party for specific orders or order items. This third-party billing customer will then appear in any multi-shipment invoice that bills these ordered parts.
