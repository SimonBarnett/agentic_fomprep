# Recording Invoices and Credit Memos for the Billing Customer

 

Invoices and credit memos that are prepared automatically (whether in a batch or by direct activation) can be opened for the customer recorded in the original sales document or for that customer's billing customer. 
 

In special cases, you can assign a billing customer to a billing customer. This is necessary when billing shipments to various branches in a division within a chain, where the invoice is sent to division headquarters but the chain's account is debited. In such a scenario, you would address the invoice to the division (defined as the billing customer for all its branches) but debit the chain's account (defined as the billing customer of each division). 
 
## To open invoices/memos for the billing customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the regular customer (the one who placed the order). 
 

 2 .  Make sure that a Billing Customer has been defined in the Customer tab. 
 

 3 .  Click the Invoices tab and flag the Inv. to Billing Cust column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        If a third-party billing customer has been recorded for a particular order or order item, it will override the customer's default billing customer. If several different billing customers need to be charged, a separate invoice or credit memo will be opened for each one. 
 

 ·        Click to assign a billing customer to a billing customer.
