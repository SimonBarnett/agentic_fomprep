# Defining Bank Accounts for Payments of Export Invoices

 

Export invoice printouts include the bank account to which the customer is requested to transfer payment. You can define a default account that will appear automatically in every export invoice. You can then link individual customers to separate payment accounts, which will override the default in export invoices opened for those customers. 
 
## To define a default payment account for export invoices:

 

 1 .  Enter the Bank Accounts form, and move to the desired account. 
 

 2 .  Flag the Payments From Abroad column. 
 
## To define a payment account for a specific foreign customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form, and retrieve the customer. 
 

 2 .  Click the Customer tab and specify the desired account in the Pay to Bank Acct column.
