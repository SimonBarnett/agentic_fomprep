# Setting Up IBAN and BIC

 

If the customer is located in another European country, your IBAN and BIC numbers can appear on the printed invoice. You can also store the customer's IBAN and BIC codes. This is necessary when payment will be received by bank transfer. 
 
## To set up your own IBAN and BIC:

 

 1 .  Enter the Bank Accounts form and move to the bank account into which payment is to be received.
 Tip: Copy the Bank Account Code for use in step 4 . 
 

 2 .  Record your codes in the BIC - Bank ID Code and IBAN - Account No. columns. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve all customers. 
 

 4 .  Click the Customer tab and, for each customer, record the code of your bank account in the Pay to Bank Account column. Invoices to local customers will display your regular bank account number; invoices to foreign customers will display IBAN and BIC. 
 
## To record your customers' IBAN and BIC:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts Receivable form and retrieve the A/R accounts in question. 
 

 2 .  For each account, enter the Bank Account Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Record the IBAN in the Bank Account column and the Bank Identifier Code in the BIC - Bank ID Code column. 
 

 4 .  Use the RIB Control Key column to check the validity of these numbers.
