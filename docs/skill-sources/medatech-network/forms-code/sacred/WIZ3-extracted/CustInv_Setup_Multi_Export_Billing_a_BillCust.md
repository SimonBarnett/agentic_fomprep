# Assigning a Billing Customer to a Billing Customer

 

You assign a billing customer to a billing customer when billing shipments to various branches in a division within a chain, where the invoice is sent to division headquarters but the chain's account is debited. 
 
## To set this up:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve all customers that are branches in the various divisions of the chain. 
 

 2 .  For each of those branches, record the customer representing the division as the Billing Customer (in the Customer tab). 
 

 3 .  For each of those branches, click the Invoices tab and flag the Inv. to Billing Cust column. 
 

 4 .  In the same form, retrieve the customers representing the divisions in the chain. 
 

 5 .  For each of those divisions, record the customer for the chain as their Billing Customer . 
 

 6 .  For each of those divisions, make sure that the Inv. to Billing Cust column is not flagged.
