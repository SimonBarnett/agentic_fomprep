# Revising an Existing Shipping Document

 

While you are free to revise or cancel pending shipping documents, final documents cannot be revised or canceled, with the exception of certain details (e.g., part prices, Checking flag, billing status). However, as long as the document has not been billed, you can reopen it and make any needed revisions. Bills of lading may also be reopened, in order to revise the packing slip(s) on which they are based. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Change the details of a final shipping document 
 

 Reopen a final shipping document (to allow its revision or cancellation) 
 

 Reopen a final bill of lading to revise the packing slip for one of its crates 
 

 Revise or cancel a pending shipping document 
 

 Cancel out a final customer shipment with a return document
