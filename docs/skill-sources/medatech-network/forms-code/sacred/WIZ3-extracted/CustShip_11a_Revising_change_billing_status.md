# Revising a Final Shipping Document

 

After a shipping document is final, you can still change certain details, such as its status; the assigned user; the billing status (i.e., whether or not the shipment will be billed); the price, discount and billing status of individual items; and (for dual-currency users) the exchange rate – all of which will affect the shipment's total price. In addition, you can flag a shipped item for checking, to prevent its accidental billing, or delete an existing checking flag. 
 
## To revise a final document, do one of the following:

 

 ·        Use the Revision of Final Shipping Doc. form and its sub-level, Revision of Details , to revise a specific shipping document. 
 

 ·        Use the Revision of Final Ship Doc Items form to retrieve and update specific line items in a variety of shipping documents. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can also change the status of a final shipping document (to another final status) or the name of the user responsible for handling it, directly in the Customer Shipments form. 
 

 ·        If you use the Delivery Scheduling module, you can also revise the Truck No. and Shipper/Driver by running the Revise Truck No./Driver in Docs program.
