# Revising a Pending Shipping Document

 

As long as a shipping document is not final, it may be revised or canceled. 
 
## To revise a pending shipping document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Shipments form, and retrieve the shipping document in question. 
 

 2 .  Make all necessary changes. You can add, delete or update items in the document. 
 

 3 .  Click to finalize and print the document . 
 
## To cancel a pending shipping document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Shipments form, and retrieve the shipping document in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Shipped Items sub-level form and check that all quantities are zero. 
 

 3 .  Return to the upper-level form, and change the status to Cancelled . 
 
## Note:

 

Click to return to the main screen .
