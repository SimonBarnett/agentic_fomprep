# Shipping Definitions

 

You can define various parameters pertaining to the shipping of goods, including shipment modes, shippers, shipping zones and the vehicles used for shipment. Once these are specified in a shipping document, shipment costs will be calculated accordingly and added to the total amount of the multi-shipment invoice in which the shipment is billed. 
 

You can also define default shipping parameters for each customer, which will be filled in automatically whenever a shipping document is recorded for this customer. 
 
## To define shipment modes:

 

 ·        Enter the Shipment Codes form, and designate the modes of shipping that are usually used in your company. 
 
## To designate shipping zones:

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipping Zones form. 
 

 2 .  For each shipping zone, designate the zone code and description.
 Tip: Use the States & Countries in Zone sub-level form to list the states (and countries) that are included in the shipping zone.
