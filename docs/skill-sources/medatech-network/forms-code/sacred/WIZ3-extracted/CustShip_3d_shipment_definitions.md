# To list vehicle types:

 

 1 .  Enter the Vehicle Types form. 
 

 2 .  For each vehicle type, designate a code and a brief description. 
 

 3 .  Specify the maximum weight that may be loaded unto vehicles of this type in the Weight column. 
 
## To list shippers and their rates:

 

 1 .  Enter the Drivers/Shippers form, and fill in the shipper/driver number and name.
 Note : The same form is used to list company drivers for the Delivery Scheduling module. 
 

 2 .  Designate the shipper's Phone Number , Fax Number and Complete Address . 
 

 3 .  If desired, designate the name and phone number of the contact that you usually deal with at the shipping company. 
 

 4 .  Specify the currency to which shipping costs (listed in the sub-level form) refer. 
 

 5 .  If desired, specify the Payment Terms agreed upon with this shipper. 
 

 6 .  Enter the Shipping Costs sub-level form. 
 

 7 .  For each vehicle type used by this shipper, designate the shipping zone in which it operates and the price it charges for that zone. When the license number of a vehicle of this type is specified in a shipping document, this determines the shipping rate for that document.
