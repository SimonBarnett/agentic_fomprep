# To create a customer shipping profile:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the customer in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Shipments tab. In the Shipment Mode column, choose the method of shipment generally used to send goods to this customer to serve as the default. Explanation . 
 

 3 .  Specify the Shipping Zone in which the customer is located. Together with the shipper details designated in the shipping document, this determines the shipping rate for that document. Explanation . 
 

 4 .  To restrict parts shipped to the customer to those listed in the Customer Parts sub-level form, click the Details tab and flag the Ship Cust Parts Only column. 
 

 5 .  If goods are regularly shipped to a given customer site, enter the Customer Sites sub-level form. For the site in question, flag the Main Address column. Then return to the upper-level form. 
 

 6 .  To assign a default contact for shipping, enter the Customer Contacts sub-level form. Next to the appropriate name, flag the Shipment Contact column.
