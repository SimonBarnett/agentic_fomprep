 5 .  Click the General tab. The warehouse from which the goods are being shipped is filled in automatically, but this may be revised. Designate a warehouse bin, if desired.
 Notes: 
 

 ·              If goods are taken from more than one warehouse (or bin), designate the main one here. When itemizing the document, indicate the warehouse for each part. 
 

 ·              Click to change the default warehouse for future shipping documents . 
 

 6 .  If the goods are to be delivered to a site other than the customer's main address, specify the shipping address in one of the following ways: 
 

 ·              Designate a customer site in the Site column of this form (in the References tab). OR 
 

 ·              Record the shipping address manually in the Shipping Address sub-level form. 
 

  Note: If goods are to be shipped to more than one site, designate the main one here. When itemizing the document, indicate the specific shipping address of each item. 
 

 7 .  Click the Shipment tab. In the Shipment Code column, choose the mode of shipment used to send the goods to the customer. This will appear on the document printout.
 Note: The customer's default shipment code appears automatically, but may be revised. Click to change the default shipping mode for this customer . This will not affect the current document. 
 

 8 .  To take into account shipping prices, fill in the License Number of the vehicle used to ship the goods. Explanation . 
 

 9 .  If desired, record any details regarding the shipment in the Details column (in the References tab).
