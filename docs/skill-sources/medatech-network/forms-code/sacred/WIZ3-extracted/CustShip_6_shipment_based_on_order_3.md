# To itemize the shipping document automatically:

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              To link the shipment to a single order , in the Customer Shipments form, click the References tab and specify either the internal order number or the customer's purchase order number. 
 

 ·              To link the shipment to several orders , enter the Sales Orders for Document sub-level form and specify the appropriate order numbers. 
 

 ·              To link the shipment to individual order items to be shipped and billed, enter the Choose Order Items sub-level form and flag the Choose Item column for each relevant item. 
 

 ·              Note: Click to record the return of goods from one or more orders. 
 

 2 .  If you have designated a single sales order, the following data are copied from the order: % Overall Discount ( Prices tab), Site , Sales Rep and Details ( References tab). Revise them, if necessary. 
 

 3 .  Click if the shipment is part of a project.
