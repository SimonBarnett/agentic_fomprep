# Recording the Return of Ordered Goods

 
## Before You Begin

 

If the order in question is already closed, it must first be reopened. To do so: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders form and retrieve the order. 
 

 2 .  Click the Follow-up tab and remove the check mark from the Closed column. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Order Items sub-level form and move to an item that has been returned. 
 

 4 .  Remove the check mark from the Closed column. 
 

 5 .  Do the same for any other order items that have been returned. 
 
## To record the return of goods from one or more orders:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders for Document sub-level form and choose the appropriate order numbers. 
 

 2 .  For each order from which goods are being returned, flag the Return column. 
 

 3 .  If you work with RMAs, return to the upper-level form ( References tab) and do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Select Assign New RMA Number from the list of Direct Activation s to open a new RMA for the returned goods. The assigned number appears in the RMA column. 
 

 ·              To include these goods in an existing RMA, press F6 in the RMA column to zoom to the RMA form and retrieve the desired RMA. Then press F8 to copy its number back into this column.
