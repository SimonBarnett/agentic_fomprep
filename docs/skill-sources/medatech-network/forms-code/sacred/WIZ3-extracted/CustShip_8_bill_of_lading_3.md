# To itemize the bill of lading:

 

 1 .  Enter the Link Packing Slips to Document sub-level form. This form lists all crates that are recorded in final packing slips, earmarked for this customer (or packed for stock), and located in the warehouse and bin specified in the upper-level form. 
 

 2 .  For each crate that is to be included in the bill of lading, flag the In Document column. Consequently, a check mark will appear in the Bill of Lading column of the upper-level form, and the packing crates will be listed in the Packing Slips in Document form (another sub-level). 
 

 3 .  Enter the Shipped Items form (another sub-level). The bill of lading is itemized with all parts in the designated crates. Prices and discounts appear automatically, but may be revised. 
 

 4 .  For each shipped item, approve the shipped quantities: 
 

 ·              If the SCratesQuant constant is set at 1 , the Approve column is flagged automatically, and the quantity is filled in automatically. 
 

 ·              Otherwise, flag the Approve column manually, and the quantity will appear automatically. 
 

 Tips: 
 

 ·              To change the value of the SCratesQuant constant, which will affect future bills of lading, enter the Logistic Constants form. 
 

 ·              For help in revising/canceling a final packing slip, run the Packing Slip Wizard . 
 

 5 .  If you work with projects, click to link any shipped item to a project or project activity. 
 

 6 .  Enter the Bill of Lading - Additional Data sub-level form. Here you can record additional information regarding the container or packing crates being shipped, including ship or flight details, due date, container and seal number. These details will appear on the printed bill of lading.
