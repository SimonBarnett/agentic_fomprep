# Finalizing and Billing a Shipping Document

 
## To finalize the document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Shipments form and retrieve the document in question. 
 

 2 .  The total price, overall discount, sales tax and final price are calculated automatically. Check their accuracy (in the Price tab).
 Note : In a document that is based on one or more sales orders (including a bill of lading where the packing slips are based on orders), the overall discount is copied from the order(s) and cannot be revised . 
 

 3 .  If, for some reason, you do not want to bill for some of the shipped items yet (you need to check some data), enter the Shipped Items sub-level form and flag the appropriate lines in the Checking column. This will prevent accidental billing of the item(s) in question. Remember to remove the check mark when the checking process is complete (using the Revision of Final Shipping Doc. form and its sub-level). 
 

 4 .  Change the status to Final .
