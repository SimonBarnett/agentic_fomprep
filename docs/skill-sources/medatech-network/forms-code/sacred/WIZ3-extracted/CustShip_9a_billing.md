# Billing the Customer

 

You can bill a customer shipment in a multi-shipment invoice, together with other shipments. Once an invoice is created for the transaction (and finalized), its number will appear in the Invoice column of the Shipped Items form. Alternatively (unless the shipment is flagged for delayed payment in the Delay Inv-Next Month column), you can bill the shipment immediately, by recording an invoice as soon as the shipping document is finalized. 
 
## To bill the shipment immediately:

 

 1 .  Select Prepare Invoice/Credit Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 2 .  An invoice is created for this shipment. Select Multi-Shipment Invoices from the list of Direct Activations to view its details.
