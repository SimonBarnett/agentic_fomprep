# Assigning a Billing Customer

 

If you have several customers that are billed via a common account, designate the customer whose account is debited/credited for the group's transactions. For example, if you ship goods to several branches of a supermarket, each of which is a separate customer in Priority , but charge all orders to the head office, you would designate the head office as the billing customer for each branch. 
 
## To designate a billing customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the customer for which you want to designate a billing customer. 
 

 2 .  Specify the billing customer in the Billing Cust. No. column of the Customer tab.
 Note: The billing customer must already be defined as a regular customer with an A/R account. 
 

 3 .  If you want Priority to address invoices to the billing customer, click the Invoices tab and flag the Inv to Billing Cust column. 
 
## Note: 

 

You can also assign a billing customer to specific documents, such as customer shipments or service calls. This will overwrite any billing customer defined in the Financ. Parameters for Customers form.
