# Converting a Potential Customer

 

When a potential customer becomes a regular customer, you must change the customer number and open an A/R account. 
 
## To convert a potential customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the potential customer in question. 
 

 2 .  Select Convert Potential Cust to Cust from the list of Direct Activation s . Priority assigns the next available number, according to the pattern defined in the customer numeration template. 
 

 3 .  If this customer works with a specific company branch, click the Details tab and specify the branch. All documents opened for this customer (e.g., sales orders) will automatically be recorded for this branch and numbered according to its numeration templates.
 Note: If you work with subsidiaries, the customer's A/R account is automatically assigned to the subsidiary to which the designated branch is linked. For more information, run the Subsidiaries Wizard . 
 

 4 .  Click to assign this customer a representative customer . 
 

 5 .  Click the Address/ Phone tab and fill in the customer's contact information. 
 

 6 .  Click the Payments tab and select the Payment Terms agreed upon with the customer.
 Tip: To define additional payment terms, run the Set Up Payment Terms Wizard . 
 

 7 .  Fill in any other pertinent information, such as Sales Rep Number ( Details tab), Type of Business ( Company Data tab), Shipment Mode ( Shipments tab). 
 

 8 .  In the Customer Sites sub-level form, record any additional addresses where shipments can be made to this customer. Make sure to flag one of the addresses as the Main Address . This will serve as the default shipping address in every sales document opened for this customer. 
 
## Note to dual-currency users:

 

If you are working in a language other than English and want to produce documents for this customer in English: 
 

 ·      Specify the customer's English name in the Name (Lang 2) column. 
 

 ·      Click the Details tab and flag the Printouts in Lang 2 column. 
 

 ·      Enter the Additional Customer Data sub-level form and record the customer's address in English.
