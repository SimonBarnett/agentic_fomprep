 8 .   Dual currency users : If you are working in a language other than English and want to produce documents for this customer in English: 
 

 ·      Specify the customer's English name in the Name (Lang 2) column. 
 

 ·      Click the Details tab and flag the Printouts in Lang 2 column. 
 

 ·      Enter the Additional Customer Data sub-level form and record the customer's address in English. 
 

 9 .  Enter the Customer Contacts sub-level form and check the names, positions and phone numbers of the key people who work at the customer/vendor. If there are specific contacts for specific types of documents (e.g., sales orders, receipts), flag them in the appropriate column. Their names will appear automatically in the corresponding documents.
 Note: It is recommended that you record the last name first (e.g., Smith, John), as Priority records these names in the Contacts form. 
 

 10 .  In the Customer Sites sub-level form, record any additional addresses where shipments can be made to this customer. Make sure to flag one of the addresses as the Main Address . This will serve as the default shipping address in every sales document opened for this customer.
