# Opening a Customer from an Existing Vendor

 

It may happen that one of your vendors wants to become a customer as well. Priority allows you to create a single ledger account for this vendor/customer, which is assigned to the Receivable & Payable balance sheet item. 
 
## Note to dual-currency users:

 

When preparing a payment to a vendor/customer, you can subtract from the payment any amounts that the vendor owes you as a customer. However, if taxes are withheld in payments from this vendor, you need to flag the Vendor ' s Cust Debt column for all such amounts in the Payment Details sub-level form, to prevent them from being taken into account when withholding tax is calculated. 
 
## To open a customer that is also a vendor:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form. 
 

 2 .  In the Number column, record the vendor's number. The vendor's name and any contacts recorded for the vendor are automatically copied to the customer record. 
 

 3 .  If this customer works with a specific company branch, click the Details tab and designate the branch. All documents opened for this customer (e.g., sales orders) will automatically be recorded for this branch and numbered according to its numeration templates.
 Note: If you work with subsidiaries, the customer's A/R account is automatically assigned to the subsidiary to which the designated branch is linked. For more information, run the Subsidiaries Wizard . 
 

 4 .  Click to assign this customer a representative customer .
