# Opening a Customer

 

If you do not work with the CRM module, or do not wish to record a potential customer, you can open a customer record directly. For example, if you are beginning to work in Priority and would like to retain your old customer numbers, you can open customer records and number them manually, and matching A/R accounts will be opened automatically. 
 

Once you are working regularly in the system, you bypass potential customers by opening an A/R account by name; the system assigns the account number based on the pattern defined in the customer numeration template, then automatically opens a matching customer record. 
 

In both cases, customers assigned a permanent customer number automatically receive the status defined for regular customers, which allows them to open shipping documents, invoices and service calls. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Open a new A/R account and customer record with automatic numeration 
 

 Open new customer records with account numbers from the previous system 
 
## Note:

 

Click to open a customer record and A/R account with different numbers. This option is not available if you work with subsidiaries.
