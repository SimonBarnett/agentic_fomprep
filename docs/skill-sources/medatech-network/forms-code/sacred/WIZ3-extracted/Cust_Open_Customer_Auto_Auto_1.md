# To open an A/R account and customer record (automatic numeration):

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts Receivable form. 
 

 2 .  Record the customer's name in the Account Description column. (Do not fill in the Account No. column!) When you leave the column, Priority automatically assigns a number to the account, based on the pattern defined in the customer numeration template. An identical number and name is automatically assigned to the customer in the Customers form. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the new customer. 
 

 4 .  If the customer uses a currency other than the local one, record it in the Currency column. 
 

 5 .  In the Customer tab, select the Payment Terms agreed upon with the customer.
 Note : You can define additional terms. For more information, run the Set Up Payment Terms Wizard . 
 

 6 .  Choose the appropriate Tax Code ( VAT Code in the dual-currency package) for this customer. 
 

 7 .  In the Billing Follow-up By column, indicate the username of the employee responsible for tracking payments due by this customer. 
 

 8 .  Click to assign this customer a billing customer .
