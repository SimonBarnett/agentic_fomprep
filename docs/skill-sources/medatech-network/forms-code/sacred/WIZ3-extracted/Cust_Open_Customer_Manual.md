# To open a customer record and A/R account with different numbers:

 

 1 .  Open the Financial Constants form and make sure the IdentCustAcc constant is set to 0 . 
 

 2 .  Enter the Customers form and record a Number and Name for the customer. Fill in all the customer's details.
 Note: Do not begin the customer number with the letter T , which is reserved for potential customers. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts Receivable form and record an Account No. and Account Description for the customer. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the new customer. 
 

 5 .  In the Account No. column, specify the A/R account you opened for the customer. Fill in any other financial parameters.
