# To open a customer record and A/R account (manual numeration):

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form. 
 

 2 .  Record a Number and Name for the customer. When you leave the form, an A/R account with the same number will be opened automatically.
 Notes: 
 

 ·      Do not begin the customer number with the letter T , which is reserved for potential customers. 
 

 ·      At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the customer to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 3 .  If this customer works with a specific company branch, click the Details tab and designate the branch. All documents opened for this customer (e.g., sales orders) will automatically be recorded for this branch and numbered according to its numeration templates.
 Note: If you work with subsidiaries, the customer's A/R account is automatically assigned to the subsidiary to which the designated branch is linked. For more information, run the Subsidiaries Wizard . 
 

 4 .  Click to assign this customer a representative customer . 
 

 5 .  Click the Address / Phone tab and fill in the customer's contact information. 
 

 6 .  Click the Payments tab and select the Payment Terms agreed upon with the customer.
 Tip: To define additional payment terms, run the Set Up Payment Terms Wizard . 
 

 7 .  Fill in any other pertinent information, such as Sales Rep Number ( Details tab), Type of Business ( Company Data tab), Shipment Mode ( Shipments tab). 
 

 8 .   Dual-currency users: If you are working in a language other than English and want to produce documents for this customer in English: 
 

 ·      Specify the customer's English name in the Name (Lang 2) column. 
 

 ·      Click the Details tab and flag the Printouts in Lang 2 column. 
 

 ·      Enter the Additional Customer Data sub-level form and record the customer's address in English. 
 

 9 .  Enter the Customer Contacts sub-level form and record the names, positions and phone numbers of the key people who work at the customer. If there are specific contacts for specific types of documents (e.g., sales orders, receipts), flag them in the appropriate column. Their names will appear automatically in the corresponding documents.
 Note: It is recommended that you record the last name first (e.g., Smith, John), as Priority records these names in the Contacts form. 
 

 10 .  In the Customer Sites sub-level form, record any additional addresses where shipments can be made to this customer. Make sure to flag one of the addresses as the Main Address . This will serve as the default shipping address in every sales document opened for this customer.
