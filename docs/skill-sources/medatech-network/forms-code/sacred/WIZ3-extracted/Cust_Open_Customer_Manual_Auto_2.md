# To record the customer's financial parameters:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the new customer. 
 

 2 .  If the customer uses a currency other than the local one, record it in the Currency column. 
 

 3 .  Choose the appropriate Tax Code ( VAT Code in the dual-currency package) for this customer. 
 

 4 .  In the Billing Follow-up By column, indicate the username of the employee responsible for tracking payments due by this customer. 
 

 5 .  Click to assign this customer a billing customer .
