# Opening a Potential Customer

 

During the marketing stages of your relationship, you can open a record for a potential customer without assigning an A/R account. Most new customers automatically receive the Potential Customer status, which you can then change to Active status (by means of a special program) when desired (e.g., upon completion of a sales order). 
 

You can use Priority 's marketing tools (e.g., the Tasks form) to handle all communication with potential customers; you can also conduct an initial survey, open price quotes and sales orders and draft service contracts. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        Some properties of the Potential Status cannot be changed; for example, you cannot open accounts, ship goods or record financial information for potential customers. On the other hand, permitted activities such as opening quotes or orders can be restricted, if your work flow requires it. All changes to customer statuses must be made by running the BPM flow chart, and can be viewed in the Statuses for Customers form. 
 

 ·        You can define financial parameters for a potential customer (e.g., payment terms, tax code), that will serve as defaults in price quotations and sales orders. 
 

 ·        Once a document has been recorded for a potential customer, you cannot delete the customer record. 
 

 ·        To convert a lead (i.e., a new contact) into a potential customer, run the CRM Wizard .
