# To record a potential customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form. 
 

 2 .  Fill in the Customer Name column without recording a customer number. When you leave the column, Priority automatically assigns a temporary number beginning with the letter T .
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the customer to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 3 .  Click the Address / Phone tab and fill in any other information you have for this customer (e.g., phone number, e-mail address). 
 

 4 .   Dual-currency users: If you are working in a language other than English and want to produce documents for this customer in English: 
 

 ·      Specify the customer's English name in the Name (Lang 2) column. 
 

 ·      Click the Details tab and flag the Printouts in Lang 2 column. 
 

 ·      Enter the Additional Customer Data sub-level form and record the customer's address in English. 
 

 5 .  Click the Payments tab and select the Payment Terms agreed upon with the customer.
 Tip: To define additional payment terms, run the Set Up Payment Terms Wizard . 
 

 6 .  In the Customer Contacts sub-level form, record the customer's contacts and flag relevant contact types. For example, if any of these individuals is the contact for price quotations, flag the Price Quote Contact column; this person's name will then appear on the printout of any price quote.
 Note: It is recommended that you record the last name first (e.g., Smith, John), as Priority records this name in the Contacts form. 
 

 7 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the new potential customer. 
 

 8 .  Specify the customer's Currency , if it differs from the local currency. 
 

 9 .  Select the appropriate Tax Code ( VAT Code in the dual-currency package) for this customer.  
 
## Note:

 

Click to return to the main screen.
