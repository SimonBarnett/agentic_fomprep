# Representative Customers

 

Designating a certain customer as the representative of a group of linked customers allows the latter to inherit a number of parameters defined for the representative, including price lists , sold parts , discounts , pricing terms of blanket orders or price quotations , special part prices and special bonuses . Nonetheless, any parameters defined manually for a given customer override any values inherited from the representative. 
 

The only condition for linking a group of customers to a representative is that they all share the same billing currency. 
 
## To link a customer to a representative:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the customer for which you want to designate a representative. 
 

 2 .  Click the Details tab and specify the representative's number in the Rep. Cust. No. column. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        The representative customer must already be defined as a regular customer with an A/R account. 
 

 ·        A General Representative is predefined in the system. Like any customer, you can assign price lists, discounts, special part prices and bonuses to the general representative. All of the above data are automatically linked to all customers in the system, according to the established hierarchy of prices and discounts, and taking into account the value of the constant that determines whether or not discounts are cumulative.
