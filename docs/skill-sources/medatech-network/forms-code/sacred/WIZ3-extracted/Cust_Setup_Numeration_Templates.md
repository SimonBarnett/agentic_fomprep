# Setting Up Numeration Templates

 

You can assign identifying numbers to your customers manually, or allow the system to do so automatically. If you choose the latter, you need to define numeration templates that reflect the way you want your customers to be numbered. 
 

Customer numbers are divided into three parts: a prefix, placeholders for the number itself and a suffix. There are default values for both the prefix and the number of placeholders, which you can change within certain limitations. 
 
## To define customer numeration templates:

 

 1 .  Enter the Cust & Vend Numeration Templates form. 
 

 2 .  On the Customers line, adjust the Prefix , Suffix or Place Holders as needed. Example .
 Note: The default customer prefix is C , but can be changed to any combination of up to five alphanumeric characters (as long as the first letter is not T) . The total number of characters may not exceed sixteen. 
 

 3 .  On the Potential Custs line, adjust the Suffix or Place Holders as needed.
 Note: The Prefix for potential customers is always T , and may not be modified.
