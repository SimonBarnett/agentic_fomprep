# Walk-in Customers

 

If your business is based on clientele who make purchases infrequently or on a one-time basis, you may want to add a walk-in customer record. You can open a general A/R account to be shared by customers that do not have their own accounts. This prevents you from having to open a new account every time you make a one-time sale. 
 
## To open a walk-in customer record:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form. 
 

 2 .  Record a general Number for walk-in customers (e.g., C000 ), and a general description (e.g., Walk-ins ) in the Name column. 
 

 3 .  Click the Details tab and flag the Change Name column. This allows you to fill in the names of the actual customers in orders and invoices. 
 

 4 .   Dual-currency users: If you are working in a language other than English and want to produce documents for this customer in English: 
 

 ·      Specify the customer's English name in the Name (Lang 2) column. 
 

 ·      Click the Details tab and flag the Printouts in Lang 2 column. 
 

 ·      Enter the Additional Customer Data sub-level form and record the customer's address in English.
