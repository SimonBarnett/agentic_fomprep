# Viewing a Customer's Credit While Recording a Document

 

You often need to view a customer's credit while recording a document (e.g., an order) or when checking the customer's project. 
 
## To view a customer's credit:

 

 1 .  Enter the desired form. Credit may be viewed for the following forms: 
 

 ·              Price Quotations for Customers 
 

 ·              Sales Orders 
 

 ·              Customer Shipments 
 

 ·              Sales Invoices 
 

 ·              Over-the-Counter Invoices 
 

 ·              Multi-Shipment Invoices 
 

 ·              Receipts 
 

 ·              Projects 
 

 Tip: To exclude an individual order (or order item) from credit calculations, remove the check mark from the Consider Credit column. To exclude an individual invoice from credit calculations, enter the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form and remove the check mark from the Consider Credit column. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired document, or record the customer number to record a new document. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Customer Credit sub-level form.
 Notes: 
 

 ·              If a customer is designated as the billing customer or the customer for credit for other customers, the credit amounts displayed for that customer represent the sum totals for all relevant customers. 
 

 ·              Dual-currency users: The customer's postdated checks are calculated automatically when the form is first entered, such that the sums displayed are always up-to-date. The Awaiting Bank Clearance column in the Postdated tab displays the total sum of checks that have come due but are awaiting validation. 
 

 4 .  To view all the transactions in a category of credit (or liability), enter the desired form (these are sub-level forms of the Customer Credit form). The following forms are available: 
 

 ·              Open Orders 
 

 ·              Unbilled Documents 
 

 ·              Unpaid Bills 
 

 ·              Credit per Company (for a customer of several companies in the same multi-company setting) 
 

 ·              for dual-currency users: Postdated Checks and Secure Postdated Checks .
