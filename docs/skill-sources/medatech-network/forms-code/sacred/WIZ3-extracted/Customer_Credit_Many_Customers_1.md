# Viewing the Credit of One or More Customers

 
## Note to dual-currency users: 

 

Click to prepare updated liability data before viewing . 
 
## To view customer credit:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Credit form and retrieve the desired customer(s).
 Notes : 
 

 ·              If a customer is designated as the billing customer or the customer for credit for other customers, the credit amounts displayed for that customer represent the sum totals for all relevant customers. A customer who has a billing customer or a customer for credit is not displayed. 
 

 ·              You can view the same information, as well as multi-company credit, by running the Customer Credit report. (Dual-currency users can also run the Customer Liability report.) 
 

 ·              You can also view customer credit via the Financ. Parameters for Customers form (use the sub-level form Customer Credit ). 
 

 2 .  To view all the customer's transactions in a category of credit (or liability), enter one of the following sub-level forms: 
 

 ·              Open Orders 
 

 ·              Unbilled Documents 
 

 ·              Unpaid Bills 
 

 ·              for dual-currency users: Postdated Checks .
