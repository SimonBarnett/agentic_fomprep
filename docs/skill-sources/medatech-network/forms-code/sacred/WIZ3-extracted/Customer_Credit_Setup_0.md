# Defining Warning and Error Messages in Sales Orders

 

You can define two financial parameters for customers that determine the extent to which a sales rep can approve a sales order from a customer that has exceeded the credit limit. The first parameter triggers a warning message, allowing the rep to approve the shipment of an order. The second triggers an error message that does not allow the order to be shipped. 
 
## To define the message parameters:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form. 
 

 2 .  Click the Credit tab. 
 

 3 .  In the Approved Payment Days column, specify the number of days that a customer invoice can remain unpaid past its due date before triggering a warning message. The message appears when a sales order from this customer receives a status that allows shipment. 
 

 4 .  In the Approved Credit Days column, specify the number of days that a customer invoice can remain unpaid past its due date before triggering an error message. The message appears when a sales order from this customer receives a status that allows shipment, preventing the change of status. 
 

 Note: 
 

Customer invoices are not considered to be paid until they are linked to a receipt of payment.
