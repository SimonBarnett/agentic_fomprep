# Defining a Customer for Credit

 

For each customer, you can define a customer for credit, whose credit calculations are determined by any relevant documents (e.g., invoices) recorded for all customers assigned the same customer for credit. Such customers are billed directly, but their credit is calculated together, based on credit definitions assigned to the shared customer for credit. 
 
## To assign a customer for credit to a given customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the "regular" customer. 
 

 2 .  Click the Credit tab and specify that customer's Customer for Credit . 
 
## Note:

 

To calculate credit for a batch of customers that are all billed together, assign them a billing customer instead.
