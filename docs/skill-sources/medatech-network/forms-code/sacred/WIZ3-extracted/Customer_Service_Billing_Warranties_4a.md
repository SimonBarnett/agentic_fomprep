# Assigning Part Families to Service Terms

 

You need to designate which part families are covered by each service terms. This can be done either in a batch (i.e., for many service terms at once), or manually. Batch assignment is generally more efficient. 
 
## Note: 

 

If needed, first define new part families in the Part Families form. 
 
## To assign part families to service terms in a batch:

 

 1 .  Run the Define Conditions for Serv Terms program. 
 

 2 .  If any service terms involve part families that are billable, you need to change some of the results of the program. Click to do so. 
 
## To assign part families to service terms manually:

 

 1 .  In the Service Terms form, move to the line for the service terms. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Conditions of Service Terms sub-level form and list the families covered by the service terms. 
 

 3 .  If a family is billable, flag the Billable column. If it is not billable, leave that column blank and select a billing exemption type.
