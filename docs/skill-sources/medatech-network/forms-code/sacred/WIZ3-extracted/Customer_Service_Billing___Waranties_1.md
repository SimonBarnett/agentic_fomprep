# Setting Up Billing and Warranties

 
## Billing Exemption Types 

 

When recording service calls, you use billing exemption types to indicate why a particular replacement part or work hour is not being charged. Billing exemption types are also used when defining service terms for service contracts and warranties (you will define service terms in the next screen). 
 

 Priority provides two predefined billing exemption types: Manufacturer Warranty and Servicing . You can work with these, or you can define as many additional types as you need. 
 
## To define additional billing exemption types:

 

 ·        Enter the Types of Billing Exemptions form. On a new line, record a description of the exemption. 
 
## Tip:

 

You can add more types of billing exemptions later, as you need them.
