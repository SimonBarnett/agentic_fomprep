# Service Contacts for Customers

 

You can designate a default service contact for each customer. This contact person will appear as the default in: 
 

 ·        Any newly opened service contract for the customer 
 

 ·        Any newly opened service call for the customer AND 
 

 ·        The Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. for any serialized part at the customer's site. 
 
## To designate a default service contact:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Contacts form (a sub-level of the Customers form) and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 2 .  In the line for the desired contact, check the Service Contact column (under the Type of Contact tab).
