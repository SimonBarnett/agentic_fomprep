# Work Hours

 

You can create as many work hour “parts” as you need (e.g., a senior technician service hour costing $75 and a junior technician service hour costing $50). After defining a work hour part, you can link it to an employee. This type of work hour serves as a default when billing customers for all work performed by that employee (e.g., service calls). 
 
## To create a work hour part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Catalogue form. 
 

 2 .  On a new line, fill in the Part Number and Description columns. 
 

 3 .  Click the Additional Details tab. In the Type column, select R . 
 

 4 .  Do not check the Inv. Control column. 
 

 5 .  In the Unit and Factory Unit columns, select hr . 
 

 6 .  Click the Details tab. In the Family column, select a work hours family (e.g., the predefined Work hours family ).
 Note: To create work hours families in addition to the predefined one, use the Part Families form. Be sure to flag the Work Hours column. 
 

 7 .  Click the Prices & Costs tab. In the List Price column, record the currency and price of the work hour.
 Note: To assign different prices for the work hour to different customers, leave the List Price column blank, and include the work hour in customer price lists instead. 
 

 8 .  Record the work hour's standard cost in the Std. Cost column.
