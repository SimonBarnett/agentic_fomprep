# Travel Charges

 

You can agree with a customer on the number of miles and work hours to charge them for travel whenever a service call is made. Priority uses this information when the warranty or service contract does not cover travel costs. 
 
## Note:

 

When you set this up, keep in mind that the same defaults are also used for projects. 
 
## To record default travel charges for customers:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form. Retrieve the desired customer(s). 
 

 2 .  For each customer, click the Charges tab and fill in the Travel Time to Charge and Miles to Charge columns. 
 
## Note: 

 

For customers with many sites, you can record site-specific values for travel time and miles in the Customer Sites form (a sub-level of the Customers form).
