Customer Service Setup - Serial Numbers.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 Priority offers an automatic utility to open a batch of serial numbers without linking them to transactions. As a default, the maximum number of serial numbers that can be opened in this way at once is 100. 
 
## To change the maximum number of serial numbers that can be opened automatically at once:

 

 1 .  Enter the Logistic Constants form. The CMaxSernOpen constant is pre-set to 100 . 
 

 2 .  Change the value of the CMaxSernOpen constant. 
 
## Tip:

 

Click if you have not yet flagged serialized parts in the Service Parameters for Part form.
