# Defining the Location of Serialized Parts

 

 Priority enables you to define a hierarchy of sites, facilities, and systems where serialized parts are stored, to facilitate their location. 
 

 To define sites, facilities, and systems for serialized parts: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Sites form. 
 

 2 .  List the sites in which serialized parts are located. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Facilities form. 
 

 4 .  List the facilities in which serialized parts are located. For each facility, indicate a Site . 
 

 5 .  Enter the Systems form. 
 

 6 .  List the systems in which serialized parts are located. For each system, indicate a Facility .
