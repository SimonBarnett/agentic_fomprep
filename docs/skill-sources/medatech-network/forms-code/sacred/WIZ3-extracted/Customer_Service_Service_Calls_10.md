# Time for Assist vs. Repair 

 

As a default, service calls with response times shorter than one hour are considered “assists” and those with longer response times are considered “repairs” . This cutoff time determines whether a call is included in the Mean Time to Repair (MTTR) report or in the Mean Time to Assist (MTTA) report. 
 
## To change the default cutoff time:

 

 1 .  Enter the Logistic Constants form. The CAssistTime constant is pre-set to 1 hour. 
 

 2 .  Change the value of the CAssistTime constant.
