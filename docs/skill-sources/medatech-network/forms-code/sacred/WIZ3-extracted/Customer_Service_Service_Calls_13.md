# How to Schedule Service Calls

 

When technicians are scheduled for service calls directly from within the Technician Scheduling Calendar , the CSrvCallTask constant determines where such scheduling information will be recorded: in the Scheduling tab of the Service Calls form, or in the sub-level Schedule Technicians form. The latter option allows you to schedule several technicians on different dates and/or times for the same service call. 
 
## To set the constant:

 

 1 .  Enter the Logistic Constants form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the CSrvCallTask constant and select the appropriate setting.
