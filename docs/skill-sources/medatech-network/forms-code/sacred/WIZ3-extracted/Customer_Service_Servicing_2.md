# Assigning Servicing Packages to Parts

 

Each servicing package can be attached to one or more serviced parts. This information is used by Priority to automatically schedule routine servicing for the part, once the part is installed at the customer site. You receive a reminder when the part is due for servicing. 
 
## To assign servicing packages to parts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Parameters for Part form. Retrieve the desired serviced part. 
 

 2 .  In the Servicing for Part sub-level form, record all types of servicing that the part normally requires, as well as the frequency (in days) with which the part requires this service.
