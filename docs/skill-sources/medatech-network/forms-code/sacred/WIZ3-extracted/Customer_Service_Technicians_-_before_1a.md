## To designate an employee as a technician:

 

 1 .  Enter the Personnel File form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired employee. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Company-Specific Information sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Click the Details tab and flag the Service Provider column. 
 

 5 .  In the Skill column, choose the predefined skill 001 ( Technician ) or use one of the additional skills you defined. 
 

 6 .  If desired, specify any of the following: 
 

 ·              Type of work hour , in the Work Hr. Part column. This will be the default when the technician reports labor for a service call.
 Note: Click if the desired type of work hour does not appear in the Choose list. 
 

 ·              Default spare parts warehouse, in the Warehouse column. This will be the default spare parts warehouse when a service call is opened and assigned to this technician. 
 
## Note:

 

You can view all technicians, together with other service providers, in the List of Service Providers form.
