# Working Hours for Technicians

 

Working hours are used by Priority when determining technicians' schedules. You can set up two types of working hours: for the office as a whole, and for individual employees. When there are no working hours set up for a particular technician, the office working hours are used as a default. 
 
## To set up working hours for the office as a whole:

 

 1 .  Enter the Office Hours form. 
 

 2 .  For each day of the week, record when work in your office begins and ends. 
 
## To set up hours for individual technicians:

 

 1 .  Enter the Office Hours per Employee form. 
 

 2 .  For each technician, record what hours he/she works on which days of the week.
