# Families per Technician 

 

 To assign part families to technicians: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Personnel File form. Retrieve the desired technician(s). 
 

 2 .  Enter the Company-Specific Information sub-level form. To restrict a technician to repairing only parts belonging to certain part families, check the Service for Fams Only column. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Families Serviced by Technician form (a sub-level of the Company-Specific Information form). Designate the part family or families that this technician is responsible for repairing.
