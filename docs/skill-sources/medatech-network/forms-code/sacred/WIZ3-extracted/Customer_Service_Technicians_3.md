# Default Technicians

 

You can assign default technicians to particular customers, shipping zones OR part families. Priority will automatically fill in this default technician when new service calls are open for that customer, zone or family. 
 

 1 .  Select ONE of the following ways to assign default technicians: 
 

 ·              By customer. 
 Enter the Additional Customer Data form (a sub-level of the Customers form). OR 
 

 ·              By shipping zone OR 
 

 ·              By part family 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired records. 
 

 3 .  In the Technician in Charge column, select a technician.
