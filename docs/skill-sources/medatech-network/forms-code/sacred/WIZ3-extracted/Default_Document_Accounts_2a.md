# The Account Hierarchy

 

The hierarchy of accounts is as follows, in descending order. Example. 
 

 ·        For income accounts only: the project for which the transaction is recorded. 
 

 ·        For all accounts: The accounting family to which the part belongs AND the warehouse in which the transaction takes place. 
 

 ·        For income accounts and COGS accounts only: The customer group of the customer AND the accounting family of the part. 
 

 ·        For purchases accounts only: The vendor group of the vendor AND the accounting family of the part. 
 

 ·        For all accounts: The accounting family to which the part belongs (regardless of warehouse). 
 

 ·        For all accounts: The warehouse in which the transaction takes place (regardless of accounting family). 
 

 ·        For income accounts only: The customer group of the customer for which the transaction takes place. 
 

 ·        For all accounts: The company-wide default setting.
