# To set up income accounts and COGS accounts for an accounting family and customer group:

 

 1 .  Enter the Accounts for Families form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired accounting family. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Acc'ts for Cust Group in Family sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  In the Cust. Group Code column, choose the desired customer group. 
 

 5 .  Choose the desired accounts. 
 

 6 . If desired, assign a Profit Center to the customer group and accounting family. 
 
## Note:

 

Alternatively, you can retrieve the customer group in the Customer Groups form and record accounts per accounting family in the Acc'ts for Cust Group by Family sub-level form.
