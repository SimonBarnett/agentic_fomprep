- 
 
 
 
# To set up purchases accounts for an accounting family and vendor group:

 

 1 .  Enter the Accounts for Families form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired accounting family. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Acc'ts for Vendor Grp in Family sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  In the Vendor Group Code column, choose the desired vendor group. 
 

 5 .  In the Purchases Account column, choose an account. 
 

 6 .  If desired, assign a Profit/ Cost Center to the vendor group and accounting family. 
 
## Note:

 

Alternatively, you can retrieve the vendor group in the Vendor Groups form and record accounts per accounting family in the Acc'ts for Vendor Grp by Family sub-level form.
