# Preparing Part Families for Delivery

 

Flag those families whose parts are to be delivered directly by the company's drivers. Only sales orders that include parts from these families can be scheduled for delivery. Also define the weight and volume of each part in the family, to ensure that the total weight and volume of the parts intended for delivery will not exceed the maximum capacity of the delivery vehicle. 
 
## To flag part families for delivery:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Families form. 
 

 2 .  Flag the Delivery Scheduling column for every part family intended for delivery. 
 
## To define the weight and volume of parts intended for delivery:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Parameters for Parts form. 
 

 2 .  Click the Shipping Expenses tab. Specify the appropriate values in the Weight , Volume and Unit columns.
 Note: The predefined unit of volume is the cubic foot (cft). To change the name of the unit of volume for all parts, use the Part Units form.
