# Setting Up a Customer Delivery Profile

 

For every customer that is to receive deliveries, define a distribution route, unloading time and regular delivery days. These will determine the scheduling of deliveries to this customer. Also set customer parameters that affect printouts of the documents that accompany the delivery. 
 
## To set up a customer delivery profile: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the customer that you wish to add to the delivery schedule. 
 

 2 .  Click the Shipments tab and choose a Distribution Route appropriate for the customer's address. When deliveries are scheduled, orders for this customer will be assigned to this route. 
 

 3 .  Indicate the number of hours and/or minutes usually required to unload delivered goods at this customer. This is particularly important if the current customer takes longer than usual (e.g., due to more thorough inspections). 
 Note: The total time required for unloading specific orders appears in the various delivery scheduling forms. 
 

 4 .  In the Delivery Days sub-level form, record the day(s) of the week on which this customer regularly receives deliveries (for multiple days, record one day on each line).
