# To schedule deliveries automatically:

 

 1 .  Enter the Deliveries by Order form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the orders you wish to schedule (e.g., by due dates or customer numbers).
 Notes: 
 

 ·      Only orders that meet the criteria for delivery scheduling can be retrieved. 
 

 ·      Both scheduled and unscheduled orders are displayed in this form. 
 

 ·      Any order that appears with a flagged Closed column has already been delivered. 
 

 3 .  Make sure that the details of the unscheduled orders are correct. 
 

 4 .  Run the Schedule Deliveries program.
 Notes: 
 

 ·      Orders will be assigned to the customer's default distribution route, as defined in the Customers form. 
 

 ·      If the total weight or volume of the orders scheduled for a distribution route and round exceeds the maximum capacity of the vehicle, a warning message will appear when the program is finished. To view the distribution routes in which deviations occur, run the Excess Weight & Volume by Route report.
