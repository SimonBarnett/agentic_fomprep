# Reviewing and Revising Scheduled Deliveries

 

Once deliveries have been scheduled for a given date, you should ensure that: 
 

 ·        these are the orders you want to schedule 
 

 ·        these are the desired distribution routes 
 

 ·        the truck's maximum weight and volume have not been exceeded 
 

 ·        the total time required for unloading does not exceed the limits of the delivery round 
 

 ·        the delivery date falls on the customer's regular delivery day. 
 

If any problems are encountered, you can revise the delivery schedule.
