# Manual Scheduling of Several Orders at Once

 

For a given delivery date, you can assign orders to the customer's default route. 
 
## Note: 

 

Click to assign an order to a different route than the customer default. 
 
## To schedule the orders: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Delivery Dates form. 
 

 2 .  In the Delivery Date column, retrieve the desired date (if it does not appear, specify it manually). 
 

 3 .  Specify the Warehouse and Bin (if any) from which the delivery will be made. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Deliveries by Route sub-level form and move to the desired route (if it does not appear, select it from the list). 
 

 5 .  The default Truck No. and Driver Code defined for this route appear automatically, but may be revised. 
 

 6 .  Flag the Sched. column. This prevents the Prepare Sales Invs+Shipping Docs program from opening shipping documents or sales invoices for the orders you are in the process of scheduling. 
 

 7 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      To schedule deliveries for all routes within the shipping zone to which the current route belongs, flag the Orders by Zone column. 
 

 ·      To schedule deliveries for all orders that meet the criteria for scheduling , regardless of shipping zone or route, flag the Display all Orders column. 
 

 ·      To schedule deliveries solely for the current route, make sure the above columns are empty.
