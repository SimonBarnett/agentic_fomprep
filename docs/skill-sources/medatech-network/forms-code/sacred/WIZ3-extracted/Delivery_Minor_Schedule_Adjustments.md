# To review and revise scheduling data:

 

 1 .  Enter the Delivery Dates form. Retrieve by the desired Delivery Date . 
 

 2 .  Enter the Deliveries by Route sub-level form to view each distribution route scheduled for the date in question. 
 

 3 .  In the Total Weight and Total Volume columns, check that totals do not exceed the capacity of the truck (defined in the Maximum Weight and Maximum Volume columns of the Vehicle Types form). Also check the Total Unload Time to ensure that there will be sufficient time for unloading all scheduled goods. 
 

 4 .  Revise the route's Truck No. and Driver Code , if desired. 
 

 5 .  To view the orders scheduled for each distribution route on the date in question, enter the next sub-level, Orders Scheduled for Delivery . If needed, revise the order's distribution route, round and/or delivery document type. 
 Tips: 
 

 ·      If the Exception column is flagged, check the delivery date with the customer. 
 

 ·      To view all orders scheduled for the date in question and revise their scheduling, use the Orders Scheduled for Delivery form that is a direct sub-level of Delivery Dates .
