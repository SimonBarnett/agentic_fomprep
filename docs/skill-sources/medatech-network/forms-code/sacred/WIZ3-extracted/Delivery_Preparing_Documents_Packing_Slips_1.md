# Preparing Documents Based on Packing Slips

 

When shipping packed goods, you can prepare shipping documents or sales invoices for the delivery (i.e., bills of lading) automatically, on the basis of one or more packing slips. If a given packing slip contains any items that are included in a prepaid order, a customer shipment will be opened. Otherwise, the type of document opened for each customer depends on whether the Delivery with Sales Inv. column (in the Financ. Parameters for Customers form) is flagged. 
 

Bills of lading are always itemized automatically, according to the packing crates they include. The quantities in the itemized bill of lading must then be approved in the sub-level form. By default, shipped quantities must be approved manually; however, if you want shipped quantities to be approved automatically, you can set a constant that will activate automatic approval. 
 
## To determine whether shipped quantities are approved automatically:

 

 1 .  Enter the Logistic Constants form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the SCratesQuant constant and set the desired value.
