Delivery Scheduling - Printing Documents for Deliveries 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To print any documents that are scheduled for delivery on a given date, round and route:

 

 1 .  Enter the Delivery Dates form. 
 

 2 .  In the Delivery Date column, retrieve the desired date. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Deliveries by Route sub-level form and move to the desired route. 
 

 4 .  Select Print All Documents from the list of Direct Activations .
