# To reschedule a single order: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Deliveries by Order form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired order (e.g., by its delivery date). 
 

 3 .  Assign a different Delivery Date , if necessary. 
 

 4 .  In the Distrib. Route Code column, choose a different route, if necessary. 
 

 5 .  In the Round column, change the delivery round, if necessary. 
 

 6 .  Exit the form.
 Tip: To view the rescheduled order, re-enter the form and retrieve the order in question by its new delivery date or route.
