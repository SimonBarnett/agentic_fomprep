## To cancel a scheduled delivery:

 

 1 .  Enter the Delivery Dates form. Retrieve by the desired Delivery Date . 
 

 2 .  Enter the Orders Scheduled for Delivery sub-level form and move to the order whose delivery you want to cancel. 
 

 3 .  Press Ctrl+Delete to delete the record. 
 
## To delete a delivery date from the schedule: 

 

 1 .  Ensure that no orders are still scheduled for this date. 
 

 2 .  Return to the Delivery Dates form and retrieve the desired delivery date. 
 

 3 .  Select Delete Delivery Date from the list of Direct Activations .
