Deposits - Depositing a Check to Another Account.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 8 .  Enter the Deposited Items form (another sub-level of the Deposit Slips form). The flagged checks appear. 
 

 9 .  Check that all the deposit information is correct. Make any corrections (e.g., check number, payment date) in the Revision of Checks form. To view the corrections in the deposit slip, refresh the screen (press Ctrl+F11 ) while in the Deposited Items form. 
 

 10 .  If the deposit slip needs to be checked before it is deposited, assign it the Checking status (in the References tab). In the Assigned to column, specify the username of the person who is responsible for checking the document. The deposit slip cannot be finalized until this status is changed.
 Tip: You can define work flow rules so that the assigned user changes automatically when the status is assigned. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Depending on how deposits are set up, you may be required to finalize the deposit slip before you can leave the form. Explanation . 
 

 ·        Until you finalize the deposit slip, the checks it contains continue to appear in the cashier. However, you cannot include these checks in another deposit slip. 
 

 ·        Click to finalize the deposit slip. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
