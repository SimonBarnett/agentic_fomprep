# Revising a Final Deposit Slip

 

Only certain columns can be revised in a final deposit slip (e.g., External Slip Number , Details ). 
 
## To revise a final invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Revision of Final Deposit Slips form and retrieve the desired deposit slip. 
 

 2 .  Make the necessary revisions in the updateable columns of this form.
