# Finalizing and Printing Deposit Slips

 

You can run a single program to simultaneously finalize and print a single deposit slip. Alternatively, you can finalize the deposit slip and print it later. 
 
## To finalize and print a deposit slip:

 

 1 .  Enter the Deposit Slips form and retrieve the desired deposit slip. 
 

 2 .  Click the References tab and check the document's Status . You will only be able to finalize the document if its current status allows for finalization (e.g., it is assigned the Approved status). 
 Note: You must also be authorized to change the status of deposit slips to Final . 
 

 3 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              To simultaneously finalize and print the deposit slip, select Finalize & Print Deposit Slip from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 ·              To finalize the deposit slip, select Finalize Deposit Slip from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 ·              To print the deposit slip, select Print Deposit Slip from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        When you finalize a deposit slip, a journal entry is recorded for the transaction. If the deposit is of credit card payments, the entry will be itemized in keeping with the account's definition in the Credit Card Accounts form. Usually, the Deposit Lump Sum column is not flagged, meaning that each credit card slip will appear on a separate line, with one exception: If payment is in installments, each installment appears separately in the entry, but installments with the same payment date (from different credit card slips) are combined. This is meant to facilitate reconciliation with the account statement received from the credit card company. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
