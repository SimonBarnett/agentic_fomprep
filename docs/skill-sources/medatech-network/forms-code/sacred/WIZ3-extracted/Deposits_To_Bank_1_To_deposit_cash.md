# To deposit cash to a bank account:

 

 1 .  Enter the Deposit Slips form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the References tab). 
 

 2 .  Record the Date the deposit was made. 
 

 3 .  If necessary, change the cashier in the From Cashier column. 
 

 4 .  In the To Cash/Bk/CredCd column, choose the bank account to which you are making the deposit. 
 

 5 .  Record the amount of the cash deposit and the Currency in which the deposit was made. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        If you have defined banknotes and coins for this currency, you can specify the quantities of banknotes and coins included in this deposit in the Banknotes and Coins in Deposit sub-level form instead of recording the cash amount. The Cash Deposi t column (in the upper-level form) is updated automatically with the total value of the specified notes and coins. 
 

 ·        To check whether the cashier balance is sufficient to cover the deposit, select Check Cashier Balance from the list of Direct Activations .
