# To prepare the deposit slip manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Deposit Slips form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the References tab). 
 

 2 .  Record the Date the deposit was made. 
 

 3 .  If necessary, change the cashier in the From Cashier column. 
 

 4 .  In the To Cash/Bk/CredCd column, choose the bank account to which you are making the deposit.
 Note: To include a cash deposit, record the amount in the Cash Deposit column. 
 

 5 .   D ual-currency users: To deposit a postdated check, flag the Post dated Checks column. 
 

 6 .  Specify the Currency in which the deposit was made. 
 

 7 .  Enter the Items at Cashier sub-level form. All cash, checks and credit card slips in this cashier appear. 
 

 8 .  For each check you are depositing, flag the Deposit column. 
Tip: To check whether the cashier balance is sufficient to cover the deposit, return to the upper-level form and select Check Cashier Balance from the list of Direct Activations .
