# Depositing to Another Cashier

 

There are two ways you can prepare the deposit slip, automatically or manually: 
 

 ·        If you are depositing checks and/or credit card slips whose payment dates fall within a defined time period, prepare the deposit automatically. 
 

 ·        If the items for deposit vary widely in their payment dates, or you are depositing cash, prepare the deposit manually. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To deposit checks and credit card slips to a single cashier, record the deposit slip manually. 
 

 ·        Dual-currency users: Deposits of postdated checks can only be recorded manually.
