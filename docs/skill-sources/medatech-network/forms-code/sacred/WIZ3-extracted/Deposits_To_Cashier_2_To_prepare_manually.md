# To prepare the deposit slip manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Deposit Slips form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the References tab). 
 

 2 .  Record the Date the deposit was made. 
 

 3 .  If necessary, change the cashier from which you are making the deposit. 
 

 4 .  In the To Cash/Bk/CredCd column, choose the cashier to which you are making the deposit. 
 

 5 .  Specify the Currency in which the deposit was made. 
 
## What type of deposit are you making?

 

 Cash 
 

 Checks and/or credit card slips 
 

 Cash and checks, cash and credit card slips, or all three
