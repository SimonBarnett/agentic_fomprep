# To deposit cash:

 

 1 .  Record the amount of the cash deposit. 
 

 Notes : 
 

 ·              If you have defined banknotes and coins for this currency, you can specify the quantities of banknotes and coins included in this deposit in the Banknotes and Coins in Deposit sub-level form instead of recording the cash amount. The Cash Deposi t column (in the upper-level form) is updated automatically with the total value of the specified notes and coins. 
 

 ·              To check whether the cashier balance is sufficient to cover the deposit, select Check Cashier Balance from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 2 .  If the deposit slip needs to be checked before it is deposited, assign it the Checking status (in the References tab). In the Assigned to column, specify the username of the person who is responsible for checking the document. The deposit slip cannot be finalized until this status is changed.
 Tip: You can define work flow rules so that the assigned user changes automatically when the status is assigned. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        Depending on how deposits are set up, you may be required to finalize the deposit slip before you can leave the form. Explanation . 
 

 ·        Until you finalize the deposit slip, the cash it contains continues to appear in the cashier. 
 

 ·        Click to finalize the deposit slip. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
