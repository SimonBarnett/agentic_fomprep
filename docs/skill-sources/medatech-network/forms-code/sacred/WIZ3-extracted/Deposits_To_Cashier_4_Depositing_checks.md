# To deposit checks and/or credit card slips:

 

 1 .   D ual-currency users: To deposit a postdated check, flag the Post dated Checks column. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Items at Cashier sub-level form. All cash, checks and credit card slips in this cashier appear. 
 

 3 .  For each item you are depositing, flag the Deposit column. 
Tip: To check whether the cashier balance is sufficient to cover the deposit, return to the upper-level form and select Check Cashier Balance from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 4 .  Enter the Deposited Items form (another sub-level of the Deposit Slips form). The flagged items appear.
