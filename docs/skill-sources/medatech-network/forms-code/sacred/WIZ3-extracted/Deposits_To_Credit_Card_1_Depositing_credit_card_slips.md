# Depositing Credit Card Slips to a Credit Card Company

 

There are two ways you can prepare the deposit slip: 
 

 ·        Manually OR 
 

 ·        Automatically, for credit card slips whose payment dates fall within a designated time period. 
 

As a general rule, if you are depositing most or all of the credit card slips at the cashier with payment dates falling within a reasonable time period, prepare the deposit automatically. Otherwise, prepare it manually.
