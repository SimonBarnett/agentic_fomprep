# Recording a Deposit Slip

 

You can make cash, check or credit card slip deposits from a cashier to any of the following: 
 

 ·        A bank account 
 

 ·        A credit card company 
 

 ·        Some other account. Examples. 
 

 ·        Another cashier 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Deposit only cash to the bank 
 

 Deposit checks to the bank (including depositing both checks and cash) 
 

 Deposit credit card slips (vouchers) to credit card companies 
 

 Make a cash payment to some other account 
 

 Make a check payment to some other account 
 

 Make a deposit to another cashier
