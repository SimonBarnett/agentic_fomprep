# To complete general details:

 

 1 .  Enter the Personnel File form and retrieve the desired employee file (e.g., by User , in the General tab).
 Note: If the employee is not a system user, open a new record, indicating the employee's Full Name and Social Security Number . 
 

 2 .  In the General tab, record the employee's Title and Gender . 
 

 3 .  Click the Position & Team tab. Record the employee's Job Title . 
 

 4 .  If you want to change the employee's main Company , select Change Employee's Company from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 5 .  If the employee belongs to a team, choose his/her team leader in the Leader's EmpID column. If this employee heads the team, flag the Team Leader column. 
 

 6 .  Click the Address & Phone tab. Record the employee's address, telephone numbers and e-mail address. 
 

 7 .  Click the Definitions tab to define additional parameters for the employee. 
 

 8 .  Click the Assigned to tab and, if necessary, change the employee status and/or person responsible for this employee. 
 

 9 .  If desired, attach a file containing a photograph of the employee. 
 
## Note:

 

The data in this form is shared by all companies in a multi-company enterprise.
