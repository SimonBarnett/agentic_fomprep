# Recording an Employee as an Applicant for a New Job in the Organization

 

 Priority has a mechanism for finding qualified people, from a pool of relevant job candidates and existing employees, for a given job. Whenever a match is found between someone's qualifications and the job's requirements, that person is automatically displayed as a potential applicant. 
 
## To include an employee in the search pool:

 

 1 .  Enter the Personnel File form and retrieve the desired employee. 
 

 2 .  Change the employee's Status ( Assigned to tab) to one defined as In Applicant Pool . 
 

 3 .  Enter the Qualifications sub-level form and check that the employee's qualifications are fully recorded. Add as necessary.
 Tip: To define qualifications and their possible values, run the Personnel Setup Wizard . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To view an employee's job history, and to view any open applications for new positions, enter the parallel Jobs Applied For sub-level form. 
 

 ·        The complete job recruitment process is discussed in the Job Openings Wizard . 
 

 ·        To record information about interviews, tests or salary offers, click to run the Job Applicants Wizard .
