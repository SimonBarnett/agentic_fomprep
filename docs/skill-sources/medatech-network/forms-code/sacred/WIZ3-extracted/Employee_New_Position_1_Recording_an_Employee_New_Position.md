Employees - Recording an Employee's New Job 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 To record an employee ' s new job within the organization: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Personnel File form and retrieve the desired employee. 
 

 2 .  Select Copy Position to Employee's Log from the list of Direct Activations to copy the employee's former position in the organization into the Log of Positions Held sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Revise information in the personnel file and its sub-levels, as needed.
