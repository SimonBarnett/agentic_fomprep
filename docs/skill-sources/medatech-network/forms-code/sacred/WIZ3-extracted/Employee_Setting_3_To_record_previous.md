# To record an employee's previous positions within the organization:

 

 Note: Click if this is a new employee 
 

 1 .   Enter the Log of Positions Held sub-level form and record any previous positions held by the employee. 
 

 2 .  Record the dates the employee held each position and any other relevant information. 
 

 3 .  The current company appears automatically as the company in which the position was held. Revise as necessary.
