# To record the employee's personal details:

 

 Note: If the employee was initially recorded as a job candidate, some of this information may already be filled in. 
 

 1 .  Enter the parallel Personal Details sub-level form. Record the employee's personal and family details, as well as information related to his/her driver's license and health insurance. 
 

 2 .  Enter the parallel Employee's Children sub-level form. Record the information about the employee's children. 
 
## Note:

 

The data in these forms is shared by all companies in a multi-company enterprise.
