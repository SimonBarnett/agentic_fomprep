# Additional Options

 

You can record the following, in various sub-levels of the Personnel File : 
 

 ·        Leaves of absence taken by the employee, in the Leaves of Absence form.
 Note: To view leave of absence data for multiple employees, use the Employees' Leaves of Absence form. 
 

 ·        Certifications granted to employees by the company, and the level of certification granted, in the Certification form. 
 

 ·        Medical tests taken by employees, and the certification toward which the employee took the medical test, in the Medical Tests form and its sub-level Medical Records . 
 

 ·        Awards given to the employee, in the Awards to Employee form. 
 

 ·        Any additional remarks about the employee, in the Remarks form.
