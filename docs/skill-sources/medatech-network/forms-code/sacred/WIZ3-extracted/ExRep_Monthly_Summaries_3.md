# Viewing Monthly Summaries

 

The results of the monthly calculations are viewed in several forms and their sub-levels. 
 

 Tip : 
 

If you have not already done so, click to update monthly data before viewing the monthly summary. 
 
## To view monthly summaries:

 

 ·        For shipped goods, enter the Monthly Sales form. 
 

 ·        For customer invoices, enter the Monthly Customer Invoices form. 
 

 ·        For sales orders and commission orders, enter the Monthly Sales Orders form. 
 

 ·        For purchase orders, enter the Monthly Purchase Orders form. 
 

 ·        For purchase invoices, enter the Monthly Purchasing Invoices form.
