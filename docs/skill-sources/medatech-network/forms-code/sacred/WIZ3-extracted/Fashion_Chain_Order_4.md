# Processing the Order

 

Once you have completed itemizing the assortments in your order, you can use its advanced features to help you execute it. 
 
## To continue processing the order:

 

 1 .  Return to the Sales Orders upper-level form and do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If you are purchasing the ordered items for resale, select Open Purchase Ord from Sales Ord from the list of Direct Activations . This program generates a purchase order that displays a separate assortment matrix for each individual store you are supplying, as well as an aggregate matrix that displays total quantities for each style, color and size combination. 
 

 ·              If you are manufacturing the ordered items, select Order Confirmation from the list of Direct Activations and select the By Base Product format. Use the Assortment Totals at the bottom of the printout to manually open work orders for the required items. 
 

 2 .  When the finished items are obtained, retrieve the order in the Sales Orders form and select Prepare Pack Lists for Distrib. from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .  Select Packing Slip from the list of Direct Activations and retrieve the new packing slips. Use them to sort the items per store and pack them into separate shipments. You will now be able to supply each store immediately upon demand. 
 

 4 .  When you are ready to ship the goods to their respective stores, open your customer shipment documents on the basis of the packing slips.
