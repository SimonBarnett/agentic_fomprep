 7 .  Enter the Part Colors (Fashion) sub-level form and select all of the colors in which the style will be produced. 
 

 8 .  Enter the parallel Assortment sub-level form. Each possible combination of size and color appears in a two-dimensional matrix. If you intend to create a new part for every combination, leave the assortment as it is. Otherwise, change the 0 to 1 for each combination for which you intend to create a part.
 Note: There is no numerical significance to the values in this form; the number 1 is simply used as a check mark. 
 

 9 .  Select Parts from the list of Direct Activations . Retrieve the new style (e.g., by its Part Number ), and define its Routing . Close the form but do not exit the Part Catalogue form. 
 

 10 .  Select Financial Parameters for Parts from the list of Direct Activations . Retrieve the new style, and define its Accounting Family , as well as any other financial attributes, as desired. Close the form and exit the Part Catalogue form.  
 
## Note:

 

To turn a unique fashion item into its own style (that is, a base product with no members other than itself), reenter the Part Catalogue form and copy the Part Number into the Base Product column (in the Details tab). The Base Product column (in the Additional Details tab) will be flagged automatically, and the part will now appear in all forms and reports that display styles.
