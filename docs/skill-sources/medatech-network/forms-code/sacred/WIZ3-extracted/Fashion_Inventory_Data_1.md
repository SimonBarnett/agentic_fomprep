# Maintaining Inventory Data for Fashion Goods

 

Inventory levels in retail warehouses are used, among other things, to monitor the restock requirements of each retailer. One of the methods of determining restock requirements is to compare current inventory levels of fashion items with a minimum inventory level defined for each part and warehouse. 
 

You can also maintain a history of inventory data by displaying inventory levels on past dates. 
 
## To define minimum inventory levels:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouses form and retrieve all retail warehouses.
 Tip: Use the For Consignment flag. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Warehouse Inventory Levels sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Open a line for every fashion item shipped to that warehouse, and specify its minimum Inventory Level . 
 
## To view restock requirements based on minimum inventory:

 

 ·        Run the Pick List by Inventory Level report.
