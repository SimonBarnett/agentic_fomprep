# How To Use This Wizard

 

The first section of this wizard concerns the creation of fashion styles and their maintenance. It shows you how to create catalogue parts for each of your fashion styles, how to convert parts from one style to another and how to maintain price lists for fashion items. 
 

The second section explains how to prepare and execute a large-scale distribution of fashion inventory to a chain of stores, from the planning stage to the actual shipment of goods, whether you are manufacturing the goods yourself or purchasing them for resale. This section also describes the various forms and reports provided for the analysis of inventory and sales data.
