# Maintaining Sales Data for Fashion Goods

 

Sales data from retail warehouses are essential in determining which styles, colors and sizes are selling most successfully. This information affects both manufacturing and stocking decisions, as well as marketing strategy. Sales data are maintained in your Priority database via billable customer shipments and sales invoices (in the case of factory outlets) and unbilled shipping documents or sales invoices (in the case of franchises) that are uploaded via special interfaces. 
 

You can also use Priority forms and reports to analyze your rate of sales – both on the company level and on the store level. 
 
# To see how your fashion items are selling:

 

 ·        Run the Sales by Style report to view the quantity of goods sold by both outlets and franchises within a designated period (though you may limit the report to designated warehouses), using assortment matrices. 
 

 ·        Run the Sales by Warehouse/Style report to view the same data as the previous report, sorted by warehouse (i.e., using a separate assortment for each style within each warehouse). 
 
## To view restock requirements based on sales:

 

 ·        Run the Pick List by Sales report. 
 
## To view restock requirements based on sales and existing inventory:

 

 ·        Run the Pick List by Sales and Inventory report.
