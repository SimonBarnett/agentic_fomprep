## To include tax in sales amounts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Logistic Constants form and move to the SRateTax constant. 
 

 2 .  Change the value of the constant to 1 .
 Note: The constant only affects sales amounts that appear in various sales rate analysis forms. It does not affect amounts displayed in customized sales rate analysis reports. 
 
## To include a part family in sales rate analysis:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Families form and retrieve all families of fashion styles. 
 

 2 .   For each family that you want to include in sales rate analysis, flag the Sold Goods column. 
 
## To exclude a warehouse from sales rate analysis:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouses form and retrieve the desired warehouse. 
 

 2 .   Flag the Omit from Sales Rates column (in the General tab).
