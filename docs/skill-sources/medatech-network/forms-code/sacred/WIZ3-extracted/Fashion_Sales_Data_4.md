# To view rate of sales data:

 

 1 .  Run the Prepare Sales Rates program. 
 

 2 .  Do any of the following: 
 

 ·              Enter the Sales Rate Analysis by Style or Sales Rate Analysis-Store&Style form to view rate of sales per style for the company as a whole, or for a given store. 
 

 ·              Enter the Sales Rate Analysis by Part or Sales Rate Analysis-Store&Part form to view rate of sales per fashion item for the company as a whole, or for a given store. 
 

 ·              Run a custom sales rate report. 
 
## To create custom sales rate reports:

 

 1 .  Use the Sales Rate by Part Report Gen. and/or Sales Rate by Store Report Gen. forms to create custom reports of sales rate data.
 Tip: For instructions on creating custom reports, see the Customized Reports Wizard-OLD . 
 

 2 .   Run the Sales Rate by Part Report Gen. reports to view any of your custom sales rate reports.
