# Setting Up Sales Reports for Franchises

 

If you are working with external franchises, merchandise that leaves your warehouse is recorded in a billable shipping document, as it is considered to have left inventory. Consequently, retail sales made by the franchise to its customers are recorded in non-billable customer shipment forms (or in sales invoices), for the purpose of sales analysis. 
 

The SFranchise constant allows you to set up sales reports to calculate the unbilled retail sales, rather than the billed franchise shipments, thus providing you with more accurate sales data. 
 
## To set the SFranchise constant:

 

 1 .  Enter the Logistic Constants form and move to the SFranchise constant. 
 

 2 .  To set up sales reports to calculate retail sales, change the value to 1 ; if you do not work with franchises, leave the pre-set value of 0 . 
 
## Note: 

 

If you work with both franchises and factory outlets, you can change the value to 1 and the system will differentiate between sales to external franchises and sales to factory outlets, calculating the unbilled retail sales in the former case, and the billed retail sales in the latter.
