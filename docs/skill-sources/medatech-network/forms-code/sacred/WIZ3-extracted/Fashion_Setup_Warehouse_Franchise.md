Fashion - Setting Up a Franchise Warehouse 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 To open a franchise warehouse: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouses form, and open a new type D warehouse in the usual way.
 Tip: For details, run the Open a Warehouse Wizard . 
 

 2 .  Remove the check marks from the Inventory Valued and Transaction Costing columns. 
 

 3 .  Flag the For Consignment column (to define the warehouse as external), the Sales Warehouse column (to indicate that goods in the warehouse are available for sale) and the Franchise column. 
 

 4 .  To set the position of the new warehouse in tabular reports (e.g., pick lists), specify a value in the Rep. Display Order column. The warehouse with the lowest number appears first in the X-axis of the report. 
 

 5 .  Click the Consignment tab. Record the owner's Customer Number . The Customer Name appears automatically.
