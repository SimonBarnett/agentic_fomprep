# Setting Up Warehouses

 

Fashion transactions are conducted between a central warehouse and one of two types of retail warehouses: 
 

 ·        Factory outlet: This type of warehouse represents a retail store that belongs to your company; thus it is subject to inventory valuation and costing control. In order to include these store warehouses in automatic distribution planning, they must each be assigned to a dummy customer. Goods are then shipped to your outlets via unbilled customer shipments. 
 

 ·        Franchise: This type of warehouse belongs to your customer, and the goods that are stored in it are customer inventory, though the customer usually retains the right to return unsold merchandise. Goods are shipped to franchises via regular billed customer shipments (although sales data is calculated on the basis of end sales to the store's customers).
