# Opening a Work Order for an Assortment

 

The Work Orders form is the only form allowing you to specify fashion assortments that does not feature the List of Styles sub-level form. Instead, the Part Number column of the work order identifies the base product (fashion style) and the Work Order Qty column displays the total quantity of items to be manufactured. 
 

Once the work order is in progress, you can adjust the assortment quantities to reflect actual production, and update the work order kit list accordingly. Example. 
 
## To open a work order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Work Orders form and open a new work order.
 Tip: For details, run the Work Orders Wizard . 
 

 2 .  Estimate the required quantity of goods in the Work Order Qty column.
 Note: This column is updated automatically when you begin defining the style assortment. 
 

 3 .  Click the Prod. Data tab and specify the Begin Production date. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Assortment sub-level form. Specify the required quantity of each size/color combination in the matrix.
 Tip: Use the totals appearing in the printout of the sales order to fill in the required quantities. 
 

 5 .  Return to the upper-level form. The number of items in the assortment appears both in the Work Order Qty column and in the Qty in Assortment column (in the Details tab).
 Note: Though the assortment may be changed after the work order is released, the work order quantity will remain the same. 
 

 6 .  Continue preparing the work order in the usual manner.
