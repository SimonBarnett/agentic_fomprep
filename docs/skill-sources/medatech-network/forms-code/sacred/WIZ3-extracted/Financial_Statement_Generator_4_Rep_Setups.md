# Creating Report Items: Preliminary Steps

 

Before you create report items, you should check the setup of your financial statements. Priority allows you to create a customized balance sheet, P&L report, trial balance, and account tree, based on the standard setup of these reports. 
 
## To check your setups:

 

 1 .  Check the balance sheet in the Bal. Sheet/P&L Report Headings form and its sub-level. 
 

 2 .  Check the P&L report in the same form. 
 

 3 .  Check the trial balance in the Trial Balance Items form. 
 

 4 .  Check the account tree in the Account Tree 
 

 5 .  For each account, check the balance sheet or P&L item, as well as the trial balance item, in the Chart of Accounts , Chart of Accounts Receivable or Chart of Accounts Payable . 
 

 Important: Any changes you make will affect all standard financial statements.
