# Creating Report Items: Viewing/Modifying Results

 

 1 .  Enter the Makeup of Financial Statements form to view results. 
 

 2 .  Move to the report you just created. 
 

 3 .  Enter the sub-level form, Items in Statement , to view the items and headings that will appear in the report. You can change any titles that appear. 
 

 4 .  Return to the Makeup of Financial Statements form and enter the Accounts in Statement sub-level form to view the accounts attached to each line item.
