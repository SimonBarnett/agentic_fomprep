# Opening a Fiscal Year and Defining Periods

 

 To open a fiscal year: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Define Fiscal Years form. 
 

 2 .  Record the first and last dates of the fiscal year. 
 

 3 .  The Prefix for Entry No. column displays a two-character prefix that will be attached to all journal entries posted during this fiscal year. This is initially the last two digits of the year (e.g., 09 ). If desired, change this default.
 Note: You can also define separate journal entry prefixes by branch. To do so, flag the Number by Branch column for this fiscal year, and record the desired Prefix in the Entry Numbers by Branch sub-level form. 
 
## To define fiscal periods:

 

 1 .  Enter the Fiscal Periods sub-level form. Twelve fiscal periods appear, one for each full month. 
 

 2 .  You can leave the periods as they are. Or, to change a period, change its end date. The next period's start date is updated automatically. 
 

 Note: The first period must begin on the start date of the fiscal year, and the last period must end on the end date of the fiscal year.
