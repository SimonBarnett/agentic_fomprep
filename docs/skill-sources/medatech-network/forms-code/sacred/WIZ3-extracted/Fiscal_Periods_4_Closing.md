# Closing a Fiscal Period

 

Close a fiscal period when you are finished working with it. This prevents users from inadvertently adding transactions to the period. 
 

You close a period separately for transaction date and reference date . Close it by transaction date once you have finalized your balance sheet and/or profit & loss statement. Close it by reference date a few months after you have closed by transaction date, to allow users to record entries whose reference dates are in the past (e.g., old vendor invoices that were recorded late). 
 

You can also authorize specific users to continue recording entries for a given fiscal period after it is closed. 
 
## To close a period by transaction date:

 

 ·        Run the Close Fiscal Period program, selecting Transaction Date . The program closes the last open period not yet closed by transaction date. 
 
## To close a period by reference date:

 

 ·        Run the Close Fiscal Period program, selecting Reference Date . The program closes the last open period not yet closed by reference date. 
 
## To authorize users to record entries in a closed period:

 

 1 .  Enter the Define Fiscal Years form and move to the fiscal year in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Fiscal Periods sub-level form and move to the fiscal period in question. 
 

 3 .  Record the users in the next sub-level form, Users Authorized for Closed Per. .
