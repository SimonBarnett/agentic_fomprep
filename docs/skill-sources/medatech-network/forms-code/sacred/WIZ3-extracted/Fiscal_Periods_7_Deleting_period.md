Fiscal Periods - Deleting the Last Fiscal Period 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To delete the last defined fiscal period:

 

 ·        Run the Delete Last Fiscal Period program. 
 
## When you are ready, you need to manually redefine the period:

 

 1 .  Enter the Define Fiscal Years form. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Fiscal Periods sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Open a new line after the last existing line. 
 

 4 .  Fill in the Period and To Date columns. The start date appears automatically.
