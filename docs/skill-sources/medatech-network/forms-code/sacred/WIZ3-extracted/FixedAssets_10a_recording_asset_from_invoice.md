# Recording a Fixed Asset Based on a Purchase Invoice

 
## Before You Begin

 

 1 . Make sure each purchased fixed asset appears in the Part Catalogue form. 
 

 2 . Enter the Financial Parameters for Parts form and retrieve each purchased asset. In the General tab, specify the Asset Group to which this part will belong. 
 
## To record the invoice and open the fixed asset records:

 

 1 . Use the appropriate form ( Multi-GRV Invoices or Vendor Invoices ) and its sub-levels to open and itemize the invoice. 
 

 2 .  Dual-currency users: Enter the sub-level form, Invoice - Add'l Details , and change the VAT Code to VAT on fixed expenses ( 003 ). 
 

 3 . Return to the upper-level form and finalize the invoice. 
 

 4 . Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Select Record Fixed Assets (1 per Line) from the list of Direct Activation s to open one fixed asset record per line . OR 
 

 ·              Select Record Fixed Assets (1 per Unit) from the list of Direct Activation s to open one fixed asset record per unit .
