# To view the resulting asset records:

 

 1 .  Enter the Fixed Assets form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the asset(s) that were created by the program.
 Tip: You can retrieve all assets that were opened from the same invoice by specifying the invoice number in the Internal Invoice (Purchase) column (under the Purchase tab). 
 

 3 .  The purchase date (the date of the invoice) is filled in as the asset's Effect Date for calculating depreciation ( Details tab). To begin calculating depreciation from a different date, revise the effect date. 
 

 4 .  The asset's Description and Asset Group appear automatically. Revise them if necessary. The asset will inherit the group's GL accounts and depreciation definitions. 
 

 5 .  The asset's Branch appears automatically in the Details tab. Revise it if necessary. The branch determines the subsidiary to which the asset accounts belong. 
 

 6 .  For an asset that was created by the Record Fixed Assets (1 per Unit) program, check that the value in the Quantity column is identical to the quantity of parts in the invoice line.
