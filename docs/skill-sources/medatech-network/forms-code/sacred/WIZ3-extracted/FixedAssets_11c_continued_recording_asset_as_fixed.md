# Additional Details

 

 1 .  In the Details tab, specify the asset's Category and Location , if desired. If GL accounts for the asset are defined per state, be sure to indicate the State in which the asset is located. 
 

 2 .  If the asset has a serial number (as in the case of machinery), specify it in the Serial Number column. 
 

 3 .  In the Identifying Info column, record any additional information that may be useful in defining the fixed asset (e.g., the name of the real estate agent from whom you purchased property). 
 

 4 .  Specify the Investment Plan and item to which the asset is linked, if desired. 
 

 5 .  Additional remarks can be recorded in the Details column. 
 

 6 .  If the asset is recorded for accounting or tax purposes only, indicate this in the Accting/Tax Only column.
 Note: An asset that is recorded solely for accounting purposes will not appear in income tax reports. No journal entries will be recorded for an asset that is maintained solely for tax purposes.
