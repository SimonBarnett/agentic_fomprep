# Viewing the Asset's Depreciation Accounts and Definitions

 

The asset inherits its depreciation accounts and definitions from the asset group to which it belongs. These may be revised if necessary (for instance, if depreciation percentages or amounts vary from those of the group, or from one period to another). If the asset has undergone prior revaluation in another system, the revaluation reserve must be updated, so as to initialize depreciation data. In addition, if the asset has already depreciated in another system, its prior nominal depreciation is recorded for the ledger and for tax purposes. Finally, if you wish the asset to depreciate to a certain residual value, you can record this value in advance. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 View and/or revise the GL accounts to which the asset is assigned. 
 

 View and/or revise definitions of depreciation. 
 

 Update the revaluation reserve for prior revaluation of the asset. 
 

 Record prior nominal depreciation of the asset. 
 

 Record the residual value of the asset.
