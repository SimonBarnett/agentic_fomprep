# Viewing and Revising GL Accounts for the Asset

 

A fixed asset automatically inherits the GL accounts that have been assigned to its asset group. This, however, can be revised for individual assets. If you work with subsidiaries, the asset's branch determines the subsidiary to which the asset accounts belong. 
 
## To view and/or revise the GL accounts to which the asset is assigned:

 

 1 .  Enter the Fixed Assets form and retrieve the desired asset. 
 

 2 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Click the Accts tab and change the defined accounts, as needed. 
 

 ·              To change the subsidiary to which the asset accounts belong, change the asset's Branch (in the Details tab). 
 
## To distribute depreciation expenses among several accounts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Depreciation Expense Accounts form, another sub-level of Fixed Assets . 
 

 2 .  Verify that the desired accounts are recorded.
 Note: If you work with subsidiaries, the accounts appearing here are determined by the asset's branch. 
 

 3 .  Verify that the accounts are linked to the appropriate profit/cost centers. 
 

 4 .  Verify that the correct percentage of total depreciation expenses is recorded for each account. 
 Note: To charge depreciation expenses to different profit groups within the same account, open a new line with the same account number for each profit group.
