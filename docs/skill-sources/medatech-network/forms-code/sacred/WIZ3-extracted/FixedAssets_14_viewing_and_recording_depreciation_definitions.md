# Viewing and Revising Definitions of Depreciation

 

A fixed asset automatically inherits the depreciation definitions (the percentage, depreciation table and/or the calculation method) from its asset group. However, you can revise these definitions for individual assets. Moreover, you can define depreciation in a more sophisticated way, in terms of percentages or monetary sums that vary from one period to the next. Example. 
 
## Note: Depreciation definitions must be recorded twice : for the ledger and for tax purposes. 

 
## To view and/or revise depreciation definitions for the ledger:

 

 ·        Enter the Depreciation%-Accounting Records form, a sub-level of Fixed Assets . 
 
## To specify different depreciation percentages or sums for different periods:

 

 1 .  Enter the Depr per Period-Acc'ting Records form, another sub-level of Fixed Assets . 
 

 2 .  In the From Date and To Date columns, specify the period during which the special depreciation percentage or sum is in effect. 
 

 3 .  Specify the annual percentage of special depreciation OR the sum to be depreciated. 
 
## To do the same for tax purposes:

 

 ·        Use the Depreciation % - Tax Records and Deprec. per Period-Tax Records sub-level forms instead.
