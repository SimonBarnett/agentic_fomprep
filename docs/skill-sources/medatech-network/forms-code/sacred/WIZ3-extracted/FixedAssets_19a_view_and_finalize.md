# To view and finalize the resulting pending entries:

 

 1 .  Enter the Entry Journal form and retrieve the journal entries in question. 
 Tip: Retrieve by the prefix T* in the Journal Entry No. column and DPR in the Transaction column. 
 

 2 .  Check the details of the journal entries. Each entry totals the depreciation expenses for all fixed assets within the period covered (month, quarter or year, depending on the AssetDates constant). 
 Note for dual-currency users: The indexed depreciated sum appears in the Credit ( Adj [ local currency ] ) column in the Itemized Entry sub-level form. Adjusted depreciation sums appear in the Cost and Depreciation report. 
 

 3 .  Select Post Journal Entry from the list of Direct Activation s to post the entries to the ledger. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·              In the Entry Number column of the Calculated Deprec.-Acc'ting Recs form (sub-level of Fixed Assets , you can see the journal entries (pending and final) that were recorded for the depreciation of each asset.
