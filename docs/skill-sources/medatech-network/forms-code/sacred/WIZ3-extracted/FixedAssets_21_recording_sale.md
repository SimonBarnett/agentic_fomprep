# Recording the Sale of a Fixed Asset

 

When you sell a fixed asset, you need to record a customer invoice and attach it to the asset, create a journal entry for the disposal of the asset, check the entry and post it to the ledger.
 Note : If the asset was appreciated, you can record the sale of the principal asset alone or with its component assets. 
 
## To record a customer invoice for the sale of the asset:

 

 1 .  Fill in details of the customer invoice (in the Sales Invoices or Multi-Shipment Invoices form). 
 

 2 .  In the Invoice Items sub-level form, select the appropriate part number. 
 

 3 .  In the Income Account column, specify the account to which the proceeds of the sale were deposited.
 Note: Do not specify the capital gains account defined for the asset. 
 

 4 .  Finalize the invoice.
