# To attach the customer invoice to the asset:

 

 1 .  Enter the Fixed Assets form and retrieve the asset in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Sale tab, and specify the Type of Cust. Invoice involved (e.g., a multi-shipment invoice). 
 

 3 .  Indicate the internal number of the invoice in the Customer Invoice column. The asset's price and sale date will be filled in automatically. 
 

 4 .  Dual-currency users: Exchange rates and index information are updated automatically (in the Sale tab) according to the sale date of the asset, but may be revised. If the recorded indexes use a different index base than the one applied to Price Index values (in the Currencies form), specify the Index Base (Code) .
 Notes: 
 

 ·              It is likely that values for the base index and the previous month's index are not yet known. These can be filled in later by a special program before recording sales entries in the ledger. 
 

 ·              To record index bases in the system, use the Index Bases form.
