# Recording the Sale or Scrapping of a Fixed Asset

 

Sales and scrapping of fixed assets are recorded in a similar way. The only difference is that when there is a sale, you also record a customer invoice and attach it to the asset. When you sell or scrap an appreciated asset, you can sell/scrap: 
 

 ·        the principal asset alone 
 

 ·        the principal together with its component assets 
 

 ·        a component asset alone. 
 

 Priority also offers a mechanism for reducing the value of an asset by partially scrapping or selling it. Example.
