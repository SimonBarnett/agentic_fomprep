# Recording the Scrapping of a Fixed Asset

 

When you scrap an asset, you need to: 
 

 ·        Record the disposal of the asset in the ledger. The resulting journal entry credits the asset's main and accumulated depreciation accounts and debits its income account. 
 

 ·        Check and post the journal entry. 
 Note : If the asset was appreciated, you can record the scrapping of the principal asset alone or with its component assets.
