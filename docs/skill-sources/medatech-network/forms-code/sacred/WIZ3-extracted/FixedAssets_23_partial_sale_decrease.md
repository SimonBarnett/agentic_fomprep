# Recording a Partial Sale/Scrapping of an Asset

 

There are two main steps for selling or scrapping part of an asset: 
 

First, the asset is split into two, creating a new component asset whose value is subtracted from the original one. The new asset is assigned the same attributes as its “parent”, i.e., the same purchase date, asset group, GL accounts and depreciation definitions. The new asset receives a percentage of the principal asset's accumulated depreciation relative to its percentage of cost. Example. 
 

Then, the new asset is scrapped or sold, leaving the principal asset to depreciate at the same rate as before. The process of recording the sale/scrapping of the ”new” asset is the same as that of any other asset. 
 
## Note:

 

You cannot split an asset that has replaced another asset. 
 
## To split the asset into two:

 

 1 .  Run the Split Asset program. A message will be displayed, notifying you of the “new” asset's number. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Fixed Assets form, and retrieve the new asset by its number. The number of the original asset will be displayed in the Principal Asset column.
 Note: The number will also appear in the Original Asset column, as a means of preserving the asset's history in case it is scrapped or sold. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to sell the new asset. 
 

 ·        Click to scrap the new asset.
