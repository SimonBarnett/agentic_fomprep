# How Often to Record Depreciation Entries

 

You can determine whether the recording of depreciation in the ledger will be annual, quarterly or monthly. 
 
## To determine when to record depreciation entries:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Constants form, and move to the AssetDates constant. 
 

 2 .  Revise the value of the constant, if necessary.
