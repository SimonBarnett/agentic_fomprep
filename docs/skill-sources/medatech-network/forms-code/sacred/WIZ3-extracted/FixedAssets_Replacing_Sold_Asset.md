# Replacing a Sold Asset with a Purchased Asset

 

If you have purchased a new asset and sold an old one within a defined time period, you can use the value of the new asset to offset the capital gains earned by the sale of the old one. 
 
## Note:

 

Whether or not you can use the purchase price to offset capital gains depends on the period that elapses between the sale and purchase (which is usually determined by law). Click to check that the permitted intervals have been correctly defined. 
 
## To reduce capital gains on an old asset based on the value of a replacement asset:

 

 1 .  Enter the Fixed Assets form and retrieve the newly purchased asset. 
 

 2 .  Click the Purchase tab and specify the asset that was sold in the Old (Replaced) Asset column. The amount of the purchase price used to offset capital gains appears in the Replacement Deduction column, though it may be revised. This amount will also be deducted from the value of the purchased asset when calculated for tax purposes. 
 Note: The number of the current asset appears automatically in the record for the old asset, in the Replaced B y column in the Sale tab.
