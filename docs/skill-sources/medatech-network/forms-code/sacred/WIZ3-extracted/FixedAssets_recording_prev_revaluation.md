# Updating the Revaluation Reserve for Prior Revaluation

 

A fixed asset may have already undergone revaluation before you recorded it into the system. Specifically, this occurs when records from another system are transferred to the Fixed Assets module in Priority . In this case, you must update the balance of the revaluation reserve accordingly. 
 
## To update the revaluation reserve based on prior revaluation of the asset:

 

 1 .  Enter the Depreciation%-Accounting Records form, a sub-level of Fixed Assets . 
 

 2 .  In the Revaluation Reserve column, record the sum that has accumulated in the revaluation reserve as a result of prior revaluation.
 Note for dual-currency users: The balance of the revaluation reserve is recorded in the Revaluation Reserve column in your local currency. The adjusted balance in the second currency is recorded in the adjacent column. 
 
## Note:

 

If the last revaluation was downward (value decreased), the balance of the revaluation reserve should be recorded as a negative number.
