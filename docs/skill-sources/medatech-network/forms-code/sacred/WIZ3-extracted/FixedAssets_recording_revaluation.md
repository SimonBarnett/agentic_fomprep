# Revaluing a Fixed Asset

 

Revaluation is often required to accurately describe the true value of the company's fixed assets. Both the asset account and the accumulated depreciation account are updated to reflect the asset's fair value. 
 
## To record revaluation of a fixed asset:

 

 1 .  Enter the Fixed Assets form and retrieve the asset in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Accts tab and make sure that the correct capital gains account and expense account are defined in the Reval. Reserve Acct and Reval Impairment Acc columns, respectively. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Depr per Period-Acc'ting Records sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  In the From Date column, specify the date on which revaluation was performed. 
 

 5 .  In the To Date column, specify the date on which this depreciation rate ceases to be in effect. 
 

 6 .  In the Valuation column, specify the asset's current value.
 Note for dual-currency users: The exchange rate to be used for the asset's new value is filled in automatically with the rate in effect on the revaluation date, but may be revised. 
 

 7 .  If the asset's depreciation rate has changed, indicate the new rate in the % Annual Deprec. or Depreciation column. 
 

 8 .  Update the Residual Value , if necessary. 
 

 9 .  Return to the upper-level form and select Calculate Depreciation from the list of Direct Activations, specifying the year in which revaluation was performed. 
 

 10 .  To record journal entries for the revaluation, run the Record Asset Trans. in Journal program, specifying Revaluation Only . 
 
## Note:

 

After recording asset revaluation in the ledger for the first time, a decrease in asset value is deducted from the revaluation reserve account until the surplus for that asset is exhausted. Conversely, an increase in value is credited to retained profits to the extent that is recognized as an expense in respect of that same asset.
