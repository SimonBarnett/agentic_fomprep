Demand Forecasts - Preparing a Forecast Manually 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To prepare a demand forecast manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Demand Forecasts form. 
 

 2 .  Indicate the Year of the forecast. The Code of the forecast (prefixed with an F ) is filled in automatically. 
 Note: When you open a forecast order, it will be assigned this code as its order number. 
 

 3 .  Record a Forecast Description . 
 

 4 .  If the forecast is for a specific customer, indicate the customer number in the For Customer column. The forecast order opened for this demand forecast will automatically be recorded for this customer. (If no customer appears here, the forecast order will be opened for the predefined "Forecast" customer.)
 Note: The customer is recorded in the forecast order for informative purposes only, and does not affect MRP. MRP takes forecast orders into account as a general demand for inventory (which can be used to fill sales orders for any customer). 
 

 5 .  The Order Due Date determines the day of the month on which all forecast order items will be due. It is filled in automatically on the basis of the SFcastOrdDay constant. Revise as necessary. 
 Note: The due date of individual forecast order items can be revised manually in the order itself. 
 

 6 .  Enter the sub-level form, Itemized Forecast . 
 

 7 .  On each line, specify the Part Number of the part for which the forecast is being recorded. 
 

 8 .  For each month in which you expect to sell this part, indicate the forecasted quantity. 
 Note: The total quantity (per month and for the entire year) of expected sales of all parts appears in the upper-level form. These totals are only of interest when all parts share the same unit.
