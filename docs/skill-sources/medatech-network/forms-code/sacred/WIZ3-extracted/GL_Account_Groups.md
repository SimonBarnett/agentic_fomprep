# GL_Account_Groups

- **Source:** `GL_Account_Groups.htm`

Open GL Accounts - Assigning Accounts to Account Groups 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To define an account group:

 

 1 .  Enter the Account Groups form. 
 

 2 .  For each group, record a code in the Group column, and a Description . 
 
## To assign accounts to an account group:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and retrieve the desired accounts. 
 

 2 .  For each account, specify the appropriate Group .
