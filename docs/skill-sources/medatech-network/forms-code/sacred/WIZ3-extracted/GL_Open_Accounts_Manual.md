Open GL Accounts - Opening GL Accounts Manually 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To open GL accounts manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 2 .  Fill in the Account No. and Account Description columns. 
 

 3 .  Click the Report tab and fill in the Trial Bal Item and Bal Sheet/P&L Item columns. 
Note: The value 000 appears by default in the Trial Bal Item column, but may be revised. 
 

 4 . Dual-currency users: If relevant, fill in the VAT Report I tem column. 
 
## Note:

 

Click to assign the new accounts to account groups .
