# Updating the Price Index

 

The monthly price index needs to be recorded twice, once for the beginning of the month and once for the end of the month. Example . 
 
## To record the price index:

 

 1 .  Enter the Exchange Rates form and record the first date of the month. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Itemized Rates sub-level form and record the index in the Current Rate column. 
 

 3 .  Repeat the above for the last day of the month.
