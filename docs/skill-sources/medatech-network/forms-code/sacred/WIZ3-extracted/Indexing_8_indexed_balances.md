# Checking Indexed Balances

 

Once adjustments have been made on the basis of the current price index, you can sample account balances and check indexed values for accuracy. You can check opening balances of accounts and/or monthly balances. 
 
## To check indexed opening balances:

 

 1 .  Enter the Fiscal Years - Indexed Balances form and retrieve the desired fiscal year. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Indexed Opening Balances sub-level form.
 Tip: Press Ctrl+F12 to enter the form without retrieving all accounts. Then retrieve those accounts whose balances you wish to view. 
 
## To check indexed monthly balances:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and retrieve the account in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Monthly Bals. YTD (Trans. Date) sub-level form. Indexed values appear in the last form columns.
