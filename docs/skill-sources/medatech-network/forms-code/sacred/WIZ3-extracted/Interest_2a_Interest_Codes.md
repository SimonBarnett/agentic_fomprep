# Defining Types of Finance Charges

 

The 001 finance charge code is predefined. If all customers are charged the same rates, you can use it to define those rates. Or you can define several types of finance charges, each represented by a different code. Each type is distinguished by its credit limit , regular rate and penalty rate. 
 
## To define a finance charge type:

 

 1 .  Enter the Finance Charge Codes form. 
 

 2 .  Record a code and description (or retrieve the predefined 001 code). 
 

 3 .  Enter the Breakdown of Finance Charge sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Specify the date as of which this type of finance charge is in effect. 
 

 5 .  In the Cred Limit From Cust column, specify the ceiling that determines whether the customer will be charged regular rates or penalty rates. 
 

 6 .  Indicate the percentage of annual finance charges and penalty charges in the Rate (%) and Penalty Rate (%) columns, respectively. 
 
## Note:

 

Click to assign finance charge rates to customers .
