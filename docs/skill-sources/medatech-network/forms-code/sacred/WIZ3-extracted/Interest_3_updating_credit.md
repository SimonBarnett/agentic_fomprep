# Updating Finance Charge Rates

 

Occasionally, you may decide to update the rates charged for late payments, as well as the ceiling distinguishing between regular finance charges and penalty rates. Such revisions will affect calculations of finance charges for any customer to which the updated finance charge code has been assigned. That is, finance charges are always calculated in accordance with the rates that are currently in effect. 
 
## To update rates:

 

 1 .  Enter the Finance Charge Codes form and retrieve the type of finance charge in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Breakdown of Finance Charge sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Specify the date as of which the revised rates and/or ceiling go into effect. 
 

 4 .  In the Cred Limit From Cust column, specify the new ceiling that determines whether the customer will be charged regular rates or penalty rates. 
 

 5 .  Indicate the new percentage of annual finance charges and penalty charges in the Rate (%) and Penalty Rate (%) columns, respectively.
