# Billing the Customer for Finance Charges

 
## Before You Begin

 

The amount of finance charges due from a given customer is calculated automatically, based on defined rates. However, the amount actually charged to the customer in the invoice is recorded manually. Therefore, before you record the invoice, it is recommended that you view the amount of finance charges calculated for the customer, as well as any previous finance charges that have already been invoiced. 
 

 ·        Click to do so . 
 
## To bill the customer for finance charges on late payments:

 

 1 .  Enter the Multi-Shipment Invoices form. 
 

 2 .  Record the number of the customer. The customer name appears automatically.
 Note: A temporary Invoice Number (prefixed with a T ) is filled in automatically when you leave the field. It will be replaced by a permanent number when the invoice is finalized. 
 

 3 .  Today's date appears automatically; revise it if necessary. 
 

 4 .  If desired, indicate that this is an invoice for finance charges in the Details column ( References tab).
