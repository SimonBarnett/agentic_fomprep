# Approving Counted Quantities

 

Counted quantities should be approved when: 
 

 ·        They are identical to calculated quantities (no variance). 
 

 ·        They are close to calculated quantities (minor variance). In this case, approval causes the inventory level in the warehouse and bin in question to be adjusted by the difference between counted and calculated quantities. Examples. 
 

You can approve the entire inventory count automatically (if no variance is found in any of the lines of the inventory count document, or if you have the authority to accept such variances) or you can approve each line manually. 
 
# To approve all quantities automatically:

 

 1 .  Enter the Inventory Count form and retrieve the inventory count documents in question (retrieval tips). . 
 

 2 .  For each document, select Approve/Revoke Inventory Count from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To approve each quantity manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Inventory Count form and retrieve the inventory count documents in question. 
 

 2 .  For each document, enter the Itemized Inventory Count sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  For each line you are approving, flag the Approve Counted Qty column. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        There is no need to approve the line if both the calculated and the counted quantities are zero (0). However, if the calculated quantity is not zero, you must approve the line, even if the counted quantity is 0 (otherwise, there will be no adjustment to the inventory balance). 
 

 ·        When you approve a negative quantity, the warehouse balance is adjusted to 0 .
