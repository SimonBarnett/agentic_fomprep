# To itemize the count:

 

 1 .  Enter the Itemized Inventory Count sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Record each Part Number . 
 

 3 .  If you maintain lot or work order numbers, record the number in the Work Order/Lot column. 
 

 4 .  If necessary, change the part's Status . 
 

 5 .  To record the existing quantity of the part in this warehouse (and bin), fill in one of the following columns: 
 

 ·      Counted Qty (Fact.) OR 
 

 ·      Counted Qty-Buy/Sell OR 
 

 ·      Click to record the counted quantity in packing crate units. 
 

 6 .  Flag the Approve Counted Quantity column. 
 

 7 .  If the part is not in the main bin, change the Bin number. 
 

 8 .  In the Cost column, record the value of the inventory. 
 
## Note:

 

If there are more than 1,000 parts, enter the Lines to be Displayed form (another sub-level of the Inventory Count form) after you record the first 1,000. Open a new line for 1,001 to 2,000. Return to the Itemized Inventory Count and continue recording parts. If there are more than 2,000 parts, open a third line, and so on.
