# Checking Inventory Transactions

 

Major variances indicate irregularities in the warehouse. Probably, inventory transactions were recorded inaccurately or not at all. Negative calculated quantities are a definite sign of such a problem. Major variances require further investigation and possibly decisions at the managerial level.
