# Recording a Simple Journal Entry

 

 1 .   Enter the Entry Journal form. 
 

 2 .  Record the Transaction Date . A temporary number with the prefix T is assigned to the entry. 
 

 3 .  In the Transaction column, do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Leave the M that appears automatically. OR 
 

 ·              To use an entry code you created, choose it. 
 

 4 .  Record the debit and credit accounts. 
 

 5 .  Record the sum of the transaction.
 Note for dual-currency users: If the transaction is in the second or any other foreign currency, click the ForeignCurr tab instead and fill in the Sum (C2) or Sum (Trans. Curr) column, respectively, and specify the Currency . The Exchange Rate in effect on the date the transaction was recorded appears automatically, but may be revised.
