 5 .  Enter the Itemized Entry sub-level form and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 6 .  Fill in the Account No. or make sure it is correct. 
 

 7 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If the Debit or Credit amount does not appear , fill it in. 
 

 ·              From the second line on, flag the Amount to Balance column. The system will fill in a Debit or Credit amount that balances the entry. 
 

 Note : You may find the Balance column useful, as it displays the remaining amount needed to balance out the entry. 
 

 8 .   D ual-currency users: If the transaction is completed in a foreign currency, do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If the transaction and account currency are the same, instead of the Debit and Credit columns, use the Debit (Acct Curr) and Credit (Acct Curr) columns in the Sums tab. 
 

 ·              If the transaction currency and the account currency are not the same (e.g., the transaction is completed in U.S. Dollars and the account is in Euros), use the Debit (Trans. Curr) and Credit (Trans. Curr) columns instead. 
 

 9 .  Revise the Due Date and/or Contra Account , as needed. 
 

 10 .  Click the Profit/Cost Ctrs tab, and link the entry item to the appropriate profit or cost centers, one per profit/cost group .
 Note: For more information about profit/cost groups, run the Cost Allocation Wizard . 
 

 11 .  Fill in additional details and references, as needed. 
 

 12 .  Press Page Down to move to the next item in the entry, and repeat these steps.
