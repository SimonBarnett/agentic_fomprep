# To record payment information for a Custom schedule:

 

 1 .  Enter the Custom Amortization Schedule form, sub-level of the Bank Loans form. 
 

 2 .  For each payment or group of payments, fill in the From Payment and To Payment columns. Example. 
 Note: If the line defines only one payment, record the same number in both columns. 
 

 3 .  Record the Amount of Each Payment and the Payment Frequency (applies to both principal and interest payments). 
 

 4 .  Record the type of amortization schedule for each payment or group of payments, (i.e., Equal Payments or Equal Principal ). 
 

 5 .  Return to the upper-level form.
