# To make the payment:

 

 1 .  Run the Redo Schedule for Early Repaymt program. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Bank Loans form and retrieve the desired loan. 
 

 3 .  Make sure the loan status is Active. 
 

 4 .  To view the amortization schedule, enter the Amortization Schedule sub-level form. 
 

 5 .  Run the Loan Payments - Posting Preview program and make sure its results are correct. 
 

 6 .  To record the journal entries, run the Record Loan Payments in Journal program.
 Note: Once the entries have been recorded, a check mark appears in the Paid column of the Amortization Schedule form. If you do not record journal entries in Priority , you can flag the column manually to indicate that you have made the payment. 
 

 7 .  Return to the Bank Loans form and change the loan status to Closed .
