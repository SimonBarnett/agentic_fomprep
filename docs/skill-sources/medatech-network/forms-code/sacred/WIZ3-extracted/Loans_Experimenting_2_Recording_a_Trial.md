# Recording General Information

 

Before recording trial parameters, you need to record some general information. The information does not have to be exact (e.g., you can estimate the date you intend the loan to begin). 
 

 1 .  Enter the Bank Loans form. 
 

 2 .  Record the Date of Loan , Loan Number and Description (e.g., Test of 5% interest). 
 

 3 .  Choose a loan Purpose . The accounts defined for that purpose appear automatically in the Accounts tab. 
 

 4 .  Record the Amount of the loan. 
 

 5 .  In the Add ' l Details tab, specify the Branch to which the asset belongs. If you work with subsidiaries, the accounts defined for the corresponding subsidiary will automatically replace the accounts appearing in the Accounts tab.
