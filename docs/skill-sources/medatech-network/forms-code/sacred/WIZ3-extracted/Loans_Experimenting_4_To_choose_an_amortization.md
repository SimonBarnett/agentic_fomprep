# To choose an amortization schedule:

 

 1 .  Click the Payment Data tab. In the Amortization Sched. column, choose the desired type of schedule. 
 

 2 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Click if you chose the Custom amortization schedule type. OR 
 

 ·      Click if you chose one of the other types.
