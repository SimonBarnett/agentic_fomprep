Bank Loans - Recording Trial Payment Information 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record trial payment information:

 

 1 .  Click the Payment Data tab and record one of the following: 
 

 ·      The amount due per payment (for equal-principal payments, this refers to the amount of principal in each payment) OR 
 

 ·      The number of payments 
 

  Note: If you did not record an interest rate (under the Interest Data tab), you must record both of these parameters. 
 

 2 .  If needed, change the frequency of principal payments and/or the frequency of interest payments.
