# Calculating Payments

 

 1 .  from the list of Direct Activations of the Bank Loans form, select whichever ONE of the following programs is needed: 
 

 ·      Calculate Payment Amount OR 
 

 ·      Calculate Number of Payments OR 
 

 ·      Calculate Interest Rate. 
 

 2 .  Select Prepare Amortization Schedule from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .  To view the amortization schedule, enter the Amortization Schedule sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  To print the amortization schedule, select Amortization Schedule from the list of Direct Activations of the Bank Loans form. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to create another trial loan. 
 

 ·        Click to make adjustments to this trial loan (e.g., change the interest rate). 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
