# Postponing Loan Payments

 

If the bank agrees to “freeze” the loan for a certain period of time, you need to record this and prepare an updated amortization schedule. Example. 
 
## To postpone a payment:

 

 1 .  Enter the Bank Loans form and retrieve the desired loan. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Postponement of Payment Dates sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Record the number of the payment you are postponing and the date until which you are postponing it. 
 

 4 .  If the bank is charging you compound interest during the payment delay period, flag the Compounded Interest column. 
 

 5 .  Return to the Bank Loans form. Select Prepare Amortization Schedule from the list of Direct Activations to produce an updated amortization schedule. 
 

 6 .  Change the status of the loan to Frozen . 
 
## Note:

 

When you are ready to make the postponed payment, change the loan status to Active.
