# To record a new loan:

 

 1 .  Enter the Bank Loans form. 
 

 2 .  Record the Date of Loan , the Loan Number , a Description of the loan, the loan's Purpose and its Amount . 
 

 3 .  The default accounts defined for the designated purpose appear automatically in the Accounts tab; revise as necessary. 
 

 4 .  In the Loan tab, specify the Account Number of the bank account from which you are to repay the loan. 
 

 5 .  Record the Contract Number and any other Reference number for the loan. 
 

 6 .  Specify the Branch to which the loan is to be linked. If you work with subsidiaries, the accounts defined for the corresponding subsidiary will automatically replace those appearing in the Accounts tab. 
 

 7 .  Record any brief Details pertaining to the loan. 
 

 8 .   Dual-currency users: Click if loan payments are linked to the last known index or a foreign currency system . 
 

 9 .  Click the Interest Data tab and do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Record the rate of Fixed Interest OR 
 

 ·      Select a Variable Interest code and Margin .
