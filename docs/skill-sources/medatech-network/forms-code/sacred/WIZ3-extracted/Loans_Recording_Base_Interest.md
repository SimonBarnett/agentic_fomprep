Bank Loans - Recording Changes in the Base Interest Rate 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record changes in the base interest rate: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Exchange Rates form. 
 

 2 .  Record the date as of which the new rate is in effect. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Itemized Rates sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Move to the line containing the desired type of base rate. 
 

 5 .  Record the rate in the Exchange Rate column.
