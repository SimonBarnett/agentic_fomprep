# Recording a Malfunction

 

The process of handling the malfunction begins once a malfunction document is opened. Then, the quality appointee or the person in charge can fill in details about the malfunction (e.g., the malfunctioning part, the place of detection). 
 
## To open a malfunction document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Malfunction Document form. 
 

 2 .  Record the D ate . The M alfunction No. , its S tatus and the Reported by (Name) will be filled in automatically.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Details tab). 
 

 3 .  Click the Details tab and indicate the Part No . 
 

 4 .  If the malfunction is based on a work order, record its number in the Work Order column. 
 

 5 .  Specify the Q uantity of malfunctioning units. 
 

 6 .  In the Where Detected column, record the place where the malfunction was detected.
