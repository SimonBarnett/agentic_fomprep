# Recording the Quality Appointee's Decision

 

Once a malfunction is recorded in the Malfunction Document form, the quality appointee is in charge of reaching a decision about handling the malfunction. 
 
## To record the decision:

 

 1 .   Enter the Malfunction Document form and retrieve the document in question. 
 

 2 .  In the Quality Appointee Decision tab, specify the reason for the malfunction. 
 

 3 .  Indicate the type of malfunction, if desired.
 Note: To describe the overall malfunction, use the Appointee's Description of Malf. sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Indicate the reasons for your decision in the Quality Appointee's Decision sub-level form. 
 

 5 .  Record your decision by selecting the appropriate malfunction Status (e.g., “Send to MRB”, “Vendor Fault”) in the Details tab of the upper-level form.
 Note: If you assign a status that is flagged as Qual Appointee Decis (in the Statuses for Malfunctions form), this status is copied automatically to the Decision column (in the Quality Appointee Decision tab) and your employee ID and name appear in the Quality Appointee column.
