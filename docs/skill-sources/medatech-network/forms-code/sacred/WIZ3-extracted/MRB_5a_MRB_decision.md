- 
 
 
 
## To record the board's decision:

 

 1 .  Make sure the malfunction status in the Malfunction Document form is "Send to MRB", or any other status that is flagged in the Send to MRB column of the Statuses for Malfunctions form. 
 

 2 .  Record a detailed explanation of the board's decision in the Explanation of Board's Decision sub-level form. 
 
## To authorize the decision:

 

 1 .  Make sure the malfunction status in the Malfunction Document form is "Send to MRB", or any other status that is flagged in the Send to MRB column of the Statuses for Malfunctions form. 
 

 2 .  Enter the MRB Authorizations sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Move to the line where your name appears. 
 

 4 .  Flag the Approve column.
