## To assign the malfunction to a review board:

 

 1 .  Enter the Malfunction Document form and retrieve the malfunction document in question. 
 

 2 .  In the Review Board tab, choose the board to which the malfunction is assigned. As a result, today's date appears as the day on which the MRB is convened. Change this date, as necessary. Names of any board members flagged as requiring a signature will appear in the MRB Authorizations sub-level form. 
 
## To process the malfunction:

 

 1 .  Record engineering recommendations in the Engineering Recommendations sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Record specific malfunctions and their resolutions in the Specific Malfs. & Resolutions sub-level form. 
 

 3 . To attach documents related to the malfunction, use the Attachments sub-level form.
 Note: If documents have already been attached to the malfunction document, they appear here automatically. 
 
## To send mail to board members and other interested individuals:

 

 1 .  Make sure all board members to whom mail should be sent are flagged in the Send E-mail column of the Board Members form, sub-level of the Material Review Boards form. If they are not system users, make sure their e-mail addresses are recorded in the Board Members form or the Personnel File form. 
 

 2 .  Return to the Malfunction Document form, and select Send Mail to Board Members from the list of Direct Activations .
