# Defining Scrap

 

Scrap is the percentage of the quantity of a given part that is routinely lost or damaged at some point in the production or supply process. 
 

When defined for a part , scrap represents the percentage of a manufactured part – or a purchased part resold to customers – that is lost during the shipping process. 
 

When defined for a child part , it represents the percentage of a part that is lost when it is joined to a particular parent in a production operation. 
 
# To define scrap for a part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Click the Additional Details tab. 
 

 3 .  In the Scrap column, record the appropriate percentage.
 Notes: 
 

 ·      If the part is manufactured (Type P ), the defined scrap is taken into account when planning work order quantities. The planned issues for these work orders are increased accordingly. 
 

 ·      If the part is purchased for resale (Type R ), without undergoing any production process, the defined scrap is taken into account in the issues plan (i.e., planned issues are increased accordingly). 
 
## To define scrap for a child part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts form and retrieve each parent part to which the part in question is joined. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Child Parts sub-level form, and move to the part in question. 
 

 3 .  In the Scrap (%) column, record the appropriate percentage.
 Note: The defined scrap is taken into account when planning issues for the parent part's work orders (i.e., planned issues are increased accordingly).
