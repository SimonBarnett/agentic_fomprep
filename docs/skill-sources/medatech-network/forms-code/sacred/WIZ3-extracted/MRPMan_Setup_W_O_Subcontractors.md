# Defining Subcontractor Capacity

 

MRP determines which subcontractor to assign to a given work order on the basis of priority and projected production capacity. By ranking your own factory together with the subcontractors, you can set up MRP to assign the overflow from your own plant floor to the next ranking subcontractor; conversely, you can assign to your floor any quantities that could not be produced by a subcontractor. 
 
## To define production capacity:

 

 1 .  Enter the Projected Prod Capacity-Subcontr form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the Year and the Part Number in question. 
 

 3 .  Specify the Subcontractor Number . 
 Tip: To specify your own plant floor, select the predefined Internal subcontractor. 
 

 4 .  Fill in monthly projections of the quantity that the subcontractor (or your own plant floor) is capable of producing. 
 

 5 .  Rank all subcontractors for the same part, using the Priority column. The lower the number, the higher the priority. 
 
## Note:

 

For more information about production by subcontractors, run the Working w/Subcontractors Wizard .
