# To define timing parameters per part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase/MRP Parameters for Part form and select the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Click the MRP Definitions tab and do one of the following: 
 

 ·      If production is managed in batches , flag the Time per Batch column. Then record the batch quantity (in buy/sell units) and the time span required for its production in the Production Quantity and Production Days columns. 
 

 ·      If production is not managed in batches, record the production quantity (in buy/sell units) and time in the Production Quantity and Production Days columns. 
 

 Note: If you record time values manually, this clears the Automatic Prod Time column. These values will not be updated by any parameters later recorded for the operations in the part's production routing. Click to synchronize part and operation parameters . 
 

 3 .  In the Overlap-Parent/Child column, specify the permitted percentage of overlap between the production times of the current part and any of its child parts. MRP will time the work orders according to the recorded percentage. Example. 
 

 4 .  Specify the quantity of part units that can be produced simultaneously (owing to multiple workers/machines) in the No. Workers/Machines column. Explanation.
