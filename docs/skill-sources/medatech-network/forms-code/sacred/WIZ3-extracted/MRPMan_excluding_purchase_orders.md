MRP for Manufacturers - Excluding Purchase Orders from MRP 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To exclude a purchase order from consideration by MRP:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Orders form. 
 

 2 .  Select the desired purchase order. 
 

 3 .  Remove the check mark from the Affects Planning column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To exclude a purchase order that has already been authorized, use the Revisions for Authorized Orders form. 
 

 ·        This setting affects: 
 

 ·      The way MRP handles purchase orders for items that are usually manufactured, but are occasionally purchased from vendors that are not subcontractors. 
 

 ·      The way purchase planning handles orders for purchased goods. 
 

 ·      Production planning
