# Overview: The Principal Functions of MRP

 
## Calculating Part Demand

 

MRP summarizes the actual demand for each processed and purchased part up to a designated date. 
 

You can choose the appropriate method of calculating the required quantities of each part – that is, whether the demand for any given part is static (i.e., stable over a given period of time), or dynamic (i.e., fluctuating according to sales orders and work orders). For those parts with dynamic demand, you can determine how the program aggregates demand when planning production – on a monthly basis, or according to a time range defined for each individual part. Explanation. 
 

 Forecast/stock orders allow you to anticipate future part demand; consequently, they are canceled out by actual sales orders recorded for that period (according to the method defined in the MnthForcast production constant). 
 

When calculating demand dynamically on the basis of work orders that were opened manually, MRP calculates the required quantities of child parts.
