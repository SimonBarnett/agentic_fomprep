# Why Customers Were Loaded More than Once

 

Customers are often loaded into the work area together with their contacts. Those that have multiple contacts are likely to appear more than once (with a different contact on each line). That might happen, for instance, if you ran the Load by Multiple Criteria program and specified “More than 1 Contact.” 
 

This multiple load is very useful when you want to send a mailing to all your customer contacts.
