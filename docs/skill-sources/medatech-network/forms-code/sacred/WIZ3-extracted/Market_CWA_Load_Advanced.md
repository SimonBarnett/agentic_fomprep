# Load by Multiple Criteria

 

You can load the work area by multiple criteria: business classifications, contacts and/or marketing activities. Example. This greatly increases your ability to reach precisely the cross-section of customers you want. 
 
## To load customers:

 

 1 .  Run the Load by Multiple Criteria program. 
 

 2 .   Priority loads the Customer Work Area with all customer records meeting the criteria and reports how many were loaded. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Customer Work Area form to see the customer records.
 Note: The E-mail in New Group column displays the e-mail or fax number as defined in the system .
