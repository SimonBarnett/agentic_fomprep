# Load by Business Classification

 

You can retrieve into the work area customers that have been assigned the same business classifications. Example. You can further restrict the results to those customer contacts who are willing to receive commercial mailings. 
 
## To load customers:

 

 1 .  Run the Load by Business Classification program. The Load by Business Classification form opens displaying the most recent business classifications chosen. 
 

 2 .  Delete any unneeded lines (press Ctrl+Delete ). 
 

 3 .  Choose any additional business classification(s) desired and exit the form. 
 

 4 .  In the input screen that opens, you can further restrict the results to those customer contacts who are willing/not willing to receive commercial mailings or who have not indicated a preference. 
 

 5 .   Priority loads the Customer Work Area and reports how many customer records were loaded. 
 

 6 .  Enter the Customer Work Area form to see the customer records.
 Note: The E-mail in New Group column displays the e-mail or fax number as defined in the system .
