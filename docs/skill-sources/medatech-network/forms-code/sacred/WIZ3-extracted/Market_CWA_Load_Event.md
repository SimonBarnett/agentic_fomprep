# Load by Marketing Event or Activity

 

You can load into the work area customers and customer contacts that participated in the same marketing events or activities. You can further restrict the results to those customer contacts who are willing to receive commercial mailings. 
 
## To load customers:

 

 1 .  Run the Load by Marketing Event/Activity program. The Marketing Events & Activities form opens. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired events or activities and exit the form. 
 

 3 .  A Customers form opens. To further limit the customer records loaded, click Yes . Otherwise, leave No and skip to Step 5 . 
 

 4 .  Retrieve the desired customers (e.g., by customer group) and exit the form. Only those customers appearing in this form, and participating in the events selected in Step 2 , will be loaded. 
 

 5 .  In the input screen that opens, you can further restrict the results to those customer contacts who are willing/not willing to receive commercial mailings or who have not indicated a preference. 
 

 6 .   Priority loads the Customer Work Area and reports how many customer records were loaded. 
 

 7 .  Enter the Customer Work Area form to see the customer records.
 Note: The E-mail in New Group column displays the e-mail or fax number as defined in the system .
