# Load by Contacts

 

You can load into the work area customers with specific contacts (e.g., people with a specific title, a certain type of contact). Example. Customers are loaded per contact. In other words, if a customer has two contacts that meet the specified criteria, that customer will be loaded twice, once for each contact. 
 
## To load customers:

 

 1 .  Run the Load by Contacts program. The Contacts form opens. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired records by position code and/or contact type. How? 
 

 3 .  Exit the form. Priority loads the Customer Work Area with all the customer records having this type of contact, once per person, and reports how many were loaded. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Customer Work Area form to see the customer records.
 Note: The E-mail in New Group column displays the e-mail or fax number as defined in the system .
