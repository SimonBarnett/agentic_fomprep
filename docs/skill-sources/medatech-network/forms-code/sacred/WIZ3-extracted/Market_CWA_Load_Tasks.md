# Load by Task

 

You can load into the work area those customers assigned to specific tasks, together with their marketing contacts. You can further restrict the results to those customer contacts who are willing to receive commercial mailings. 
 
## Note:

 

The contact that is loaded into the work area is always the customer's marketing contact (as defined in the Customer Contacts form, sub-level of Tasks ), regardless of the contact appearing in the selected task. 
 
## To load customers:

 

 1 .  Run the Load Marketing People by Task program. The Tasks form opens. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired tasks and exit the form. 
 

 3 .  In the input screen that opens, you can further restrict the results to those customer contacts who are willing/not willing to receive commercial mailings or who have not indicated a preference. 
 

 4 .   Priority loads the Customer Work Area with records of any customers with defined marketing contacts for which tasks were selected and that meet the specified mailing criteria, and reports how many records were loaded. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Customer Work Area form to see the customer records.
 Note: The E-mail in New Group column displays the e-mail or fax number as defined in the system .
