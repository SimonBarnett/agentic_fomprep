# Using the Customer Work Area

 

The Customer Work Area acts as a temporary storage facility for customer records and their contacts, based on a variety of retrieval criteria. Once records are loaded in the work area, you can: 
 

 ·              Record the same task for a group of customers 
 

 ·              Synchronize contacts with MS-Outlook 
 

 ·              Create groups of contacts 
 

 ·              Prepare mailing labels 
 

 ·              Assign business classifications 
 

 ·              Record participants in marketing events 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To take full advantage of the power of the work area, make sure that your customer records have business classifications, contacts and/or marketing events and activities recorded. Make sure that you have also defined whether each customer contact has consented to receive commercial mailings. 
 

 ·        You can also use the work area to maintain customer records (e.g., assign prices and discounts). This may be more convenient than using the Customers form, as you can retrieve a batch of records and “freeze” the results until you are finished working with them.
