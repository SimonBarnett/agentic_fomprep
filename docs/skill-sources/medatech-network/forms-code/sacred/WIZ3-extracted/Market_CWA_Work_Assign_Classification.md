# To assign business classifications:

 

 1 .  Make sure the classification has been defined in the Business Classification Codes form. 
 

 2 .  Run the Assign Business Classif to Custs program. The Customer Work Area opens. 
 

 3 .  Retrieve the records for those customers you want to assign the business classifications to and exit the form. How? 
 

 4 .  In the input screen that appears, choose the business classification to assign to the customers. Priority reports how many customers were assigned the classification. 
 

 5 .  To view the classification and all customers to which it is assigned, enter the Business Classification Codes form and its sub-level.
