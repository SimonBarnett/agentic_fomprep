# Recording Marketing Events

 

 1 .  Make sure the event has been recorded in the Marketing Events & Activities form. 
 

 2 .  Run the Record Marketing Event for Custs program. The Customer Work Area opens. 
 

 3 .  Retrieve the customers you want to record marketing events for and exit the form. How? 
 

 4 .  In the input screen that appears, choose the marketing event or activity to assign to the customers. Priority reports how many customers were assigned to the event. 
 

 5 .  To view the marketing event and all customers assigned to it, enter the Marketing Events & Activities form and its sub-level.
