# Preparing a Group

 
## Before You Begin

 

Enter the Customer Work Area form. For each customer and contact, the E-mail in New Group column displays the e-mail address or fax number to which messages will be sent whenever mail is sent to the group you are about to prepare. 
 

Make sure that this e-mail address or fax number is correct. If it is not, use the Customers form or its sub-level, Customer Contacts , to revise it. Explanation . 
 

 1 .  Run the Prepare Group from Work Area program. 
 

 2 .  Record a name and description for the new group. The Customer Work Area opens, displaying all the customers and contacts that have been loaded into the work area. 
 

 3 .  If you wish, you can narrow down the records to be included in the group. How? 
 

 4 .  Exit the form. Priority reports how many records were added to the new group. 
 

 5 .  To view the group, enter the Groups form. 
 
## Tip for advanced users:

 

You can also use this program to add the customers and contacts in the work area to an existing group. Record the name of that group in Step 2 .
