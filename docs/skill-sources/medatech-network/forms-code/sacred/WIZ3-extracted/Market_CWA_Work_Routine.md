# Routine Work With Customers

 

You can use the work area and its sub-levels to perform routine work for customers whose records have been loaded. Even though the records loaded in the work area are stored in a temporary database, any information you record or revise in the sub-level forms automatically affects the permanent customer database. 
 
## Note:

 

If the work area is empty, click to load it. 
 
## To use the work area for routine work with customers:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Work Area form and move to the desired customer record. 
 

 2 .  Enter the appropriate sub-level form (e.g., Part Discount ) and record or update any relevant information.
