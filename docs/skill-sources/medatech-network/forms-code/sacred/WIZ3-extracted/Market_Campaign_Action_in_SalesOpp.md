# Opening Activities for Sales Opportunities

 

You can open a variety of activities (e.g., tasks, price quotes, sales orders), and link them to a sales opportunity. As these activities progress, so too does the sales opportunity. 
 

Depending on how the campaign is set up, tasks may also be opened automatically for each sales opportunity in the campaign once the opportunity reaches a designated status. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Open a task for a sales opportunity 
 

 Open a price quote for a sales opportunity 
 

 Open a sales order for a sales opportunity
