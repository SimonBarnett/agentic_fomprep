# To open a sales order for a sales opportunity:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the appropriate Customer Number . 
 

 3 .  Click the References and select the current Sales Opportunity Number from among the list of opportunities opened for this customer in past marketing campaigns. The Order Items sub-level form will automatically list all line items in this opportunity.
 Note: The business rules defined for sales order statuses determine the status the sales opportunity will receive as work on the order progresses. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Order Items sub-level form to check the items recorded for the order and revise them as necessary. 
 
## Tip:

 

For more information about opening sales orders, run the Sales Order Wizard .
