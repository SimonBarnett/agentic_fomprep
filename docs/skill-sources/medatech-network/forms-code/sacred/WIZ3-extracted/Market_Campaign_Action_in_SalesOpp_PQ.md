# Opening a Price Quote for a Sales Opportunity

 

When creating a price quotation from a sales opportunity, you can choose between two options: 
 

 ·        Open the price quote from within the sales opportunity. 
 

 ·        Open a quote and link it to the sales opportunity. 
 

In both cases, all line items in the sales opportunity are copied to the quote, but may be revised. 
 
# To open a quote from within a sales opportunity: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Opportunities form and retrieve the sales opportunity in question.
 Note: Make sure parts have been defined for the sales opportunity. 
 

 2 .  Select Open Price Quote from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .  Open the Price Quotations for Customers form and retrieve the new quote. Check it and revise as necessary. 
 
# To copy the sales opportunity to the quote:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quotations for Customers form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the appropriate Customer Number . The customer's name, today's date and a quote number are filled in automatically. 
 

 3 .  In the References tab, make sure the No Auto Copy column is blank and select the desired Sales Opportunity Number from among the list of opportunities opened for this customer in past marketing campaigns. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Itemized Quotation sub-level form to check the items recorded for the quote and revise them as necessary. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To open a quote linked to the sales opportunity without copying all line items in the sales opportunity into the quote, flag the No Auto Copy column in the Price Quotations for Customers form. 
 

 ·        The business rules defined for price quote statuses determine the status the sales opportunity receives each time the status of the quote changes. 
 

 ·        For more information about price quotes, run the Price Quotations Wizard .
