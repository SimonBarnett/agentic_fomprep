# Running a Marketing Campaign

 

 Priority allows you to plan, track and manage marketing campaigns (e.g., advertising campaigns, direct marketing promotions). Marketing campaigns are built on sales opportunities involving specific potential customers. Every sales opportunity is linked to activities (e.g., tasks, price quotations, sales orders) whose success or failure determine the opportunity's status and estimated monetary value. The cumulative value of all sales opportunities provides an indicator of the general progress of the entire campaign. 
 

You can maintain multiple marketing campaigns, targeting different customer categories with a variety of products and media, or you can assign multiple sales opportunities to the default "General" campaign. 
 

Alternatively, you can work with individual sales opportunities by defining a general marketing campaign comprising all of your customers and products.
