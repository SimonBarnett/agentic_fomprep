# Opening a Marketing Campaign

 

Marketing campaigns consist of a number of sales opportunities, each of which is assigned a status that represents a certain chance of success (that is, whenever a sales opportunity advances to a new status, its odds of closure increase accordingly). 
 

You can also define a set of tasks that are linked to the progress of individual sales opportunities in a given marketing campaign. Such tasks are opened automatically for each sales opportunity in the campaign once the opportunity reaches a designated status, and are assigned to the employee who is currently responsible for the sales opportunity in question. 
 
# To open a marketing campaign:

 

 1 .  Enter the Marketing Campaigns form. 
 

 2 .  Designate the Campaign Code and the Campaign Description . 
 

 3 .  Record a Start Date and End Date for the campaign. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Employees Involved in Campaign sub-level of the Marketing Campaigns form, and record the user name of any employee that will be working on this campaign. Aside from its informative purpose, this also facilitates linkage of a task recorded by this employee to the campaign. Details. 
 

 5 .  Click to define a set of tasks for sales opportunities in the campaign.
