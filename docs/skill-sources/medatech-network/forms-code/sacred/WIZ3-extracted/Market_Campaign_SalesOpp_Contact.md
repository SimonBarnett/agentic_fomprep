# To open a sales opportunity for a customer contact or lead:

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              For a contact, enter the Contacts form and retrieve the contact.
 Note: If the contact is associated with several customers, make sure you are on the line for the right one. 
 

 ·              For a lead, enter the Leads form and retrieve the lead.
 Note: The lead must be linked to a regular or potential customer. 
 

 2 .  Select Open Sales Opportunity from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .  Choose the appropriate Campaign Code . If you also want to open an initial task for the sales opportunity, flag the Open Task column as well. 
 

 4 .  The program will return a message displaying the number of the new sales opportunity. Make a note of the number. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Sales Opportunities form and retrieve the new record. The customer's marketing contact, sales rep and additional sales rep are filled in automatically. 
 

 6 .  Click the Details tab and specify the Estimated Days to Close . The Projected Close Date is automatically calculated accordingly. 
 

 7 .  In the Launch Date column, specify the date as of which you forecast that invoices will be sent to the customer, in the event that the opportunity is closed. 
 

 8 .  If relevant, you can specify the source of this opportunity in the Source of Opp column. 
 

 9 .  If desired, click the Add'l Details tab and assign the opportunity a Sales Opp Type and/or Add'l Sales Opp Type . 
 

 10 .  Click the Prices tab. If you want prices to be taken from a particular price list, specify the Price List Code . To restrict the items that can be included in the opportunity to parts that appear in this price list, flag the PriceList Parts Only column. 
 

 11 .  Click the Last Task tab and indicate the relative Urgency of this sales opportunity.
 Note: The Next Contact Date is updated automatically, based on the contact frequency defined for opportunities with this degree of urgency and the date of the last recorded task, unless a later date has already been recorded in this column. In any case, it may be revised. 
 

 12 .  Enter the Parts in Sales Opportunity sub-level form. Record each part for which the sales opportunity has been created (the parts being sold), as well as its Quantity . If you set the price list for this sales opportunity, prices will be taken from there but may be revised.
 Note: The Projected Revenue from the potential sale of the part is calculated automatically, by multiplying the Unit Price by the Quantity . 
 

 13 .  Return to the upper-level form. Click the Prices tab to check the total Projected Revenue (the sum for all parts) and Weighted Revenue (based on the odds of closing defined for the opportunity's current status). You can also record the Manager's Estimate of the monetary value of the potential sale. 
 

 14 .  If necessary, transfer handling of the sales opportunity to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To open sales opportunities for a group of contacts/leads, run the Open Sales Opportunity program instead. 
 

 ·        You can view changes to the sales opportunity in the History of Changes sub-level form. 
 

 ·        Once the customer expresses intentions to take advantage of the sales opportunity, flag the Customer Committed column in the Sales Opportunities form.
