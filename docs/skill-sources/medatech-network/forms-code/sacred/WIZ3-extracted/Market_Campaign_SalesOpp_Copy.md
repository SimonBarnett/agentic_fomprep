- 
 
 
 
# To copy an existing sales opportunity:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Opportunities form and retrieve the sales opportunity that you want to copy. 
 

 2 .  Select Copy Sales Opportunity from the list of Direct Activations . When the program is completed, a message appears with the number of the new entry. 
 

 3 .  Retrieve the new sales opportunity in the Sales Opportunities form. 
 

 4 .  Click the Details tab and specify the Estimated Days to Close . The Projected Close Date is automatically calculated accordingly. 
 

 5 .  Click the Prices tab and make sure that the correct price list appears. To restrict the items that can be included in the opportunity to parts that appear in this price list, flag the PriceList Parts Only column. 
 

 6 .  Make any necessary revisions, and then enter the Parts in Sales Opportunity sub-level form. 
 

 7 .  Check the list of items copied from the original sales opportunity. Delete any unnecessary lines. 
 

 8 .  For each part, check the offered Quantity and Unit Price . Revise if necessary. 
 Note: The Projected Revenue from the potential sale of the part is calculated automatically, by multiplying the Unit Price by the Quantity . 
 

 9 .  Return to the upper-level form. Click the Prices tab to check the total Projected Revenue (the sum for all parts) and Weighted Revenue (based on the odds of closing defined for the opportunity's current status). You can also record the Manager's Estimate of the monetary value of the potential sale, if desired. 
 

 10 .  If needed, enter the Attachments sub-level form and attach any relevant files to the sales opportunity. 
 

 11 .  If necessary, update the opportunity's Status . You can also transfer handling of the sales opportunity to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can view changes to the sales opportunity in the History of Changes sub-level form. 
 

 ·        Once the customer expresses intentions to take advantage of the sales opportunity, flag the Customer Committed column in the Sales Opportunities form.
