# To open a sales opportunity for a customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the desired customer.
 Note: You can also open a sales opportunity in the Sales Opportunities form, or in the Sales Opportunities sub-level of the Marketing Campaigns form. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Sales Opportunities for Customer sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Choose the appropriate Campaign Code . A Sales Oppor . Number is automatically assigned to the sales opportunity, based on the pattern defined in the Document Numeration Templates form, as is the initial sales opportunity S tatus . The customer's marketing contact (defined in the Customer Contacts form), sales rep and additional sales rep are also filled in, but may be revised. 
 

 4 .  Press F6 from the Sales Oppor. Number column to enter the Sales Opportunities form. 
 

 5 .  Click the Details tab and specify the Estimated Days to Close . The Projected Close Date is automatically calculated accordingly. 
 

 6 .  In the Launch Date column, specify the date as of which you forecast that invoices will be sent to the customer, in the event that the opportunity is closed. 
 

 7 .  If relevant, you can specify the source of this opportunity in the Source of Opp column. 
 

 8 .  If desired, click the Add'l Details tab and assign the opportunity a Sales Opp Type and/or Add'l Sales Opp Type . 
 

 9 .  Click the Prices tab. If you want prices to be taken from a particular price list, specify the Price List Code . To restrict the items that can be included in the opportunity to parts that appear in this price list, flag the PriceList Parts Only column. 
 

 10 .  Click the Last Task tab and indicate the relative Urgency of this sales opportunity.
 Note: The Next Contact Date is updated automatically, based on the contact frequency defined for opportunities with this degree of urgency and the date of the last recorded task, unless a later date has already been recorded in this column. In any case, it may be revised. 
 

 11 .  Enter the Parts in Sales Opportunity sub-level form. Record each part for which the sales opportunity has been created (the parts being sold), as well as its Quantity . If you set the price list for this sales opportunity, prices will be taken from there but may be revised.
 Note: The Projected Revenue from the potential sale of the part is calculated automatically, by multiplying the Unit Price by the Quantity . 
 

 12 .  Return to the upper-level form. Click the Prices tab to check the total Projected Revenue (the sum for all parts) and Adjusted Revenue (based on the odds of closing defined for the opportunity's current status). You can also record the Manager's Estimate of the monetary value of the potential sale. 
 

 13 .  If necessary, transfer handling of the sales opportunity to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can view changes to the sales opportunity in the History of Changes sub-level form. 
 

 ·        Once the customer expresses intentions to take advantage of the sales opportunity, flag the Customer Committed column in the Sales Opportunities form.
