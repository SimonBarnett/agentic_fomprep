# Opening Sales Opportunities for a Campaign

 

A sales opportunity consists of one or more contacts with a potential customer within a marketing campaign. 
 

After opening a sales opportunity you can link marketing activities to it. You can also open a sales opportunity for a customer contact. Alternatively, you can copy an existing sales opportunity and then revise it as needed. 
 

Once a sales opportunity is open, you need to record preliminary information, such as projected revenue and projected end dates. 
 

Depending on how the campaign is set up, tasks may also be opened automatically for each sales opportunity in the campaign once the opportunity reaches a designated status. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Open a sales opportunity for a customer 
 

 Open a sales opportunity for a customer contact or lead 
 

 Copy an existing sales opportunity
