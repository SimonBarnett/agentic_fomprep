# Setting Up Sales Opportunity Types

 

You can classify sales opportunities by type and use additional sales opportunity types to further categorize the potential sale. Example. 
 
## To define opportunity types:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Opportunity Types form. 
 

 2 .  For each type you wish to define, record a brief description. 
 Tip: To restrict user access to documents recorded for sales opportunities of specific types, run the Data Authorization Wizard . 
 
## To define additional sales opportunity types:

 

 1 .  Enter the Add'l Sales Opportunity Types form. 
 

 2 .  For each additional sales opportunity type you wish to define, record a brief description. 
 
## Note: 

 

You can add sales opportunity types and additional types later, as needed.
