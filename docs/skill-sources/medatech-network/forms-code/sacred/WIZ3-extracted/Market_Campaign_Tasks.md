# To define a check list of tasks for sales opportunities in the campaign:

 

 1 .  Enter the parallel sub-level form, Check List for Sales Opps . 
 

 2 .  Designate the Task Code and the Task Type of the task to be opened for sales opportunities in this campaign. 
 

 3 .  Record a Subject for the task. 
 

 4 .  In the Sales Opp Status column, select a status. Whenever a sales opportunity in this campaign is assigned that status, the current task will be opened for it.
 Note: You can record the same opportunity status for multiple tasks. 
 

 5 .  In the Task Status column, specify the initial status to be assigned to the new task. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Tasks that are opened in this manner will appear in the Tasks form and in the To Do Item sub-level of the linked sales opportunity. 
 

 ·        Click to open sales opportunities for the campaign. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the Campaign menu.
