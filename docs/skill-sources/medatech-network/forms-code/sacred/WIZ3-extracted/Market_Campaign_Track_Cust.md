# To track customer progress:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Opportunities form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the customer(s) or campaign(s) in question. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        The Status column reflects the customer's progress in this campaign. 
 

 ·        A check mark in the Customer Committed column ( Add'l Details tab) indicates that the customer has committed to the sales opportunity in question. 
 

 ·        The Projected Close Qtr column ( Add'l Details tab) displays the quarter in which the sale to this customer (in the framework of the current campaign) is expected to close. This is based on the Projected Close Date (in the Details tab). 
 

 ·        The Weighted Revenue column ( Price s tab) is a function of the sales revenue expected from this customer and the chances of closing the deal at the current campaign stage. It reflects how worthwhile it is to continue pursuit of this sale. 
 

 ·        The Last Task column ( Last Task tab) displays the task number of the last task completed for this customer and sales opportunity. Press F6 to access the task itself. 
 

 ·        The Urgency column ( Last Task tab) displays the relative urgency of this sales opportunity. The Next Contact Date column displays the date of the next scheduled contact with the customer with regard to this campaign. This is filled in automatically, based on the contact frequency defined for opportunities with this degree of urgency and the date of the Last Task , but may be revised. 
 

 ·        To print a sales opportunity document, select Print Sales Opportunity Info from the list of Direct Activations .
