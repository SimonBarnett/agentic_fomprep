# Creating Direct Contacts

 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Direct contacts can be imported from MS-Outlook. Click to run the interface . 
 

 ·        When recording a contact in Priority , always designate the last name first (e.g., Smith, John). 
 
## To link a contact to a customer:

 

 1 .  Open the Customers form and retrieve the customer in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Customer Contacts sub-level form and record the name of the contact. Add any other available data (e.g., phone numbers, e-mail address). 
 

 3 .  In the Consents to Rcv Mail column, indicate whether the contact has consented to receive commercial mailings. 
 

 4 .  Flag the relevant contact types, if any. For example, if this person is the contact for price quotations, flag the Price Quote Contact column.
