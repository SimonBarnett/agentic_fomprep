## To link a contact to a vendor:

 

 1 .  Open the Vendors form and retrieve the vendor in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Vendor Contacts sub-level form and record the name of the contact, phone numbers, e-mail address and the like. 
 

 3 .  If this individual is the contact for specific types of documents (e.g., RFQs, purchase orders), flag the appropriate column. 
 
## To view direct contacts:

 

 ·        Retrieve them in the Contacts form.
