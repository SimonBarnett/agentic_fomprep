# Maintaining Customer (and Vendor) Contacts

 

Customer and vendor records essentially represent companies. Individuals in those companies, with whom you do business or otherwise interact, are considered contacts. 
 

There are two types of customer contacts: those directly associated with the customer and those indirectly associated (from other companies, such as consultants). In contrast, vendor contacts are always directly associated with the vendor. 
 
## Direct Contacts

 

All contacts directly linked to a customer or vendor can be viewed in a single form: Contacts . This form is also used to store data for contacts that are not associated with any customer or vendor, such as new leads. 
 
## Networked Contacts

 

You can create and view a network of relations between your customers and their contacts. The network is built for a single customer (Customer A), and includes all other customers (companies) and their contacts related to Customer A. This is useful when some of your customers are involved in a business deal you are conducting with another customer. Networked contacts appear together with direct contacts in the Choose list when selecting a customer contact (e.g., for a task, an order).
