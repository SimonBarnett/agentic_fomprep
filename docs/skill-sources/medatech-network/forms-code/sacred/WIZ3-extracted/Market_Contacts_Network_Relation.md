# Defining Types and Areas of Relations Between Customers

 

When including a customer/contact in a given network, you also have the option of specifying the type of relation involved and the area of relation. Example. 
 
## To create a list of types of relations:

 

 ·        Enter the Types of Relations (Networks) form and record types of relations. 
 Tip: Use the sub-level Networked Customers per Type form to view all customers which share the type of relation. 
 
## To create a list of areas of relations:

 

 ·        Enter the Areas of Relations (Networks) and record areas of relations. 
 Tip: Use the sub-level Networked Customers per Area form to view all customers which share the area of relation. 
 
## To assign these types to a networked contact:

 

 ·        Return to the External Contacts form and, for the contact in question, specify the Type of Relation and/or Area of Relation .
