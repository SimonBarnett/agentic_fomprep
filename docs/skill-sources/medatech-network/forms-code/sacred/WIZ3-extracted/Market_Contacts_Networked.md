# Creating Networked Contacts

 

You can add as many customers to a network as you want, along with any of their contacts. 
 
## Before You Begin

 

 ·              Make sure that both customers you are about to connect are recorded in the system.
 Tip: If you do not intend to open orders or an A/R account for the customer you are adding to the network, open a potential customer. For details, see the Open Customers Wizard . 
 

 ·              Make sure that the networked contact is in the list of direct contacts for the networked customer. Click to record a direct contact. 
 
## To create networked contacts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the customer for which you are building the network. 
 

 2 .  Enter the External Contacts sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Choose the customer to be added to the network (e.g., a consulting firm). 
 

 4 .  Choose the customer's contact. This should be the person who is the usual contact with the customer in the upper-level form.
 Note: To create two networked contacts from the same customer, open a second line in the External Contacts form.
