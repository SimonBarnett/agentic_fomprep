## To view the networks in which a contact is included:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the customer in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Customer Contacts sub-level form and move to the contact in question. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Who Networked With sub-level form. All the customers with which this contact is networked appear. 
 
## To view the networks in which a customer is included:

 

 ·        Return to the Customers upper-level form and enter the Who Networked With sub-level form. All the customers with which this customer is networked appear, regardless of the networked contact.
