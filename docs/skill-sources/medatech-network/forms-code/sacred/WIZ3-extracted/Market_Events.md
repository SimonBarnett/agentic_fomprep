# Marketing Events and Activities

 

 Priority offers a log of marketing events and activities held by your company. Example . This list of events and activities can help you track your customers' participation. You can also use this information to decide on the type of customer or market niche to focus on in future activities. 
 
## To record a marketing event or activity:

 

 1 .  Enter the Marketing Events & Activities form. 
 

 2 .  Record a code and description for the event. 
 

 3 .  In the Date column, record the date the activity took place. This helps you distinguish between two or more activities of the same type. 
 

 4 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Enter the Customers Who Participated sub-level form to list the customers and their contacts who participated in the event. OR 
 

 ·              Use the Customer Work Area to record an event for many participants in a batch. That is, after loading the work area with the desired customers, run the Record Marketing Event for Custs program. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can view the marketing events and activities attended by a customer in the List of Marketing Events form (sub-level of the Customers form). 
 

 ·        Click for full instructions on using the work area.
