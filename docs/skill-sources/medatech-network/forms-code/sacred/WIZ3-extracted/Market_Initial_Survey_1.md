# The Initial Survey

 

The Initial Survey provides a quick and easy way for you to assign business classifications automatically to new customers. It is especially useful for recording information acquired during the first contact with your customer, typically a telemarketing questionnaire. The initial survey is comprised of questions and multiple-choice answers generated by the business parameters you choose to include. 
 

When you record a new customer in Priority , the initial survey automatically includes the designated parameters. The Business Classifications form (a sub-level of the Customers form) is filled in automatically by the recorded answers.
