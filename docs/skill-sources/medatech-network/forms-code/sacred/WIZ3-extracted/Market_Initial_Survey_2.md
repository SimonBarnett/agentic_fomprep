CRM - Initial Surveys: Recording 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To include a business parameter in the initial survey:

 

 1 .  Enter the Business Classific. Parameters form. 
 

 2 .  In the Incl in Init Survey column, flag each parameter that you want to include in the Initial Survey. 
 
## To fill in the Initial Survey:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the new customer. 
 

 2 .  In the Initial Survey Results sub-level form, select the customer's answer to each question (i.e., the classification within each parameter) in the Classification Desc column.
