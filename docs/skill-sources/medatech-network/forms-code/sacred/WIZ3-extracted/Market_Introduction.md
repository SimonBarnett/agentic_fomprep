CRM - Introduction.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A facility for interfacing with Microsoft Outlook allows you to synchronize calendar appointments, contacts and to-do items (tasks) between the two systems, as well as to automatically open tasks (or service calls) based on incoming customer/vendor messages. An additional mechanism stores all new contacts imported from Outlook, as leads. These can then be assigned to existing customers or used to open new potential customers. 
 

The Customer Work Area acts as a temporary storage facility for customer records and their contacts, based on a variety of retrieval criteria (e.g., marketing contacts, participation in marketing events). Once records are loaded in the work area, you can perform various activities for these customers and/or contacts in a batch. 
 

 Note: For more information regarding sales reps and commission transactions, run the Sales Reps Wizard .
