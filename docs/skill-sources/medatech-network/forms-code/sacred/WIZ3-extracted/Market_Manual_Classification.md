# To assign business classifications manually:

 

 ·        For customers, use the Business Classifications sub-level of the Customers form. 
 

 ·        For customer contacts, enter the Customer Contacts sub-level of the Customers form, then its own sub-level, Business Classifications . 
 

 ·        For vendors, use the Business Classifications sub-level of the Vendors form. 
 
## Note:

 

To assign classifications automatically in a batch, click to use the Customer Work Area.
