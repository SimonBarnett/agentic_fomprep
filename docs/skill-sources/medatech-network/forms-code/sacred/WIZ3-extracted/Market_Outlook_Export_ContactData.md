# Exporting Data to Outlook

 

Based on the data in Priority , you can update existing contacts or tasks in Outlook and add new contacts or tasks. 
 
## To add or update all direct contacts of a specific customer or vendor:

 

 1 .  Retrieve the customer/vendor in the Customers or Vendors form, respectively. 
 

 2 .  Select Sync Contacts With Gmail/Outlook from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To add or update a single contact:

 

 1 .  Retrieve the Priority contact in one of the following forms: 
 

 ·              Customer Contacts , a sub-level of Customers 
 

 ·              Vendor Contacts , a sub-level of Vendors 
 

 ·              Contacts . 
 

 2 .  Select Sync Contacts With Gmail/Outlook from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To add or update a batch of contacts via the Customer Work Area:

 

 ·        After loading the work area with the desired contacts, run the Sync Contacts With Gmail/Outlook program.
 Note: Click for detailed instructions on using the work area. 
 
## To add or update selected tasks:

 

 ·        Run the Load CRM into MS-Outlook program. In the form that opens, retrieve the tasks you want to load into Outlook, then exit the form.
