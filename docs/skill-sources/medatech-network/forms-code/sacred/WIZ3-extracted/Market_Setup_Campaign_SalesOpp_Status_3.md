# To link opportunity status to price quotations:

 

 1 .  Enter the Statuses for Price Quotations form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the desired status and enter the Additional Status Definitions sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  In the Sales Opp Status column, select the status to be assigned automatically to a sales opportunity when a price quotation linked to the opportunity receives the status in the upper-level form. 
 
## To link opportunity status to sales orders:

 

 1 .  Enter the Statuses for Orders form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the desired status and enter the Additional Status Definitions sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  In the Sales Opp Status column, select the status to be assigned automatically to a sales opportunity when a sales order linked to the opportunity receives the status in the upper-level form. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        To define new statuses, run the BPM Flow Chart-Sales Opps program. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the Campaign Setup menu.
