# Setting Up the Odds of Closing Sales Opportunities

 

The adjusted monetary value of a sales opportunity is determined by the anticipated revenue to be generated by the opportunity, if it succeeds in ripening into a deal, and by the odds of closure defined for its current status. 
 
## To define odds of closure:

 

 1 .  Enter the Statuses for Sales Opportunities form. 
 

 2 .  For each status, enter the More Definitions sub-level form and specify, in percentages, the odds that a sales opportunity that has reached that status will close successfully.
