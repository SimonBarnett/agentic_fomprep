# Setting Up Customer Contact Positions

 

You can record a list of positions by which to identify the contacts associated with your prospective and current customers. You can also assign a priority to each position. 
 

For customers that have more than one contact, that with the highest priority becomes the priority contact for that customer. Using the Customer Work Area, you can create a group of priority contacts, open a task for priority contacts or add such contacts to MS-Outlook. 
 

Assign the highest priority to the position you want to receive your marketing information (e.g., VP for Purchasing). Example . 
 
## To record a list of positions:

 

 1 .  Enter the Job Positions form. 
 

 2 .  Record a code, description and, if desired, a priority for each job position. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        In the Priority column, the lower the number, the higher the position's priority. However, “0” is the lowest possible priority. 
 

 ·        You can add more positions and change priorities later, as needed. 
 

 ·        Positions recorded in this form are also used to define jobs in the Human Resources module.
