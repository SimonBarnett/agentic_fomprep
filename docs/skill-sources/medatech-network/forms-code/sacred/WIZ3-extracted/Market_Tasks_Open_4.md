 11 .  If the task is an appointment involving other company employees, click the Participants tab and choose all required employees. Priority will update the calendar of each of these workers accordingly, and will send them e-mail to remind them of the appointment.
 Note: If additional customer (or vendor) contacts are to participate, choose them in the Additional Contacts for Task sub-level form. 
 

 12 .  If the task is an appointment involving members of one or more groups, click the Participants tab and choose the group(s).
 Note: You must be authorized to access the group. If you are not, contact your system manager. 
 

 13 .  If an appointment requires certain resources (e.g., projector), click the Resources tab. You can select up to three resources that will be reserved for that appointment.
 Tip: To view the projected usage of a given resource and/or to add resources to the Choose list, use the Resources form and its sub-level, Planned Resource Utilization . 
 

 14 .  If desired, assign a Priority to the task. This is useful for sorting tasks.
