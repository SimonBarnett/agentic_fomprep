# Working With Leads 

 

When the synchronization program imports a new contact from Outlook, it adds that person and his/her contact data to the Contacts form and opens a lead. You can then: 
 

 ·        Link the lead to an existing customer 
 

 ·        Open a new potential customer for the lead 
 

 ·        Make the lead inactive (if no business is anticipated). 
 
## To work with leads:

 

 1 .  Open the Leads form and retrieve all records. In this form and its sub-levels, make any needed additions or corrections.
 Tip: To update the contact in Outlook after making revisions, select Sync Contacts With Gmail/Outlook from the list of Direct Activations .
