CRM - Outlook: Working With Leads.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 2 .  Do one of the following in the Leads form: 
 

 ·              To link a lead to an existing customer, specify the Customer (in the General tab). The lead will be included as a contact for that customer. 
 

 ·              To open a new customer, record a customer name in the Company column; then select Open Potential Customer from the list of Direct Activations . A potential customer will be opened with the designated name and the lead will be added to the list of customer contacts. 
 

 ·              To define the lead as inactive, assign the appropriate status in the Status of Lead column (in the Follow Up tab). 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To open a sales opportunity for a lead that has been assigned to a customer, select Open Sales Opportunity from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 ·        To print data on a lead, select Print Lead Data from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 ·        For information on potential customers and how to make them into regular customers, run the Open Customers Wizard . 
 

 ·        Click to return to the Outlook menu.
