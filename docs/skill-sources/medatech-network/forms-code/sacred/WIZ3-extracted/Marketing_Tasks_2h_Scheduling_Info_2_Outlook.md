# To define an e-mail address for a customer or vendor contact:

 

 1 .  Enter the Contacts form and retrieve the desired customer or vendor contact. 
 

 2 .  Click the Company tab and check that an e-mail address has been defined for this contact. If not, record it now. 
 
## To send appointment information to Outlook:

 

 1 .  Enter the Tasks form and retrieve the desired task/appointment. 
 

 2 .  Select Send Outlook Msg to Participants from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        The e-mail message that is created is not limited to use with Outlook, but may also be viewed and uploaded into other scheduling programs. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the Tasks and Appointments menu. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the Outlook menu.
