# Production Departments

 

Company departments may have already been defined and assigned employees in the Human Resources module. For example, all of the factory workers may have been assigned to the “Production Department.” However, factory modeling requires a more precise division of the manufacturing process, into sections consisting of several work cells that host operations serving the same general purpose. Example. In such a case, your human resource manager needs to define new departments and reassign the relevant employees. 
 
## To define a department:

 

 1 .  Enter the Departments form. 
 

 2 .  For each new department, specify a code and description. 
 

 3 .  Assign a work schedule to the department by selecting the appropriate Work Pattern .
 Tip: Click to define a new work pattern. 
 

 4 .  Fine-tune the selected work pattern by entering the Department Exceptions sub-level form and assigning the appropriate work exceptions.
 Tip: Click to define a new work exception. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        Click to reassign an employee to a production department . To view the employees that belong to each department, use the Department Employees sub-level of the Departments form. 
 

 ·        Click to define work cells for each department.
