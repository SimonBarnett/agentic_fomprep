Modeling - Introduction.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Several operations of universal application are predefined in the system, together with their corresponding routings and work cells. Click to see a chart. In addition to these, you can define an unlimited number of new modeling entities to suit your needs. 
 

Due to the hierarchical nature of this data, you should begin by defining your production departments, followed by the work cells that belong to them, then the operations performed in the work cells, and finally the routings to which the operations belong. 
 

Before you begin, you will need to define the work calendar by which operations are assigned to the departments and their cells. The work calendar consists of work patterns and their exceptions (e.g., maintenance breaks, extra shifts), and is taken into account during MRP and production planning. 
 

Finally, there are various setups an individual part must undergo prior to manufacture, such as defining a bill of materials (BOM) and byproducts. General parameters, such as part units and part families, as well as the addition of new parts to the part catalogue, are covered in the Part Definition Wizard .
