# Defining Scrap as a Part Parameter

 

Scrap is the percentage of a part that is lost either during production or during shipment to a customer. You can define scrap as a child part parameter (i.e., the percentage lost whenever the part is joined to any parent), or as a parent part parameter (i.e., the percentage lost of any child joined to the parent). When defined for a processed part, scrap refers to the percentage of parts that are usually lost or damaged during shipment to a customer. 
 

 Note: You can also define scrap as an operation processing parameter that affects all parts on which an operation is performed. For instructions, see the "Define an Operation" section. 
 
## To define scrap for a child part (production) or a processed part (shipment):

 

 1 .   Enter the Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .   Click the Additional Details tab. 
 

 3 .   In the Scrap column, record the appropriate percentage. 
 
## To define scrap as a function of a specific parent:

 

 1 .   Enter the Parts form and retrieve the parent part to which the part in question is joined. 
 

 2 .   Enter the Child Parts sub-level form, and move to the part in question. 
 

 3 .   In the Scrap (%) column, record the appropriate percentage.
