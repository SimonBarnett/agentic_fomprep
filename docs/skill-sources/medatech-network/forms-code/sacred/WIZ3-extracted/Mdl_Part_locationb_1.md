# Child Part Designations

 

Child part designations are informative in nature; included in the printed work order, they instruct the worker where to join a specific child part to its parent, such as resistors on a circuit board, or memory chips on a motherboard. 
 

You can set a constant that compels the system to check the designations you record, and compare their quantity against the calculated quantity of child parts, as determined by the child-parent ratio in the bill of materials. Any discrepancies result in a warning message. 
 
## To set the ChkDesignatn constant for automatic checking:

 

 1 .   Enter the Planning Constants form and move to the ChkDesignatn constant. 
 

 2 .   Change the value of the constant to 1.
