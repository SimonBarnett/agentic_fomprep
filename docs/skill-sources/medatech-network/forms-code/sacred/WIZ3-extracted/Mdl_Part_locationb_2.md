# To record designations:

 

 1 .   Enter the Parts form and retrieve the desired parent part. 
 

 2 .   Enter the Child Part Designations sub-level form, and open a line for each child part that has designations. 
 

 3 .   The method of designation depends on the required quantity of each child part per parent (i.e., the child/parent ratio): 
 

 ·      If only one unit of the child part is required, record its location in the Designation column. The number 1 will appear automatically in the Quantity column. Example. 
 

 ·      If more than one unit of a child part is defined for each parent, use the Designation and To Designation columns to define the range of designations for that child part. The total number of units of each child part will appear automatically in the Quantity column. Example. 
 

 4 .   You can use the X, Y and Z Coordinate columns to specify the precise location of a single child part on a grid. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        If the ChkDesignatn constant is set to 1 , you can flag the Designation Control column in the Parts form to prevent the authorization of a part revision or BOM revision in which there is a discrepancy between the quantities of child parts and designations. 
 

 ·        In addition to the work order printout, the following reports also display child part designations: 
 

 ·      Single-level BOM w/Designations and 
 

 ·      Designat'n of Components in Assm , a Direct Activation from the work order. 
 

 ·      Click to return to the main screen.
