# Defining Parts for Production 

 

Parts are defined in the Part Catalogue form, where they are classified into three types: P (processed parts), R (purchased parts – usually raw materials) and O (others, including various services), assigned part units and specifications, and assigned to part families. This process is described in the Part Definition Wizard . 
 

The following section consists of instructions for those definitions and setups that apply solely to processed parts, such as part routings, production parameter values and child part designations. A special topic addresses the subject of byproducts , in which a unique type of operation produces inventory of multiple parts from a single source. 
 

 Note: Instructions pertaining to the bill of materials (BOM) appear both in the current wizard and in the Part Definition Wizard .
