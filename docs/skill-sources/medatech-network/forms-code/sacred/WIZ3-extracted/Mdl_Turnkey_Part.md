# Creating a Turnkey Part

 

When a part that you used to process is now solely being purchased, you can change it into a turnkey part. Such a part is then treated like a raw material. 
 
## To make a P part into a turnkey part:

 

 1 .   Enter the Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .   Click the General tab and change its routing to Assemb . 
 

 3 .   Flag the Turnkey column.
