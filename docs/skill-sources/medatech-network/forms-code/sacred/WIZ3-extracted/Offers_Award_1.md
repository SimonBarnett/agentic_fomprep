# Awarding Customer Benefits

 

Offer benefits are awarded by opening sales orders and/or credit memos for a closed special offer (depending on the benefit defined for each eligible customer). Once a special offer has been closed and benefits have been calculated for eligible customers, you can change the benefits to be awarded to specific customers. 
 
## Before you begin:

 

 1 .  Make sure all of the relevant special offers are closed. 
 

 2 .  Adjust the offer benefit for all relevant customers in the Customers Participating in Offer sub-level of the Special Offers form or the Special Offers for Cust sub-level of the Customers form. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Special Offerstlch form and change the status of all closed offers to Payment Authorized .
