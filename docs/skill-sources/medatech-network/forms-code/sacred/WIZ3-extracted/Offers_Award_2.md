# To award customer benefits for a single special offer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Special Offers form and retrieve the desired offer. 
 

 2 .  Select Prepare Credit/Order for Offer from the list of Direct Activation s .
 Note: Only the user assigned to the offer can run this program. 
 
## To award customer benefits for multiple special offers:

 

 ·        Run the Prepare Credit/Order for Offer program. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Eligible customers may choose not to order the full amount of parts to which they are entitled. Once the orders have been opened, you can revise their quantities manually, in consultation with your customers. 
 

 ·        You can change the offer status to Paid when you are satisfied that all customer benefits have been awarded. 
 

 ·        Click to view the sales orders and credit memos opened for eligible customers. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
