# Closing a Special Offer

 

In addition to closing the offer, the closing program also calculates the benefits to be awarded to the eligible customers. This calculation is based on customer invoices opened during the defined offer period, which concludes on the offer's End Date . It is therefore not possible to run the closing program before that date. 
 
## To close a single special offer:

 

 1 .  Retrieve the desired offer in the Special Offers form. 
 

 2 .  Select Close Special Offer from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 
## To close multiple special offers:

 

 ·        Run the Close Special Offers program. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Closed offers automatically receive the Closed status. 
 

 ·        Closed offers can be reopened by Direct Activation or from the menu. 
 

 ·        Click to award customer benefits to the eligible customers.
