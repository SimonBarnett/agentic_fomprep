- 
 
 
 
# To define the offer benefit as a monetary credit:

 

 1 .  Click the Offer Details tab, and do one of the following: 
 

 ·      If the offer benefit is a fixed monetary credit, specify the amount in the Credit Sum column. 
 

 ·      If the offer benefit is a percentage of the customer purchase, specify the percentage in the Credit (%) column. 
 

The Credit/Part Offered columns are filled in automatically with the predefined “Credit” part. 
 

 2 .  Put the offer into effect by changing its status to In Effect .
 Note: Once an offer is in effect, you cannot change its definitions (i.e., target parts, benefits).
