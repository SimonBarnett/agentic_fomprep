# Defining a Billing Customer for Special Offers

 

Customers that are assigned a given billing customer for offers can also be included in any special offers defined for the billing customer. This is useful, for example, in the case of a head office (the billing customer) and various branches. Eligibility for the offer will be determined by the total purchases of all attached customers. 
 
# To define a billing customer for special offers:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the desired billing customer. 
 

 2 .   Click the Customer tab, and flag the Billing Cust-Offers column.
