# Linking Customers to a Special Offer

 

Linkage to a special offer entitles a customer to participate in that offer. Special provisions can be made for specific customers, such as changing the offer priority, which can be done at any time, or changing the benefit to be awarded, which should only be done after the offer is closed. This is because the closing program calculates the benefits to be awarded to each customer on the basis of the general offer definitions – any revisions that you make beforehand will be erased. 
 

When you link a billing customer for offers to a special offer, eligibility for the offer is determined by examining the total purchases of all customers that have been assigned that billing customer. 
 
## To link customers to a single special offer:

 

 1 .  Retrieve the desired offer in the Special Offers form. 
 

 2 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Select Link Offer to Customers from the list of Direct Activations and specify the customer(s) in question. 
 

 ·      Enter the Customers Participating in Offer sub-level form and open a new line for each customer.
 Note: If the Incl. Attached Custs column is flagged, this indicates that the designated customer is defined as a billing customer for offers (in the Financ. Parameters for Customers form). 
 
## To link customers to multiple special offers:

 

 ·        Run the Link Offer to Customers program. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can also link customers to special offers in the Special Offers for Cust sub-level of the Customers form. 
 

 ·        You can change the priority of a special offer for a specific customer, or change the benefit to be awarded, in the Customers Participating in Offer sub-level of the offer, or in the Special Offers for Cust sub-level of the customer. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
