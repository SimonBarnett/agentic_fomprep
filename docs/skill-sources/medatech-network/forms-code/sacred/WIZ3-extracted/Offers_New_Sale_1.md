# To open a special offer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Special Offers form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the offer to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 2 .  Record a name for the special offer in the Offer Description column. The Offer Code will appear automatically. 
 

 3 .  Define the Priority of this offer in relation to other offers that involve the same target parts. The higher the priority, the lower the number.
 Notes: 
 

 ·      You can also define priority differently for specific customers, in the Customers Participating in Offer sub-level form, which will override the value designated here. 
 

 ·      Customers that do not meet the requirements of a high-priority offer can still be eligible for a lower-priority offer. 
 

 4 .  In the Target Details tab, define a Start Date and End Date .
 Notes: 
 

 ·      These dates determine which customer invoices are taken into account when determining customer eligibility for the offer benefits; they do not determine when the offer is put into effect or closed. 
 

 ·      Both dates must fall within the same calendar year.
