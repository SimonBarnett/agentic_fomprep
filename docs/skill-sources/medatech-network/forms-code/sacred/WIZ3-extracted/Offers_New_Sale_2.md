 5 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      If the offer is based on the quantity of parts purchased, specify the Target Quantity . Customers who purchase this quantity of the target part during the period defined by the start and end dates will be eligible to receive the offer benefits. 
 

 ·      If the offer is based on the monetary value of parts purchased, specify the Target Sum . Customers who purchase target parts of this value during the period defined by the start and end dates will be eligible to receive the offer benefits. 
 

 6 .  Enter the Offer Items sub-level form. 
 

 7 .  Open a line for each target of the special offer, specifying either a Family or a Part Number . 
 Note: If multiple lines are recorded, the target is defined as the total quantity/sum of purchases from among the specified parts and/or part families. 
 

 8 .  Return to the upper-level form and do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Click to define the offer benefit as a quantity of parts that can be purchased at a reduced price. 
 

 ·      Click to define the offer benefit as a monetary credit , either a fixed sum or a percentage of the customer's total purchase of the target parts. 
 

 Notes: 
 

 ·      Customers that purchase complete multiples of the target quantity or sum are eligible to receive corresponding multiples of the offer benefit. Example. 
 

 ·      After the offer is closed, you can revise the benefits awarded to specific customers.
