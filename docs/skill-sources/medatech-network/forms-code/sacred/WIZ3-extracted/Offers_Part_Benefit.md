- 
 
 
 
# To define the offer benefit as a part:

 

 1 .  Click the Offer Details tab, and specify a part in the Credit/Part Offered column. 
 

 2 .  In the Offer Quantity column, specify the quantity of parts an eligible customer is entitled to purchase. 
 

 3 .  In the Unit Price column, specify the special price at which an eligible customer is entitled to purchase the designated quantity of parts.
 Tip: If the offered part is to be awarded for free, leave the preset value of 0.00 . 
 

 4 .  Put the offer into effect by changing its status to In Effect .
 Note: Once an offer is in effect, you cannot change its definitions (i.e., target parts, benefits).
