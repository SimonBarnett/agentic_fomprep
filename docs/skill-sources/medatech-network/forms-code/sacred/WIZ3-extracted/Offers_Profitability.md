 Analyzing the Profitability of a Special Offer
 

The Offer Profitability report calculates the net profitability of a special offer in the following manner: 
 

 ·        If the offer benefit is in parts, the cost of the awarded parts recorded in each sales order is subtracted from their price to the customer. The sum total of these results represents the offer's profit. Example. 
 

 ·        If the offer benefit is in credit, the sum total of all awarded credit represents the offer's profit, represented as a negative amount. 
 

 ·        If the offer combines both benefits, the total credit is subtracted from the total profit from the sales orders, and the result represents the offer profit. 
 
## To analyze the profitability of a single special offer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Special Offers form and retrieve the desired offer. 
 

 2 .  Select Offer Profitability from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 
## To analyze the profitability of multiple special offers:

 

 ·        Run the Offer Profitability report.
