# Tracking the Progress of a Special Offer

 

Special offer progress is measured by comparing the actual sales of target parts to the participating customers with the target quantity or sum defined for the offer. 
 
## To view sales data for one or more offers and customers:

 

 ·        Run the Special Offer Details report. 
 
## To view sales data for all offers to which a specific customer is linked:

 

 ·        Enter the Special Offers for Cust sub-level of the Customers form. 
 
## To view the invoice lines for target parts recorded for all participating customers:

 

 ·        Enter the Invoice Items Linked to Offer sub-level of the Special Offers form. 
 
## To view the invoice lines for target parts recorded for all offers to which a specific customer is linked:

 

 ·        Enter the Invoice Items Linked to Offer sub-level of the Special Offers for Cust sub-level form. 
 
## Note:

 

Click to remove customers from one or more special offers.
