# Removing Customers from a Special Offer

 

Customers can be removed manually from a single special offer, or automatically in batch, from multiple offers. 
 
## To remove customers from a single special offer:

 

 1 .  Retrieve the desired offer in the Special Offers form. 
 

 2 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Select Remove Customers from Offer from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 

 ·      Enter the Customers Participating in Offer sub-level form and delete the lines for the customers in question. 
 
## To remove customers from multiple special offers:

 

 ·        Run the Remove Customers from Offer program. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can also remove customers from special offers in the Special Offers for Cust sub-level of the Customers form. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
