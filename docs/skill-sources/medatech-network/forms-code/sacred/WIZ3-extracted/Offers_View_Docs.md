# Viewing the Orders and Credit Memos Opened for Eligible Customers

 

Sales orders opened for a special offer display the quantity of parts each eligible customer is entitled to order at a reduced price. You can change the actual quantity to be ordered in consultation with your customers. 
 
## To view the orders and credit memos opened for a single special offer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Special Offers form and retrieve the desired offer. 
 

 2 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Enter the Special Offer Orders and Credit Memos sub-level forms. 
 

 ·      Select Special Offer Details from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 
## To view the orders and credit memos opened for multiple special offers:

 

 ·        Run the Special Offer Details report. 
 
## To view the orders and credit memos opened for a specific customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Special Offers for Cust sub-level form. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can change the offer status to Paid when you are satisfied that all customer benefits have been awarded. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
