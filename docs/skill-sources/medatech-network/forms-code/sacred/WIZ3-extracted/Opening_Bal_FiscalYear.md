Opening Balances - Opening the Fiscal Year 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To open the fiscal years:

 

 1 .  Enter the Define Fiscal Years form. 
 

 2 .  In the Fiscal Year column, record the previous year. 
 

 3 .  Record the Start Date and End Date of that year. 
 

 4 .  The Prefix for Entry No. column displays a two-character prefix that will be attached to all journal entries posted during this fiscal year. The last two digits of the year (e.g., 02 ) appear automatically, but may be replaced. 
 

 5 .  Repeat these steps for the current fiscal year.
