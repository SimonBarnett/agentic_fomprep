# To record separate entries for financial transactions requiring reconciliation:

 

 1 .  Enter the Entry Journal form. 
 

 2 .  In the Transact. Date column, record December 31 of the previous year. A temporary Entry No. (with the prefix T ) appears automatically. 
 

 3 .  In the Transaction column, choose OPN (Opening Balance). 
 

 4 .  Enter the Itemized Entry sub-level form.
 Tip: Move to Full-Record Display Mode for easier data entry (use the Page Up and Page Down keys to navigate between lines). 
 

 5 .  Record each financial transaction in a separate line. For each transaction, record the accounts involved, the sum, the reference number and the due date.
 Notes: 
 

 ·              Remember to use the Opening Balance account (e.g., for a customer invoice, credit the Opening Balance account and debit the A/R account). 
 

 ·              Make sure to record the exact due date of the financial transaction, as it will determine when sums are transferred to the target account (for example, in the case of prepaid expenses) or reconciled against other transactions (for example, in the case of an invoice and a receipt). 
 

 Notes to dual-currency users: 
 

 ·              If the balance is in the second currency (e.g., dollars), click the Sums tab and fill in the debit and credit columns that are designated for that currency (instead of the regular sum columns). 
 

 ·              If the balance is in a (third) foreign currency, click the Sums tab and fill in the Debit (Trans. Curr) and Credit (Trans. Curr) columns (instead of the regular sum columns).
