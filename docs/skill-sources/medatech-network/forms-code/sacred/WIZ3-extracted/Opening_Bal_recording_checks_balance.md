# Recording the Balance of a Postdated Account (Incoming or Outgoing)

 

You need to record the balance of each postdated account separately. That is, record one journal entry for the incoming postdated account and one for the outgoing postdated account. 
 
## To record the balance of a postdated account:

 

 1 .  Enter the Entry Journal form. 
 

 2 .  In the Transact. Date column, record December 31 of the previous year. A temporary Entry No. (with the prefix T ) appears automatically. 
 

 3 .  In the Transaction column, choose OPN (Opening Balance). 
 

 4 .  Enter the Itemized Entry sub-level form.
 Tip: Move to Full-Record Display Mode for easier data entry (use the Page Up and Page Down keys to navigate between lines). 
 

 5 .  For the incoming postdated account, record the details of all postdated checks: the amount, the reference number, and the check date (in the Due Date column of the General tab).
 Notes: 
 

 ·              Make sure to record the exact due date of the postdated check, as it is used by the Cash Postdated Checks program to transfer the sum to the current account. 
 

 ·              If the balance is in the second currency (e.g., dollars), click the Sums tab and fill in the debit column that is designated for that currency (instead of the regular debit column). 
 

 ·              If the balance is in a (third) foreign currency, click the Sums tab and fill in the Debit (Trans. Curr) column (instead of the regular debit column). 
 

 6 .  Open a new line for the Opening Balance account. Record the sum total of the previously recorded postdated checks in the Credit column. 
 

 7 .  Return to the upper-level form and select Post Journal Entry from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 8 .  Follow the same steps for the outgoing postdated account, this time filling in the Credit column for the checks and the Debit column for the Opening Balance account. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to record a single balance per account for financial transactions requiring reconciliation. 
 

 ·        Click to record separate journal entries for financial transactions requiring reconciliation. 
 

 ·        Click to transfer opening balances.
