## Using Predefined Warehouses

 

You can adapt any of the system's three predefined warehouses to your needs by changing the warehouse name, address or other details. 
 
## To customize a predefined warehouse:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouses form. 
 

 2 .  If desired, change the name of the warehouse in the Warehouse and Description columns.
 Note to dual-currency users: If you are working in a language other than English and intend to produce inventory documents in English, record an English description of the warehouse in the Descrip.-Lang2 column. 
 

 3 .  All warehouses are automatically flagged for inventory valuation and transaction costing. If necessary, revise the flags in the Inventory Valued and/or Transaction Costing columns (in the General tab). 
 

 4 .  If the warehouse mainly contains inventory that is available for general sale (e.g., not packed for shipment to a specific customer), flag the Sales Warehouse column. 
 

 5 .  Click the Address and Phone tab, and record the address of the warehouse in the appropriate columns. This address appears on printouts of warehouse transfers, purchase orders and the like.
 Note to dual-currency users: If you are working in a language other than English and intend to produce inventory documents in English, click the Address (Lang 2) tab and fill in the warehouse address and city in English.
