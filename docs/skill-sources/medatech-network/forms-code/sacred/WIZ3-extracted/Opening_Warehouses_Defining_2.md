## To define a new warehouse:

 

 1 .  In the Warehouses form, fill in the Warehouse and Description columns.
 Note to dual-currency users: If you are working in a language other than English and intend to produce inventory documents in English, record an English description of the warehouse in the Descrip.-Lang2 column. 
 

 2 .   Fill in the Type (D/F/W) column. Choose one of these options: 
 

 ·      F - Plant floor warehouse (i.e., made up of work cells). 
 

 ·      D - Contains inventory that is considered during production planning, MRP and purchase planning. 
 

 ·      W - Contains finished products that are not available for current sales purposes (e.g., a remote factory outlet). This inventory is not taken into account during production planning, MRP or purchase planning. 
 

 ·      Tip: In a non-manufacturing setting, almost all warehouses are of type D . 
 

 3 .  All warehouses are automatically flagged for inventory valuation and transaction costing. If necessary, revise the flags in the Inventory Valued and/or Transaction Costing columns (in the General tab). 
 

 4 .  If the warehouse mainly contains inventory that is available for general sale (e.g., not packed for shipment to a specific customer), flag the Sales Warehouse column.
