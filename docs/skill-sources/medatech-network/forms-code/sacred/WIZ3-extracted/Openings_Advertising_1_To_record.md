# To record advertisements placed for a job: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Ads for Job sub-level form and record the date of the advertisement. 
 

 2 .  Choose the newspaper or other medium where you are placing the ad. 
Note: You can record new media in the Newspapers and Other Media form. 
 

 3 .  Record the number of ads placed. 
 

 4 .  In the Documentation (File) column, you can attach any documentation associated with the ad. 
 

 5 .  Return to the upper-level form and change the job status (e.g., to Recruiting).
