# Comparing Applicants for the Job 

 

There are several ways you can compare applicants for a job. You can view any of the following, in various sub-level forms of the Jobs form: 
 

 ·        A list of qualified applicants, in the Current Job Applicants form 
 

 ·        A list of "final" applicants for all jobs in the same position and department, including only those who have passed the interview and are still in the running for the job, in the Interviews Passed for Same Job form 
 

 ·        Tests given to applicants, in the Tests Taken by Job Applicants form 
 

 ·        Any salary offers made to applicants, in the Salary Offers form 
 

 ·        Interviews held with applicants for the same position, including interviews for other jobs, in the Interviews Completed for SameJob form, a sub-level of the List of Applicants form 
 

 ·        Interviews held with applicants for this job only, in the Interviews of Job Applicants form.
