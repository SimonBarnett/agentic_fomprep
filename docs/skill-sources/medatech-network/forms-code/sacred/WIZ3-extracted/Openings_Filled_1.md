# To record the filling of a position: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Jobs form and retrieve the desired job. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Current Job Applicants sub-level form. All applicants with the “Applying” status are displayed. 
 

 3 .  Move to the record of the applicant that you want to hire for the current job and change the Status to “Hired”.
 Notes: 
 

 ·              If this is the first applicant hired for this job, the Filled column in the upper-level form ( Position tab) is automatically flagged and the Status changes to “Filled”. 
 

 ·              In the Hiring Data tab, the Filled Qty and Filled % columns are automatically updated according to the Percent of Full-Time defined in the applicant record of the person hired. 
 

 4 .  To fill a position with more than one employee, change the status of each of the desired candidates to “Hired”. 
 

 5 .  To convert a hired job candidate to an employee and to record employee information, click to run the Employee Wizard .
