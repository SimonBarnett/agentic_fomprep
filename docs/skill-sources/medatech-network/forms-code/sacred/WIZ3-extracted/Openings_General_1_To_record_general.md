# To define a new job:

 

 1 .  Enter the Jobs form and record the Date . 
 

 2 .  A job number appears automatically. It may be revised, as needed.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the job to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 3 .  In the Job Desc. column, record a title for the new job (e.g., Sales Manager, C++ Programmer). 
 

 4 .  Choose the desired position code. The description will appear automatically. 
 

 5 .  Select the company in which the job is being offered. 
 

 6 .  Fill in any other available information about the position, such as the department involved and the team leader of that department. 
 

 7 .  Choose the appropriate Boss ' s Job . The description of the boss's job is filled in automatically.
 Note: Assigning bosses' jobs creates an organizational hierarchy that can be displayed in the Jobs Explorer . 
 

 8 .  Select the Branch in which the hired applicant will be placed. 
 

 9 .  Click the Add ' l Details tab and record all relevant data. 
 

 10 .  Click the Hiring Data tab and specify the Date Needed . 
 

 11 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              In the Position Qty column, specify the number of employees needed to fill this position. 
 

 ·              In the Position (%) column, specify the percentage of hours defined for the current position, where 100% represents one full-time employee. 
 

 12 .  Enter the Job Description sub-level form and record an extended description, as necessary. 
 

 13 .  You can attach any files associated with the job in the Attachments sub-level form.
