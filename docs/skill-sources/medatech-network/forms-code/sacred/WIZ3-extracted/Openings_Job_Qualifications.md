# Selecting the Best Applicant for the Job 

 

 Priority provides a mechanism for displaying the most eligible applicants for a given job, according to their qualifications. 
 
## To view qualified applicants:

 

 1 .  Enter the Jobs form and select the desired job. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Qualified Applicants for Job sub-level form. Candidates and employees with a status defined as part of the applicant pool, and whose qualifications meet the job requirements (as defined in the parallel Qualifications Required for Job sub-level form), appear automatically.
 Tip: If you have not yet defined the qualifications of job applicants, run the Job Applicants Wizard . 
 

 3 .  Flag the Add to Applic. List for each candidate or employee that you want to consider for the job. When you leave the form, a record with the “Applying” status is opened for each applicant in both the List of Applicants and Current Job Applicants sub-level forms.
 Note: You can also link applicants to the job manually in the List of Applicants sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Enter the List of Applicants sub-level form. 
 

 5 .  Specify the scope of the current position in the Percent of Full-Time column.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the job applicant to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column.
