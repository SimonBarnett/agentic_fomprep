## Notes:

 

 ·        The profit on the entire quote appears in the Actual Profit and Profit (%) columns of the upper-level form. 
 

 ·        The profit on individual line items appears in the Actual Profit column of the Itemized Quotation sub-level form, while the Profit (%) column displays the percentage of profit on the individual item. The percentage of profit can be calculated in one of two ways: the amount of profit divided by the part cost, or the amount of profit divided by the extended price. This is determined by the SOrdProfit constant (in the Logistic Constants form).
