# Recording a Sale Price

 

 1 .  Prepare the price quote (in the Price Quotations for Customers form), including parts, quantities, prices and discounts (in the Itemized Quotation sub-level form).
 Note: Click to create a customized bill of materials for a quoted item. 
 

 2 .  Check the accuracy of the sales rep and any discounts awarded, as these affect the final price. 
 

 3 .  View the total profit for the quote in the Actual Profit and Profit (%) columns of the upper-level form. To view profit per line item, access the same columns in the Itemized Quotation sub-level form. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        The Part Price Options form (sub-level of the Itemized Quotation form) displays a list of all prices in effect for the part as of the date of the price quotation. This can help you determine the price to quote. 
 

 ·        The percentage of profit can be calculated in one of two ways: the amount of profit divided by the part cost, or the amount of profit divided by the extended price. This is determined by the SOrdProfit constant (in the Logistic Constants form). 
 

 ·        Click to run the Price Quotations Wizard .
