# Calculating Profitability While Recording a Sales Order

 

 Priority automatically calculates profit both for the order as a whole and for individual ordered items. 
 
## To view profit in a sales order:

 

 1 .  Prepare the order (in the Sales Orders form), including parts, quantities, prices and discounts (in the Order Items sub-level form).
 Note: Click to create a customized bill of materials for an order item. 
 

 2 .  Check the accuracy of the sales rep and any discounts awarded, as these affect the final price. 
 

 3 .  The Profit column (of the upper-level form) displays the amount of profit made on the sale, while the Profit (%) column displays the percentage of profit. 
 

 4 .  The Profit column of the Order Items sub-level form displays the profit on the individual order item, while the Profit (%) column displays the percentage of profit on the individual item. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        The percentage of profit can be calculated in one of two ways: the amount of profit divided by part costs, or the amount of profit divided by the price after discount. This is determined by the SOrdProfit constant (in the Logistic Constants form). 
 

 ·        Click to run the Sales Order Wizard .
