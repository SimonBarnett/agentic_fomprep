# Closing an Outgoing Shipping Voucher

 

After you have finished recording and finalizing the invoices for parts and expenses in the voucher, you need to close it. Closing the voucher triggers the final calculation of burden distribution, and allows you to analyze the profitability of each customer invoice in the relevant sales reports. 
 

If you receive additional expense invoices after the voucher is closed, you will need to reopen it, add the new expense items, and close it again. 
 
## To close an outgoing shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the desired voucher. 
 

 2 .  Select Close Shipping Voucher from the list of Direct Activations . The document status will change to a final status and the Closed column (in the References tab) will be flagged automatically. 
 
## Note:

 

Click to view the results of expense distribution in customer invoice profitability reports.
