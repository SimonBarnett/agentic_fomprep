# Linking Customer Invoice Items to an Outgoing Shipping Voucher 

 

You can include individual items in a customer invoice in several different outgoing shipping vouchers. Besides sold parts, customer invoices can also include expense items (signified by a negative sum). 
 
## To add an individual invoice item to a voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Cust Invs for Outgoing Shipmts form and retrieve the desired invoice.
 Tip: Retrieve by Invoice Type and Custome r Number . 
 

 2 .  Enter the Invoice Items sub-level form. Each invoice item appears on a separate line. 
 

 3 .  Move to the sold item or expense item you want to include in the voucher, and record the voucher number in the Shipping Voucher column.
 Tip: Press F6 from that column to enter the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the relevant voucher. Press F8 to leave the form and automatically copy the voucher number. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To view all sold items included in an outgoing shipping voucher, use the Additional Parts sub-level of the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 ·        To view all expense items included in an outgoing shipping voucher, use the Additional Expenses sub-level of the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 ·        Click to manually distribute any expense items among the sold parts in the voucher .
