# To link customer invoices to an outgoing shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Cust Invs for Outgoing Shipmts form and retrieve the invoices for the sold parts that you want to include in an outgoing shipping voucher. 
 

 2 .  For each invoice, specify the appropriate voucher number in the Outgo Ship Voucher column.
 Tip: Press F6 from that column to enter the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the relevant voucher. Press F8 to leave the form and automatically copy the voucher number. 
 
## Notes:

 

You can view all customer invoices that have been linked to a given outgoing shipping voucher in the Customer Invoices sub-level of the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form.
