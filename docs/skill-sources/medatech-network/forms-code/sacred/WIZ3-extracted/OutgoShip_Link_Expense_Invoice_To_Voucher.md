Outgoing Shipping Vouchers - Linking Expense Invoices to a Voucher 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To link an expense invoice to an outgoing shipping voucher while recording the invoice:

 

 1 .  Record and itemize the invoice, then click the References tab. 
 

 2 .  Select C in the Ship. Voucher Type column. 
 

 3 .  Select the desired voucher from the Choose list of the Shipping Voucher column. 
 
## To link an existing expense invoice to an outgoing shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Retrieve the invoice in the Purch Invs for Outgoing Shipmts form. 
 

 2 .  Record the voucher number in the Shipping Voucher column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can link a purchase invoice that contains goods as well as expenses. 
 

 ·        You can view all expense invoices that have been linked to a given outgoing shipping voucher in the Purchase Invoices sub-level of the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 ·        Click to manually distribute any items in an expense invoice among the sold parts in the voucher , thereby overriding their default burden types.
