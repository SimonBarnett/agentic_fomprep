# Adding Expenses to an Outgoing Shipping Voucher 

 

Both sold goods and the expenses related to their shipment can be added to the shipping voucher in the same manner – by linking the invoice in which they are billed to the voucher. When the voucher is closed, the system identifies the expense items and distributes them, together with any additional expenses recorded in the voucher, among the part items in the customer invoices. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Link an entire expense invoice to an outgoing shipping voucher 
 

 Link specific lines from an expense invoice 
 

 Record expenses manually
