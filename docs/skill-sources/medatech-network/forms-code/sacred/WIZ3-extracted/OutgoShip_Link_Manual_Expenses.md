# Manually Recording Expenses in a Shipping Voucher 

 

You can record expenses in a shipping voucher if the expense item's exact cost is known in advance. 
 
## To record expenses in an outgoing shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the desired voucher. 
 

 2 .  To record expenses, enter the Additional Expenses sub-level form and fill in the details of each expense.
 Note: This form also displays individual expense items linked to the shipping voucher from invoices. In this case, the Invoice and Ln (line) columns will be filled in (these columns will remain empty for expenses recorded manually). 
 
## Tip:

 

Click to distribute an expense in a different manner than its default burden type.
