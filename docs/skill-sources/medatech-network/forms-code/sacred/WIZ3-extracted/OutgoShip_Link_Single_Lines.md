# Linking Individual Expense Invoice Items to an Outgoing Shipping Voucher 

 

When individual items in a single invoice are for expenses incurred by parts included in several different outgoing shipping vouchers, you can assign each expense item to the appropriate voucher. You can also distribute such expenses manually, thus overriding their default burden types. 
 
## To add an individual expense item to a voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purch Invs for Outgoing Shipmts form and retrieve the desired invoice.
 Tip: Retrieve by Invoice Type and Vendor Number . 
 

 2 .  Enter the Invoice Items sub-level form. Each invoice item appears on a separate line. 
 

 3 .  Move to the expense item you want to include in the voucher, and record the voucher number in the Shipping Voucher column.
 Tip: Press F6 from that column to enter the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the relevant voucher. Press F8 to leave the form and automatically copy the voucher number. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To view all expense items included in an outgoing shipping voucher, use the Additional Expenses sub-level of the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 ·        Click to manually distribute any items in an expense invoice among the sold parts in the voucher , thereby overriding their default burden types.
