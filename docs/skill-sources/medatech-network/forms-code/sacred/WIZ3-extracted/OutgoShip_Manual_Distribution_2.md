# Manually Distributing an Expense Item 

 

You can manually define the ratio by which an expense item is to be distributed among the sold parts in the outgoing shipping voucher. You can also target expenses to one or more specific invoices. 
 
## To manually distribute an expense item:

 

 1 .  Enter the Manual Distribution of Expenses sub-level of the Invoice Items form. Each sold part in the voucher appears in a separate line. 
 

 2 .  For each item, if you want a certain portion of the expense to be added to a specific invoice, record the number of the Invoice in question. Examples. 
 

 3 .  For each item, fill in the Proportion of Burden column with a value representing the relative portion of the expense to be added to that sold item. Example. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can record multiple lines for a single part, specifying a different invoice in each line. 
 

 ·        When the shipping voucher is closed, the expenses added to each purchased part include both the specified portion of each expense that is assigned to that part throughout the voucher and the specified portion of each expense that is assigned directly to a particular invoice. 
 
## To view the results of manual distribution:

 

 1 .  Leave the Manual Distribution of Expenses form open. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form and retrieve the voucher in question. 
 

 3 .  Select Calculate Expense Burdening from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 4 .  Return to the Manual Distribution of Expenses form and press Ctrl+F11 to refresh the screen. The exact amount of the expense assigned to each part appears in the Expenses column.
