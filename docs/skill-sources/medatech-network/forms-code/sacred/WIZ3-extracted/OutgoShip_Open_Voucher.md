# To open an outgoing shipping voucher:

 

 1 .  Enter the Outgoing Shipping Vouchers form. 
 

 2 .  Record the date in the Date Opened column. The Voucher Number appears automatically.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 3 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·     If you want to link the voucher to a sales order, specify the Order in the References tab. The Customer Number , Customer Name and order Currency appear automatically. 
 

 ·     If you are opening the voucher for a single customer, specify the Customer Number and the voucher Currency . If the voucher includes invoices with different customers and currencies, specify the main ones, and those recorded in each invoice will appear automatically in the itemized sub-level form.
 Note for dual-currency users: You can also specify an Exchange Rate Date , which determines the exchange rate to use when converting customer part prices and additional expenses included in the voucher. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Attachments sub-level form and attach any relevant documents to the voucher. 
 

 5 .  Change the Status of the outgoing shipping voucher.
 Tip: Later you can review the voucher's history of changing statuses in the History of Statuses sub-level form. 
 
## Note:

 

You can use the checklist in the Outgoing Ship Voucher Checklist form, a sub-level of Customers to check the desired order of tasks for this customer.
