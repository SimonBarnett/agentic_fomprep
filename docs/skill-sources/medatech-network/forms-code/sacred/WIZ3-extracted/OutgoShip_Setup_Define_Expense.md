# Defining a Part as an Expense 

 

When defining expenses, it isn't necessary to open a new part for every possible expense item; rather, you can define a part that represents a category of expenses, and revise the part description in each invoice. 
 
## To define an expense:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Catalogue form and record a new Part Number and Description . 
 

 2 .  In the Additional Details tab, select O in the Type column. 
 

 3 .  Leave the Inv. Control column blank, and ensure that the Change Desc. column displays a check mark, to enable you to change the part's description in specific instances.
 Tip: For assistance, run the Part Definition Wizard . 
 

 4 .  Enter the Financial Parameters for Parts form and retrieve the part you defined.
 Tip: You can also enter this form from the Part Catalogue form by Direct Activation . 
 

 5 .  Click the Shipping Expenses tab and assign the part its appropriate Burden Type . 
 
## Note:

 

If the burden type is by weight or volume, click to record the weight and/or volume of the sold parts.
