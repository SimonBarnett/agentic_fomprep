# Viewing Invoice Profitability

 

Once the shipping voucher is closed, you can analyze the profitability of your sales in reports that compare the total cost of the shipped goods with the prices charged to your customers in the relevant invoices. 
 
## To view the profitability of customer invoices:

 

 ·        Run the Profit from Part Sales (Inv.) or Monthly Profit from Part (Inv.) report, selecting the Cost input option. 
 

 ·        Run the Invoice Analysis (BI) report. 
 

 ·        Create custom reports in the Customer Invoice Rept Generator .
