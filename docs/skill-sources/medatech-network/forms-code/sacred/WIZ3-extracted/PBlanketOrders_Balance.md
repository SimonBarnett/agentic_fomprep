# Keeping Track of Blanket Purchase Orders

 

Each time a purchase order is opened on the basis of a blanket purchase order, the remaining quantity of goods that your company is obligated to buy is reduced accordingly. This quantity appears in the Balance to Order column of the Itemized Blanket Order sub-level form, for each ordered item. 
 

When all of the order items are closed, the blanket purchase order is closed automatically, and a check mark appears in the Closed column of the upper-level form (in the Authorization & Follow-up tab). You can also close the blanket purchase order at any time by flagging the Closed column manually.
