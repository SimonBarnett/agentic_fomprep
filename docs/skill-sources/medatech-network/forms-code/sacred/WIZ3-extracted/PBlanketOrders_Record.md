- 
 
 
 
# To record a blanket order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Blanket Purchase Orders form. 
 

 2 .  Record the vendor number. The vendor name, date and order number are filled in automatically.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the order to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Authorization & Follow-up tab). 
 

 3 .  Check the Contact column to make sure that the relevant blanket purchase order contact appears. Change it if necessary.
 Tip: To define new contacts for the vendor or to assign a default contact for orders, use the Vendor Contacts form, a sub-level of the Vendors form. For more information, see the Open Vendors Wizard . 
 

 4 .  In the Expiration Date column, specify the date until which the conditions of the blanket order apply. After this date, you can no longer base purchase orders on the blanket order. 
 

 5 .  Click the Price tab. If the blanket purchase order is based on a price quotation received from the vendor, record its number in the Quote Number column. When the order items are recorded in the itemization sub-level, their prices and discounts will be copied from the price quote (provided that the minimum quantity specified in the quote was ordered). 
 

 6 .  If the order price is in a foreign currency, make sure that the correct Currency appears. 
 

 7 .  If you want all lines of the blanket order to be copied automatically into any purchase order that is based on it, click the Authorization & Follow-up tab and flag the Auto Copy column. 
 

 8 .  Click the Payment Info tab and make sure that the Payment Terms are correct. 
 

 9 .   Du al-currency users: 
 

 ·      Make sure the VAT Code is correct. 
 

 ·      If the order price is linked to a foreign currency or price index, specify the currency or index in the Linked Currency column. In the case of an index, specify the Base Rate .
 Note: For more information, see the Price Adjustment Wizard .
