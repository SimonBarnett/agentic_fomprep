PP_AccRec_example_retro_invoice 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Let's say you issued an invoice to a certain customer, with a payment date of January 31. The customer made a partial payment on February 15, and undertook to pay the balance by the end of the month. Priority allows you to split the invoice into two amounts, and assign them payment dates of February 15 and February 28, even though a journal entry was already posted for the invoice. This prevents the invoice from appearing as an outstanding debt in aging reports.
