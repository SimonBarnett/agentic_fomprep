# PP_Adv_Pay_percent_cust

PP_Adv_Pay_percent_cust 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Assume that the customer paid $140 in advance for an order worth $1200, and the percentage to be deducted is 20%. Consequently, 20% will be deducted from the total amount of each multi-shipment invoice billing a shipment of these ordered goods, until the advance payment balance is 0. If there are three invoices, each for $400, $80 (20% of $400) will be deducted from the first invoice and $60 from the second invoice. The third invoice will bill for the entire $400, as the advance payment has already been fully deducted. 
 

In other words: 
 

$140 (advance) + $320 (first invoice) + $360 (second invoice) + $400 (third invoice) = $1200 (full amount owed).
