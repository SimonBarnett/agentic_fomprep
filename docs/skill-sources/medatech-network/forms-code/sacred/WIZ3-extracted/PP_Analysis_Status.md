# PP_Analysis_Status

- **Source:** `PP_Analysis_Status.htm`

PP_Analysis_Status 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The status of inventory indicates how it may be used. For instance, the Goods status generally indicates that the parts can be shipped to a customer and cannot be disposed of. Parts with a Reject status, on the other hand, cannot be shipped to a customer and can be disposed of.
