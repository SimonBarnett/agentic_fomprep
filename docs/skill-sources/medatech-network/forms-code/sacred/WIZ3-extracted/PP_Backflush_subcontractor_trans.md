# PP_Backflush_subcontractor_trans

- **Source:** `PP_Backflush_subcontractor_trans.htm`

PP_Backflush_subcontractor_trans 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 The standard working procedure with subcontractors contains two basic steps: 
 

 ·        Shipment of children and parts-in-progress to a subcontractor, recorded in a Shipmt to Subcontr/Issue of Kit document. Such inventory is "stored" in the subcontractor’s warehouse. 
 

 ·        Receipt of finished parts from the subcontractor, recorded in a Goods Receiving Vouchers . Such inventory is returned from the subcontractor’s warehouse. 
 

However, any transaction involving parts that enter or leave a subcontractor’s warehouse are treated by the Backflush program. This includes, for instance, warehouse transfers to or from the subcontractor, disposal of inventory by the subcontractor and inventory conversions.
