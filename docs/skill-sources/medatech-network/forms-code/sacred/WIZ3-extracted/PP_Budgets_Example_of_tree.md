## Example: Budget for Personnel Division

 

The PERSON budget has three levels: the personnel division at the highest level, the company’s southern and northern branches at the second level, and the individual budget items at the lowest level. 
 

 ·        PERSON (personnel division) 
 

 ·        PERSON.STH (personnel, southern division) 
 

 ·        PERSON.STH-TRV (travel budget for southern personnel division) 
 

 ·        PERSON.STH-SAL (salary budget for southern personnel division) 
 

 ·        PERSON.NTH (personnel, northern division) 
 

 ·        PERSON.NTH-TRV (travel budget for northern personnel division) 
 

 ·        PERSON.NTH-SAL (salary budget for northern personnel division)
