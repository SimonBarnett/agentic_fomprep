# PP_CMNF_Changing_Costing_Option

- **Source:** `PP_CMNF_Changing_Costing_Option.htm`

PP_CMNF_Changing_Costing_Option 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The transition from option T (by BOM) to W (by work orders) for a given part is usually necessitated by an actual change in the factory's method of production management. 
 

When performing such a transition, you must first record a past balance and run costing for the date of the transition. Otherwise, some of the data recorded during the current costing period may not relate to work orders, and the costing program will simply ignore such data. Similarly, if option T was assigned to a part that is actually produced in work orders (due to problems resulting in unreliable production reporting), and was later replaced by W (after the problems were solved), the costing program will take into account all data from the entire period – including the unreliable reports.
