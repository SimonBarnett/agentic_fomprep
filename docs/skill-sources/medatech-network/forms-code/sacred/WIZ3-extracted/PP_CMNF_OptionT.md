# PP_CMNF_OptionT

- **Source:** `PP_CMNF_OptionT.htm`

PP_CMNF_OptionT 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

When a part is assigned a Costing Option of T , part cost is calculated by multiplying the standard quantities of its child parts (i.e., those recorded in the BOM) by their actual costs, and adding standard production costs. This is in contrast to parts with a Costing Option of W , whose cost is calculated on the basis of closed work orders.
