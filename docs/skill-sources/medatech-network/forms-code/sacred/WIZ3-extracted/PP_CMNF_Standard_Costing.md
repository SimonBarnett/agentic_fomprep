# PP_CMNF_Standard_Costing

- **Source:** `PP_CMNF_Standard_Costing.htm`

PP_CMNF_Standard_Costing 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The standard cost of a manufactured part is calculated by multiplying the costs of its child parts by their standard quantities (i.e., the quantities recorded in the BOM). When working with Std Material Cost , standard quantities are multiplied by the child part's standard cost. When working with Actual Material Cost , standard quantities are multiplied by the actual cost of purchased child parts, and by the standard cost of manufactured or assembled child parts.
