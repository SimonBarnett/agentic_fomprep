PP_CMNF_supplies_std 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 Materials issued as supplies to the plant floor are not included in the BOMs of manufactured parts, but their cost should be taken into account when calculating production costs (e.g. machine oil or soap, routinely transferred to the plant floor).
