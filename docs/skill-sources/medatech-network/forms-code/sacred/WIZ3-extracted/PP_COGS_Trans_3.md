# PP_COGS_Trans_3

- **Source:** `PP_COGS_Trans_3.htm`

PP_COGS_Trans_3 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

When the Post Cost of Goods Sold program is run, a journal entry (code = LH ) is recorded debiting the Inventory – Labor account of the plant floor and crediting the Labor Offset account with the total cost of employee time reported in production reports over the course of the COGS period. The same entry also includes an item that adjusts the balance of the Inventory – Labor account of the plant floor to reflect the difference between reported hours and standard labor time for the manufactured parts received into the finished goods warehouse over this period.
