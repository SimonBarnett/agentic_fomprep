# PP_COGS_Trans_4

- **Source:** `PP_COGS_Trans_4.htm`

PP_COGS_Trans_4 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

When the Post Cost of Goods Sold program is run, a journal entry (code = FB ) is recorded debiting the Inventory – Burden account of the plant floor and crediting the Burden Offset account with the sum of all actual burdens recorded for the manufactured part over the course of the COGS period. The same entry also includes an item that adjusts the balance of the Inventory – Burden account of the plant floor to reflect the difference between the reported burden and the standard burden of the manufactured parts received into the finished goods warehouse over this period.
