# PP_COGS_completeprod

- **Source:** `PP_COGS_completeprod.htm`

PP_COGS_completeprod 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

End-of-production transactions create COGS entries derived from the following: 
 

 ·        Standard material cost, determined by the standard cost of purchased child parts belonging to the standard BOM of the manufactured part. 
 

 ·        Standard labor cost, calculated by multiplying standard production time for unskilled labor by the direct hourly cost of unskilled labor, or multiplying standard production time for skilled labor by the number of skilled workers and the direct hourly cost of skilled labor. If both skilled and unskilled labor are defined, the costs are added together. 
 

 ·        Standard burden: 
 

 ·      Direct machinery costs, calculated by multiplying standard production time for machines by the direct hourly cost of machines. 
 

 ·      Indirect machinery costs, calculated by totaling production time defined for set-ups (see below) and machinery and multiplying the result by the indirect hourly machine cost. 
 

 ·      Indirect labor costs, calculated by multiplying standard production time for unskilled workers by the indirect hourly cost of unskilled labor, or multiplying the standard production time for skilled labor by the number of skilled workers and the indirect hourly cost of skilled labor. If both skilled and unskilled labor are defined, the costs are added together. 
 

 ·      Set-up costs, calculated by totaling standard production time defined for set-ups (the percentage of machine time devoted to set-ups + load time + unload time) and multiplying the result by the hourly set-up cost, as defined per work cell. 
 

 ·      Subcontracting costs, non-standard costs, supply costs and rework costs, as defined per part.
