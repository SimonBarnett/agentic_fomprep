PP_COGS_invacc_std 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

In the Manufacturing package, the Purchases /Inventory account is used only for the cost of materials. Labor and burden are recorded against the Inventory – Labor and Inventory – Burden accounts, respectively. Similarly, the COGS – Material account is used for material costs, while labor and burden are recorded against the COGS – Labor and COGS – Burden accounts. 
 

If separate inventory accounts have been defined for each warehouse, the account of the warehouse from which inventory is removed is credited, and the account of the warehouse in which inventory is received is debited.
