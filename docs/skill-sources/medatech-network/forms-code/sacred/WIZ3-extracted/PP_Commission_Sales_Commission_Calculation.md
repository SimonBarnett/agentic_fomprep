# PP_Commission_Sales_Commission_Calculation

- **Source:** `PP_Commission_Sales_Commission_Calculation.htm`

PP_Commission_Sales_Commission_Calculation 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The commission is calculated by taking the price quoted for the customer (usually the commission sales price) and subtracting the price you would normally pay the vendor (the regular list price). The former appears in the Extended Price column; the latter in the List Price (Reg/Comm ) column.
