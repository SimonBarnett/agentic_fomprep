PP_CostAllocation_Direct_Costs_Example 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A hotel has two types of rooms (Suite and Standard), each of which is defined as a profit center. The laundry bill is an indirect cost that must be divided between these profit centers. 
 

The direct cost of suites last month was $10,000, and the direct cost of standard rooms was $15,000. The laundry bill of $3000 is thus distributed at a ratio of 1:1.5, or $1200 for suites and $1800 for standard rooms.
