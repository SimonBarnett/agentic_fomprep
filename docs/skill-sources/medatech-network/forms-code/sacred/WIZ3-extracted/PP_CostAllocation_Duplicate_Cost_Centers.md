PP_CostAllocation_Duplicate_Cost_Centers 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The company uses three profit groups, whose profit centers are based on branch offices, accounting families and projects. The same cost centers, office expenses and salaries, will be allocated to each of the different profit groups to determine their profitability. In this case, you must define six cost centers: 
 

 ·        101 – Office Expenses, Group 1 (per branch) 
 

 ·        201 – Office Expenses, Group 2 (per accounting family) 
 

 ·        301 – Office Expenses, Group 3 (per project) 
 

 ·        102 – Salaries, Group 1 (per branch) 
 

 ·        202 – Salaries, Group 2 (per accounting family) 
 

 ·        302 – Salaries, Group 3 (per project)
