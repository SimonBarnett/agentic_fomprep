# PP_CostAllocation_Profit_Groups

PP_CostAllocation_Profit_Groups 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A given company uses three groups, each with a different type of profit center: 
 

 ·        Group 1 is based on branch location, and includes the profit centers 101 Chicago , 102 New York , 103 Detroit . 
 

 ·        Group 2 is based on customer groups, and includes the profit centers 201 Walk-in , 202 Online , 203 Wholesale . 
 

 ·        Group 3 is based on the accounting families assigned to products, and includes the profit centers 301 Food , 302 Textiles and 303 Cosmetics . 
 

Each item in a customer invoice can then be linked to one profit center in each group (e.g., 102 in the Profit Center column, 201 in the Profit Center 2 column, 301 in the Profit Center 3 column).
