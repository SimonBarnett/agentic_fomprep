# PP_CostAllocation_Trees

- **Source:** `PP_CostAllocation_Trees.htm`

PP_CostAllocation_Trees 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

One of your company's profit groups consists of profit centers based on the company's warehouses, organized in the following tree: 
 

 ·        Level 1 – Total costs and revenues of all company warehouses 
 

 ·        Level 2 – Total costs and revenues of the warehouses in each region 
 

 ·        Level 3 – Total costs and revenues of the warehouses in each city 
 

By comparing data on Level 2, you can determine the relative profitability of each region.
