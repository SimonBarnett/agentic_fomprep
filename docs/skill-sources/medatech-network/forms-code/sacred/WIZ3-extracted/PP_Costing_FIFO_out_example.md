# PP_Costing_FIFO_out_example

- **Source:** `PP_Costing_FIFO_out_example.htm`

PP_Costing_FIFO_out_example 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The current inventory of a certain part consists of 2 groups at different costs: 
 

Group 1: 15 units, at the cost of $15 per unit 
 

Group 2: 10 units, at the cost of $10 per unit 
 

The overall cost of the part is $13 per unit, according to the formula: (15 ´ 15 + 10 ´ 10)/ (10 + 15) = 325 / 25. 
 

When ten parts are removed from inventory, they are taken from the first group. The overall cost of the remaining inventory is then $11.6 per unit, according to the formula: [(15 - 10) ´ 15 + 10 ´ 10] / [10 + (15 - 10)] = 175 / 15.
