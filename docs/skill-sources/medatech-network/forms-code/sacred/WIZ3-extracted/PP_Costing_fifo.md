PP_Costing_fifo 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The FIFO ( First In First Out ) method views each warehouse like a pipe – inventory that enters first exits first, and each item maintains its original cost until it leaves inventory. Hence, the average cost of a part will be affected by the removal of parts from inventory. 
 

For example, assume that there are 200 units of a certain part, as a result of three different receipts into inventory: 
 

 ·        20 units, at $30 each 
 

 ·        70 units, at $35 each 
 

 ·        110 units, at $42 each. 
 

The part's average cost is $38.35, according to the formula: (20 ´ 30 + 70 ´ 35 + 110 ´ 42) / 200. 
 

If 30 units of the part are then removed from inventory, the cost of the part will be updated as follows: 
 

(20 ´ 30 + 70 ´ 35 + 110 ´ 42 - 20 ´ 30 - 10 ´ 35 ) / 170 = $39.53. The least expensive units have left inventory, resulting in a higher average cost for the remaining inventory.
