# PP_Costing_moving_mean_example

- **Source:** `PP_Costing_moving_mean_example.htm`

PP_Costing_moving_mean_example 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The current inventory of a certain part is 10 units, at an average cost of $10 per unit. 
 

If 5 units are added to inventory at the cost of $16 per unit, the result, according to Moving Average, will be 15 parts, with an average cost of $12, according to the formula: (10 ´ 10 + 5 ´ 16) / (5 + 10).
