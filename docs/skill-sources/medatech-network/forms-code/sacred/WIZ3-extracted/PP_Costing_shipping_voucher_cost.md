PP_Costing_shipping_voucher_cost 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Shipping vouchers are used to distribute expenses incurred by the purchase of parts (e.g., shipping, storage) among the purchased parts. Costs calculated on the basis of a shipping voucher are therefore more accurate than those recorded in an invoice, which does not include additional expenses.
