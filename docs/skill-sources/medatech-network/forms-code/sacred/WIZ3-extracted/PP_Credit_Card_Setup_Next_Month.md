# PP_Credit_Card_Setup_Next_Month

- **Source:** `PP_Credit_Card_Setup_Next_Month.htm`

PP_Credit_Card_Setup_Next_Month 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

For vouchers dated between the 1st and 14th of the month, the credit card company issues payment to you on the 2nd of the following month. 
 

 ·        Next to each of the numbers 1 to 14 in the Day on Voucher column, record 2 in the Day of Payment column and flag the Next Month column.
