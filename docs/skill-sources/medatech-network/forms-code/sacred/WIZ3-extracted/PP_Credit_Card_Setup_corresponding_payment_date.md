PP_Credit_Card_Setup_corresponding_payment_date 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

For vouchers dated between the 1st and 14th of the month, the credit card company issues payment to you on the 31st of the month. 
 

 ·        Next to each of the numbers 1 to 14 in the Day on Voucher column, record 31 in the Day of Payment column. 
 

For vouchers dated between the 15th and 31st of the month, you are paid on the 8th of the following month. 
 

 ·        Next to each of the numbers 15 to 31 in the Day on Voucher column, record 8 in the Day of Payment column, and flag the Next Month column.
