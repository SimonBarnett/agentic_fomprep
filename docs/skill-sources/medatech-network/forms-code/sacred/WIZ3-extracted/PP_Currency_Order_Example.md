# PP_Currency_Order_Example

- **Source:** `PP_Currency_Order_Example.htm`

PP_Currency_Order_Example 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Assume that, in addition to the local currency (e.g., U.S. dollars), you typically work with Canadian dollars and Yen. You could assign Canadian dollars position 3 and Yen position 4 . As a result, in the Itemized Rates sub-level form (and in all currency Choose lists throughout the system), Canadian dollars and Yen would appear near the top, after U.S. dollars and the second currency.
