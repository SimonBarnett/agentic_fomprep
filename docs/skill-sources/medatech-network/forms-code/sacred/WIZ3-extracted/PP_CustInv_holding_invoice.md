# PP_CustInv_holding_invoice

- **Source:** `PP_CustInv_holding_invoice.htm`

PP_CustInv_holding_invoice 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A holding invoice is a special type of sales invoice (or over-the-counter invoice), opened for goods that are stored in a holding warehouse until they are transferred to the customer. 
 

When a holding invoice is recorded, the goods are assigned the customer number as their status. When the goods are ultimately delivered to the customer, a separate shipping document is recorded. This shipping document must be flagged as non-billable, and the recorded sending warehouse must be the holding warehouse in which the goods have been stored.
