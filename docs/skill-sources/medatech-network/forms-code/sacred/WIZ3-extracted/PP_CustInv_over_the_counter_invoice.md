# PP_CustInv_over_the_counter_invoice

- **Source:** `PP_CustInv_over_the_counter_invoice.htm`

PP_CustInv_over_the_counter_invoice 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Use an over-the-counter invoice when the customer pays for goods at the moment of sale. This invoice therefore serves also as a shipping document and receipt. 
 

As an invoice, it generates a journal entry for the sale; as a shipping document, it deducts the purchased goods from inventory; and as a receipt, it records the receipt of payment from the customer.
