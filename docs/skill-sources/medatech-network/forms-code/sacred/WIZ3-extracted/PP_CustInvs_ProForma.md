# PP_CustInvs_ProForma

- **Source:** `PP_CustInvs_ProForma.htm`

PP_CustInvs_ProForma 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A pro forma invoice allows you to bill the customer for services and keep track of customer credit and aging without recording an actual entry in the ledger. In this way, you do not debit the customer's account in the ledger until after receiving payment for services rendered (when a multi-shipment invoice is recorded).
