# PP_CustShip_order_tolerance

- **Source:** `PP_CustShip_order_tolerance.htm`

PP_CustShip_order_tolerance 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Order tolerance (defined in the Inventory Parameters for Parts form) is the extent to which the shipped quantity may exceed the balance of the order item. That is, the total shipment of a given part (which may encompass several shipping documents) cannot exceed its ordered quantity by more than the designated percentage. 
 

For instance, if the order tolerance is 10% and the order is for 200 units, you cannot record shipments covering a total quantity of more than 220 units.
