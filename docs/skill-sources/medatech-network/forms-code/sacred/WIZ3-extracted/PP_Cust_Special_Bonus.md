# PP_Cust_Special_Bonus

- **Source:** `PP_Cust_Special_Bonus.htm`

PP_Cust_Special_Bonus 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Special bonuses are free or specially priced goods offered to customers when they order a minimum quantity of a given product or of goods in a given part family. By assigning a special bonus to a representative customer, all customers in the group are awarded the bonus.
