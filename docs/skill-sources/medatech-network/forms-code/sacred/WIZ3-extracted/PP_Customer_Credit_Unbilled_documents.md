PP_Customer_Credit_Unbilled_documents 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Unbilled documents may include any or all of the following: 
 

 ·        customer shipments (unbilled shipments and uncredited returns) 
 

 ·        unbilled service calls 
 

 ·        contract payment installments that have not been invoiced 
 

 ·        project payment installments that have not been invoiced.
