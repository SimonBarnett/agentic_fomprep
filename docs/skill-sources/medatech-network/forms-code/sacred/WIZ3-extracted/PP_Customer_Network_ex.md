PP_Customer_Network_ex 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

You do business with Hamilton & Co., a consulting firm which has brought you a number of clients, among them ABC Toy Stores. You would therefore define both Hamilton & Co. and ABC Toy Stores as customers. If Mary W. is the consultant who works with ABC Toy Stores, she would be defined as a direct contact of Hamilton & Co. and a networked contact of ABC Toy Stores. Thus, her name would appear on the Choose list of contacts in both companies, and any mail from her could be used to open a task for either company.
