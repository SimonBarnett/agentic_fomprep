PP_Customer_Service_Payment_frequency_options 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The options are: 
 

 ·        Monthly 
 

 ·        Bi-monthly (every two months) 
 

 ·        Quarterly 
 

 ·        Semi-annual 
 

 ·        Annual OR 
 

 ·        Record installments manually (i.e., you will not use the Priority utility).
