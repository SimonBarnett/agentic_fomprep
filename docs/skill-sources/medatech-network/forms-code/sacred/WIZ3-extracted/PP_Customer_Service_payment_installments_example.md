PP_Customer_Service_payment_installments_example 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

If payments are quarterly, they are always calculated – for all contracts – for Jan. 1, April 1, July 1 and Oct. 1. 
 

 Exception: The very first period is likely to be somewhat longer, since the service contract does not necessarily begin at the beginning of a quarter. So, if a service contract begins on Nov. 15, the customer's first installment covers the period Nov. 15 through March 31. The second installment covers April 1 through June 30, and so on.
