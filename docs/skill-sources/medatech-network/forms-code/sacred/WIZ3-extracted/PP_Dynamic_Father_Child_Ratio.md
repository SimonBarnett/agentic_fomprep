PP_Dynamic_Father/Child_Ratio 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The BOM of a play table includes a wooden board and three types of emulsion: pre-coat, paint and varnish. The required quantity of each emulsion is as follows: 
 

 ·        Pre-coat – 1 liter per cubic meter 
 

 ·        Paint – 1.5 liter per cubic meter 
 

 ·        Varnish – 0.5 liter per cubic meter. 
 

The same values are recorded in the Coefficient column of each part respectively, the Operand remains “M” (multiplication) and “Area” is selected in the Variable column. 
 

The board used to produce the play table is 2 ´ 4 meters. The value “8” is thus specified for the Area variable in the Part Processing Parameters sub-level form. 
 

The Qty(Child) column in the Child Parts form will now display the following values: 
 

 ·        Pre-coat – 8 liters 
 

 ·        Paint – 12 liters 
 

 ·        Varnish – 4 liters. 
 

If it ever becomes necessary to change the area of the wooden board, or to add larger or smaller variations of the play table to the part catalogue, it will be sufficient to alter the value of the Area , and the emulsion quantities will be updated automatically.
