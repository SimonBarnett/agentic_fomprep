PP_EnryCodes_example_of_pattern 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

This is the entry pattern of the predefined Vendor Invoice entry code, in the U.S. package: 
 

 ·        The A/P account is credited for the sum of the invoice. 
 

 ·        The LineItems account is debited for the sum of the invoice. 
 

In the dual-currency package: 
 

 ·        The A/P account is credited for the sum of the invoice. 
 

 ·        The LineItems account is debited for the sum of the invoice without VAT. 
 

 ·        The Tax account is debited for the VAT amount.
