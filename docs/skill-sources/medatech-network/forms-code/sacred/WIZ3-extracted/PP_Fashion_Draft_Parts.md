For example, if the style Running Shoe has been assigned three possible color values: Black , Blue and White , and three possible size values: 28 , 30 and 32 , the Part Specs Work Area will display a draft part for each of the nine possible combinations: 
 

 ·        Running Shoe – Black – 28 
 

 ·        Running Shoe – Black – 30 
 

 ·        Running Shoe – Black – 32 
 

 ·        Running Shoe – Blue – 28 
 

 ·        Running Shoe – Blue – 30 
 

 ·        Running Shoe – Blue – 32 
 

 ·        Running Shoe – White – 28 
 

 ·        Running Shoe – White – 30 
 

 ·        Running Shoe – White – 32
