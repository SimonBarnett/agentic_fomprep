# PP_Loans_Postponing_Example

PP_Loans_Postponing_Example 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

You are scheduled to make ten monthly installment payments. The first payment is due on Feb. 15, the second on March 15, etc. You postpone the second payment until April 1. The updated amortization schedule shows the second payment due on April 1, the third payment due on May 1, etc.
