# PP_MRPBus_Dynamic_Demand

- **Source:** `PP_MRPBus_Dynamic_Demand.htm`

PP_MRPBus_Dynamic_Demand 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Parts with replenishment type R are required in different amounts at different times, according to the volume of sales orders. This is called dynamic demand , as it fluctuates dynamically depending on the quantity of orders for the part itself or its immediate parent.
