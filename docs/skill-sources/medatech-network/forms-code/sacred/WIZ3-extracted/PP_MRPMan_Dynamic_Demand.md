# PP_MRPMan_Dynamic_Demand

- **Source:** `PP_MRPMan_Dynamic_Demand.htm`

PP_MRPMan_Dynamic_Demand 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Parts with Replenishment Type R are required in different amounts at different times, according to the volume of sales or forecast orders for the part itself (if the part is manufactured, you can base the calculation of demand on work orders rather than sales orders). The replenishment type affects the calculation of direct part demand, which is added to the demand derived from open or planned work orders for parent parts, and from safety stock.
