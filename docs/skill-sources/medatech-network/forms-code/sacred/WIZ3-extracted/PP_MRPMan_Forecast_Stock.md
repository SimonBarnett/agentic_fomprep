# PP_MRPMan_Forecast_Stock

- **Source:** `PP_MRPMan_Forecast_Stock.htm`

PP_MRPMan_Forecast/Stock 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Orders to stock are used to generate inventory that will be on hand for sales orders (i.e., ready to deliver off the shelf). 
 

Forecast orders are opened automatically on the basis of monthly demand forecasts. Even though forecast orders may be opened on the basis of forecasts linked to specific customers, parts purchased and/or processed on the basis of such orders enter the general inventory. 
 

Sales orders that are opened manually for one of these purposes can be flagged as such ( S = Stock, F = Forecast) in the Forecast/Stock column. 
 

Forecast orders can also be opened automatically on the basis of demand forecasts. Such orders are automatically flagged with an F . 
 

The planning programs relate to both types of orders in the same manner.
