That is, the amount of time required to produce a certain quantity (batch) of parts is equal to the amount of time it takes to produce a smaller quantity. 
 

 Example: The duration of a laundering operation in a standard machine is 90 minutes for up to 60 kg. of laundry. In the Operations form, we define the production time as 1.5 hours, for a production quantity of 60 kg., and flag the MRP-Time Per Batch column.
