# PP_Marketing_Calendar_Synchronization_Example

- **Source:** `PP_Marketing_Calendar_Synchronization_Example.htm`

PP_Marketing_Calendar_Synchronization_Example 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

You can record a meeting in the Priority calendar and synchronize later with Outlook. Staff in the field can record the outcome of the meeting in Outlook and synchronize the data with Priority upon returning to the office. Additional updates can then be made directly in Priority . 
 

When calendars are synchronized, your existing appointments in Outlook are copied into your calendar in Priority , and/or vice versa. If the appointment already exists in the receiving calendar, its details are updated. If it does not, it is added.
