PP_Marketing_Task_Checklist_Example 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The following tasks can be opened automatically and assigned to the relevant employees whenever an employee leaves the company for any reason: 
 

 ·              Issue/obtain a letter of termination/resignation (assigned to the HR manager) 
 

 ·              Terminate access to computer/telephone systems (assigned to the network administrator) 
 

 ·              Recover any company property that was issued to the employee (assigned to the office manager) 
 

 ·              Deduct unpaid payroll advances/add unpaid business expenses to the final pay check (assigned to the head of accounting)
