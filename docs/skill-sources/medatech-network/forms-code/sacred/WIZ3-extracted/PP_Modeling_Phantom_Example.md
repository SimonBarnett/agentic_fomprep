PP_Modeling_Phantom_Example 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A bakery that produces wedding cakes requires the same basic ingredients to decorate each variety of cake. A phantom part called “Dressing” is defined and assigned to each cake, consisting of five jars of white frosting, ten tubes of white icing, one hundred small pink candy flowers, and one hundred small blue candy flowers. 
 

When it becomes known that yellow flower arrangements will be in fashion in the coming season, it is easy to open the phantom part and replace the small blue flowers with yellow ones. This will alter the kit list of the entire line of cakes, without having to adjust each cake individually.
