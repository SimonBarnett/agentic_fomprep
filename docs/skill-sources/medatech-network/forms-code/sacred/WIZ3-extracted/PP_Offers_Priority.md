# PP_Offers_Priority

- **Source:** `PP_Offers_Priority.htm`

PP_Offers_Priority 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Whenever a customer is linked to multiple special offers that share the same target part, the Priority column determines the offer to which the customer's invoices should be assigned. 
 

Let's say two offers have been opened for the same part: 
 

 ·        Offer 1: Customers that purchase $1000 worth of lawn furniture (a part family) in August receive a free video camera. 
 

 ·        Offer 2: Customers that purchase 8 lawn chairs (a member of the part family) during August and September are entitled to purchase a beach umbrella at half the price. 
 

If the customer purchases a $1200 set of lawn furniture, including a table and eight lawn chairs, which benefit is received? 
 

If Offer 1 is assigned a higher priority than Offer 2 (e.g., 1 and 2 , respectively), the customer receives the video camera; if Offer 2 is assigned the higher priority, the customer receives the umbrella on sale. 
 

Nonetheless, a customer that fails to meet the requirements for the higher priority offer is still eligible for the lower priority one; that is, a customer that purchases eight lawn chairs for $800 will still receive the umbrella, even if Offer 2 is lower in priority.
