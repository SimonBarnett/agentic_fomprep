Mary W. works in a consultancy firm and is your contact for dozens of customers, including her own company. In this case, you create a direct link between Mary and her company, and link her as a networked contact to all the other customers. To open a task or service call for ABC Toy Stores based on mail received from Mary W., assign her message to the ABC category.
