# PP_Outlook_Import_Mail

- **Source:** `PP_Outlook_Import_Mail.htm`

PP_Outlook_Import_Mail 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

When e-mail messages are imported as tasks, these are opened in the Tasks form and assigned to you. The contact for the task is determined by the e-mail address. The task is defined for the customer/vendor assigned to the e-mail message (either by means of categories or directly in Outlook, when working with the Synchronize with Priority feature). If no customer/vendor is assigned to the e-mail, the customer or vendor for the task is taken from the Contacts form (provided that the contact for the task is directly associated with a single Priority customer/vendor in that form). The message text is copied to the Notes sub-level form and the message itself is added as an attachment to the task in the Customer Documents for Task sub-level form. 
 

When e-mail messages are imported as service calls, these are opened in the Service Calls form for a customer associated with the recipient/sender of the mail. Again, this is determined either by the customer assigned to the e-mail message (by means of categories or directly in Outlook) or by a direct link between the contact and customer in the Contacts form. The message text is copied to the Details of Malfunction sub-level form. 
 
## Note:

 

Once a new task is opened, if you assign it an end time later than its start time, an appointment will be opened automatically in your calendar.
