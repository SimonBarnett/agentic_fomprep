# PP_Outlook_custcontact

- **Source:** `PP_Outlook_custcontact.htm`

PP_Outlook_custcontact 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A contact is linked to a customer in one of two sub-levels of the Customers form: 
 

 ·        Customer Contacts , used to record direct company contacts 
 

 ·        External Contacts , used to link up to other customers in a network, along with one or more of those customers' own contacts.
