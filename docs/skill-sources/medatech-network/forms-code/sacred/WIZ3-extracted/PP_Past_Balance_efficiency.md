# PP_Past_Balance_efficiency

- **Source:** `PP_Past_Balance_efficiency.htm`

PP_Past_Balance_efficiency 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Calculations for costing, inventory counts and many inventory reports start from the last past balance. When no past balances are available, calculations begin from the first recorded transaction. After a few months of activity, this may take a long time and interfere with everyday work. Furthermore, when calculating costs over a long period of time, it becomes more difficult to analyze the resulting costs.
