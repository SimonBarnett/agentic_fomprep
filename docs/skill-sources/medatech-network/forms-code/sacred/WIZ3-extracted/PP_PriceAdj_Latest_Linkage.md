# PP_PriceAdj_Latest_Linkage

- **Source:** `PP_PriceAdj_Latest_Linkage.htm`

PP_PriceAdj_Latest_Linkage 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Linkage to this currency will stop on the earliest of two dates: 
 

 ·        The order item's due date, less the number of months appearing in this column. 
 

 ·        The actual date of delivery. 
 

For example, if the due date is June 1, and a 1 appears in this column, linkage will stop on May 1. However, if the merchandise was delivered two months ahead of schedule, linkage will stop on April 1.
