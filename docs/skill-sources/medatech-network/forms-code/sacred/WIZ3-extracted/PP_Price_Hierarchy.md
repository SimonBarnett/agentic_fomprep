# The Customer Price Hierarchy

 

In Priority , a hierarchy of prices, from the most specific to the most general, determines the unit price of a part. When filling in a price automatically (e.g., in a sales order), Priority starts at the top of the hierarchy and works down until a price is found. You can manually override an automatic price, and you can view the source of an automatic price in the Price Source column. 
 

 Priority assigns prices according to the following order: 
 

 1 .  Price list assigned to the document in question 
 

 2 .  Special part prices 
 

 3 .  Customer price list 
 

 4 .  Representative customer price list 
 

 5 .  Base price list 
 

 6 .  Vendor price list 
 

For more information, see the Prices and Discounts Wizard .
