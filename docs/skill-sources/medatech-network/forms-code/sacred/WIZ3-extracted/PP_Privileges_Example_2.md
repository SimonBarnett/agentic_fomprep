PP_Privileges_Example_2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The History of Statuses form appears as a sub-level of both the Customers form and the Sales Orders form. A group was assigned Read privileges to this form when it is accessed from the Customers form and Write privileges to it when accessed from the Sales Orders form. The report shows these and other discrepancies in assigned privileges.
