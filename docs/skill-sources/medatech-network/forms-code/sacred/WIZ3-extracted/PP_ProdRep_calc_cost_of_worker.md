# PP_ProdRep_calc_cost_of_worker

- **Source:** `PP_ProdRep_calc_cost_of_worker.htm`

PP_ProdRep_calc_cost_of_worker 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 The actual cost of labor (direct and indirect) is calculated in the following manner: 
 

The cost percentage defined for each shift ´ the reported labor span ´ the standard cost (direct and indirect) of one work hour of the relevant skilled laborer or the standard cost (direct and indirect) of one work hour for unskilled labor at this work cell. 
 

The cost is added to the general cost of the work order, which affects the actual cost of parts whose costing option is W . 
 
## Note:

 

Labor time is calculated from times recorded in production reports for C , L , T and R operations. If the operation is not defined for inventory/cost control, costs are calculated according to the standard times defined for the operation.
