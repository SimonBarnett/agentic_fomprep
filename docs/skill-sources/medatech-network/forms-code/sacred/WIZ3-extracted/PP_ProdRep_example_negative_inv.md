# Example of negative inventory:

 

There is a report of 20 units of the "Table" part for the "Sawing" operation. Consequently, 20 units of "Table" are added to inventory in the "Sawing" operation. The next operation is called "Sanding," and as 30 units of "Table" have been reported, 30 units are added to inventory in that operation. 
 

Whereas inventory contains only 20 units of "Table" in the "Sawing" operation, 30 units are reduced as a result of the next production report, leaving a deficit of 10. This deficit appears as negative inventory .
