# PP_Project_Management_exp_job_percentage

- **Source:** `PP_Project_Management_exp_job_percentage.htm`

PP_Project_Management_exp_job_percentage 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The system divides the quantity of work hours required for the activity between the designated employees according to the ratio of their percentages of full-time. 
 
## Example:

 

Two employees are assigned to an activity, one who works on the project 30% of full-time and the other 20%. The activity requires 100 work hours. These hours will be divided between them at a ratio of 3:2 (30%/20%). Thus, the first employee will work 60 hours and the second 40 hours.
