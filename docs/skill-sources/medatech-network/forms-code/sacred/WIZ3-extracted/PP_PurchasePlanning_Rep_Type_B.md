# PP_PurchasePlanning_Rep_Type_B

- **Source:** `PP_PurchasePlanning_Rep_Type_B.htm`

PP_PurchasePlanning_Rep_Type_B 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Parts assigned Replenishment Type B are required in fixed quantities over regular intervals. This is called static demand , as it is not influenced by the volume of sales orders (for the part or its parent part) during the period in question. 
 

The planning programs relate to these parts according to their part type. If the part is purchased (Type R ), MRP does not prepare issues for its routine consumption; rather, purchase planning plans for it directly according to its defined usage parameters. If the part is manufactured (Type P ), MRP opens work orders for it according to its routine consumption definitions, and issues are prepared for these work orders as usual.
