# PP_SAddiscounts

- **Source:** `PP_SAddiscounts.htm`

PP_SAddiscounts 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A customer has been assigned an overall discount of 10%, whereas the representative customer has been assigned an overall discount of 5%. 
 

 ·        When discounts are not cumulative, the customer receives an overall discount of 10% (as the customer's discount overrides the representative's). 
 

 ·        When discounts are cumulative, the customer receives an overall discount of 15% (10%+5%).
