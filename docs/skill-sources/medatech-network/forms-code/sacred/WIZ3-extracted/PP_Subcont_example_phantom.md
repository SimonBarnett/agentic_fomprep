PP_Subcont_example_phantom

When kitchen cabinets are produced in your factory, cabinet doors enter the parent part as separate child parts. 
 

When kitchen cabinets are produced at a subcontractor, the subcontractor assembles the cabinet doors from wood boards, hinges, handles and formica that you supply. 
 

In the first case, when you report the production of kitchen cabinets, the Backflush program reduces from the subcontractor warehouse the appropriate number of cabinet doors. In the second case, when you record a GRV for kitchen cabinets received from the subcontractor, the Backflush program reduces from the subcontractor warehouse the appropriate number of boards, hinges, handles and formica.
