# PP_Subcont_model_TL

- **Source:** `PP_Subcont_model_TL.htm`

PP_Subcont_model_TL 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 T operations are handled like regular factory operations (i.e., R operations); they are performed by a subcontractor and upon receipt from the subcontractor, the resulting parts enter inventory with the new operation, and the relevant child parts are reduced. 
 

 L operations accommodate the requirements of both factory and subcontracted production: 
 

 ·        when performed in the factory, an L operation is handled like an R operation. 
 

 ·        when performed by a subcontractor, it is regarded as an intermediate step – no inventory transactions are created until a T operation is performed and the processed parts are returned to the factory.
