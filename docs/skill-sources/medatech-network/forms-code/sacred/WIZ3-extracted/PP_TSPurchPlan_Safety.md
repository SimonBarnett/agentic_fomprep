# PP_TSPurchPlan_Safety

- **Source:** `PP_TSPurchPlan_Safety.htm`

PP_TSPurchPlan_Safety 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The Safety Stock column, in the Purchase/MRP Parameters for Part form, is used to record the minimum quantity of parts that must always remain in inventory in Type D warehouses. 
 

Purchase planning also handles cases where a future inventory balance drops below the safety stock level any time during the planning period.
