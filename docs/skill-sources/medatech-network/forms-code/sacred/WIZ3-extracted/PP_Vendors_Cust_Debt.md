# PP_Vendors_Cust_Debt

- **Source:** `PP_Vendors_Cust_Debt.htm`

PP_Vendors_Cust_Debt 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Tax should be withheld from vendors that are also customers only with regard to their vendor-related transactions (e.g., purchase invoices processed in the system). When customer invoices (negative sums) and purchase invoices are flagged for payment in the same payment document, the customer invoices are flagged automatically in the Vendor ' s Cust Debt column of the Payment Details form. Consequently, withholding tax is calculated for the total amount of the purchase invoices.
