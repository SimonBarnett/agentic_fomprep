- 
 
 
 

If payments are quarterly, they always cover three months, but different contracts are likely to have different payment dates, depending on when the contract went into effect. The installments for one contract could cover March - May, June - Aug., etc., while the installments for another cover Feb. - Apr., May - July, etc. 
 

 Exception: The very first period is likely to be somewhat longer than three months, since the service contract does not necessarily begin at the beginning of a month. So, if a service contract begins on Nov. 15 and the first payment is calculated in Dec., the customer's first installment covers the period Nov. 15 through Feb. 28. The second installment covers March 31 - May 31, and so on.
