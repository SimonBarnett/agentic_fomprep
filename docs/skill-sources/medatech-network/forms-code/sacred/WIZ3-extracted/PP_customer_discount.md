# PP_customer_discount

- **Source:** `PP_customer_discount.htm`

PP_customer_discount 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A part's extended price is $100 and the customer receives a part discount of 20% for this item and an overall discount of 10%. 
 

First, 20% will be deducted from the extended price of the line item, leaving the price at $80. Then, an additional 10% will be deducted, leaving a final price of $72.
