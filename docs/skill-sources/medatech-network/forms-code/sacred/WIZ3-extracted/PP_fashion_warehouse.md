# PP_fashion_warehouse

- **Source:** `PP_fashion_warehouse.htm`

PP_fashion_warehouse 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A fashion outlet warehouse belongs to your company; thus it is included in inventory and costing calculations, and shipments from the factory to the warehouse are not billed. 
 

A franchise warehouse belongs to a customer; thus its goods are not subject to inventory valuation or costing control, and shipments to the warehouse are billed. 
 

For more information, see the Fashion Wizard .
