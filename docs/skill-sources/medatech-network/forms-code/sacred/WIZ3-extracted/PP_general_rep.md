# PP_general_rep

- **Source:** `PP_general_rep.htm`

PP_general_rep 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

The general representative is a predefined customer in the system, to which you can assign price lists, discounts, special part prices and bonuses. All of these values may be automatically applied to all customers in the system, following the rules of the price and discount hierarchies, and based on the value of the constant that determines whether or not discounts are cumulative. 
 

The general representative is useful when you want to offer a special deal to any customer that lacks more specific price definitions. For example, at the end of a sales season, you can link the general representative to a special price list containing end-of-season prices for winter fashion items. This list will apply to all customers that do not have another price list or special part price in effect for those items (their own or via a representative customer). The general representative price list takes precedence over the Base price list.
