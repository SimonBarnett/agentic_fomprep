# PP_item_discount

- **Source:** `PP_item_discount.htm`

PP_item_discount 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 Item discounts apply to an individual line item in a sales document. They derive from two sources: 
 

 ·        the part discount applied to a specific item purchased by the customer 
 

 ·        the family discount applied to all parts purchased by the customer that belong to the designated part family.
