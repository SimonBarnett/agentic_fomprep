# PP_laboratory_warehouse

- **Source:** `PP_laboratory_warehouse.htm`

PP_laboratory_warehouse 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

You need to define a repairs warehouse when it is company policy, in the course of service calls, to fix defective parts in the factory and return the repaired parts to the original customer (rather than providing replacement parts). In such a case, the inventory in the repairs warehouse belongs to the customer that originally purchased the part. Therefore, goods in this warehouse should not be included in inventory valuations. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        If company policy is to give the customer replacement parts and to sell the parts that were returned (after repairing them) to another customer, you can store the defective/repaired parts in any regular D warehouse. Since such parts are company inventory, the warehouse in question should be included in inventory valuations. 
 

 ·        For more details, see the Serial Numbers Wizard .
