## Examples

 

 ·        The system has calculated 150 units of Part A in the North Warehouse, whereas the physical count indicates that there are actually 152 units. When the line is approved, the warehouse balance of Part A is increased by 2 units. 
 

 ·        There is a calculated quantity of 2003 units of Part B in the South Warehouse, while the physical count was of 1999 units. When the line is approved, the warehouse balance of Part B is reduced by 4 units.
