PP_revise_retro_example 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

Let's say you've broken down a $1000 invoice amount into two installments of $500 each, due January 31 and February 28, respectively. You flag one of the installments, reconciling it with a receipt dated January 31. A month later, you break down the remaining invoice amount into another two installments of $250 each (for February 28 and March 31), reconciling the first with a new receipt dated February 28. Finally, on March 31 you reconcile the last installment with yet another receipt.
