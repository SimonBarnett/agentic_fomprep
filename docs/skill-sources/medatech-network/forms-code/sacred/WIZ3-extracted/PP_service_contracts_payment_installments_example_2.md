If payments are quarterly, they always cover three months; however, different contracts will generally have different payment dates, according to the date the contract went into effect. The payments for one contract may be for March-May, June-August, etc., whereas for another contract they are for February-April, May-June, etc. 
 

 Exception: The first period is likely to be slightly longer, since a service contract does not necessarily begin at the start of a quarter. Consequently, if a service contract begins on November 15 and the first payment is calculated in December, the first payment will cover the period between November 15 and February 28. The next payments will be for March 31, May 31, and so on.
