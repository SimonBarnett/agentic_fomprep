# PP_special_price

- **Source:** `PP_special_price.htm`

PP_special_price 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A special part price differs from the regular price in the customer price list. Throughout the period in which the special price is valid, list prices and item discounts do not apply (however, the customer's overall discount remains in effect). 
 

Setting a special price is useful for sale items, where the price is already low and you don't want the regular item discount to apply.
