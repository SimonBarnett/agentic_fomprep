# PP_special_pricelist

- **Source:** `PP_special_pricelist.htm`

PP_special_pricelist 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A special price list is one whose part prices are final, so that item discounts do not apply (although the customer's overall discount remains in effect). 
 

Special price lists are useful for sale periods, where prices are already low and you do not want regular item discounts to apply. They are often assigned to a specific sales document.
