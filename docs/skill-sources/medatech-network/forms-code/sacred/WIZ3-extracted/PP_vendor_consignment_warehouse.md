# PP_vendor_consignment_warehouse

- **Source:** `PP_vendor_consignment_warehouse.htm`

PP_vendor_consignment_warehouse 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

A vendor consignment warehouse is used to store goods belonging to the vendor, without affecting company inventory. The transfer of goods from the consignment warehouse into a company warehouse is recorded in a billable GRV. It is the responsibility of the vendor to maintain inventory balances and to replenish stock when necessary.
