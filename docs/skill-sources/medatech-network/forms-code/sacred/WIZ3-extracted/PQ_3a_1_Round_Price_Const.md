# Rounding the Total Price

 

You can round the total quoted price to the nearest whole unit of currency, or to the nearest ten sub-units. Example. 
 
## To round the total quoted price:

 

 ·        Enter the Financial Constants form, move to the RoundOrd constant, and assign one of the following values: 
 

 1 - Rounds to the nearest whole currency unit 
 

 2 - Rounds to the nearest ten sub-units 
 

 RoundOrd is preset at 0, such that price quotations are not automatically rounded. 
 
## Note: 

 

This constant also affects total prices in sales orders and in shipping documents.
