# To create a new price quotation from an existing one:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quotations for Customers form and retrieve the quote you want to copy. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Follow-up tab). 
 

 2 .  Select the Quote Number and copy it to the clipboard (press Ctrl-C ). 
 

 3 .  Move to a new record (press PgDn ) and enter the References tab. Paste the quote (press Ctrl-V ) in the Quote to Copy line. 
 

 4 .  Choose the type of sale, if desired.
 Tip: To add a new sale type, enter the Types of Sales form. 
 

 5 .  Specify the customer for which the price quotation is intended. A new Quote Number will be filled in automatically. 
 

 6 .  Choose a customer contact, if one has not been defined. The quote printout will be addressed to that person.
 Tip: You can define a default contact for all price quotations to this customer in the Customer Contacts form, a sub-level of the Customers form. 
 

 7 .  The present date appears automatically as the quote date. Revise it as necessary. 
 

 8 .  The quote's expiration date (derived from the SQuoteDays constant) appears automatically. Revise it as necessary.
