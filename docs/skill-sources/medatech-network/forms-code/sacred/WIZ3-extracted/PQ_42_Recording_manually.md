# To record a new price quotation:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quotations for Customers form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Follow-up tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the customer for which the price quotation is intended. The Quote Number will be filled in automatically. 
 

 3 .  Choose a customer contact, if one has not been defined. The quote printout will be addressed to that person.
 Tip: You can define a default contact for all price quotations to this customer in the Customer Contacts form, a sub-level of the Customers form. 
 

 4 .  The present date appears automatically as the quote date. Revise it as necessary. 
 

 5 .  The quote's expiration date (derived from the SQuoteDays constant) appears automatically. Revise it as necessary. 
 

 6 .  In the Price tab, a check mark appears in the Copy All column, indicating that all parts designated in the Itemized Quotation sub-level form and their details will be copied automatically to any sales order based on the quote. To itemize orders manually, remove the check mark.
