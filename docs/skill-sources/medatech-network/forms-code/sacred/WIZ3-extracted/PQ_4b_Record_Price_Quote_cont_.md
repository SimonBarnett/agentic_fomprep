 7 .  In the References tab, select the price list, if any, on which the quote is to be based. 
 

 8 .  Choose the type of sale, if desired.
 Tip: To add a new sale type, enter the Types of Sales form. 
 

 9 .  If the goods are to be delivered to a site other than the customer's main address, specify the shipping address in one of the following ways: 
 

 ·      Designate a customer site in the Site column of this form (in the References tab). OR 
 

 ·      Record the shipping address manually in the Shipping Address sub-level form. 
 

 10 .  If you work with projects, click to link the quote to a project or project activity. 
 

 11 .  In the Follow-up tab, record the date by which the price quotation must be sent to the customer. 
 

 12 .  Make sure that the payment terms displayed in the Payment Info tab are correct. These terms will be copied to any sales orders based on this quote, and from there to customer invoices. 
 

 13 .  To revise or delete remarks pertaining to the price quotation, enter the Price Quotations - Remarks sub-level form. 
 

 14 .  If someone else is going to be handling the quote, use the Internal Dialogue sub-level form to record specific instructions regarding the quote. These instructions are for internal use and will not be seen by the customer.
