# Pricing Guidelines

 

You can consult the following resources when pricing an item manually: 
 

 ·        To view all discounts currently in effect for the item in question (i.e., part discount, family discount), select Discounts Applying to Item from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 ·        To view all prices in effect for this customer and part, enter the Part Price Options sub-level form. If the customer is linked to a representative customer, the relevant prices and discounts will appear in this form. 
 

 ·        To view price quotations including this part that were prepared for other customers, enter the Additional Quotes for Part sub-level form. 
 

 ·        To view all active price quotations for this customer, enter the Active Price Quotations form, sub-level of Price Quotations for Customers . 
 

 ·        To calculate the profitability of this part (or of the entire order), see the Profitability Wizard . 
 

 ·        For more information on prices and their sources, see the Prices and Discounts Wizard .
