# Printing Price Quotations: Before You Begin

 
## To check the status that appears on the printout:

 

 1 .  In the Statuses for Price Quotations form, retrieve the status assigned to the price quotation. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Additional Status Definitions sub-level form and check the status description. Revise as necessary. 
 
## To check the remarks that appear on the printout:

 

 1 .  In the Price Quotations for Customers form, retrieve the desired quote. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Price Quotations - Remarks sub-level form. The text appearing in this form will appear as a remark on the printout. Make sure that it is correct. 
 

 3 .  If you are printing quotes with item remarks , move to the Itemized Quotation sub-level form. Make sure that the remarks displayed in its sub-level, Quoted Item - Remarks , are correct for each item. Explanation. Revise them if necessary.
 Note: The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable). 
 

 4 .  Return to the Price Quotations for Customers form.
