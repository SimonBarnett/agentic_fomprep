# Revising a Final Price Quote

 

As long as a price quotation has a status that allows revision, it can be updated without limitation in the Price Quotations for Customers form. Once a quote is final, there are two ways to revise it: 
 

 ·        If the quote has not yet served as the basis for a sales order, you may be able to reverse its finalization by changing its status. The quote can then be freely revised in the Price Quotations for Customers form. 
 

 ·        Final price quotations can be partially revised using the Revisions for Final Price Quotes form. For example, you can change its Expiration Date and its Copy All column. 
 To partially revise a final price quotation: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Revisions for Final Price Quotes form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the price quotation you want to revise. 
 

 3 .  Make the desired revision.
 Note: The expiration date may only be revised if the quote has not yet served as the basis for a sales order.
