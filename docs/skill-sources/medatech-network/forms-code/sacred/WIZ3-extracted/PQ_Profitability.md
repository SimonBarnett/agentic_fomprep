- 
 
 
 
# To calculate prices based on a desired markup:

 

 1 .  Clear the Unit Price column, if it contains a price. 
 

 2 .  Check the accuracy of the sales rep and any discounts awarded, as these affect the final price. 
 

 3 .  In the Markup (%) column (of the upper-level form), record the desired markup. 
 

 4 .  Select Estimate/Recommend Prices from the list of Direct Activations to have Priority calculate recommended prices based on the specified markup. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Itemized Quotation sub-level form. The price recommended by Priority for each quoted item appears in the Estim ' d/Rec ' d Price column. 
 

 6 .  Flag the Approve Estimate/Rec column to have the recommended price become the quoted price. The price will be copied into the Unit Price column when you leave the column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        The profit on the entire quote appears in the Actual Profit column of the upper-level form. 
 

 ·        The profit on individual line items appears in the Actual Profit column of the Itemized Quotation sub-level form. 
 

 ·        To view the breakdown and calculation of recommended prices, select Analysis of Estim'd/Rec'd Prices from the list of Direct Activations (of the upper-level form).
