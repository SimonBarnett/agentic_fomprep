# Price Quote Versions

 

 Priority allows you to save versions of any price quote and compare them. 
 
## To save a quote version:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quotations for Customers form and retrieve the quote in question. 
 

 2 . Select Save Version from the list of Direct Activations . The Current Version ( Follow-up tab) is increased by 1 and details of the current quote (i.e., the saved version) are copied into the Price Quote Versions sub-level form and its sub-levels. 
 
## To compare two versions of a quote:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quotations for Customers form and retrieve the quote in question. 
 

 2 .  Select Comparison of Quote Versions from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .  Indicate the two versions to compare. 
 
## To print a quote version:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quote Versions sub-level form of Price Quotations for Customers and move to the version in question. 
 

 2 .  Select Print Price Quote Version from the list of Direct Activations .
 D ual-currency users: If you wish to print the quote version in English and are working in another language, click Print Quote Version-Foreign Lang instead.
