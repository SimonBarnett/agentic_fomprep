# To record a packing slip for stock:

 
## Note:

 

Click if the goods you are packing are already packed (in smaller crates) . 
 

 1 .  Enter the Packing Slip form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the General tab). 
 

 2 .  In the Customer Number column, specify Goods . The Packing Slip No. is assigned automatically. 
 

 3 .  Today's date is filled in automatically as the packing date. Change it if necessary. 
 

 4 .  Click the General tab. The warehouse in which the goods have been packed is filled in automatically, but this may be revised. Designate a warehouse bin, if desired.
 Note: If goods are taken from more than one warehouse, designate the physical location of the packed goods. When itemizing the packing slip, indicate each part's original warehouse.
