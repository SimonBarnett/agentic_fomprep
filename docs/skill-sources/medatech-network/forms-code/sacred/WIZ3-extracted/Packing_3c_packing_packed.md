# Packing Packed Goods

 

In some cases, several packed crates (each with its own packing slip) are included in a principal crate, which is accompanied by a main packing slip. The main packing slip is automatically itemized by the contents of the crates included. You can prepare a main packing slip for stock or for a specific customer. 
 
## Note:

 

All crates involved must be in the same warehouse. 
 
## To prepare a main packing slip:

 

 1 .  Enter the Packing Slip form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the General tab). 
 

 2 .  In the Customer Number column, do one of the following: 
 

 ·      If the goods are packed for stock, specify Goods . 
 

 ·      If the goods are packed for a specific (regular) customer, choose the customer number. 
 

 The Packing Slip No. is assigned automatically.
