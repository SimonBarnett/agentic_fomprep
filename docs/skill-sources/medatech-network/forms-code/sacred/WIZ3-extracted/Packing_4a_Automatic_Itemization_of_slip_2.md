# To itemize the packing slip:

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      To pack for a single order , in the Packing Slip form, click the References tab and specify either the internal order number or the customer's purchase order number. 
 

 ·      To pack for several orders , enter the Orders for Packing Slip sub-level form and specify the appropriate order numbers. 
 

 ·              To pack for individual order items , enter the Choose Order Items sub-level form and flag the Choose Item column for each relevant item. 
 

 2 .  Click if the packed goods are for a project. 
 

 3 .  The packing slip is itemized according to the designated sale order(s). Enter the Packed Items sub-level form, and make any necessary changes. You may delete items from the list and add items that do not appear. 
 

 4 .  If you work with projects, click to link an item to a project or project activity. 
 

 5 .  For each packed item, Work Order (or lot), Warehouse and Bin are filled in automatically. Make sure they are correct.
