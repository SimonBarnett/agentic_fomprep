# To itemize the packing slip:

 

 1 .  Enter the Link Packing Slips to Document sub-level form. This form lists all crates that are recorded in final packing slips, earmarked for this customer (or packed for stock), and located in the warehouse and bin specified in the upper-level form. 
 

 2 .  For each crate that is to be included in the principal crate, flag the In Document column. Consequently, the packing crates will be listed in the Packing Slips in Document form (another sub-level). 
 

 3 .  Enter the Packed Items form (a third sub-level). The packing slip is itemized with all parts in the designated crates. 
 

 4 .  For each item, approve the packed quantities: 
 

 ·      If the SCratesQuant constant is set at 1 , the Approve column is flagged automatically, and the quantity is filled in automatically. 
 

 ·      Otherwise, flag the Approve column manually, and the quantity will appear automatically. 
 

 Tip: To change the value of the SCratesQuant constant, which will affect future packing slips, enter the Logistic Constants form. 
 

 5 .  If you work with projects, click to link an item to a project or project activity. 
 

 6 .  Return to the upper-level form.
