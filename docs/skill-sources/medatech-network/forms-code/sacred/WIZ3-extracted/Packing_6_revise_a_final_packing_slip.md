# To revise/cancel a final packing slip:

 

 1 .  Enter the Packing Slip form, and retrieve the packing slip in question. 
 

 2 .  Select Reopen Document from the list of Direct Activation s . The document will return to its initial status. 
 

 3 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      To revise the packing slip, make any necessary changes, and then change the status to Final . OR: 
 

 ·      To cancel the slip, enter the Packed Items sub-level form and check that all quantities are zero. Then, return to the upper-level form, and change the status to Canceled .
