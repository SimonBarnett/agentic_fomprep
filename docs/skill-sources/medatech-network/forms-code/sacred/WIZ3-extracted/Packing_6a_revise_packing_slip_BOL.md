# Revising a Packing Slip for a Crate in a Bill of Lading

 

To revise (or cancel) a packing slip for a crate in a bill of lading, you must first delete the crate from the bill of lading. 
 
## To delete a crate from a bill of lading:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Shipments form, and retrieve the desired bill of lading. 
 

 2 .  If the bill of lading is final, select Reopen Document from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .  Enter the Shipped Items sub-level form. For each item belonging to the crate in question, remove the check mark from the Approve column. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Packing Slips in Document form (another sub-level), and delete the problematic crate (press Ctrl+Delete ). 
 
## To revise/cancel the packing slip:

 

 1 .  Enter the Packing Slip form, and retrieve the packing slip in question. 
 

 2 .  Select Reopen Document from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Make any necessary changes to the packing slip, and then change the status to Final . OR 
 

 ·      To cancel the slip, enter the Packed Items sub-level form and check that all quantities are zero. Return to the upper-level form, and change the status to Canceled . 
 

 4 .  If desired, return to the Customer Shipments form, and reincorporate the crate in the bill of lading.
