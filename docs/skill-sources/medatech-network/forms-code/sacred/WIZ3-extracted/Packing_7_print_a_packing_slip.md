# To print a packing slip:

 

 1 .  Enter the Packing Slip form, and retrieve the packing slip in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Customer Details sub-level form and check the customer's shipping address, which will appear on the printed packing slip. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Remarks sub-level form, and check the remarks recorded there. 
 

 4 .  Return to the upper-level form, and select Print Packing Slip from the list of Direct Activations .
 D ual-currency users: If you wish to print the packing slip in English and are working in another language, click Print Packing Slip-Foreign Lang. instead.
