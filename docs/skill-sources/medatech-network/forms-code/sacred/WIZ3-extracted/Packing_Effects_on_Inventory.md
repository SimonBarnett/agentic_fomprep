# Effects on Inventory

 

When goods are packed for stock or earmarked for a specific customer, the following occurs: 
 

 ·        The balance of the unpacked inventory, which has a status that allows it to be sold (generally Goods ), is decreased in the warehouse in question. 
 

 ·        When goods are packed for a specific customer, the balance of the part allocated to that customer is increased in the warehouse in question. That is, the status of the packed quantity is changed to the customer number. 
 

 ·        When goods are packed for stock, their status is changed to Packed . 
 

 ·        If the goods are packed for a specific sales order, the quantity displayed in the Qty Packed/InternRep column of the Order Items form is increased. 
 
## Note:

 

If packing takes place in a plant-floor warehouse, inventory balances are affected only after the Backflush program is run.
