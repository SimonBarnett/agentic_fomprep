 6 .  For each packed item, the system displays the Balance to Pack (the order balance less any goods already packed for this customer) and the Warehouse Balance (the balance of this part in the designated work order, warehouse and bin). Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      To pack as much of the balance to pack as is permitted by the warehouse balance, flag the Approve column. The Quantity column will be filled in automatically. 
 

 ·      Select the Crate Type Code and specify the number of packed crates in the Packing Crates (No.) column. The Quantity column will be filled in automatically. 
 

 ·      Otherwise, specify the packed quantity manually in the Quantity column. 
 

 7 .  To record serial numbers for the packed part, use the Serial Numbers in Transaction or Auto Recording of Serial Nos. sub-level form.
