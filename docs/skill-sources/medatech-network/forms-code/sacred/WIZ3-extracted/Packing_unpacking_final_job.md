# Unpacking Goods from a Packed Crate

 

If you transferred a packed crate (recorded in a final packing slip) from Warehouse A to Warehouse B (or from the customer to a warehouse), and now you want to unpack some of the goods in their new location, you need to open a new packing slip based on the original one. 
 
## To open a new packing slip to replace the old one:

 

 1 .  Enter the Packing Slip form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the General tab). 
 

 2 .  In the Customer Number column, specify the customer for which the goods were originally packed. The Packing Slip No. appears automatically. 
 

 3 .  Today's date appears automatically in the Date column. Revise it if necessary. 
 

 4 .  Click the References tab and specify the Current Warehouse (and Current Bin , if relevant), that is, the warehouse in which the crate being unpacked is now located. 
 

 5 .  Fill in the rest of the packing slip details in the normal manner.
