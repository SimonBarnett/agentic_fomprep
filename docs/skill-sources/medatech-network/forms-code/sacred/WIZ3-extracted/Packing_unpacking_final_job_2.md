 6 .  Enter the Link Packing Slips to Document sub-level form. 
 

 7 .  Flag the In Document column next to the packing slip number of the original crate (i.e., the one you want to unpack. 
 

 8 .  Enter the parallel Packed Items sub-level form. 
 

 9 .  Select the parts and their quantities that will be included in the new packing slip: 
 

 ·      For parts that are removed in their entirety from the new packing crate, remove the check mark from the Approve column, and make sure that 0 appears in the Quantity column. 
 

 ·      For parts that will partially remain in the new packing crate, specify the remaining quantity in the Quantity column. 
 

 ·      For parts that will remain completely in the new packing crate, flag the Approve column and the full quantity will appear automatically. 
 

 Note: After making any change in the composition of the new packing crate, the status of the original packing slip will change automatically to Unpacked . This status does not allow the original packing slip to be linked to any other document. 
 

 10 .  If the unloaded parts are intended for a customer, open a customer shipment document specifying the current warehouse. If you are moving them to a different company warehouse, open a warehouse transfer document.
 Tip: For instructions, run the Customer Shipment/Return Wizard or Warehouse Transfers Wizard , respectively.
