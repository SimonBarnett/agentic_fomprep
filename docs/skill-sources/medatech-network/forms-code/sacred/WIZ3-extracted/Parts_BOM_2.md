# Restricting Customized Bills of Materials

 

When recording a price quotation or sales order, you can create a customized bill of materials to alter a part at the customer's request. If there is a specific child part that should never be deleted from a custom bill of material for this part, you can put a restriction on it. 
 
## To restrict deletion of a child part from a custom BOM:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Catalogue form and retrieve the desired parent part. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Custom BOM for Item: Conditions sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Move to the child part in question. 
 

 4 .  Flag the Deletion Warning column. A warning message will appear whenever an attempt is made to delete this child part from a custom BOM. 
 
## Note: 

 

To make the warning into an error message for any group of users, run the Privilege Explorer . For more details, run the Privilege Explorer Wizard .
