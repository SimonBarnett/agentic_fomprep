# Defining a Part Manually

 
## Important: 

 

Once a transaction of any kind has been recorded for the part, it will be difficult to change its type, units or conversion ratio. Consequently, incorrect designation of these columns can have severe consequences for your future work in the system. Make sure that all your definitions are accurate. 
 
## To define a part manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Catalogue form. 
 

 2 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Record a Part Number and Description . 
 

 ·      Select a Number Template (in the Details tab) and record a Description of the part. A Part Number will be assigned by the system, according to the pattern defined in the selected template.
 Tip: Click to define a new template. 
 

 3 .  Click the Additional Details tab. Your username appears automatically in the Assigned To column, and the new part is assigned the In Use status. You can transfer handling of the part to another employee at any point in the following process by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 4 .  Specify the part's Type : 
 

 ·      R – a raw material or “buy” item. 
 

 ·      P – a processed part or “make” item. 
 

 ·      O – any other type of part (e.g., non-inventory parts such as legal fees or shipping costs).
