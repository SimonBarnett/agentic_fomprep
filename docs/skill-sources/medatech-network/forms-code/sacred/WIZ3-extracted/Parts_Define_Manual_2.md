 10 .  Click the Details tab and assign the part to a part Family . The general part family ( 0 ) appears automatically, but may be revised.
 Tip: Click to define a new part family. 
 

 11 .  Link the part to a Base Product , if needed. 
 Tip: Click for a detailed explanation of base products. 
 

 12 .  Record a Preferred Vendor for the part (if relevant). 
 

 13 .  Click the Prices & Costs tab and record a List Price for the part (alternatively, you can wait until you update the base price list). 
 

 14 .  Click the Specs tab and select up to twenty different specifications that define the part.
 Tip s : 
 

 ·      Click to define new part specifications or add new spec values. 
 

 ·      You can also use the Part Specifications form to assign (and view) specifications that define parts. 
 

 15 .  Enter the Parts - Remarks sub-level form and record any remarks pertinent to the part. These remarks will appear for this part in any document in which it is included (when printed in an extended format, such as W/Extended Part Desc. ).
 Note: Click to block revision of part remarks in documents. 
 

 16 .  Use the parallel Part Remarks for Internal Use sub-level form to record a brief message that will pop up whenever the part is specified in an inventory document.
