# To enter the Explorer from a form:

 

 1 .  Move to the Part Number column. 
 

 2 .  Press Shift+F6 , or right-click the Choose button. 
 

 3 .  Open the levels of the Explorer until you find the desired part. Part details are displayed in the right window of the Explorer. 
 

 4 .  Select the part and click OK to retrieve its number into the Part Number column.
