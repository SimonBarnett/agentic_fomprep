# Defining Part Families

 

Every part belongs to a part family, for the purpose of tracking sales, purchasing, inventory and service. For example, you can award your customers a discount on all items belonging to a particular part family, or simultaneously flag all parts belonging to a certain family as work hours. 
 
## To define a part family:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Families form. 
 

 2 .  Record the part family code in the Family column. 
 

 3 .  Record a brief Family Description (e.g., furniture, office supplies). 
 

 4 .  If the parts belonging to this family are work hours, flag the Work Hours column. Only items belonging to such a family can be reported as work hours for a service call or project. 
 

 5 .  If you maintain family types, designate the Family Type Code .
 Tip: Define new family types in the Family Types form.
