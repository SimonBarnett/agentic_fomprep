# Assigning Payment Terms to Customers

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the customer in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Customer tab and choose the desired payment terms. This will then be the default payment term in any sales document opened for this customer.
