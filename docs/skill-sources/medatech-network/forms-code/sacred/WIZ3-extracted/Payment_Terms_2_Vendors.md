# Assigning Payment Terms to Vendors

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Parameters for Vendors form and retrieve the vendor in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the General tab. Move to the Payment Terms column and choose the desired payment terms. This will then be the default payment term in any purchase document opened for this vendor.
