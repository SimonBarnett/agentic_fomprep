# Assigning Payment Terms in Invoices

 

 1 .  Enter the appropriate invoice form and retrieve the desired invoice. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Choose the desired Payment Terms Code . 
 
## Note:

 

To manually record payment terms for an invoice (e.g., a specific payment schedule), use the Payment Installments sub-level form.
