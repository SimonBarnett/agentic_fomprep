## Setting Up Payment Terms

 
## Before You Begin

 

 ·        Make sure that GL accounts for customer and vendor discounts have been created (in the Chart of Accounts form) and that they have been assigned to the Discounts to Customers and Discounts to Vendors default accounts (in the Default Accounts form). 
 
## To set up payment terms:

 

 1 .  Enter the Payment Terms form. All predefined payment terms appear.
 Note: You can change any properties of predefined payment terms. 
 

 2 .  To define any additional payment terms, open a new line and fill in the Payment Terms Code and Payment Terms columns.
 Note to dual-currency users: If you are working in a language other than English and intend to produce financial documents in English, define an English title for each payment term in the Paymt Terms (Lang 2) column. Only those payment terms that have an English title will appear correctly in English printouts of financial documents. 
 

 3 .  To calculate payments from the end of the month rather than from the invoice date, flag the End of Month column. 
 

 4 .  Fill in the Months and Days columns. 
 

 5 .  To break payments down into installments, record the number of installments in the No. of Payments column. 
 

 6 .  To indicate a discount for payment within a certain number of days, fill in the Early Payment Disc. and Days for Discount columns.
