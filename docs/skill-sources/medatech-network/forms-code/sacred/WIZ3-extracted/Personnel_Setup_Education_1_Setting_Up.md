# Setting Up Education

 
## Institutions and Departments

 

In order to maintain records of the educational backgrounds of employees and job candidates, you need to list educational institutions, as well as specific departments or faculties at those institutions and the degrees or certificates they grant. You also need to categorize the institutions by type (e.g., university, training college). 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        As a start, record institutions and departments that many of your employees have attended or are currently attending. You can record additional institutions and departments later as needed. 
 

 ·        If your organization grants certificates you need to include it as an educational institution and record the certificates you award.
