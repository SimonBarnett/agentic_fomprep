# Recording an Invoice Involving Prepaid Expenses/ Deferred Income

 

When you record an invoice for a transaction involving prepaid expenses or deferred income, you also define the period (start and end dates) over which the expenses/income are transferred to the P&L accounts. The total amount is then divided into equal monthly payments, due at the end of each month within the designated period. Example . 
 
## Note: 

 

Whenever you base an invoice on a customer's service contract (as recorded in the Customer Service module), the period over which deferred income is to be distributed is filled in automatically based on the period covered by the contract payment.
