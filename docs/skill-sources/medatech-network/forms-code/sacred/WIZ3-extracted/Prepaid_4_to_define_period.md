# To define accounts and due dates for the prepaid expenses/deferred income:

 

 1 .  Record the invoice. 
 

 2 .  In the Invoice Items sub-level form, for each item involving prepaid expenses or deferred income, specify the relevant prepaid expense account in the Expense Account column (vendor invoice), or the deferred income account in the Income Account column (customer invoice). 
 

 3 .  The target P&L account is taken automatically from the Default Target Acct column in the Chart of Accounts form. To use a different account, record it in the TargetAcc-PrepaidExp (vendor invoice) or the Target-DeferredInc (customer invoice) column.
