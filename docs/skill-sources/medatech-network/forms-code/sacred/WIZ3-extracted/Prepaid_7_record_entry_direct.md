# To record an entry in the journal:

 

 Tip: Before creating the journal entry, run the Manual Journal Entries Wizard to make sure you are acquainted with the process. 
 

 1 .  Enter the Entry Journal form and record the Transaction Date . A temporary number with the prefix T is assigned to the entry. 
 

 2 .  In the Transaction column, choose the entry code suitable for this type of prepaid expense or deferred income. The entry will be itemized automatically according to the pattern defined for that entry code.
 Tip: If you cannot find a suitable entry code, click to define a new one. 
 

 3 .  Record the amount of the entry in the Sum column. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Itemized Entry sub-level form and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 5 .  Make sure the Credit or Debit amount of the first item is correct, as well as the Due Date (in the General tab). 
 

 6 .  Click the Add ' l Info tab. The target P&L account is filled in automatically in the Target Acct-Prepaid column. Revise it, if necessary. 
 

 7 .  Press Page Down to move to the next entry item. Repeat steps 5 and 6 for each item. 
 

 8 .  Return to the upper-level form, and post the entry to the ledger by selecting Post Journal Entry from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 
## Notes:
