# Creating a Base Price List

 

There are two ways to record prices in the predefined Base price list: directly in the part catalog or via the price list facility. 
 

Use the part catalogue when: 
 

 ·      unit prices are the same regardless of quantity purchased 
 

 ·      the part is always sold in the same unit of measure 
 

 ·      you are not interested in maintaining a history of prices. 
 

Use the price list facility when: 
 

 ·      prices differ according to minimum quantity 
 

 ·      the part is sold in varied units of measure 
 

 ·      you want to maintain a history of the list prices valid on different dates. 
 
## Note:

 

Any prices recorded in the part catalogue are automatically added to the Base price list in the price list facility. Similarly, any prices recorded for the valid Base price list in the price list facility are automatically added to the part catalogue, provided the price is for a quantity of 1.
