Prices and Discounts - Recording Prices for the Base Price List 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record prices for the Base price list:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Lists form and move to the Base price list . 
 

 2 .  Enter the Part Prices sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Designate the Part Number and the minimum Quantity for which the price is valid. 
 

 4 .  The part's buy/sell unit appears automatically in the Unit column. If the part is sold in a different unit, change the unit.
 Note: Make sure a conversion ratio between the buy/sell unit and the unit you have recorded has been defined in the Unit Conversions form. 
 

 5 .  Specify the Price of the part and the Currency to which this price refers. 
 

 6 .  Attach a Picture of the part if desired. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To record different prices for different quantities of the same part, record each quantity on a separate line, indicating the correct minimum quantity and price. Example. 
 

 ·        Click to use other price list tools.
