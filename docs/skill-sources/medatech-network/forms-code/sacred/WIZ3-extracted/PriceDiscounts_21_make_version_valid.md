# Putting a Price List Version Into Effect

 

Only one version of a price list can be valid at a time. By putting a given version into effect, the existing list is automatically invalidated. Parts and prices in the valid version appear in the Part Prices sub-level of the Price Lists form. Other versions are accessed in the Price List Dates form; their parts and prices can be viewed in their own Part Prices sub-level form. 
 
## To put a price list into effect:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Lists form and move to the price list in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Price List Dates sub-level form and move to the date of the desired version. 
 

 3 .  Flag the Valid column.
