Prices and Discounts - Adding Parts to an Existing Price List 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To add parts to an existing price list:

 

 ·        Run the Prepare Price Lists program. 
 
## To assign prices to the new parts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Lists form and retrieve the price list in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Part Prices sub-level form and move to the lines for the newly added parts. 
 

 3 .  Revise quantities and units as necessary and record prices for the new parts.
