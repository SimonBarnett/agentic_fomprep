# Pricing a Linked Item Differently from its Base Product

 

Even if your price lists are maintained purely on the basis of base products, Priority allows you to change the price of each individual item linked to a base product, and exclude it from automatic price updates. This process is fully reversible, so it may be used as a temporary or seasonal measure. 
 
## To assign a different price to a linked item:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Lists form and move to the desired price list. 
 

 2 .  If the Base Products Only column is flagged, remove the check mark. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Part Prices sub-level form and move to the desired item. 
 

 4 .  Change the item's Price . 
 

 5 .  To prevent the item price from being updated automatically by the base product price in the future, remove the check mark from the Update by Base Prod column. 
 

 6 .  If necessary, return to the upper-level form and reflag the Base Products Only column. 
 Note: This allows you to update linked products online without running the Use Base Prod. to Update Prices program.
