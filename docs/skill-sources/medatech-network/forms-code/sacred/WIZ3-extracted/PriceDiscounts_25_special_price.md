# Setting Special Part Prices

 

You can offer customers special prices for individual parts (e.g., sale items) over a limited period. During this period, any part or family discounts for these items are not applicable; however, the customer's overall discount remains in effect. 
 
## To record special part prices in the customer record:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Special Part Price sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Record the part, its special price and the period over which this price is valid. 
 
## To record special part prices for a customer in a single-level form:

 

 1 .  Enter the Special Part Prices for Custs form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the number of the customer for which you want to define a special part price. 
 

 3 .  Specify the number of the desired part. 
 

 4 .  Record the special price and the period over which this price is valid. 
 

 Note: You can also use this form to view and/or change the special prices defined for a batch of customers.
