# Assigning a Price List to a Customer

 

You can assign a price list to any number of customers. Moreover, each customer can be assigned several price lists; if a given part appears in more than one price list, the system will take the price from the one with the highest priority. If priorities have not been assigned to customer price lists, the price will be taken from the most recent one. 
 
## To assign a price list from the customer record:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  In the Customer Price Lists sub-level form, specify the code of the price list that you wish to assign to the customer. 
 

 3 .  Define the Priority of this price list in relation to other price lists defined for the same customer and part. The lower the number, the higher the priority. 
 
## To assign it via the price list record:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Lists form and retrieve the desired price list. 
 

 2 .  In the Customers Assigned the PriceList sub-level form, specify the number of the customer to which you wish to assign the price list. 
 
## To assign a price list in a single-level form:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Price Lists form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the number of the customer to which you wish to assign a price list. 
 

 3 .  Specify the code of the price list that you wish to assign to the customer. 
 

 Note: You can also use this form to view and/or change the price lists assigned to a batch of customers.
