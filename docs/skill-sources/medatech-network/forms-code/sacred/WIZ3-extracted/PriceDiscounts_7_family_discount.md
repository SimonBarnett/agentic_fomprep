- 
 
 
 
# To assign a part family discount to a customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Family Discount sub-level form and specify the desired Family . 
 

 3 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Choose the appropriate discount code. 
 

 ·      Specify the percentage of the offered discount in the Discount% column and indicate the period in which the discount is valid ( Start Date and Expir. Date ). A discount code will be assigned automatically, and the Type of Discount will reflect the percentage offered and the period covered. 
 

 Note: If you record the discount manually, you can reuse the same record to specify a later discount, simply by changing the details of the awarded discount. The Type of Discount will automatically be revised accordingly. 
 
## Tip:

 

To reverse this process (i.e., to remove the part family discount from several customers), run the Delete Family Discounts program.
