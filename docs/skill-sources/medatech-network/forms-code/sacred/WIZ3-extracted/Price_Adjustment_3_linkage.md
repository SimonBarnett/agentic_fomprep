# Recording Linkage in an Order

 

Prices can be linked in a sales order, blanket sales order, purchase order or blanket purchase order. In blanket orders, linkage definitions are copied into each individual sales or purchase order based on the blanket order. 
 

Prices are adjusted automatically in Priority for: 
 

 ·        Orders whose part prices are in a foreign currency, where the total is charged in the local currency (a dual-currency order) 
 

 ·        Orders in the local currency which are linked to a foreign currency (or index) 
 

 ·        Orders in the local currency which are linked to a currency basket (not available for blanket purchase orders) 
 

 ·        Sales orders (and blanket sales orders) which are linked to a price list with a linkage date.
