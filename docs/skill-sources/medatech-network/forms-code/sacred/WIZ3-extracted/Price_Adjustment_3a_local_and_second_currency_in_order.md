# To record a dual-currency order:

 

 1 .  Enter one the following forms to record the order: 
 Sales Orders 
 Purchase Orders 
 Blanket Sales Orders 
 Blanket Purchase Orders . 
 

 2 .  In the Price tab, select your local currency in the Currency column. 
 

 3 .  Specify the order items in the sub-level form. All unit prices will be recorded in the foreign currency defined for the part. All extended prices will be converted to the local currency. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Unit prices remain in the foreign currency in the corresponding shipments/receipts and invoices. Regardless of how the linkage option is defined, price adjustments are always made to the extended prices. 
 

 ·        For purchase documents, the linkage date initially determines whether extended prices in the document continue to be adjusted. However, this may be manually changed by flagging/removing the flag from the Adjust Prices column. 
 

 ·        You can also record a dual-currency price quote to a customer or from a vendor (in the same manner). Prices will be adjusted in any order based on the quote.
