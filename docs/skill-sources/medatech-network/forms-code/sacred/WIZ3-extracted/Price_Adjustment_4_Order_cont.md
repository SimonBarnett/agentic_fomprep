# To link an entire order to a single currency or price index:

 

 1 .  Enter one the following forms to record the order: 
 Sales Orders 
 Purchase Orders 
 Blanket Sales Orders 
 Blanket Purchase Orders . 
 

 2 .  In the Payment Info tab, select the foreign currency or price index in the Linked Curr column. 
 

 3 .  The current exchange rate for that currency/index appears in the Base Rate column. Revise as necessary. 
 
## For sales and blanket sales orders only: 

 

 4 .  To change the default linkage date , revise the value in the Price Linkage column. 
 

 5 .  If the Negative Linkage column is flagged, linkage will take place even if the exchange rate has dropped. A check mark appears automatically if the LinkNegative constant is set to 1 , but may be removed. 
 

 6 .  To define a minimum percentage of exchange rate fluctuation, beneath which linkage will not take place, specify the percentage in the Minimum Linkage% column. 
 
## For sales orders only: 

 

 7 .  To change the default linkage option , record the desired value in the Link Option column. 
 

 8 . To set a date from which to start calculating linkage prices for the order, record the desired date in the Link as of Date column. 
 

 9 . To link only a portion of the price to the foreign currency or index, record the appropriate percentage in the % of Linkage column. If no percentage is specified, the entire price will be linked. 
 
## Note:

 

You can also link a price quote to a customer or from a vendor (in the same manner). The linked currency and base rate will be copied into any order based on the quote.
