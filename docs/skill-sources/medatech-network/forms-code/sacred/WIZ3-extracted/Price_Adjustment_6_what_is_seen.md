# Viewing Adjustment Results

 

Price adjustments differ when unit prices are recorded in a foreign currency, as opposed to when they are linked to a foreign currency, index, currency basket or price list. 
 

When unit prices are recorded in a foreign currency, and the total price is recorded in the local currency (dual-currency orders), adjusted extended prices (based on fluctuating exchange rates) appear in each subsequent document – the sales or purchase order, the shipping document or goods receiving voucher, and the purchase or customer invoice. The defined linkage date determines the last exchange rate that is used. For instance, if linkage is to the shipment date, the exchange rate in the invoice will be identical to the one in the shipping document. 
 

In contrast, when prices are linked, the adjustment only appears in the invoice (even if the linkage date has been defined as the order date or the shipment/receipt date). That is, prices in the shipping document or GRV are identical to those of the order, which are the same as those in the blanket order. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        The Adjust Prices column in the shipping/receiving document and in the invoice indicates whether prices have been adjusted. In a purchasing document, this flag may be removed or added, to manually determine whether to adjust extended prices when part prices are in a foreign currency and the document itself is in the local currency. 
 

 ·        When prices are linked to a currency basket, linkage may be stopped prior to the designated linkage date (e.g., one month before the order's due date), depending on how the basket has been defined. 
 

 ·        Once an order has been invoiced, you can view the linkage calculations for each line item in the Linkage Calculations sub-level of the relevant invoice item form.
