# Linking a Contract to a Price Index

 

You can link an entire service contract, or individual contract items, to a price index. The contract must be recorded in the local currency. You can also ensure that contract payments are always linked to the highest price index; that is, if the index drops, the previous one is used instead. 
 
## To link an entire contract:

 

 1 .  Record the contract in the Service Contracts form. 
 Tip: For help in opening a service contract, run the Service Contract Wizard . 
 

 2 .  In the Terms tab, record the Base Index , which will determine the starting point for price adjustments to all payments for this contract. 
 

 3 .  To link a certain percentage of each payment amount to the index, record that percentage in the % of Linkage column. 
 
## To link a contract item:

 

 1 .  Enter the Items in Contract sub-level form and move to the line in question. 
 

 2 .  In the Linked Currency column, select the price index to which to link the item. 
 

 3 .  The current value for the index appears in the Base Rate column (i.e., the base index). Revise as necessary. 
 
## To use the highest price index:

 

 1 .  Return to the Service Contracts form. 
 

 2 .  Click the Terms tab and flag the Max. Linkage column.
