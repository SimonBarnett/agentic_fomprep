# Linking a Contract to a Foreign Currency

 

You can link an entire service contract, or individual contract items, to a foreign currency. The contract must be recorded in the local currency. You can also ensure that contract payments are always linked to the highest exchange rate; that is, if the rate drops, the previous one is used instead. 
 
## To link an entire contract:

 

 1 .  Record the contract in the Service Contracts form. 
 Tip: For help in opening a service contract, run the Service Contract Wizard . 
 

 2 .  To link a certain percentage of each payment amount to the currency, click the Terms tab and record that percentage in the % of Linkage column. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Linkage Details sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  In the Curr column, specify the currency in question. 
 

 5 .  Specify 100 in the Percent column. 
 

 6 .  The exchange rate in effect on the displayed Base Date appears automatically in the Base Rate column. Revise if necessary.
 Tip : To automatically fill in the exchange rate in effect on a desired date, change the Base Date to that date.
