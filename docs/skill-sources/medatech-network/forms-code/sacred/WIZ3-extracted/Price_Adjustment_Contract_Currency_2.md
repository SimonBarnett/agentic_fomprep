Price Adjustment Wizard - Linking Contract to Currency.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 To link a contract item: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Items in Contract sub-level form and move to the line in question. 
 

 2 .  In the Linked Currency column, select the currency to link the item. 
 

 3 .  The current exchange rate appears in the Base Rate column. Revise as necessary. 
 
## To use the highest exchange rate:

 

 1 .  Return to the Service Contracts form. 
 

 2 .  Click the Terms tab and flag the Max. Linkage column.
