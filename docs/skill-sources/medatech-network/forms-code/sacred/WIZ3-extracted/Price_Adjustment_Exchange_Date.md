# Setting the Exchange Rate Date for Payments

 

When the currency of the payment differs from the currency of the invoice being paid, you need to determine the exchange rate date to be used when adjusting the payment sum. You can use the exchange rate in effect on the date of the invoice, or the one in effect on the date of the payment. In the latter case, an exchange rate adjustment entry will be created automatically to cover the difference, when necessary. 
 
## To set the exchange rate date for payments:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Constants form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the InvPayExch constant and choose the desired value. 
 
## Note:

 

The constant affects all types of vendor payments, as well as receipts of payments from customers. If you want the adjustments in a specific payment to be calculated according to the other date (i.e., the date that was not chosen for this constant), you can indicate this in the payment form itself.
