# To link a service contract to a currency basket:

 

 1 .  Record the contract in the Service Contracts form in the local currency. 
 Tip: For help in opening a service contract, run the Service Contract Wizard . 
 

 2 .  To link a certain percentage of each payment amount to the currency, click the Terms tab and record that percentage in the % of Linkage column. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Linkage Details sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  In the Curr column, specify each currency (or price index) to be included in the basket. 
 

 5 .  Specify the Percent of each currency in the basket. The sum of percentages for all currencies must equal 100. 
 

 6 .  The exchange rate in effect on the displayed Base Date (usually the date the contract goes into effect) appears automatically in the Base Rate column. Revise if necessary.
 Tip : To automatically fill in the exchange rate in effect on a desired date, change the Base Date to that date.
