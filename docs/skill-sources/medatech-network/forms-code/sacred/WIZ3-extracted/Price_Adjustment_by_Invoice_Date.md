# Linking a Sales Order to Prices on the Invoice Date

 

 Priority allows you to link a sales order to the prices that are in effect on the invoice date. When the invoice is linked to the order, part prices are assigned according to the usual hierarchy: 
 

 ·        The special part price offered 
 

 ·        The valid customer price list 
 

 ·        The base price list 
 

 ·        A vendor price list defined as a price source for customers. 
 
## To link an order to prices on the invoice date:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders form and record the order. 
 

 2 .  Click the Payment Info tab, and define the linkage date in the Price Linkage column as 6 .
 Note: Leave the Price List column blank.
