# Linking a Sales Order (or Blanket Sales Order) to a Price List

 

For both sales orders and blanket sales orders, Priority offers the option of linking order prices to a price list. Whatever linkage date is defined determines which price list date is used. Example. 
 
## To link a sales order to a price list:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders form and record the order. 
 

 2 .  In the References tab, select the Price List in question. 
 

 3 .  Click the Payment Info tab, and define the linkage date in the Price Linkage column (choose a value between 4 and 6 ). 
 
## To link a blanket sales order to a price list:

 

 1 .  Enter the Blanket Sales Orders form and record the blanket order. 
 

 2 .  In the Payment Info tab, select the Price List in question. 
 

 3 .  Define the linkage date in the Price Linkage column (choose a value between 4 and 6 ). 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Prices in the order will be taken from the designated price list for the list date currently in effect. 
 

 ·        Regardless of how the linkage option is defined, adjustments are always made directly to the unit price.
