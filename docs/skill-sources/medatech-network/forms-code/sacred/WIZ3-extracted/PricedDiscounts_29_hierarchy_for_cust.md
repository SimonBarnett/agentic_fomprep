# Viewing Prices and Discounts

 

 Priority provides a variety of ways of viewing price and discount data either per part, per price list, per customer or per base product. 
 
## What do you want to do? 

 

 View the special part prices and item discounts awarded to each customer 
 

 Compare and/or update part prices in all price lists 
 

 View and/or update special prices and discounts 
 

 View a summary of the prices and discounts applying to a specific customer 
 

 View the prices of parts purchased from vendors and resold to customers (including parts sold in commission sales). 
 

 View prices per base product
