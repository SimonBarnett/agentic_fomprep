# To view and/or update special prices and discounts:

 
## To update part family discounts:

 

 ·        Enter the Customer Discounts for Families form and retrieve by part family or discount code. 
 
## To update overall discounts:

 

 ·        Enter the Customers' Overall Discounts form and retrieve by discount code. 
 
## To update part discounts:

 

 ·        Enter the Customer Discounts for Parts form and retrieve by part number. 
 
## To update special part prices:

 

 ·        Enter the Special Part Prices for Custs form and retrieve by part number.
