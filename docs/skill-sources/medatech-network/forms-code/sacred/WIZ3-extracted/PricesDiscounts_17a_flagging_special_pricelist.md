# Creating a Special Price List

 

A special price list usually has particularly low prices and is used on special occasions (e.g., end-of-season sales). As a result, no customer part or family discounts are applied; overall discounts, however, remain in effect. 
 

Creating a special price list simply involves opening any price list and flagging it as special. 
 
## To flag a price list as a special price list:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Lists form and retrieve the desired price list. 
 

 2 .  Flag the Special Part Prices column.
