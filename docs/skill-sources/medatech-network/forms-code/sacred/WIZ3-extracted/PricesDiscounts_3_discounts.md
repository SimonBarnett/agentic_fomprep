# Setting Up Discounts

 
## Single Versus Cumulative Discount

 

In any sales document, the customer can be assigned an item discount and an overall discount . When the customer is assigned a representative customer , discounts (and prices) are inherited from the representative customer. If discounts of any kind are defined for the general representative , these may apply to all customers in the system. 
 

When multiple discounts have been defined for parts and/or customers, including representative customers and the general representative, you need to choose between: 
 

 ·        applying a single discount (the highest one in the hierarchy) to sales documents OR 
 

 ·        applying all discounts cumulatively. Example. 
 

The default is for a single discount to apply. 
 
## Tip:

 

Click to view the hierarchy of discounts in Priority . 
 
## To make discounts cumulative:

 

 ·        Enter the Logistic Constants form and change the value of the SAddDiscount constant to 1 .
