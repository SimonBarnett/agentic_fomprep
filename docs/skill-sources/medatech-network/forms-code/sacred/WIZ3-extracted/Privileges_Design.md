# Assigning Design Privileges

 

 Priority allows you to customize the design of forms, menus and documents as needed by your company. 
 

Form design includes the revision of regular and screen-painted forms, the organization of sub-level forms and Direct Activations and the linking of record colors to column values. 
 

Menu design includes the rearranging or hiding of menu items. 
 

Document design includes the formatting of reports and printouts. 
 

 Note : 
 

Permission to design forms and menus is assigned separately from permission to design documents. 
 
## To assign design privileges:

 

 1 .  Enter the User Permissions form and retrieve the desired user. 
 

 2 .  Click on the Permissions tab. 
 

 3 .  Flag the Form and Menu Design column and/or the Document Design column to assign the desired privileges.
