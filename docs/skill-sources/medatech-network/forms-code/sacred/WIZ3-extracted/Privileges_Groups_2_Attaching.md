# Assigning Users to Groups

 

Users are assigned to a default group by linking them to the desired group leader. You can also override these definitions for a particular company by assigning the user to a different group when working in that company. 
 
# To assign group leaders to users: 

 

 1 .  In the Users form, choose the desired user. 
 

 2 .  In the Group Leader (Privs) column, choose the desired group leader. 
 
# To assign users to a different group when working in a particular company: 

 

 1 .  In the Users form, choose the desired user. 
 Note: This user cannot be a group leader or a member of the "tabula" privilege group. 
 

 2 .  Move to the Privilege Groups by Company sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Select the Company for which you want to assign a different privilege group and specify the desired Privilege Group . 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        You can only assign privilege groups by company if you are working on an Oracle or SQL (rather than Tabula) installation. 
 

 ·        You cannot assign privilege groups by company if users are defined via the Windows Active Directory. 
 

 ·        You can add users to the group and take users out of the group whenever you need to, even after privileges have been assigned. Privileges will be reassigned automatically. (Each user will be affected once he or she exits and re-enters Priority .) 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen .
