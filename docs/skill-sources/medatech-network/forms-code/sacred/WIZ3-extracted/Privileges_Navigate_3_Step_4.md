Privilege Explorer - Navigating in the Privileges Explorer.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 3 .  Navigation is quite similar to the Windows Explorer. When a menu is closed (as is now the case), a + (plus sign) appears to the left of the colored symbol. This indicates that entities (e.g., forms) are included in the menu. 
Click the + to open the Main menu and see a list of the menus it contains. The + changes to a – (minus sign). Note that a colored symbol appears to the left of each menu. 
Note: If an X appears to the left of the Main menu (or any other menu), you will not be able to open it. In such a case, you must actually assign privileges to the menu before you can continue navigating. 
 

 4 .  Continue opening menus until you reach a form. 
 

 5 .  Browse until you find a form with a + sign to its left. This indicates that the form contains sub-level forms and/or direct activations. Click the + sign to see a list of them.

Remain in the Privilege Explorer .
