# When There is a Billing Customer

 

When there is a billing customer, you have several options. 
 
## To both address the pro forma invoice to the billing customer and record that customer's account in the pro forma journal entry:

 

 ·        Specify the billing customer's number when opening the invoice. 
 
## To address the invoice to the regular customer and record the billing customer's account in the entry:

 

 ·        Specify the regular customer's number when opening the invoice. 
 
## To address the invoice to the regular customer and record that customer's account in the entry:

 

 1 .  Specify the regular customer's number when opening the invoice. 
 

 2 .  In the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form, designate the regular customer's account number in the A/R Acct to Charge . 
 
## To address the invoice to the regular customer and record a third party's account in the entry:

 

 1 .  Specify the regular customer's number when opening the invoice. 
 

 2 .  In the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form, designate the third party's account number in the A/R Acct to Charge .
