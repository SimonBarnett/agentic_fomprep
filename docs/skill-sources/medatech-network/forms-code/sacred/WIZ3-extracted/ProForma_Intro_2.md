# Working on a Cash Basis in Priority 

 

In brief, the steps to take when working on a cash basis are: 
 

 ·        Open a pro forma invoice on the basis of reported project hours and project payments (or on the basis of service calls or a service contract). By finalizing the invoice, a pro forma journal entry is opened automatically. 
 

 ·        Upon receipt of payment, record a customer receipt on the basis of one or more pro forma invoices. By finalizing the receipt, a journal entry is automatically opened for the receipt and reconciled with the pro forma entry. 
 

 ·        Open a multi-shipment invoice on the basis of one or more receipts. By finalizing this invoice, a regular journal entry is opened automatically for the invoice and reconciled with the entry for the receipt(s). 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can also open and reconcile pro forma entries manually. 
 

 ·        Dual-currency users: If postdated checks are received, do not record the multi-shipment invoice until they come due.
