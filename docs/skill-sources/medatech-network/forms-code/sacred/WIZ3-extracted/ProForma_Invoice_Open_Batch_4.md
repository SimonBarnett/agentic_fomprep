# To check the invoice items:

 

 1 .  Enter the Invoice Items sub-level form. The services offered, with their quantities and other details, are filled in automatically according to the document(s) linked to the invoice. 
 Note: If you revise the invoiced quantity manually, the original document will be flagged as billed, even though it was only partially invoiced. 
 

 2 .  Make sure that prices and discounts are correct.
 Note: If you set the percentage of overall discount in the upper-level form, the new discount percentage appears in the % Overall Discount column in this form. 
 

 3 .  The sales rep and the commission percentage due for the line item appear automatically. Revise them as necessary. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Invoice Item - Remarks sub-level form and check the remarks that appear there. Revise or add to them, as desired.
 Notes: 
 

 ·      The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable). 
 

 ·      To include these remarks in the printout of the invoice, select a suitable print format .
