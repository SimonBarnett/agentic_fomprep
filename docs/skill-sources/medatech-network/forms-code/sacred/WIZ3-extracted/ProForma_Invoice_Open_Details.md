# To record additional details:

 

 1 .  Return to the Pro Forma Invoices upper-level form. 
 

 2 .  Click the Price tab. The Total Before Discount , Overall Discount , Sales Tax and Amount Owing appear automatically, according to the itemized invoice. If necessary, revise the overall discount or the amount owing. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  The payment terms appear automatically. To revise them, do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Select a different Payment Terms Code . 
 

 ·      Define payment installments for this particular invoice in the parallel Payment Installments sub-level form. These installments will override the customer's default payment terms. 
 

 Tip: To change the default payment terms for future transactions with this customer, enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and click the Customer tab. 
 

 5 .  Make sure that the Payment Date is correct.
 Note: If payment is in installments, this should be the date of the first installment.
