# Opening a Pro Forma Invoice for a Single Service Document

 

You can open a pro forma invoice by Direct Activation from within a specific service call or contract. 
 
## Note:

 

When billing for contract payments, the invoice date will be today's date. When billing for a service call, the invoice date will be taken either from the original document date or today's date, depending on the value assigned to the InvoiceDate constant in the Financial Constants form. 
 
## To prepare a pro forma invoice for a specific document:

 

 1 .  Enter the appropriate form for the document you wish to bill: 
 

 ·      Service Calls 
 

 ·      Service Contracts 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired document. Make sure its details are correct and that it has a status that allows for billing. 
 

 3 .  Select Prepare Pro Forma Invoice from the list of Direct Activations . You will receive a message informing you that a pending pro forma invoice has been opened; make a note of the temporary invoice number that appears.
