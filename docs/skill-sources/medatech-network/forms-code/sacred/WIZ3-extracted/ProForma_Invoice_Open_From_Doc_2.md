 Note: The invoice date determines the reference date in the pro forma journal entry. It is also taken into account in calculating the payment date. 
 

 5 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      To bill a sales order, click the References tab and specify the Order Number . Details are filled in automatically, but may be revised. 
 

 ·      To base the invoice on a single service document, fill in the Document Number column with the call or contract number and the document type. Details are filled in automatically, but may be revised. 
 

 ·      To base the invoice on multiple documents, leave the Document Number column empty and enter the Documents for Invoice sub-level form. Record a line for each document you want to bill in the current invoice. Return to the upper-level form and record a remark in the Details column, as needed. 
 

 6 .  If the invoice has already been recorded externally, specify its number in the External Inv. No. column. Because the external document has already been issued, the Printed column in the Price tab is flagged. 
 

 7 .  Check the Rep No. column to make sure that the correct sales rep appears.
 Note: If the invoice is for several documents that have been made through more than one sales rep, designate the main one here. When itemizing the invoice, indicate the sales rep for each line item. 
 

 8 .  Click the Financial Terms tab. Make sure that the Tax Code (for dual-currency users: VAT Code ) is correct. 
 

 9 .  The overall discount, applied to the entire invoice, is calculated automatically from the overall discount assigned to the service document or order. If you want to override that discount, or if no discount was given and you want to award one now, use the Set Overall Discount % column.
