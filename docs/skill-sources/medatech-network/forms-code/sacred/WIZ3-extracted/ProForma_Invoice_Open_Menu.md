# Recording a Pro Forma Invoice

 

There are various ways to record a pro forma invoice: 
 

 ·        You can use a program to prepare a batch of invoices for transactions linked to a given project (reported project hours and project payments, as well as service calls and contract payments linked to the project), or for groups of service documents (service calls and contracts). 
 

 ·        You can prepare an invoice for a single service document from within that document. 
 

 ·        You can open an invoice manually, itemizing it automatically on the basis of one or more service documents or a sales order. 
 

 ·        You can record and itemize the invoice manually. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Prepare a batch of pro forma invoices automatically 
 

 Prepare an invoice for a single service document automatically 
 

 Record an invoice and itemize it automatically on the basis of service documents or a sales order 
 

 Record and itemize an invoice manually
