# Recording a Multi-Shipment Invoice for the Receipt

 

Once the customer has remitted payment for a pro forma invoice and you have recorded a customer receipt for the payment, you need to open a multi-shipment invoice for the paid amount. For this purpose, you must prepare a multi-shipment invoice on the basis of one or more receipts. You can do so automatically (in a batch) or manually. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·      The following instructions focus on those steps unique to cash-based accounting, and disregard steps common to recording multi-shipment invoices in general. If you are unfamiliar with how to prepare multi-shipment invoices, run the Customer Invoices Wizard . 
 

 ·      If you are using the dual-currency package, these instructions also apply to the recording of export invoices. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Prepare multi-shipment invoices based on receipts in a batch 
 

 Record a multi-shipment invoice for receipts manually
