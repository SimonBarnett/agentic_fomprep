# To record a multi-shipment invoice (or export invoice, for dual-currency users) based on receipts manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Multi-Shipment Invoices form.
 Note: To record an export invoice, enter the Export Invoices form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the number of the customer in question. 
 

 3 .  Fill in the rest of the invoice information, as usual. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Link Receipts to Invoice sub-level form. A list of the customer's receipts that have not yet been linked to a multi-shipment invoice appears. 
 

 5 .  Flag those receipts for which you are now recording an invoice. 
 

 6 .  Enter the parallel Invoice Items sub-level form. The invoice is itemized automatically according to the pro forma invoices on which the flagged receipts were originally based. 
 

 7 .  Make sure the details in this form are correct. Revise or add to them, as desired. 
 

 8 .  Continue to record and finalize the invoice, as usual.
