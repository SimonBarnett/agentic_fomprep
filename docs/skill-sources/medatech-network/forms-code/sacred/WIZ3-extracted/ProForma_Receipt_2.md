# To record a receipt when working on a cash basis:

 

 1 .  Enter the Receipts form and record the Customer ' s Account from which payment was received. 
 

 2 .  Fill in the rest of the receipt information, as usual. 
 

 3 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      If payment is against one or more pro forma invoices recorded for this customer, enter the Flag Pro Forma Invoices sub-level form and flag the invoices in question in the Paid column.
 Note: Also flag invoices that are only partially paid. 
 

 ·      If payment is unrelated to a specific pro forma invoice, flag the Pay Pro Forma Debt column (in the Check Invoice Data tab), then enter the Invoice Amounts Being Paid sub-level form and record the received Sum . 
 

 4 .  Continue to record and finalize the receipt, as usual.
