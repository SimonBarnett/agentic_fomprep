# Defining a Billing Customer

 

For each customer, you can define a billing customer, against whose account the pro forma entry is recorded (even if the order or the pro forma invoice is addressed to the regular customer). 
 
## Note: 

 

This definition also affects the account that is debited by multi-shipment invoices. 
 
## To define the billing customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form, and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  Click the Customer tab and specify the billing customer in the Billing Cust. No. column. 
 

 Note : 
 

You can change the billing customer to a third party for specific orders or order items, shipping document, service calls or service contracts. This third-party billing customer will then appear in any pro forma invoice that bills these ordered items or documents.
