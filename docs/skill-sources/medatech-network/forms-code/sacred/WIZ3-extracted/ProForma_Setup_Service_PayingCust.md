# Addressing Invoices to the Billing Customer

 

Pro forma invoices that are prepared automatically (whether in a batch or by direct activation) can be opened for the customer recorded in the original service document or for that customer's billing customer. 
 
## To open invoices for the billing customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the regular customer (the one appearing in the service call or contract). 
 

 2 .  Make sure that a Billing Customer has been defined in the Customer tab. 
 

 3 .  Click the Invoices tab and flag the Inv. to Billing Cust column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        This definition also affects the customer recorded when multi-shipment invoices are opened automatically. 
 

 ·        If a third-party billing customer has been recorded for a particular order or order item, that will override the customer's default billing customer. If several different billing customers need to be charged, a separate invoice or credit memo will be opened for each one.
