# To remove a bundle from use or reactivate it:

 

 1 .  Enter the Product Bundles (Quotes/Orders) form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the line for the product bundle in question. 
 

 3 .  To take a product bundle out of use, remove the check mark from the In Use column; to reactivate it, flag this column.
