Production Report - Recording an Issue Manually 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record an issue manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Manual Issues to Work Order sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  In the Part Number column, choose the issued part. The Choose list includes all parts appearing in the work order's kit list that have been flagged for manual issue in the Parts form (if no such parts exist, all parts in the catalogue are displayed).
 Notes: 
 

 ·              You can also issue parts that are not defined for manual issue, though you will receive a warning message when you do so. 
 

 ·              If you are reporting production for repairs to a work order that was already completed, specify the manufactured part itself, the work order number and the quantity of parts to be repaired, then flag the Rework column. 
 

 3 .  Specify the Quantity of the part issued for the current operation.
 Note: If the issued part has a serial number, specify 1 . 
 

 4 .  Make sure the correct Part Revision number appears. 
 

 5 .  Make sure that all other inventory details are correct (e.g., Warehouse , Bin , Work Order/Lot , Status , Operation ). 
 

 6 .  If the issued part has a serial number, record it in the Serial Number column. 
 Note: You cannot assign a serial number here unless the manufactured part also has a serial number.
