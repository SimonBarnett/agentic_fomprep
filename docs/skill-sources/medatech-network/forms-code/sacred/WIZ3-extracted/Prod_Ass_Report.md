# Running Product Bundle Reports

 

You have the option of viewing the contents of product bundles with or without substitute components. 
 
## To view the contents of a single product bundle:

 

 1 .  Enter the Product Bundles (Quotes/Orders) form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the line for the product bundle in question. 
 

 3 .  Select Product Bundles or Product Bundles w/Substitutes from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To view the contents of multiple product bundles:

 

 ·        Run the Product Bundles or Product Bundles w/Substitutes report.
