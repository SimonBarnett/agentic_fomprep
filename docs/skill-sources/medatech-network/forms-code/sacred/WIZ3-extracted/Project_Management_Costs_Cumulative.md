Project Management - Updating Cumulative Costs 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To update cumulative planned costs and work hours for the project plan:

 

 1 .  Enter the Projects form and retrieve the desired project. 
 

 2 .  Select Calculate Cumulative Planning from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To update cumulative planned costs and work hours for a planning version:

 

 1 .  Enter the Projects form and retrieve the desired project. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Planning Versions sub-level form and select the desired version. 
 

 3 .  Select Calculate Planned Costs/Hours from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Note:

 

If the Automatic Cost Calc column has been flagged for the project in question (in the Planning tab of the Projects form), cumulative planned costs and work hours will be updated automatically whenever the project plan is updated and exited.
