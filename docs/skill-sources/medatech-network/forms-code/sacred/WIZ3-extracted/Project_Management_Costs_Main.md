# Viewing Cost, Revenue and Profit

 

You can view an activity's planned costs and work hours in its cumulative cost/hour columns. In order for this data to be correctly displayed, it must be updated after any changes to the project plan (or planning version). 
 

You can compare the planned costs/hours of a project and each of its activities with their actual calculated costs and reported hours in the Planning and Execution sub-level form. The data in this form is updated automatically whenever a work-hour report, transaction document or journal entry is linked to the project. 
 

Once material and human resources have been planned for activities, you can run a program that calculates the cumulative planned work hours of the SOW items to which the activities are linked. 
 

You can view revenue from a project (including its sub-projects), as well as actual and projected profit, based on total costs and revenues. You can also compare projects in terms of their costs, revenues and profits. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Update cumulative planned costs 
 

 View a project ' s planned and actual costs and work hours 
 

 View an activity ' s planned and actual costs and work hours 
 

 View the planned work hours of activities linked to SOW items 
 

 View project revenues and actual and projected profit
