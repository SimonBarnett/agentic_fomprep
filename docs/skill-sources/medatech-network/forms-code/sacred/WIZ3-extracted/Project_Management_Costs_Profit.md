# Viewing Project Revenue and Profit

 

You can view actual and projected revenue and profit for a single project, as well as details of each of the project's cost/revenue types in various sub-level forms (e.g., all the purchase invoices or customer invoices recorded for the project). You can also run reports that provide summary data for one or more projects. 
 
## To view the details of a project's revenue and profit:

 

 1 .  Enter the Projects form and retrieve the desired project. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Revenue and Profit sub-level form. The form displays: 
 

 ·              the project's total actual costs, total actual revenues, the resulting profit and a breakdown of actual costs into their cost types 
 

 ·              the project's total projected cost, total projected revenues, the resulting profit and a breakdown of projected costs into their cost types 
 

 ·              the project's total profit, calculated by subtracting the total combined cost (actual and projected) from the total combined revenue. 
 

 3 .  To view an itemized list for each type of actual or projected cost and revenue (e.g., customer invoices, open purchase orders) linked to the project, enter the corresponding sub-level form (e.g., Customer Invoices, Open Purchase Orders ). 
 
## To view this data in a report, select one of the following:

 

 ·        Project Profitability (BI) . 
 

 ·        Revenue and Profit per Project .
