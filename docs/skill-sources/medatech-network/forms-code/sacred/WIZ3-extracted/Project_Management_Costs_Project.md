Project Management - Viewing a Project's Costs/Hours 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To view a project's costs and hours:

 

 1 .  Enter the Projects form and retrieve the desired project. If you want to view costs for a planning version that is not in effect, enter the Planning Versions sub-level form and select the desired version. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Planning and Execution sub-level form. The form displays: 
 

 ·              the project's total planned costs, total actual costs (including production costs), the resulting balance and a breakdown of actual costs into their cost types 
 

 ·              the project's total projected cost and its component cost types 
 

 ·              the project's planned hours, reported hours and the resulting balance. 
 

 3 .  To view an itemized list for each type of actual or projected cost (e.g., open purchase orders) linked to the project, enter the corresponding sub-level form (e.g., Open Purchase Orders ). 
 
## Note:

 

You can also use the reports in the Planning and Execution Reports menu to view various cross-sections of data relating to planned and actual costs, and the reports in the Planned Cost Reports menu to view data relating solely to planned costs.
