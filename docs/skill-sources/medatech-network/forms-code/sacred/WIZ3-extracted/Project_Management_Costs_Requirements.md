Project Management - Calculating SOW Costs/Hours 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To calculate the work hours of items in a SOW document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Scope of Work (SOW) form and retrieve the desired document. 
 

 2 .  Select Calculate Cumulative Planning from the list of Direct Activations . The program will compute the planned work hours of every item linked to a project activity. 
 
## To view planned work hours:

 

 1 .  Enter the SOW Items sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the desired item. 
 

 3 .  Move to Full-Record Display Mode.
 Notes: 
 

 ·              Planned work hours are displayed in the Details tab. 
 

 ·              Cumulative planned hours represent the sum of the hours calculated for the item itself and the hours calculated for all sub-items.
