Project Management - Recording Work Hours and Expenses.3 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 8 .  Click the Hours/Expenses to Charge tab and check the price of the work hour/expense in the Price/Hour column. 
 

 9 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If you are reporting work hours: Record the time that you worked on the project in the From Hour and To Hour columns OR record the total number of hours in the Total to Charge column. 
 

 ·              If you are reporting an expense: Record the quantity of the part in the Total to Charge column (make sure that the Miles to Chrg column is blank). 
 

 10 .  If you are billing for travel time and/or mileage, flag the Add Travel Tm column. The default amount of travel time is added to the total hours to charge.
 Note : You can change the Travel Time to be charged, if necessary. 
 

 11 .  If you are billing for mileage, make sure that the default amount in the Miles to Chrg column is accurate.
 Note : Priority opens another line in this form to charge for the miles, using the price recorded for the "miles" part in the Part Catalogue form. After you have finished recording this report, you can view the added record by retrieving it by date and username.
