## To set the project's default mileage and travel time:

 

 1 .  Enter the Projects form and retrieve the project in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Additional Details tab. The Miles to Charge and Travel Time to Charge columns are filled in with the customer's default values. Revise them as necessary. 
 

 3 .  To maintain separate defaults for internal reports, record them in the Miles – Internal and Travel Time – Internal columns. 
 

 To set default mileage and travel time for a specific project site: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Projects form and retrieve the project. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Project Sites sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Record the Miles to Charge and Travel Time to Charge , as well as the internal mileage and travel time defaults, if desired.
