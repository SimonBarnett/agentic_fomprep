# To record the finished products manually:

 

 1 .  If the project is not already open, enter the Projects form and retrieve the new project. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Finished Products sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  For each product, designate the part number (in the Product Number column) and its required Quantity .
 Notes: 
 

 ·              If the products are serialized, record each part and its Serial Number on a separate line. 
 

 ·              Recording parts in this form does not affect their inventory level.
