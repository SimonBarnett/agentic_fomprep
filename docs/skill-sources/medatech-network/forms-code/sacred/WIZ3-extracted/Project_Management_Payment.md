# Recording Payment Installments for a Project

 

When you bill your customer for the project, payment installments that are due and that are free for billing will appear automatically in the invoices you prepare. 
 
## To record payment installments:

 

 1 .  Enter the Projects form and retrieve the desired project. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Payments for Project sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Record the Date on which payment is due. 
 

 4 .  Record the Payment Amount due. 
 

 5 .  If the payment is for a sales order, select the Order number.
 Note: The Choose list includes all sales orders linked to the project. 
 

 6 .  If payment is for a milestone activity, enter the Projects/Accounts sub-level form and specify the activity in question. In the upper-level form, the Checking column is flagged automatically.
 Note: When the activity is completed (i.e., 100% completion is reported), the flag is removed automatically, freeing the payment for billing. 
 

 7 .  If you want to hold back on billing the payment, flag the Checking column.
