Project Management - Authorizing Purchases 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To authorize purchases in the project:

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Enter the Projects form and retrieve the project; then enter the Project Planning sub-level form. 
 

 ·              Enter the Project Activities form and retrieve the activities for which you want to authorize purchases. 
 

 2 .  For each relevant activity, enter the BOM for Activity sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  For each required part, move to the Purch for Project column. If a default purchasing method was defined for the project, it appears automatically, but may be revised. Otherwise, select a purchasing method for the part. 
 

 4 .  If the quantity to be purchased differs from the required quantity, adjust the Purchase Quantity . 
 

 5 .  In the Vendor Number column, specify the vendor from which the part is to be purchased. 
 

 6 .  Authorize each part for purchase by flagging the Start Purchasing column.
 Note: Only flagged parts will generate a purchase demand or purchase order.
