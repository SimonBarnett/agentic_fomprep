# Recording Requested Changes

 

You can record requests from your customer for changes in the SOW document. This will assist you in the preparation of future versions, and in the maintenance of a precise record of the development process. 
 
## To record requested changes:

 

 1 .  Enter the Scope of Work (SOW) form and retrieve the SOW document in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the SOW Items sub-level form and move to the item for which the request was received. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Requests for Changes sub-level from and record the Request Date . 
 

 4 .  Record a brief description of the change in the Desc. of Request column. 
 

 5 .  In the Change Initiator column, specify the username of the company employee responsible for implementing the change. 
 

 6 .  Enter the Text sub-level to record a more extensive description of the requested change.
