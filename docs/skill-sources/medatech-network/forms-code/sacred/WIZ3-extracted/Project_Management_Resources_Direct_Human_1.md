# Planning an Activity's Human Resources 

 

You need to assign employees or teams to the activity, plan the number of work hours required and schedule the assigned workers. The planned cost is automatically calculated and added to the activity's total planned cost. 
 
## Tip: 

 

Click if you have not yet created project teams or listed their members . 
 
## To assign human resources to the activity:

 

 1 .  Enter the Project Planning sub-level form and move to the activity in question.
 Tip: Make sure you are in Full-Record Display Mode. 
 

 2 .  Click the Details tab. In the Number of Hours column, record the activity's required quantity of work hours. 
 

 3 .  In the Resource Names column, list all the assigned employees or teams, separating their names with commas (for example, SusanB,JohnS,JosephH,MaryM). Tips . 
 Note: Only one employee can be designated as the activity's Owner (i.e., assigned user), which affects the To Do List . 
 

 4 .  To indicate that an employee is assigned to the activity on a part-time basis, record the percentage of full-time in brackets after his or her name (e.g., George[50%]). The system will divide the total quantity of hours between the assigned employees, according to their percentages of full-time. Explanation. 
 Notes: 
 

 ·              To assign percentages of full-time, the SHrPercent constant in the Logistic Constants form must be set to 0 . Otherwise, the system will ignore the assigned percentages and assign each employee to the activity full time. 
 

 ·              If no percentage is recorded, the employee is assigned to the activity full time. 
 

 ·              If the employee is a team leader and all the team members are assigned as well, the percentage of full-time you record is split equally between all the team members. In this case, you may want to record a percentage higher than 100% (e.g., to assign a team of three on a full-time basis, record 300% ).
