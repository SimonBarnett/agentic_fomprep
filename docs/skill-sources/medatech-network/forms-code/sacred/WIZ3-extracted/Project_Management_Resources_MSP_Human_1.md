# Fine Tuning the Human Resource Plan

 

The data from MS-Project consists of the names of employees assigned to each activity, the percentage of full-time for each employee and the quantity of work hours the activity requires. When converted to Priority , the system automatically links each employee with his or her default work hour part (thereby determining the cost of the resource) or with the general default work hour part. 
 

The system also calculates the quantity of work hours each employee needs to devote to the activity, according to his or her percentage of full-time. Explanation. 
 

Once actual hours are reported, you can compare planned costs and hours to actual ones.
