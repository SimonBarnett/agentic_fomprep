Project Management - Planning Versions: MSP - Human Resources.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 To check the human resource data: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Project Planning sub-level form and move to the activity in question.
 Tip: Make sure you are in Full-Record Display Mode. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Resources for Activity sub-level form. The employees assigned to this activity are listed in the form.
 Note: If a team leader was assigned to an activity in MS-Project, the other team members may have been assigned automatically in Priority . Check that these additions are appropriate. 
 

 3 .  For each employee, check that the Work Hour Part Number is correct. 
 

 4 .  In the Cost/Hr column, check that the cost of the work hour is correct. 
 

 5 .  In the No. of Work Hours column, check that the allocated quantity of work hours is correct. 
 

 6 .  In the Ratio to Planned Hrs column, check that the employee has been assigned the correct proportion of work hours. Explanation . 
 

 7 .  If you have made any changes that affect planned costs and/or hours, you need to recalculate the cumulative planned costs and hours. Return to the Planning Versions form and select Calculate Planned Costs/Hours from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Note:

 

You may find that you need to add or delete data. Make the necessary corrections in the MS-Project file, and re-import it.
