# To record the material requirements of a project activity: 

 

 1 . Enter the Project Planning sub-level form and move to the activity in question.
 Tip: Make sure you are in Full-Record Display Mode. 
 

 2 . If the activity results in a product, click the Product tab and record the part number and its quantity. 
 

 3 . Enter the BOM for Activity sub-level form. If a product was specified, its child parts appear automatically. 
 

 4 . To add a part to the required materials list, open a new line and specify its number in the Child Part Number column. 
 

 5 . For every added part, do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If the planned quantity of the added part depends upon the quantity of the product, designate the ratio in the Ratio to Parent column. OR 
 

 ·              Specify a fixed quantity of the added part in the Child Part Qty column. 
 

 6 .  For every part in the list, check that its Unit Cost is correct. 
 

 7 .  To recalculate the activity's cumulative planned costs, return to the Planning Versions form and select Calculate Planned Costs/Hours from the list of Direct Activations .
