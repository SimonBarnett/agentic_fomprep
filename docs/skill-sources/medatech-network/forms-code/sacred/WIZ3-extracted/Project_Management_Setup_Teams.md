# Assigning Teams

 

There are two types of teams in Priority : company teams and project-specific teams. If you plan to assign entire company teams to project activities, you can set up the system so that by assigning the team leader, you automatically assign all team members to the activity, either including or excluding the team leader. This setting will affect all projects. 
 
## To automatically assign team members to an activity:

 

 1 .  Enter the Logistic Constants form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the SAllTeam constant and do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If team leaders in your system are fictitious users, specify 2 in the Value column. Then, when you designate a team leader, only the team members will be assigned to the activity. 
 

 ·              Otherwise, specify 1 . Then, when you designate the team leader, both the leader and the team members are assigned to the activity. 
 
## Note:

 

If you work with project-specific teams, you can set up automatic team assignment for each project team separately (you can only do so after opening the project).
