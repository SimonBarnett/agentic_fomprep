# Deleting a Pending Credit Memo

 

The following instructions apply to credit memos recorded separately in the Multi-GRV Invoices form. 
 

Pending credit memos are documents that have not yet been finalized and can be deleted from the system. 
 
## To delete a pending credit memo:

 

 1 .  Enter the Multi-GRV Invoices form and retrieve the desired memo. 
 

 2 .  Select Delete Pending Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To delete a group of credit memos:

 

 ·        Run the Delete Pending Multi-GRV Inv's. program.
