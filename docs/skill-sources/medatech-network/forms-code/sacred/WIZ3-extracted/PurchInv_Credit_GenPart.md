# Defining a General-Purpose Part

 

A general-purpose part can be used to record miscellaneous expenses or credits. Simply change its part description as needed. 
 
## To define the part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Catalogue . 
 

 2 .  Record a new Part Number . 
 

 3 .  Record an appropriate Description , such as “General” or “Miscellaneous.” 
 

 4 .  Click the Additional Details tab and assign the part Type “O”. 
 

 5 .  Do not check the Inv. Control column. 
 

 6 .  Check the Change Desc. column.
