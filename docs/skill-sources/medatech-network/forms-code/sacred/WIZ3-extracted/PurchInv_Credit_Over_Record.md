# Recording a Credit Memo for Overcharging

 

If there has been an overcharge but no inventory is being returned, you should record a credit memo manually. 
 
## To record a credit memo:

 

 1 .  Enter the Multi-GRV Invoices form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 2 .  Specify the number of the vendor that is crediting you. The Vendor Name is filled in automatically.
 Note: In the case of a one-time vendor, specify the one-time vendor number. Then revise the name in the Vendor Name column, and fill in the address and other details in the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  In the Date column, record the date appearing on the vendor's original credit memo. A temporary memo number (with the prefix T ) appears automatically in the Internal Invoice column. 
 

 4 .  In the Invoice Number column, record the number of the vendor's credit memo.
