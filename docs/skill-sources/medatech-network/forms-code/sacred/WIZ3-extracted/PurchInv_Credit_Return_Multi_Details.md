# Additional Details

 

 1 .  Move to the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  In the Code – Orig. Inv. column, enter the code of the original invoice in which you were billed for the returned goods (GRV). 
 

 3 .  In the Original Invoice column, press F6 to open the invoice form and retrieve the invoice in question. Then press F8 to copy its number back into this column. The current invoice will be reconciled automatically with the original invoice. 
 

 4 .  Move to the Invoice Items sub-level form and check the items to be credited. Make sure that all quantities for returned goods are negative. 
 

 5 .  If you want the corresponding line in the entry journal (created when the invoice is finalized) to belong to an Entry Item Group , specify the group code. 
 Note: Linking an entry item to a group facilitates data retrieval from the entry journal, as well as data sorting in the general ledger and in transaction reports. 
 

 6 .  Click to link the line item to a project or project activity .
