Purchase Invoices/Memos - Finalizing Debit Memos.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## Before you begin:

 

 1 .  Enter the Debit Memos form and retrieve the desired debit memo. 
 

 2 .  Click the Price tab and make sure that the Amount Owing is correct. 
 

 3 .  Click the References tab and make sure that the Entry Code is correct. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Debit Memo - Add'l Details sub-level form. Make sure that the Transaction Date is correct. 
 

 5 .  Return to the upper level form, click the References tab and check the memo's Status . You will only be able to finalize the memo if its current status allows for finalization (e.g., it is assigned the Approved status).
 Note: You must also be authorized to change the status of debit memos to Final . 
 
## To finalize the memo:

 

 ·        Select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To simultaneously finalize and print the memo, select Finalize & Print Debit Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen .
