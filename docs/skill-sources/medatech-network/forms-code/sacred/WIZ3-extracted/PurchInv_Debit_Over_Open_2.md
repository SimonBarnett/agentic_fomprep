 8 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If the memo is based on a multi-GRV invoice for which a GRV was recorded, it is itemized automatically with all line items from the original invoice that contain a discrepancy between the total price in the GRV and the total price in the invoice. Enter the Memo Items sub-level form and check the details of the overcharged parts. 
 

 ·              If the memo is based on a multi-GRV invoice for which no GRV was recorded, it is itemized automatically with all line items from the original invoice that contain a discrepancy between their order price and their price in the invoice. Enter the Memo Items sub-level form and check the details of the overcharged parts. 
 

 ·              If the memo is based on a vendor invoice, click to itemize the memo manually .
