# Itemizing the Invoice

 

The way you itemize the invoice depends on whether you have been billed for an entire order or for individual order items (from one or more orders). 
 
## To base the invoice on a single order:

 

 ·        Click the References tab and specify the Order Number . The invoice will be itemized automatically with all lines in that order. 
 
## To base the invoice on individual order items from one or more orders:

 

 1 .  Leave the Order Number column blank and enter the Choose Order Items sub-level form instead. The form displays all open order items recorded for the vendor in question. 
 

 2 .  For each order item that you want to include in the invoice, flag the Choose Item column. 
 

 3 .  If the billed quantity differs from the ordered quantity, specify the former in the Indicate Quantity column. 
 

 4 .  If you want to open more than one invoice line for the order item (to match the line items in the shipping document, e.g., when the same item is packed in different types of crates), designate the quantity for the first invoice item in the Indicate Quantity column and designate quantities for the other invoice items in the sub-level form, Create Additional Line Items . 
 
## Note:

 

The next time you enter the Choose Order Items form, the Already in Invoice column will be flagged automatically for any order item that was flagged for inclusion, and the quantity recorded in the invoice will appear in the Quantity in Invoice column.
