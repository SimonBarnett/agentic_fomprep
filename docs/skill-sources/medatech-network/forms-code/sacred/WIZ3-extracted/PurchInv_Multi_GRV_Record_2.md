Purchase Invoices/Memos - Recording a Multi-GRV Invoice Based on GRVs.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 5 .  Do one of the following 
 

 ·              To base the invoice on a single GRV, specify its number in the Document Number column of the References tab. The Details column is filled in automatically from the GRV, but may be revised. 
 

 ·              To base the invoice on more than one GRV, leave the Document Number column blank and enter the GRVs for Invoice sub-level form. Record a line for each GRV being billed in this invoice, designating either the internal document number or the vendor's shipping document. Return to the upper-level form and record a brief remark in the Details column, if desired. 
 

 Notes: 
 

 ·              If you have already linked the invoice to one GRV in the upper-level form and wish to link it to additional GRVs, you can record them in the GRVs for Invoice sub-level form. 
 

 ·              If a GRV is also being used to record the return of goods (using negative quantities), the returned goods are included in the invoice itemization and their cost is deducted from the total amount owing.
