# Recording a Multi-GRV Invoice Based on Orders

 

Record a multi-GRV invoice based on a purchase order, or even individual order items, when you are billed before you receive the shipment (e.g., in the case of imported goods). In this way, you can itemize the invoice automatically with the ordered items. 
 
## Note:

 

Once you receive the goods in question, you will need to link the GRV to the invoice. Click for details. 
 
## To record the invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Multi-GRV Invoices form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 2 .  Specify the number of the vendor that is billing you. The vendor's name is filled in automatically.
 Note: In the case of a one-time vendor, specify the one-time vendor number. Then revise the name in the Vendor Name column, and fill in the address and other details in the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form.
