# Linking a GRV to a Multi-GRV Invoice

 

You can link a GRV to a final multi-GRV invoice (for example, if the invoice was recorded and finalized before the goods were received). 
 
## To link an open GRV to an invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Goods Receiving Vouchers form and retrieve the desired open GRV. 
 

 2 .  Click the References tab and designate the desired invoice, specifying either the original Vendor Invoice number or the Internal Invoice number. 
 
## To link a final GRV to an invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Link Invoices/Memos to Documents form and retrieve the desired GRV. All received items that have not yet been invoiced will appear. 
 

 2 .  For each received item, specify the invoice that bills for the item and the invoice line on which it appears. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Once all items in a GRV have been linked to invoices, the Billed column in the Goods Receiving Vouchers form is flagged. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen .
