# Preparing a Multi-GRV Invoice

 

A multi-GRV invoice bills your company for the receipt of goods in one or more shipments, each recorded in a separate goods receiving voucher, or for ordered goods that have not yet been delivered. If you are recording an invoice for received goods, you can itemize it automatically on the basis of one or more GRVs. If you are recording an invoice for ordered goods that have not yet been received, you can itemize it automatically on the basis of the open orders. You can also itemize the invoice manually. 
 
## Note:

 

If you want the invoice to also serve as a goods receiving voucher, affecting inventory levels, click to open a vendor invoice instead. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Record a multi-GRV invoice on the basis of one or more GRVs 
 

 Record a multi-GRV invoice on the basis of one or more purchase orders 
 

 Record a multi-GRV invoice manually 
 

 Credit your company for returned goods in a multi-GRV invoice 
 

 Finalize a multi-GRV invoice 
 

 Revise a final multi-GRV invoice 
 

 Cancel a final multi-GRV invoice 
 

 Link a final multi-GRV invoice to an unbilled GR V 
 

 Delete a pending multi-GRV invoice 
 

 Print a multi-GRV invoice
