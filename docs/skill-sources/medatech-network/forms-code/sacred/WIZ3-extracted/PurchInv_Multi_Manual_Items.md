# To itemize the invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Invoice Items sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Record the received part in one of the following ways: 
 

 ·              Specify the system's internal Part Number .
 OR 
 

 ·              If the vendor's part number is recorded in the system, specify the number in the Vend/Manuf Part No. column, and the internal part number will appear automatically. 
 

 Tip: For more information about defining vendor parts, run the Vendor Parts and Prices Wizard . 
 

 3 .  Make sure that the Part Description is suitable.
 Note: You can change the description of any part that is flagged in the Change Desc. column of the Part Catalogue form. 
 

 4 .  Record the quantity of parts being billed in the Inv. Quantity column. 
 

 5 .  Check the item's Unit Price and the Item Discount . The item's Extended Price is filled in automatically, as well as the total Amount Owed in the upper-level form.
 Note: If the invoice is based on a purchase order, the unit price is taken from the order. Otherwise, it is taken from the vendor's price list, if any, or from the purchase price listed in the part catalogue. If no vendor prices are available, it will be taken from the last purchase order for this part.
