# Revising a Final Multi-GRV Invoice

 

Only certain columns can be revised in a final invoice (e.g., Status , Details) . In addition, you can record new remarks and update existing ones. 
 
## To revise a final invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Revision of Invoice Details form and retrieve the desired invoice.
 Tip: For best results, retrieve by Invoice Number or Internal Number together with the Invoice Type (GRV). 
 

 2 .  In the References tab, make the necessary revisions in the updateable columns.
 Note: You can only change the invoice status to another status defined as final. 
 

 3 .  To record/update remarks for the invoice, use the Invoice/Memo - Remarks sub-level form.
