# Determining Whether to Copy Quantities from Invoices to Debit Memos

 

When debit memos are opened to cover price discrepancies between a purchase invoice and GRV, a quantity of 1 is recorded automatically in the debit memo for the difference. Alternatively, you can record the quantity of items found in the original purchase invoice. 
 

To copy original quantities into the debit memo: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Constants form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the DebitVendor line and specify a value of 1 .
