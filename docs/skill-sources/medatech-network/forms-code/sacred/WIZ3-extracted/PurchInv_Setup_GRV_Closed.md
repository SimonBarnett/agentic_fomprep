## Determining Whether to Include Closed Order Items in Multi-GRV Invoices 

 

When recording a multi-GRV invoice based on a purchase order, the invoice is itemized automatically with the ordered items. You can determine whether such invoices should include all ordered items, regardless of whether they are open or closed, or whether these invoices should include open order items only. 
 
## To include closed order items:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Constants form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the InvForClosedOrd constant and assign it a value of 1 . 
 
## Note:

 

The constant also affects multi-shipment invoices that are based on sales orders.
