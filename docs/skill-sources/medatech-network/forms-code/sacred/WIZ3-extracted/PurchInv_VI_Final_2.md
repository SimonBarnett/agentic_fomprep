Purchase Invoices/Memos - Finalizing Vendor Invoices.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## Before you begin:

 

 1 .  Enter the Vendor Invoices form and retrieve the desired vendor invoice. 
 

 2 .  Click the Price tab and make sure that the Amount Owing is correct. 
 

 3 .  Click the Payment Info tab and make sure that the Transaction Date is correct. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form and make sure that the Entry Code is correct. 
 

 5 .  Return to the upper level form, click the References tab and check the invoice's Status . You will only be able to finalize the invoice if its current status allows for finalization (e.g., it is assigned the Approved status).
 Note: You must also be authorized to change the status of vendor invoices to Final . 
 
## To finalize the invoice:

 

 ·        Select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To finalize and print the invoice:

 

 ·        Select Finalize & Print Invoice from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To finalize a group of invoices:

 

 ·        Run the Finalize Vendor Invoices program.
