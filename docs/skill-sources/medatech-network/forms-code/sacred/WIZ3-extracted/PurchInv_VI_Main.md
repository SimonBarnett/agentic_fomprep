# Preparing a Vendor Invoice

 

A vendor invoice both records the receipt of goods into inventory and bills your company for those goods. You can itemize the invoice manually, or automatically on the basis of purchase orders that the current shipment supplies. 
 

Click if a goods receiving voucher has already been recorded for the current shipment . 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Record a vendor invoice on the basis of one or more purchase orders 
 

 Record a vendor invoice manually 
 

 Credit your company for returned goods in a vendor invoice 
 

 Finalize a vendor invoice 
 

 Revise a final vendor invoice 
 

 Cancel a final vendor invoice 
 

 Delete a pending vendor invoice 
 

 Print a vendor invoice
