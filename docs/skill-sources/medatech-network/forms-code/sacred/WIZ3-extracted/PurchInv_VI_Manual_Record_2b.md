 8 .  In the Payment tab, the Payment Date is filled in automatically on the basis of the payment terms agreed upon with the vendor. If no default payment terms have been defined, the invoice date appears. In both cases, it may be revised.
Notes: 
 

 ·              To revise the payment terms (which may affect the payment date), use the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 ·              To manually define payment installments for this invoice, enter the Payment Installments sub-level form. These installments will override the vendor's default payment terms. 
 

 ·              To change the default payment terms for future transactions with this vendor, enter the Financial Parameters for Vendors form and click the General tab. 
 

 9 .  If you want to record remarks pertaining to this invoice that will appear in the printout, move to the Invoice/Memo - Remarks sub-level form and record your remarks.
