# Recording an Order Based on One or More Sales Orders

 

You can open one or more purchase orders automatically from within a specific sales order, or you can manually link a new purchase order to one or more existing sales orders. 
 

When you use the automatic option, you can open several purchase orders – one for each preferred vendor of the parts appearing in the sales order, or you can choose to order all parts from a designated vendor. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Open one or more purchase orders automatically from within a sales order 
 

 Open a purchase order manually and link it to a sales order 
 

 Open a purchase order manually and link it to multiple sales orders.
