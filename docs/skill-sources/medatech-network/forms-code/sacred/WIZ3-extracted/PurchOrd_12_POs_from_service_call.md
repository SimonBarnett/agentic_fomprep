# Recording an Order Based on a Service Call for a Part

 

You can open a new purchase order based on a service call. Purchase order data will be taken from the service call, and details of the part malfunction will appear in the purchase order remarks. After the purchase order is opened, it must be itemized manually. 
 
## To open the purchase order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the service call for which you wish to open a purchase order. 
 

 2 .  Select Open Purchase Order for Call from the list of Direct A ctivation s , indicating a vendor. 
 

 3 .  A message appears indicating the number of the purchase order that was opened. Enter the Purchase Orders for the Call sub-level form, and move to the line for that order. 
 

 4 .  Press F6 from the Order column to access the new order. 
 
## Note:

 

To access the order directly in the Purchase Orders form, retrieve by the Service Call number (in the References tab).
