# Working with Vendor Price Lists

 

The PPriceByList constant determines whether you assign each purchased part a single price or you maintain vendor price lists. It is important to work with price lists when the part is purchased from several vendors and when prices vary according to the quantity ordered. 
 
## Note:

 

Industrial concerns that receive new price lists for each purchase order should not maintain vendor price lists. 
 
## To work with vendor price lists:

 

Enter the Logistic Constants form, move to the PPriceByList constant, and do one of the following: 
 

 ·        If vendor price lists are maintained in your system, leave the pre-set value 1 . 
 

 ·        If you do not maintain vendor price lists, change the value to 0 .
 Note: You will not be able to change the value of the constant once vendor price lists are recorded in your system, unless you first delete all of the price lists.
