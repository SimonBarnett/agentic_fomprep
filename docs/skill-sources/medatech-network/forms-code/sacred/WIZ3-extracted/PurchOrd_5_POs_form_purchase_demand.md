# To create purchase orders automatically from a purchase demand document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Demands form and retrieve the desired purchase demand document. 
 

 2 .  Select Open Purchase Orders for Doc. from the list of Direct Activations . Separate purchase orders will be opened for each vendor and buyer to which purchase requisitions are assigned. 
 Note s : 
 

 ·              You can also run this program only for those PRs that are assigned to a specific buyer or for PRs that are not assigned to any buyer. 
 

 ·              If no buyer is designated for a given purchase requisition, the part will be included in a purchase order for the buyer who runs this program. 
 

 ·              If no vendor is designated for a given purchase requisition or if the purchase requisition is flagged as Do Not Open Order , the part will be ignored (no purchase order will be opened for the part). 
 

 3 .  Move to the Document N o. column and select Purchase Orders from the list of Direct Activations to retrieve the new orders.
