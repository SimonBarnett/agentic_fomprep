# Copying the Price Quote to the Order

 
## Before you begin:

 

 ·        Enter the Vendor Price Quotations form and retrieve the price quotation you want to use. In the Price tab, make sure that the Copy All to Order column is flagged.
 Note: Click if the column is blank. 
 
## To copy the price quote to the order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Orders form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the order to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Authorization & Follow-up tab). 
 

 2 .  Record the vendor number. The vendor name, date and order number are filled in automatically.
 Note: In the case of a one-time vendor, record the vendor number defined for one-time vendors and revise the name in the Vendor Name column. Then fill in the address and other details in the Purchase Order - Vendor Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Click the References tab. In the Price Quote column, choose the desired price quotation. Click if the quotation is not in the Choose list. 
 Note: If the PCopyTxtToPO constant is set at its default value of 1 , any remarks recorded for the price quote or its line items are copied automatically into the purchase order.
