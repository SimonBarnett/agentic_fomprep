# Additional Details 

 

 1 . Check the Contact column to make sure that the relevant purchase order contact appears. Change it if necessary.
 Tip: To define new contacts for the vendor or to assign a default contact for orders, use the Vendor Contacts form, a sub-level of the Vendors form. For more information, see the Open Vendors Wizard . 
 

 2 .  In the References tab, fill in any order details in the Details column. 
 

 3 .  Record the warehouse and bin into which the ordered materials are to be received. This will affect the shipping address that appears in the printout of the order. 
 

 4 .  Record the code for the mode of shipment that will be used to deliver this order. 
 

 5 .  Designate the type of order, if desired.
 Tip: To add a new order type, enter the Types of Purchase Orders form. 
 

 6 .  If the order is in a foreign currency, make sure that the correct currency appears in the Price tab. 
 

 7 .   Dual-cu rrency users: If the order is in the local currency, but is linked to a single foreign currency or index, record this in the Linked Curr column ( Price tab). If the order is linked to a basket of currencies, enter the Linkage Details sub-level form and itemize the basket’s components.
 Tip: For further details, see the Price Adjustment Wizard . 
 

 8 .  Click if you work with projects. 
 

 9 .  In the Payment Info tab, make sure that the payment terms are correct.
 Dual-cu rrency users: Also check that the correct Tax Code appears. 
 

 10 .  Enter the Internal Remarks for PO sub-level form to record any remarks regarding this purchase order that you do not want to appear in the printout to the vendor.
