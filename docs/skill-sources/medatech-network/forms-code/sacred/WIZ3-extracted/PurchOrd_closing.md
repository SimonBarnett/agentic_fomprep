# Closing Purchase Orders or Order Items That Were Not Received

 

When the remaining items in a partially filled purchase order (or the remaining balances of partially supplied items) are no longer to be supplied, you can close the order or any of its items manually. If you need to close several orders and/or items from several different orders, Priority provides a program that enables you to close them all simultaneously. 
 
## To close an order manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Orders form and retrieve the desired order. 
 

 2 .  Click the Authorization & Follow-up tab and flag the Closed column. All open items in the purchase order are closed automatically. 
 
## To close one or more purchase order items manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Order Items sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the desired line and flag the Closed column. 
 
## To close orders or order items automatically:

 

 ·        Run the Close Purchase Order Items program.
