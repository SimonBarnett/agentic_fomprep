# Revising an Authorized Order

 

Once a purchase order has been authorized, you need to use a special form to revise it. You can also run a program that splits a single order item into two separate lines. This reduces the quantity of the original order item by a designated quantity, and opens a new line for the same part with the designated quantity and due date. This is useful when you want to change the due date of a certain quantity of the order item after the order is final (e.g., because the vendor can't supply it all on time). 
 
## To revise an authorized purchase order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Revisions for Authorized Orders form and retrieve the desired purchase order. 
 

 2 .  Make the desired revisions in the columns that can be updated in this form (e.g., order type or vendor order number), and its sub-level Order Items (e.g., changes of date, part revisions, operations). 
 
## Note:

 

To change the status of an authorized purchase order, use the Purchase Orders form. 
 
## To split a purchase order item:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Orders form and retrieve the desired purchase order. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Order Items sub-level form, move to the item in question and select Split Purchase Order Items from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .  In the program input, specify the quantity and due date to be recorded for the new line being opened for this item. The quantity in the current line will also be reduced by this amount.
 Tip: To view the new order item, exit and re-enter the sub-level form.
