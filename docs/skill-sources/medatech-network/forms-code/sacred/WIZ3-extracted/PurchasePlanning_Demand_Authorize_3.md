# To authorize purchase demands:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Demands form and retrieve those demands you need to authorize.
 Tip: The username of the next person to sign appears in the Next Signature column. Consequently, you can click the Authorization & Follow-up tab and retrieve all demands for which your username appears in that column. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Purchase Demand Authorization sub-level form. The names of all users whose signatures are needed for authorization will appear. 
 

 3 .  Authorize next to your own name by flagging the Approve column. 
 

 4 .  Return to the Purchase Demands upper-level form and click the Authorization & Follow-up tab: 
 

 ·      If the document is now fully authorized, the Status changes automatically to Authorized. 
 

 ·      If the document is not yet fully authorized, the name of the next user who is supposed to authorize it appears in the Next Signature column. This user will receive an e-mail message regarding this authorization. 
 

 5 .  In the Assigned to column, designate the next user who is to handle the document (e.g., the next authorizer, the purchase manager).
