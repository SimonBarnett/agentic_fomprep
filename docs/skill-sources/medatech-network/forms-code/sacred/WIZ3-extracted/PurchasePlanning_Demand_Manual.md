# Opening a Purchase Demand Document Manually

 

In order to record purchase requisitions, you must first open a purchase demand document. You can do so manually, using the Purchase Demands form, or automatically, by running the Purchase Planning program. 
 

Even if you work routinely with purchase planning, you may need to open supplementary purchase requisitions between runs, or for items that are unrelated to sales orders (e.g., office supplies). 
 

As opposed to purchase planning, in which one demand document is opened for all purchase requisitions generated by the program, working manually allows you to open separate demand documents for distinct groups of purchase requisitions (e.g., that are recorded for the same warehouse, department, branch or vendor). 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Purchase planning does not take into account existing purchase requisitions that were recorded manually; if a certain quantity of a given part is required after existing inventory has been taken into account, and a purchase requisition is recorded manually, purchase planning automatically opens a requisition for the full required amount, regardless of the manual requisition you opened. However, if a purchase order is linked to the manual requisition, purchase planning does take the order into account.
