# To open a purchase demand document manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Demands form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Authorization & Follow-up tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the Date . The Document N o. will appear automatically. 
 

 3 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      If the purchase demand is entirely for a particular vendor, specify the Vendor Number . This vendor will appear automatically in each purchase requisition that you open in this document, where it cannot be changed. 
 

 ·      If the purchase demand is mainly but not entirely for a particular vendor, skip this step and follow the relevant instructions when you are recording individual purchase requisitions. 
 

 4 .  In the References & Cost tab, specify the Warehouse and Bin for which the parts are intended, if necessary. 
 

 5 .  Specify the Department and Branch for which the parts are intended, and the user who requested them (in the For User column). 
 

 6 .  Specify the Reason for the purchase demand. You can record a brief additional remark in the Details column.
 Tip: Use the Reasons for Demand form to define additional reasons. 
 

 7 .  If you are working with authorizer lists (rather than authorizing via BPM rules), click the Authorization & Follow-up tab and select an Authorizer List Code .
 Tip: Click to define a new authorizer list. 
 

 8 .  Click if you work with projects.
