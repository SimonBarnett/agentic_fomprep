# Printing a Purchase Demand Document

 
## To check remarks that will appear in the printout:

 

 1 .  In the Purchase Demands form, retrieve the desired demand document. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Remarks sub-level form. The text that appears in this form will appear as a remark in printouts of purchase demand documents. Make sure it suits your needs. 
 

 3 .  If you want to print PRs with remarks , enter the parallel Itemized Purchase Requisitions sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  In each line, make sure that the remarks recorded in the Purchase Requisition - Remarks sub-level form suit your needs. Revise them if necessary.
 Note s : 
 

 ·      Any part remarks recorded in the part catalogue appear in this form automatically if Copy Part Remarks has been flagged for purchase demands (in the Copy Part Remarks to Document form). 
 

 ·      The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable). 
 

 5 .  Return to the upper-level form. 
 
## To print one demand document:

 

 ·        Select Print Purchase Demand from the list of Direct Activations .
 D ual-currency users: To print the demand document in English when working in another language, select Print Purch Demand-Foreign Lang. instead. 
 
## To print more than one demand document:

 

 ·        Run the Print Purchase Demand report.
 D ual-currency users: To print in English when working in another language, run the Print Purch Demand-Foreign Lang. report.
