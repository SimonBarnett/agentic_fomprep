# Recording a Purchase Requisition in a Purchase Demand

 

You can add individual purchase requisitions to any open purchase demand, including those opened automatically by the Purchase Planning program. 
 
## To record a purchase requisition:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Demands form and retrieve the desired purchase demand. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Itemized Purchase Requisitions sub-level form. For each requisition, specify the Part Number of the required part. Make sure that the Part Description is suitable.
 Tip: You can change the description of any part that is flagged in the Change Desc. column of the Part Catalogue form. 
 

 3 .  In the Quantity to Order column, specify the required quantity of the part. 
 

 4 .  In the Demand Date column, specify the date by which the part is required. This value will appear automatically in the Best On-hand Date column, which may be revised. 
 

 5 .  If a particular user is responsible for purchasing the part, record his or her username in the Buyer column.
 Note: The assigned buyer can affect the requisitioned items that are included automatically in RFQs and purchase orders that are opened for this purchase demand.
