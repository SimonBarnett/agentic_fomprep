# To cancel a purchase demand:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Demands form and retrieve the desired purchase demand. 
 

 2 .  In the Authorization & Follow-up tab, change the Status of the purchase demand to Canceled . A check mark will appear automatically in the Closed column.
