Purchase Planning - Overview.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 Priority 's purchase recommendation reports can help you optimize your turnaround by calling to your attention expected inventory shortages as well as any purchase orders that should be expedited or added to fill the gaps. After implementing the system's purchase recommendations, you can run purchase planning again to view the new projected balances, and check for any new recommendations. By continuing this cycle you can fine-tune your purchasing schedule with each run, until you receive optimal results. 
 

Each purchase demand document is linked to an authorizer list, which is empty by default. You can use this list to prevent the generation of purchase orders based on demand documents, before the latter are authorized. 
 

For detailed instructions in opening RFQs, vendor quotes and purchase orders for requisitioned parts, run the Vendor Price Quotations Wizard or the Purchase Order Wizard .
