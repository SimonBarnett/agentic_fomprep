# To define planning quantities for a part:

 

 1 .  Click the Planning Quantities tab. 
 

 2 .  In the Min for Order/W. Order column, record the minimum quantity of this part that may be ordered. 
 

 3 .  In the Increment for Ord/WO column, record the permitted increment of purchase order quantities. Purchase requisition quantities will be calculated accordingly. Example 
 

 4 .  In the Demand Range column, specify the number of days for which material issues of the current part will be united into a single purchase requisition. Leave the column empty to open a separate requisition for each part issue, but remember: the defined minimums and increments will be applied to each requisition individually. Example. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To perform purchase planning on a monthly basis (i.e., to open a separate purchase requisition for each calendar month), define a demand range of 31 days. 
 

 ·        Click to perform additional setups.
