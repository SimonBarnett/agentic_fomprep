# Defining Times and Quantities for Purchase Planning

 

Planning times for purchased parts (together with the date the part is needed) determine the due date and on-hand date that appear in purchase requisitions opened manually or automatically. 
 

The quantities defined for purchase planning (together with the exact required amount of the purchased part) determine the quantity that appears in purchase requisitions opened automatically. They define the minimum quantities of each part that may be requisitioned, the permitted increments in purchase quantities, and the demand range, which determines how many material issues will be united into a single purchase requisition. 
 
## To define planning times for a part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase/MRP Parameters for Part form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Click the Planning Time tab. 
 

 3 .  In the Purchase Lead Time column, record the average number of days that pass between the purchase order date and the date the part is shipped from the vendor's site (lead time represents the time spent by the vendor in preparing the shipment, as of the day the order is received). 
 

 4 .  In the Shipping Days column, record the average number of days that pass between the date the part leaves the vendor's site and the date it is received in your warehouse. 
 

 5 .  In the Testing Days column, record the average number of days from the date the part was received in your warehouse until it is available for use.
