Purchase Planning - Excluding Purchase Orders 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To exclude a specific purchase order from purchase planning calculations:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Orders form and retrieve the desired purchase order. 
 

 2 .  Click the Authorization & Follow-up tab and remove the check mark from the Affects Planning column.
