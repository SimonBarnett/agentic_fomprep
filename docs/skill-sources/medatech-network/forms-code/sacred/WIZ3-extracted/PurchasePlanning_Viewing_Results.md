# Viewing Planning Results

 

Use the Purchase Recommendation Reports to view the system's recommendations for purchase requisitions and orders that should be expedited, cancelled or postponed in light of the new planning results. Once you have implemented these recommendations to the extent possible, run purchase planning again (in the Update Projected Balances mode). Repeat the process until there are no significant improvements for the system to recommend. 
 
## To view projected balances:

 

 ·        Enter the Purchase/MRP Parameters for Part form and retrieve the desired part, then use the Projected Balances (buy/sell) or Projected Balances (fact. units) sub-level form to view the projected changes in the part's inventory balance. 
 

 OR 
 

 ·        Run the Projected Balances (with PRs) report.
 Note: To view the precise date on which the inventory of purchased parts are expected to fall below their safety stock, run the Exceptions report. 
 
## Note:

 

Use the Required Materials Reports to view the projected balances of raw materials required to manufacture one or more given top-level parts or sub-assemblies. 
 
## To view the purchase requisitions opened by the program:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase Demands form and retrieve the demand documents generated by the program.
 Tip: Use the Date column (i.e., the date you ran purchase planning). 
 

 2 .  Use the Itemized Purchase Requisitions sub-level form to view the individual purchase requisitions.
