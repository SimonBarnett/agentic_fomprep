# Receiving Goods on Consignment from a Vendor

 

When supplying goods on consignment, the vendor keeps a warehouse on your company's premises, in which it maintains fixed inventory levels of the needed parts. When consigned goods arrive, they are entered into the consignment warehouse and recorded in a non-billable GRV, with no effect on your company's inventory balances. Each time inventory is removed from the consignment warehouse for use by your company, it is recorded in a regular GRV and becomes company inventory. Copies of each billable GRV are sent to the vendor, who will use them to monitor inventory levels and bill your company for the sold goods. 
 

Before you can begin receiving consigned goods, you must define a consignment warehouse and assign it to the vendor. The consignment warehouse will appear automatically as the default warehouse in every GRV that you open for that vendor. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Define a vendor consignment warehouse 
 

 Assign a consignment warehouse to a vendor 
 

 Open a non-billable GRV for goods entering the consignment warehouse 
 

 Open a regular GRV for goods leaving the consignment warehouse
