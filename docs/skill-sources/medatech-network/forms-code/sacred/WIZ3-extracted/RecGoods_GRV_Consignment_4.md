# To open a GRV for goods entering inventory from the consignment warehouse:

 

 1 .  Enter the Goods Receiving Vouchers form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the General tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the Date the goods were received. The Document Number will be filled in automatically. 
 

 3 .  Specify the Vendor Number . The Vendor Name is filled in automatically. 
 

 4 .  In the General tab, replace the default Receiving Warehouse and Bin with a company warehouse flagged for inventory valuation. The Billable column (in the Price tab) will be flagged automatically. 
 

 5 .  In the Details column, record a brief remark regarding this GRV. 
 

 6 .  To record remarks pertaining to this receipt, use the Remarks sub-level form. 
 

 7 .  Click if you work with projects .
