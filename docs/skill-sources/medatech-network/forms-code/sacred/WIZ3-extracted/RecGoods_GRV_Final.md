# To finalize a GRV:

 

 1 .  Enter the Goods Receiving Vouchers form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired GRV. 
 

 3 .  In the General tab, change the status to Final . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        If you have finalized a GRV recording the transfer of goods from a vendor consignment warehouse to company inventory, send the vendor a printout of the GRV; this is how the vendor monitors inventory levels, so as to know when to replenish the consigned stock. 
 

 ·        Click to print the GRV . 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen .
