# Revising a Final GRV

 

Once a GRV is final, you can still revise certain details – such as its billing status, document status and the user responsible for handling it – in a special form. Moreover, as long as the document has not been billed, you can reopen it and make any needed revisions directly within it. 
 

 Note: 
 

You can change the status of a final GRV (to another final status) or the name of the user responsible for handling it, in the Goods Receiving Vouchers form. 
 
## To revise a final GRV:

 

 1 .  Retrieve the desired document in the Revision of Final GRV form. 
 

 2 .  Make any necessary revisions in that form and in its sub-level, Change Bill Status-Received Part . 
 
## To reopen a final GRV:

 

 1 .  Retrieve the desired document in the Goods Receiving Vouchers form. 
 

 2 .  Select Reopen Document from the list of Direct Activations . The document will return to the initial status.
