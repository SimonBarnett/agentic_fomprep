 5 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      To link the GRV to a single purchase order , click the References tab and select the order in the Order No. column. 
 Note: Warehouse/Bin and Contact information, as well as Details , are filled in automatically from the order, but may be revised. 
 

 ·      To link the GRV to several purchase orders , enter the Purchase Orders for GRV sub-level form and specify the appropriate orders. 
 

 ·      To link the GRV to individual purchase order items , enter the Choose Order Items sub-level form and flag the Choose Item column for each received item. 
 

 6 .  Click the References tab. If you are including returned goods in this GRV: 
 

 ·      Specify the original GRV in which they were received in the GRV for Cancellation column. 
 

 ·      Enter the Received Items sub-level form. The items from the original GRV will appear with negative values in the Balance to Receive column. 
 

 ·      Delete any parts that are not being returned. 
 

 ·      For each returned item, flag the Approve column to return the entire quantity, or record the quantity to be returned in the Quantity column, using a negative value. 
 

 7 .  If the GRV is based on multiple purchase orders, or includes returns, specify the Contact at the vendor for this transaction, and record a brief remark in the Details column.
 Notes: 
 

 ·      You can only link the GRV to purchase orders and order items intended for the warehouse and bin designated in the upper-level form. 
 

 ·      The designated bin will be assigned to all received items, with the exception of parts for which a default warehouse bin is defined (in the Inventory Parameters for Parts form) – in that case, the default will appear instead. In any case, the bin (and warehouse) can be revised for specific items.
