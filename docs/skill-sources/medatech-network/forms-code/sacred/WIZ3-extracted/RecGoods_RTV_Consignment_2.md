# To open a return document for goods reentering the consignment warehouse:

 

 1 .  Enter the Returns to Vendor form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the General tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the Date the goods were returned. The Document Number will be filled in automatically. 
 

 3 .  Specify the number of the vendor that supplied the goods. The vendor's consignment warehouse and bin (if any) appear automatically in the From Warehouse and Bin columns. 
 

 4 .  Click the References tab, and specify the GRV in which the returned goods were originally received into inventory. The company warehouse and bin (if any) in which the goods were received automatically replaces the default From Warehouse and Bin , and the vendor's consignment warehouse and bin reappear in the Vendor Warehouse and Bin columns. The Contact and Project for the original shipment are also filled in, and the Credit from Vendor column in the Price tab is flagged. 
 

 5 .  Record a brief remark regarding this document in the Details column. 
 

 6 .  To record remarks pertaining to this receipt, use the Remarks sub-level form.
