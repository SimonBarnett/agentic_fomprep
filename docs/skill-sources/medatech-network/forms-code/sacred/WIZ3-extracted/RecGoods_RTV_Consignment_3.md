# Returning Consigned Goods to the Vendor

 

 1 .  Enter the Returns to Vendor form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the General tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the Date the goods were returned. The Document Number will be filled in automatically. 
 

 3 .  Specify the number of the vendor that supplied the goods. The vendor's consignment warehouse and bin (if any) appear automatically in the From Warehouse and Bin columns, and the Credit from Vendor column in the Price tab will be cleared. 
 

 4 .  Specify the Vendor Warehouse and Bin (if any) to which you are returning the goods. 
 

 5 .  Record a brief remark regarding this document in the Details column. 
 

 6 .  To record remarks pertaining to this receipt, use the Remarks sub-level form. 
 

 7 .  Return to the upper-level form and do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Click to itemize the return document automatically from the original GRV . 
 

 ·      Click to itemize the return document manually (when the original GRV is particularly long).
