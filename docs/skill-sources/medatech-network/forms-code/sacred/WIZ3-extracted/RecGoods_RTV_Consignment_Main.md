# Returning Goods That Were on Consignment From a Vendor

 

Since the consignment warehouse and its contents are considered the property of the vendor, it follows that any removal of goods from the warehouse into your inventory must be recorded in a billable GRV. Similarly, any return of goods to the warehouse must be recorded in a credited Return to Vendor document. When the goods are eventually shipped back to the vendor's premises, they are recorded in a non-credited Return document. Copies of each credited Return Document are sent to the vendor, who will use them to monitor inventory levels and credit your company for the returned goods. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Open a credited Return to Vendor document for goods reentering the consignment warehouse 
 

 Open a regular Return to Vendor document for goods leaving the consignment warehouse
