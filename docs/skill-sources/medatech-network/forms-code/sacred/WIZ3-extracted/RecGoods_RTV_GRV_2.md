 8 .  If you work with RMAs, do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Select Assign New RMA Number from the list of Direct Activations to open a new RMA for the returned goods. The assigned number appears in the RMA column. 
 

 ·      To include these goods in an existing RMA, press F6 in the RMA column to access the RMA form and retrieve the desired RMA. Then press F8 to copy its number back into this column. 
 

 9 .  If you were not billed for the goods you are returning, click the Price tab and remove the check from the Credit from Vendor column. 
 Note: If only part of the goods were not billable, leave the check mark, and change the credit status of those parts during itemization of the return document. 
 

 10 .  Click the Shipment tab and fill in the necessary information. The vendor's default shipper is filled in automatically, but may be revised.
 Tip: To define additional shipping codes, use the Shipment Codes form; to define additional shippers, use the Drivers/Shippers form. 
 

 11 .  To record remarks pertaining to this return that will appear in the printout, use the Remarks sub-level form.
