# Receiving Goods

 

Incoming goods are recorded in a Goods Receiving Voucher (GRV), with the following effect on inventory balances: 
 

 ·        If the GRV specifies a receiving warehouse subject to inventory valuation, the received parts are automatically added to the balance of those parts in that warehouse. 
 

 ·        If the GRV specifies a vendor consignment warehouse, the received parts continue to belong to the vendor (i.e., there is no adjustment of inventory balances) and the GRV is not billed. As parts are needed, additional, billable GRVs are used to record the transfer of goods from the consignment warehouse to a company warehouse. In most cases, unused parts are ultimately returned to the vendor. 
 

GRVs of the first type can be itemized automatically on the basis of one or more purchase orders, or manually. Moreover, you can include returned goods in a GRV by specifying negative qualities; this will reduce the inventory balances for the returned parts. 
 

If you have already been billed for the goods you have received, you can itemize the GRV automatically from the invoice. When the GRV is finalized, it is automatically flagged as billed.
