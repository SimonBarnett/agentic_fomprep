# Canceling a Final GRV

 

In order to cancel a final GRV, you need to open a new GRV that cancels out the receipt of goods by creating an inventory transaction with negative quantities. 
 
## To cancel a final GRV:

 

 1 .  Enter the Goods Receiving Vouchers form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the General tab). 
 

 2 .  Click the References tab. Press F6 in the GRV for Cancellation to open an additional instance of the Goods Receiving Vouchers form and retrieve the desired document. Press F8 to copy the document number back to this column. 
 

 3 .  The current Date , Document Number and the Vendor Number from the original GRV appear automatically. Revise the date as needed. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Received Items sub-level form. All line items from the original GRV appear automatically. 
 

 5 .  The quantities recorded for the original receipt of goods appear as negative values in the Balance to Receive column. Flag the Approve column in each line. The value will be copied to the Quantity column. 
 

 6 .  Return to the upper-level form. 
 

 7 .  In the General tab, change the S tatus to Final .
