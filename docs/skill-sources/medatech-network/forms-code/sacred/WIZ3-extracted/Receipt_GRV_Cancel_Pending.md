Receipt of Goods - Canceling a Pending GRV 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 To cancel a pending GRV: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Goods Receiving Vouchers form and retrieve the desired document. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Received Items sub-level form and change the quantity of each line item to 0 .
 Tip: If a quantity of crates is specified in the Packing Crates (No.) column, you need to change the value of that column. 
 

 3 .  Return to the upper-level form. 
 

 4 .  In the General tab, change the status to Canceled .
