## To set default lot numbering definitions for parts that are received from a vendor:

 

 1 .  Enter the Logistic Constants form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the PLotByVendor constant and choose the desired setting.
 Note: You can override this definition for individual parts (see below). 
 
## To assign specific numbering templates to certain parts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Work Order & Lot No. Templates form and define the required numbering templates. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Assign WO/Lot Templates to Parts form and assign the appropriate template to each desired part. 
 

 3 .  Make sure that the Open Automatically column is flagged for these parts. 
 

 4 .  To override the numbering definitions determined by the constant for specific parts, choose the desired value in the Assign No. by (GRV) column for these parts. 
 
## To define the shelf life of a part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Inventory Parameters for Parts form and retrieve the part in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Inventory tab and designate the following parameters: 
 

 ·      Days in Effect 
 

 ·      Months in Effect 
 

 ·      Max. No. Extensions 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can extend a lot's shelf life in the Expirations and Extensions form. For more details, see the Laboratory Analysis Wizard . 
 

 ·        Click to perform additional setups.
