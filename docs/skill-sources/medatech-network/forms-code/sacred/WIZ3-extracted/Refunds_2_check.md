# To record a refund by check:

 

 1 .  Enter the Check Payments form. 
 

 2 .  In the Vendor ' s Account column, indicate the customer account to which the refund is being made. 
 

 3 .  Specify the Paying Bank Account for the refund check. 
 

 4 .  If desired, indicate that this is a customer refund in the Details column ( References tab). 
 

 5 .  Enter the Flag Invoices for Payment sub-level form. This form displays all invoices and credit memos recorded for this customer. 
 

 6 .  Move to the line for the credit memo for which the refund is being made, and flag the Pay column. 
 

 7 .  Move to the parallel sub-level form, Payment Details . The amount of payment due, as recorded in the credit memo, appears in the Sum column. If the refund is partial, revise this amount. 
 

 8 .  Enter the Check Payment - Add'l Details sub-level form and verify that this is the address that you want to appear on the printed check. 
 

 9 .  Return to the upper-level form and select Finalize Check Payment from the list of Direct Activation s .
 Note: You will only be able to finalize the document if its current status allows for finalization (e.g., it is assigned the Authorized status in the References tab). 
 

 10 .  To print the check, run the Print Checks program.
