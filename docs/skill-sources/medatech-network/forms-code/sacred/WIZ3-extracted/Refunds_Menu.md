# Recording a Customer Refund

 
## Before You Begin

 

As the refund must be linked to a credit memo, you must ensure that such a credit memo has been recorded for the customer for the amount in question. If the refund is being made by check, and you want to send the check to a different address than the customer's regular mailing address, be sure to record a remit to address as well. 
 
## To record a credit memo:

 

 ·        Run the Customer Invoices Wizard . 
 
## To record a remit to address:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts Receivable form and retrieve the A/R account in question. 
 

 2 .  In the Remit to Address (Checks) sub-level form, record the name and address you want to appear on the check.

 Record a check refunding the customer
