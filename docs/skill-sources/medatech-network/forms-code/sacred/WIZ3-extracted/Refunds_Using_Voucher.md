# Using a Credit Voucher

 

When the customer uses the credit voucher, record a regular over-the-counter invoice (or another type of invoice, together with a receipt). If the purchase is for a sum larger than the value of the credit voucher, record additional payments as usual. 
 
## Important:

 

In the over-the-counter invoice or receipt (i.e., in the Payment by Credit Card/Coupon or Other Forms of Payment (Details) form, respectively), specify the amount of credit as the Payment Amount , “Credit Voucher” as the Payment Code and the credit voucher cashier in the BkAcct/Credit Card No. column. 
 
## Note:

 

You need to periodically reconcile entries for the credit voucher account, using the Reconciliation Worksheet . The total amount of unreconciled entries indicates the total value of credit vouchers held by customers. For details on account reconciliation, run the Account Reconciliation Wizard .
