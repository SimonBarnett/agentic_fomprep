# Issuing a voucher for credit only (no returns)

 
## Before you begin

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Documents form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the Receipt line, and make sure that the Check for Neg. Sums column is not flagged. 
 
## To issue a voucher for credit only:

 

 1 .  Enter the Receipts form. 
 

 2 .  Record the Cashier defined for credit vouchers. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Other Forms of Payment (Details) sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Specify “Credit Voucher” as the Payment Code . 
 

 5 .  Designate a negative Payment Amount equal to the total amount of credit. 
 

 6 .  Specify today's date as the Payment Date . 
 

 7 .  Enter the parallel Invoice Amounts Being Paid sub-level form. 
 

 8 .  Specify a negative amount in the Sum column. 
 

 9 .  Return to the upper-level form and finalize the receipt. A journal entry will be created with a negative debit of the Credit Vouchers account and a negative credit of the customer account. 
 
## Note:

 

For more details on recording receipts, run the Customer Receipts Wizard .
