# Issuing a Credit Voucher for Returned Merchandise

 
## Before you begin

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Documents form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the Over-the-Counter line, and make sure that the Check for Neg. Sums column is not flagged. 
 
# To issue a credit voucher for returned merchandise:

 

 1 .  Enter the Over-the-Counter Invoices form. 
 

 2 .  In the Invoice Items sub-level form, record the returned goods, specifying negative quantities. 
 

 3 .  Enter the parallel Payment by Credit Card/Coupon sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Specify “Credit Voucher” as the Payment Code. 
 

 5 .  Designate a negative Payment Amount equal to the total amount of credit. 
 

 6 .  Specify today's date as the Payment Date . 
 

 7 .  Return to the upper-level form and finalize the invoice. A journal entry will be created with a negative debit of the Credit Vouchers account. 
 
## Note:

 

For more details on recording over-the-counter invoices, run the Customer Invoices Wizard .
