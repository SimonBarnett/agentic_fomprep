- 
 
 
 
## To deposit the returned payment:

 

 1 .  Enter the Deposit Slips form. 
 

 2 .  Record the date the payment was returned. 
 

 3 .  In the From Cashier column, choose the cashier to which the payment was returned. 
 

 4 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              To charge the customer account, choose the account in the To A/R Acct column. OR 
 

 ·              To re-deposit a check, choose the appropriate bank in the To Cash/Bank/CredCd column. OR 
 

 ·              To transfer the returned payment to a third party, choose the party's GL account in the To A/R Acct column. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Items at Cashier sub-level form. All cash, checks and credit card slips in this cashier appear. 
 

 6 .  On the line for the returned payment, flag the Deposit column. 
 

 7 .  Move to the parallel Deposited Items sub-level form. The flagged item appears. 
 

 8 .  Select Finalize Deposit Slip from the list of Direct Activations .
