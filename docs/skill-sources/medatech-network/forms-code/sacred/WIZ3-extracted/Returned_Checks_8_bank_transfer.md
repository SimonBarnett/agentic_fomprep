# To record a return of a standing order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Returned Payments form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 2 .  In the Customer Account column, choose the account of the customer whose payment was returned.
 Notes: 
 

 ·              In the case of a walk-in customer, record the walk-in customer number. Then revise the name in the Customer Name column, and fill in the address and other details in the Returned Payment - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 ·              A temporary Document No. (prefixed with a T ) is filled in automatically when you leave the field. It will be replaced by a permanent number when the document is finalized. 
 

 3 .  Choose the customer contact to which the returned payment notice is to be addressed.
 Tip: To define new contacts for the customer, use the Customer Contacts form, a sub-level of the Customers form. 
 

 4 .  Today's date is filled in automatically. Change it to the date that payment was returned. 
 

 5 .  In the BkAcc/CredC Code column, choose the bank account into which payment was originally deposited.
