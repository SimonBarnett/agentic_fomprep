Returned Payments - Finalizing a Returned Payment.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## Before you begin:

 

 1 .  Enter the Returned Payments form and retrieve the desired document. 
 

 2 .  Click the References tab and check the document's Status . You will only be able to finalize the document if its current status allows for finalization (e.g., it is assigned the Approved status).
 Note: You must also be authorized to change the status of returned payment documents to Final . 
 
## To finalize the document:

 

 ·        Select Finalize Document from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to print the return notice. 
 

 ·        Click to prepare an invoice billing the customer for the return fee and/or finance charges. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
