## To finalize the invoice:

 

 1 .  Move to the Invoice (Charges) column (in the Charges and Commission tab) and press F6 to access the invoice that was prepared. 
 

 2 .  Check all details recorded in the form and all its sub-levels. 
 

 3 .  Select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To print the invoice:

 

 ·        Select Print Invoice/Credit Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        For a detailed explanation of customer invoices, including setups, run the Customer Invoices Wizard .
