# Changing an Authorized BOM Revision

 

Provided that the ChnConfRev constant (in the Planning Constants form) has been defined accordingly, you can change an authorized BOM revision by revoking its authorization. If the BOM revision was authorized together with an ECO, you will first need to revoke the authorization of the ECO. On the other hand, if a part revision was created automatically with the BOM revision, it remains unaffected by any changes to the BOM revision. 
 
## To change an authorized BOM revision:

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Enter the BOM Revisions sub-level form and move to the BOM revision in question. 
 

 3 .  If the BOM revision is based on an authorized ECO, click to reopen the ECO. 
 

 4 .  Click to reopen the BOM revision . 
 

 5 .  Make the necessary changes. 
 

 6 .  Click to re-authorize the BOM revision.
