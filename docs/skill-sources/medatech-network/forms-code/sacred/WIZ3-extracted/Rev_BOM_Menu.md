# Working With BOM Revisions

 

Your company may decide to revise the BOM of a given part for any number of reasons (e.g., market analysis, cost efficiency). When a BOM revision is opened due to a new ECO, the two processes become so closely linked that authorization of the ECO automatically extends to the BOM revision to which it is linked. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Open a BOM revision (not based on an ECO) 
 

 Open a new BOM revision based on an ECO 
 

 Authorize a BOM revision 
 

 Compare BOM revisions 
 

 Change an authorized BOM revision
