# Reopening an ECO

 

In order to reopen an ECO, each authorizer in the authorizer list must remove his or her authorization from the ECO in the reverse order from that in which they signed. 
 
## Authorizer instructions:

 

 1 .  Move to the ECO Number column and press F6 twice to enter the Engineering Change Orders (ECO) form. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Authorize ECO sub-level form in reverse order (i.e., the last authorizer first) and remove the check mark from the Authorize column next to your name. 
 
## Note:

 

When the final check mark has been removed from the Authorize ECO sub-level form, the check mark will no longer appear in the Authorized column of the BOM revision in the BOM Revisions form.
