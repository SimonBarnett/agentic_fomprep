# To authorize an ECO:

 

 1 .  Enter the Engineering Change Orders (ECO) form. 
 

 2 .  Use the Next Signature column to retrieve any ECOs you need to authorize (retrieve by your username). 
 

 3 .  For each retrieved ECO, enter the Authorize ECO sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Flag the Authorize column next to your name.  
 

 5 .  Return to the upper-level form and assign the ECO to the next authorizer. That is, in the Details tab, copy the username from the Next Signature column to the Assign to column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Once an ECO is partially authorized, a P appears in the Authorized column. After the last authorizer has signed, a Y appears. When the ECO is fully authorized, its status changes automatically to Authorized . 
 

 ·        If the ECO is linked to a BOM and/or part revision, the revision is authorized automatically, together with the ECO. Until then, the revision cannot be authorized or put into effect.
