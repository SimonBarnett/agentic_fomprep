# To record an ECO:

 

 1 .  Enter the Engineering Change Orders (ECO) form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the ECO to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Details tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the Date the engineering change was made. An ECO Number will appear automatically. 
 

 3 .  Specify the Reason for ECO . You can record a brief additional remark in the Details column.
 Tip: Click to define additional reasons. 
 

 4 .  Your username appears automatically in the Initiator of Change column. Revise if necessary. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Parts in the ECO sub-level form and specify the catalogue numbers of the parts affected by the engineering change. 
 Note: Once a part has been recorded it can only be removed by clicking Delete Part from ECO on the Direct Activation bar, provided that no BOM revision, work order or transaction document has since been opened for the part. 
 

 6 .  In the parallel Attachments sub-level form, attach any relevant files (e.g., blueprints, specs) to the ECO.
 Tip: You can print the attached files by clicking Print ECO Attachments on the Direct Activation bar. 
 

 7 .  In the parallel Implications for the ECO sub-level form, specify any implications of the current engineering change. 
 Tip: Click to define additional implications. 
 

 8 .  In the parallel ECOs - Remarks sub-level form, record extensive remarks regarding the engineering change. 
 

 9 .  Return to the upper-level form. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to open a new BOM revision based on the ECO. 
 

 ·        Click to print one or more ECOs .
