# Deleting or Deactivating a Part Revision

 

If a part revision is not fully authorized, you can delete it, provided that no inventory transactions have been recorded for it. Alternatively, you can deactivate the part revision, in which case it can no longer be recorded in a transaction. 
 
## To delete a part revision:

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Part Revisions sub-level form and press Ctrl-Del to delete the part revision in question. 
 
## To deactivate a part revision:

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Part Revisions sub-level form and move to the part revision in question. 
 

 3 .  Flag the Not In Use column. 
 Tip: This flag may be removed later if you decide to authorize the part revision.
