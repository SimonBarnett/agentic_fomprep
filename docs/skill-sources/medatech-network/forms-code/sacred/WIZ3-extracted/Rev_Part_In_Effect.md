# To put a part revision into effect:

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Part Revisions sub-level form and move to the desired part revision.
 Note: Only a fully authorized part revision may be put into effect. When the revision is linked to an ECO, it cannot be put into effect until the ECO is authorized. 
 

 3 .  Flag the In Effect column.
