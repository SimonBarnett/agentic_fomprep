# To open a new part revision: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Part Revisions sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Open a new line and record a number for the revision in the Part Revision No. column. 
 

 4 .  In the Use From column, specify the date on which the revision is intended to go into effect. 
 

 5 .  If the part revision is based on an ECO, record the ECO Number . 
 
## Note:

 

To view all part revisions for all parts, enter the Part Revisions form.
