# Discounts

 

You can define three types of discounts for sales: 
 

 ·      part discount 
 

 ·      family discount 
 

 ·      overall discount 
 

The defined part discount and/or family discount determine the discount assigned to the line item in a given sales document. The overall discount applies to all items in the document and is in addition to any item discounts. Example. 
 

All discounts are assigned for a limited period, and they only apply to sales documents recorded while the discounts are valid. You can also assign discounts to a representative customer , which then apply to all customers to which the representative is assigned, as well as to the predefined general representative , which supplies data to all customers in the system. When several discounts are assigned together (to the customer and/or representative customer), a user-defined constant determines whether the dominant discount applies (according to the system's discount hierarchy) or whether all discounts, including those assigned to the general representative, are applied cumulatively.
