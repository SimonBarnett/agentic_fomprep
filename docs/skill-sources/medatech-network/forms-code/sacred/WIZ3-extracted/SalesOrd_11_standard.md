# To record a standard order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders form. 
 

 2 .  Choose the customer's number. The customer's name, today's date and an order number are filled in automatically, and the order is assigned the Draft status.
 Note: In the case of a walk-in customer, record the walk-in customer number. Then revise the name in the Cust. Name column, and fill in the address and other details in the Sales Order - Customer Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Check the Contact column to make sure that the relevant sales order contact appears. Change it if necessary.
 Tip: To define new contacts for the customer or to assign a default contact for orders, use the Customer Contacts form, a sub-level of the Customers form. For more information, see the Open Customers Wizard . 
 

 4 .  Click the Price tab. If the order price is in a foreign currency, make sure that the correct Currency appears.
 Tip for dual-currency users: If the order price is linked to a foreign currency, price index or currency basket, fill in the various linkage parameters. For details, run the Price Adjustment Wizard . 
 

 5 .  In the References tab, record the customer's purchase order number in the Cust ' s PO column. Fill in any other brief details in the Details column.
