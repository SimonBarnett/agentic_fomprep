# Scheduling Workers for the Order

 

 Priority enables you to schedule employees to handle the order. Once the work is scheduled, a task is opened for all of the employees involved and an appointment is recorded in each employee's calendar. 
 
## To schedule workers for the order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders form and retrieve the desired order. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Calendar (All Users) sub-level form to view all your employees' existing schedules. 
 

 3 .  Return to the upper-level form and click the Scheduling tab. 
 

 4 .  Fill in the main employee for the order in the Assigned to column. 
 

 5 .  Fill in the dates and times work is scheduled to begin and end.
