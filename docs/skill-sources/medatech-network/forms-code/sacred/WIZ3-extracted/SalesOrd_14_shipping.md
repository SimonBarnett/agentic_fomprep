# Recording Shipping Information

 

You can record the customer site to which the goods are to be delivered, as well as the method by which they are shipped. 
 
## Note:

 

If the order is based on a quote, this information is copied from the quote automatically. 
 
## To determine the site to which the goods are to be delivered:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders form and retrieve the desired order. 
 

 2 .  If the goods are to be delivered to a site other than the customer's main address, choose the appropriate customer site in the Site column of this form (in the References tab). 
 Notes: 
 

 ·              You can add to the list of customer sites in the Customer Sites form, a sub-level of the Customers form. 
 

 ·              To specify a site that is not in the Choose list (e.g., in the case of a walk-in customer), record the shipping address manually in the Shipping Address sub-level form. 
 
## To determine how goods will be shipped:

 

 ·        In the Ship Mode column (in the References tab), choose the appropriate code.
 Tip: You can add to the list of shipment types in the Shipment Codes form. 
 
## Note: 

 

Click to continue processing the order.
