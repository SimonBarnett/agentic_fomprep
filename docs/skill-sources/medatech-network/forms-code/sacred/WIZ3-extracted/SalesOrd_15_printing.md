# Printing the Order Confirmation: Before You Begin

 
## To check the status that appears on the printout:

 

 1 .  In the Statuses for Orders form, retrieve the status assigned to the order. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Additional Status Definitions sub-level form and check the status description. Revise as necessary. 
 
## To check the remarks that appear on the printout:

 

 1 .  In the Sales Orders form, retrieve the desired order. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Sales Orders - Remarks sub-level form. The text appearing in this form will appear as a remark on the printout. Make sure that it is correct. 
 

 3 .  If you are printing order confirmations with item remarks , return to the upper-level form and enter the Order Items sub-level form. Make sure that the remarks recorded for each item in the Order Items - Remarks sub-level form are suitable. Revise them if necessary. Explanation. 
 Note: The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable). 
 

 4 .  Return to the Sales Orders form.
