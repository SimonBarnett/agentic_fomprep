# Printing the Order Confirmation: Verifying the Customer's Address

 

The order confirmation printout displays both the customer's mailing and shipping addresses (the latter is determined by customer site). 
 
## To verify the customer's mailing address:

 

 ·        Enter the Sales Order - Customer Details sub-level form. Make sure that the address recorded is correct.
 Notes: 
 

 ·              To update the address of a regular customer (as opposed to a walk-in), enter the Customers form. 
 

 ·              For dual-currency users: To update the address in English, enter the Additional Customer Data sub-level of the Customers form. 
 
## To verify the customer's shipping address:

 

 ·        If the goods are to be delivered to a site other than the customer's main address, make sure that the site is correctly defined in the Shipping Address sub-level form.
 Tip: To add to the list of customer sites, use the Customer Sites form, a sub-level of the Customers form.
