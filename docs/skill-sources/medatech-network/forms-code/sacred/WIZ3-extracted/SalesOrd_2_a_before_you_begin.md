# Setting Up the Rounding Constant

 

You can round the total price of orders to the nearest whole unit of currency, or to the nearest ten sub-units. Example. 
 
## To round the total prices of orders:

 

Enter the Financial Constants form, move to the RoundOrd constant, and assign one of the following values: 
 

 ·        1: to round to the nearest ten sub-units 
 

 ·        2: to round to the nearest whole currency unit 
 

 RoundOrd receives a default value of 0, such that order prices are not automatically rounded. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        The constant also affects total prices in price quotations and in shipping documents. 
 

 ·        Click to set different values for different customers.
