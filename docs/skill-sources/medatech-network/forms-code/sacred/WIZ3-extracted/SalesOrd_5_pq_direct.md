# To open an order from within a quote:

 

 1 .  Enter the Price Quotations for Customers form and retrieve the quote in question. 
 

 2 .  Select Open Order from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .  Enter the Sales Orders form and retrieve the newly opened order.
