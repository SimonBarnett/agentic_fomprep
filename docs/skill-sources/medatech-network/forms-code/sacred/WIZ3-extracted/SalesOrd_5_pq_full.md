# To copy one or more price quotes to the order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders form. 
 

 2 .  Choose the customer's number. The customer's name, today's date and an order number are filled in automatically, and the order is assigned the Draft status.
 Note: In the case of a walk-in customer, record the walk-in customer number. Then revise the name in the Cust. Name column, and fill in the address and other details in the Sales Order - Customer Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Active Price Quotations sub-level form and make sure that the quote you want to use is flagged in the Copy All to Order column.
 Tip: To change this flag, use the Revisions for Final Price Quotes form. 
 

 4 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              To copy multiple price quotations, enter the parallel Price Quotes for Order sub-level form. 
 

 ·              To copy a single price quotation to the order, return to the upper-level form and click the References tab. 
 

 5 .  In the Price Quote column, choose the desired price quote. Click if the quotation is not in the Choose list. 
 

 6 .  Exit the Price Quote column. The order will be itemized automatically according to the price quote.
