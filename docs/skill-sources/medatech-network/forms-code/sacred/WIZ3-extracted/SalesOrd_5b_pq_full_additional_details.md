# To record additional details:

 

 1 .  Check the Contact column to make sure that the relevant sales order contact appears. Change it if necessary.
 Tip: To define new contacts for the customer or to assign a default contact for orders, use the Customer Contacts form, a sub-level of the Customers form. For more information, see the Open Customers Wizard . 
 

 2 .  In the References tab, record the customer's purchase order number in the Cust ' s PO column. Fill in any other brief details in the Details column. 
 

 3 .  Click the Price tab. If the order price is in a foreign currency, make sure that the correct C urrency appears.
 Tip for dual-currency users: If the order price is linked to a foreign currency, price index or currency basket, fill in the various linkage parameters. For details, run the Price Adjustment Wizard . 
 

 4 . Click if the order is for a specific project . 
 

 5 .  Click the Payment Info tab. Make sure that the T ax C ode (for dual-currency users: VAT Code ) and the P ayment T erms are correct. 
 

 6 .  As a default, the order is taken into account when calculating credit for this customer. If you want this order to be disregarded during credit calculations, remove the check mark from the Consider Credit column.
 Note: Leave the check mark here if you want to disregard a specific order item. 
 

 7 .  Enter the Attachments sub-level form to attach to the order any relevant documents (e.g., a price quotation confirmation sent by the customer). 
 

 8 .  If you want the order to be billed to a third party rather than the customer's usual billing customer, enter the Sales Order - Customer Details sub-level form and choose the desired Billing Customer. The account of that third party will be debited in the subsequent invoice.
