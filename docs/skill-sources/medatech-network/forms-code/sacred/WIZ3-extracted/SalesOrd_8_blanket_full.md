# To copy a blanket order to a sales order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders form. 
 

 2 .  Choose the customer's number. The customer's name, today's date and the order number are filled in automatically, and the order is assigned the Draft status. 
 Note: In the case of a walk-in customer, record the walk-in customer number. Then revise the name in the Cust. Name column, and fill in the address and other details in the Sales Order - Customer Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Blanket Sales Orders form and retrieve the blanket order in question. Make sure that the Disable Auto Copy column ( Follow-up tab) does not have a check mark. 
 

 4 .  Return to the Sales Orders form and click the References tab. In the Blanket Order column, choose the desired blanket order. 
 

 5 .  Leave the Blanket Order column. The sales order will be itemized automatically according to the blanket order.
