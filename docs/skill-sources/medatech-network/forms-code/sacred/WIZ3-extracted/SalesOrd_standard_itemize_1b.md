 Tip: To check part availability, use the Inventory for Part and the Part Availability sub-level forms. 
 

 8 .  If you are working with order item statuses, choose the desired Status . 
 

 9 .  If you want this particular order item to be disregarded when calculating customer credit, remove the check mark from the Consider Credit column.
 Note: If the entire order is disregarded (as defined in the upper-level form), a check mark will not appear. 
 

 10 .  If you will be billing the order in a multi-shipment invoice, and you want the order item to be billed to a third party rather than the customer's usual billing customer (or the one specified for this entire order), choose the desired Billing Customer . The invoice for the ordered part will consequently debit the account of that third party.
