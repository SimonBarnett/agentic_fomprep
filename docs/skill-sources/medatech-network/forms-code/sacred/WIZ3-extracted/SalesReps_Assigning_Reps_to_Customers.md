# Assigning Sales Reps to Customers

 

When the same sales rep usually makes sales to a particular customer, you can assign that sales rep to the customer. The sales rep appears as the default in any order recorded for that customer, though a different sales rep may be used for any given transaction. 
 
## To assign a sales rep to a customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  Click the Details tab and specify the Sales Rep Number . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Enter the History of Cust.GrpOLD sub-level form to view the history of sales reps assigned to the current customer. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
