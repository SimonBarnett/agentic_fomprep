# Recording Commission Documents

 

Sales rep commission documents provide an itemized record of commissions that have accrued to external sales reps. You can prepare them automatically in a batch on the basis of invoices recorded by the sales rep, or manually on the basis of invoices or receipts. 
 

After you have recorded a purchase invoice for a given commission, you can link the commission document to the invoice, which will facilitate your transactions with sales reps.
