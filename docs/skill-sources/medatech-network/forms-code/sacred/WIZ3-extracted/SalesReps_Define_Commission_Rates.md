# Defining Commission Rates

 

The percentage of commission due the sales rep can be defined per customer, per part family, or per sales rep. Where conflicting rates are defined, the customer rate overrides the part family rate, and the part family rate overrides the sales rep rate. Still, whatever default rate appears in an invoice can be revised manually. 
 
## To assign a commission per sales rep:

 

 ·        In the Sales Representatives form, designate the Commission % in the Details tab. 
 
## To assign a commission per part family:

 

 ·        Enter the Discounts/Commissions by Family sub-level form. For each part family, designate the % Commission . 
 
## To assign a commission per customer:

 

 ·        Enter the Customers form and designate the Rep ' s Commission in the Details tab.
