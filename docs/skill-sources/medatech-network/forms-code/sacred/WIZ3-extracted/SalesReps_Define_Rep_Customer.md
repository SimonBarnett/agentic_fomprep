# Defining a Customer as a Sales Rep

 
## Before You Begin

 

In order to allow your customer to issue purchase invoices for commissions due, you need to open a vendor account parallel to the existing customer account. Run the Open Vendors Wizard for details. 
 
## To record a sales rep from customer data:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  Select Define Customer as Sales Rep from the list of Direct Activations .
 Note : You cannot run this program if there is already a sales rep with the same number as the customer. Instead, click to record the sales rep manually. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Sales Representatives form and retrieve the new sales representative.
 Note: The Defined as Customer column in the Details tab is flagged automatically. 
 

 4 .  Click the Details tab and record the maximum total discount that this sales rep is allowed to give customers. If this differs for different part families, do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If the discount is the same for most part families, record it in the Maximum Discount (%) column. Then enter the Discounts/Commissions by Family sub-level form, and record a different value for individual part families. OR 
 

 ·              If the discount is different for each family, do not record it in the upper-level form. Instead, enter the Discounts/Commissions by Family sub-level form and record the discount for each family. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Sales Rep Managers sub-level form and specify the sales rep's immediate superior in the sales rep hierarchy. You can define different managers for different time periods. 
 Notes: 
 

 ·              You can view all sales reps below the current rep, on all levels, in the parallel Sales Rep Hierarchy sub-level form. 
 

 ·              Run the Sales Rep Hierarchy report to view the entire sales rep hierarchy for designated managers and time periods. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to define the sales rep ' s commission rates. 
 

 ·        Click to assign the sales rep to a customer.
