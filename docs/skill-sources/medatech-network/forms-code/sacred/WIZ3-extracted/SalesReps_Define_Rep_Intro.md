# Defining a Sales Rep

 

Sales reps can either be company employees working for a salary, or external agents, including your customers. External sales reps bill your company for their commissions via purchase invoices; consequently, they must be defined in the system as vendors as well as sales reps. Furthermore, sales reps that are also customers must be defined as customers, vendors and sales reps. Before you can define an external sales rep, you need to create the necessary accounts. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Define an employee or an independent agent as a sales rep 
 

 Define a customer as a sales rep
