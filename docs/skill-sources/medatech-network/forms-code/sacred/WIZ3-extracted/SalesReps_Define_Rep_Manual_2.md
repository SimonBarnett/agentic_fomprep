Sales Reps - Defining a Customer as a Sales Rep Manually 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To define a customer as a sales rep manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Representatives form. 
 

 2 .  Record a Sales Rep Number and Sales Rep Name . 
 

 3 .  Click the Address & Phone tab and record the sales rep's contact info. 
 

 4 .  Click the Details tab and flag the Defined as Customer column. 
 

 5 .  Record the maximum total discount that this sales rep is allowed to give customers. If this differs for different part families, do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If the discount is the same for most part families, record it in the Maximum Discount (%) column. Then enter the Discounts/Commissions by Family sub-level form, and record a different value for individual part families. OR 
 

 ·              If the discount is different for each family, do not record it in the upper-level form. Instead, enter the Discounts/Commissions by Family sub-level form and record the discount for each family. 
 

 6 .  Enter the Sales Rep Managers sub-level form and specify the sales rep's immediate superior in the sales rep hierarchy. You can define different managers for different time periods. 
 Notes: 
 

 ·              You can view all sales reps below the current rep, on all levels, in the parallel Sales Rep Hierarchy sub-level form. 
 

 ·              Run the Sales Rep Hierarchy report to view the entire sales rep hierarchy for designated managers and time periods.
