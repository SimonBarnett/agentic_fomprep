# Defining a Sales Rep as Inactive

 

Once you stop working with a given sales rep, you should define that rep as inactive. Consequently, a warning will be given if the rep is assigned to a customer or recorded in any document. 
 
## To define a sales rep as inactive:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Representatives form and retrieve the rep in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Details tab and flag the Inactive column.
