# To record a commission document manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Rep Commission form. 
Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 2 .  Record the Sales Rep Number of the rep to whom commission is due. The Date and Commission Doc No. are filled in automatically. 
 

 3 .  In the Details tab, designate the Planned Payment Date . 
 

 4 .  If the document is for commission due on sales to a particular customer, record the Customer Number . 
 

 5 .  If the sales rep has already billed your company for the current commission, record the number of the Purchase Invoice . Otherwise, you can fill in this column later. 
 

 6 .  Enter the Commission Items sub-level form and open a new line for each invoice or receipt for which a commission is due. 
 Tip: Use the Document Code column to distinguish between invoices and receipts. 
 

 7 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If the commission is based on an invoice, the Amount of Commission due for each item appears automatically. Revise as necessary. 
 

 ·              If it is based on a receipt, specify the Amount of Commission manually.
