# Defining Invoices for Inclusion in EC Sales Lists

 

When exporting goods to member states of the European Community, each customer invoice must indicate whether the invoiced sale should appear in the EC Sales List and whether your company is an intermediary supplier in a triangular transaction . You can record this information in a sub-level of any one of the following types of invoices: 
 

 ·        Multi-Shipment Invoices 
 

 ·        Sales Invoices 
 

 ·        Over-the-Counter Invoices 
 
# To define invoices for inclusion in EC Sales Lists:

 

 1 .  Enter the relevant form (for example, Multi-Shipment Invoices ) and retrieve the desired invoice. 
 

 2 .  Go to the Invoice - Add'l Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  In the ECSL Indicator column, choose the appropriate code for the invoice in question.
