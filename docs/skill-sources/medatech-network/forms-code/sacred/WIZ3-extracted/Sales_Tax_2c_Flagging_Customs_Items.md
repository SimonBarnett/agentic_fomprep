- 
 
 
 
# To flag customs items for inclusion in Intrastat and EC Sales List reports:

 

 1 .  Enter the Itemized Customs Rates form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired item or record a new Customs Item number and a Description . 
 Note : The list of items is based on an international list, common to all EU countries, used to determine customs duties. 
 

 3 .  Flag the Incl. in Intrastat column. 
 

 4 .  Flag the Supp Units-Intrastat column if supplementary units are required for this item in the Intrastat declaration. 
 
## To assign a customs item to an imported part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Parameters for Parts form and retrieve the imported part in question. 
 

 2 .  Click on the Shipping Expenses tab and choose the Customs Item .
