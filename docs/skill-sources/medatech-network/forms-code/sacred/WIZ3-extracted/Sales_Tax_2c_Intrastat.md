# Setting Up for Intrastat Declarations

 

Using Priority , you can produce monthly reports for Intrastat Declarations of the movement of goods between member states of the European Community. Extended declarations, including mode of transport and delivery terms, are generated automatically. 
 

When working with Intrastat, customs items must be defined and flagged for inclusion in the declarations, a customs item must be assigned to imported parts and Intrastat codes must be assigned to countries, terms of delivery and shipping methods. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Flag customs items for inclusion and assign to imported parts reports 
 

 Record each country ’s Intrastat code 
 

 Record Intrastat codes for terms of delivery 
 

 Record Intrastat codes for shipping methods
