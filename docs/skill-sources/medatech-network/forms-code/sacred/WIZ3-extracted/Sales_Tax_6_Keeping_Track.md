# Keeping Track of Tax Payable

 

When you finalize a customer invoice, the sales tax account attached to the tax code is credited for the amount of sales tax charged on the invoice. 
 
## To view sales tax owed:

 

 ·        Enter the Chart of Accounts form and move to the account associated with the tax code. The Balance column displays the total tax owed to tax authorities. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You may find the Sales Tax report useful. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main sales tax screen.
