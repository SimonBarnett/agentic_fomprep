# Setting Up Serial Numbers

 
## Maintaining Serial Numbers for Parts

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Parameters for Part form and retrieve the parts to which you intend to assign serial numbers.
 Note: You can only assign serial numbers to parts whose buy/sell unit and factory unit are both ea (a single whole unit). Check the units in the Part Catalogue form (under the Additional Details tab). 
 

 2 .  For each part, make sure the Maintain Serial Nos column is flagged.
