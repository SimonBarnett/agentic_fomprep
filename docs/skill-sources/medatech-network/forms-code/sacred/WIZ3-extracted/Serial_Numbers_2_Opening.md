# Opening Serial Numbers

 
## Before you can assign serial numbers to transactions, you must open them in the Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. form. You have two options. You can either:

 

 ·        Open serial numbers independently of any transaction (and attach them to transactions later) OR 
 

 ·        Open them as you attach them to the first inventory transaction that requires serial numbers 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Open serial numbers without a transaction 
 

 Open and attach serial numbers while recording a transaction 
 

 Attach an existing serial number to a transaction
