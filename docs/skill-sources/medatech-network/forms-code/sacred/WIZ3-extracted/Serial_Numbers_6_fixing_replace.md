# When the Customer Receives a Replacement Part

 

When a defective part is sent to the company for repairs and the customer receives a replacement part instead, the following steps are taken: 
 

 ·        The part is sent by the customer or technician to a regular company warehouse. This is recorded in a customer return document. 
 

 ·        The replacement part is sent to the customer. This is recorded in a shipping document. 
 

 ·        The defective part is repaired in the company laboratory. Any parts that are used, components that are replaced and work hours that are devoted to the repair are recorded in a service call opened for the fictitious customer “Goods”. 
 

 ·        The repaired part is sold to another customer. 
 
## For assistance in recording the customer return or the shipping document:

 

 ·        Run the Customer Shipment/Return Wizard . 
 
## For assistance in recording the service call:

 

 ·        Run the Service Call Wizard .
