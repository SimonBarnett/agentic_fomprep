# When the Repaired Part is Returned to the Original Customer

 
## When a defective part is sent to the company for repairs and then returned to the original customer, the following steps are taken:

 

 ·        The part is sent by the customer or technician to a repairs warehouse. This is recorded in a customer return document. 
 

 ·        The part is repaired in the company laboratory. Any parts that are used, components that are replaced and work hours that are devoted to the repair are recorded in the original service call. 
 

 ·        The repaired part is returned to the customer. This is recorded in a shipping document. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Define a repairs warehouse 
 

 Record receipt of a serialized part for repair 
 

 Record the return of the repaired part to the customer 
 
## Note:

 

To report usage of parts and components, as well as work hours, related to the repair, run the Service Call Wizard .
