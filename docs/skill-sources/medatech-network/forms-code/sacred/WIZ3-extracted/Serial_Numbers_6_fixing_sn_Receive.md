# To record receipt of a serialized part for repair:

 
## Tip:

 

For assistance in recording a customer return, run the Customer Shipment/Return Wizard . 
 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Returns form and open a return document as usual. 
 

 2 .  After designating the Customer Number , specify the repairs warehouse into which the part was received in the Receiving Warehouse column (in the General tab).
 Note: Click if you have not yet defined a repairs warehouse. 
 

 3 .  Itemize the return as usual in the Returned Items sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Serial Numbers in Transaction sub-level form to record the serial number of the returned part. 
 

 5 .  Continue filling out the form as usual (e.g., assigning an RMA number, finalizing the document).
