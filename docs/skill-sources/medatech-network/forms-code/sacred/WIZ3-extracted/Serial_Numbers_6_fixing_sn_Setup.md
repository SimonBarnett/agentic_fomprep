# Defining a Repairs Warehouse

 

Because defective parts will be returned to the customer, they maintain the customer number as their status and do not enter company inventory. It is therefore necessary to define a separate repairs warehouse which does not participate in inventory valuation. 
 
## To define a repairs warehouse:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouses form and open a new record. 
 

 2 .  Record the name of the laboratory in the Warehouse column. 
 

 3 .  In the Type column, specify W . 
 

 4 .  In the Inventory Valued column ( General tab), remove the check mark. 
 
## Note: 

 

For assistance filling in more warehouse details, run the Open a Warehouse Wizard .
