# To record the return of the repaired part to the customer:

 
## Tip:

 

For assistance in recording a shipping document, run the Customer Shipment/Return Wizard . 
 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Shipments form and open a shipping document as usual. 
 

 2 .  After designating the Customer No. , specify the repairs warehouse in which the part was repaired in the Sending Warehouse column (in the General tab). 
 

 3 .  Click the References tab, and specify the RMA Number assigned to the part. 
 

 4 .  Itemize the shipment as usual in the Shipped Items sub-level form. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Serial Numbers in Transaction sub-level form to record the serial number of the shipped part. 
 

 6 .  Continue filling out the form as usual (e.g., finalizing the document).
