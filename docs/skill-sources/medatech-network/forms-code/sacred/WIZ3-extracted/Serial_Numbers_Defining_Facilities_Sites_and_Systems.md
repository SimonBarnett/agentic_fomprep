Parts - Defining Facilities, Sites, and Systems for Serialized Parts 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To define sites, facilities, and systems for parts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sites form. 
 

 2 .  List the sites in which serialized parts are located. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Facilities form. 
 

 4 .  List the facilities in which serialized parts are located. For each facility, indicate a primary Site .
 Note: This site serves as the default for the facility in various forms (e.g., Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. ), but can be revised. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Systems form. 
 

 6 .  List the systems in which serialized parts are located. For each system, indicate a primary Facility . 
 Note: This facility serves as the default for the system in various forms (e.g., Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. ), but can be revised.
