# To attach a batch of consecutive serial numbers:

 

 1 .  In the correct form for the type of transaction (e.g., the Customer Shipments form), make sure you are in the desired document. 
 

 2 .  Enter the itemization sub-level form (e.g., Shipped Items ) and move to the line containing the desired part number. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Auto Recording of Serial Nos. sub-level form. Fill in the Prefix , From Serial Number , To Serial Number and Serial Number Length columns. 
 Note: Leave the Open New Serial Nos column blank. 
 

 4 .  If the part is a serialized component of a serialized part (e.g., a replacement fan belt for a refrigerator), enter the Serial Numbers in Transaction form (another sub-level of the itemization form) and fill in the Parent ' s Serial No. column.
