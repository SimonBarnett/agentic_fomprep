Serial Numbers - Attaching Existing Serial Numbers to Transactions: Manually 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To manually attach serial numbers: 

 

 1 .  In the correct form for the type of transaction (e.g., the Customer Shipments form), make sure you are in the desired document. 
 

 2 .  Enter the itemization sub-level form (e.g., Shipped Items ) and move to the line containing the desired part number. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Serial Numbers in Transaction sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  In the Serial Number column, open the Choose list. It lists all serial numbers that have been opened for the part in this warehouse and bin. Choose the serial number(s) you want.
 Note: Click if this is the first transaction being recorded for this serialized part, and the serial number you want does not appear in the Choose list. 
 

 5 .  If the part is a serialized component of a serialized part (e.g., a replacement fan belt for a refrigerator), fill in the Parent ' s Serial No. column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to view and record information about the serialized part .
