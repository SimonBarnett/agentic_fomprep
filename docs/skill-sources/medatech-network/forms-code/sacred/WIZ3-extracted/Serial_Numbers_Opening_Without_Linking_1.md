# Opening Serial Numbers

 

You can open serial numbers for a part either automatically (in a batch) or manually. 
 
## To open a batch of serial numbers:

 

 1 .  The default limit to the number of serial numbers that can be opened at once is 100. Click to change this value. 
 

 2 .  Run the Open Serial Numbers program. 
 

 3 .  You can view the newly created serial numbers in the Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. form. 
 
## To manually open single serial numbers: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. form. 
 

 2 .  Record the part number and the serial number you are assigning to the part. Click the Part Location tab and record the warehouse and bin where it is found.
