# Opening Serial Numbers as You Attach Them to the Transaction

 

While recording the first inventory transaction involving a given serialized part, you can open serial numbers and attach them to the transaction. 
 
## Before You Begin

 

If you are attaching the serial number to a customer shipment , do the following: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Customer Shipments form and retrieve the desired shipping document. 
 

 2 .  If the shipment is being made to a particular customer site, make sure the site appears under the References tab (since it will be assigned to the serialized part).
