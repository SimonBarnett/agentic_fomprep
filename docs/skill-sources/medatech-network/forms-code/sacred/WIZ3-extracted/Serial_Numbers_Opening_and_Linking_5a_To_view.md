# To view and/or record information about a serialized part:

 

 1 .  Enter the Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. form and retrieve the desired serialized part.
 Tip : To retrieve all serialized instances of a certain part, retrieve by Part Number . 
 

 2 .  You can record details of the part’s location in the Part Data tab. Indicate the system where the part is located; the facility and the site will be filled in automatically, but can be revised.
 Note : Click to define additional sites, facilities, and systems . 
 

 3 .  If the part was already shipped to a customer, you can view the customer details (e.g., name, site, contact) under the Customer Details tab. 
 

 4 .  If the part has already been installed, specify its Installation Date . 
 

 5 .  If the warranty for the part went into effect, you can view its details in the Warranty tab.
 Tip : To change the warranty conditions for serialized parts, enter the Logistic Constants form and change the value of the C A utoWarrant constant. 
 

 6 .  Click the Servicing tab to view the details of the last servicing received by the part, and/or to schedule the next one.
 Tip : To view and update the conditions of servicing required by this serialized part, enter the Servicing Requirements of Item sub-level form. 
 

 7 .  If the part is still on your premises, you can record the Warehouse and Bin in which it is stored under the Part Location tab.
