Service Calls - Preparing an Invoice for a Service Call.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To prepare a multi-shipment invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the desired service call. 
 

 2 .  Select Prepare Invoice/Credit Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To finalize the invoice:

 

 1 .  Select Multi-Shipment Invoices from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 2 .  Check all details recorded in the form and its sub-levels. 
 

 3 .  Select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## To print the invoice:

 

 ·        Select Print Invoice/Credit Memo from the list of Direct Activations . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can also prepare an invoice by running the Prepare Invoices program. This program opens multi-shipment invoices based on various documents, including service calls. It is recommended to use this program when working with projects, or when you want to prepare a batch of invoices together. 
 

 ·        For a detailed explanation of customer invoices, including setups, see the Customer Invoices Wizard . 
 

 ·        If you work on a cash basis, you can automatically prepare a pro forma invoice for a service call. For more details, run the Cash-Based Accounting Wizard . 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
