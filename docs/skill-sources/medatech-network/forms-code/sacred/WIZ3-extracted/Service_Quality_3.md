Customer Surveys - Recording Customer Responses 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record customer responses to a survey:

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Enter the Customers form and retrieve the customer in question. Enter the Customer Survey Responses sub-level form and record the date the survey was taken and the format code of the questionnaire. 
 

 ·              Enter the Customer Survey Responses form. Record the date the survey was taken, the format code of the questionnaire and the customer that is responding. 
 

 2 .  You can also record the name and position of the person who is actually answering the questions and the number of the service call to which responses are related. 
 

 3 .  Enter the sub-level form, Responses to Questions . All questions in the questionnaire appear automatically. 
 

 4 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·              If the customer selected an answer from the predefined list, choose that answer in the No. Response column. 
 

 ·              To record a longer customer response, use the Response (cont.) sub-level form instead. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Respondent's Remarks sub-level form (parallel to Responses to Questions ), and record any additional comments made by the customer.
