# Analyzing Results

 

Several reports are available to help you analyze the results of customer surveys. 
 

 ·        Responses to Surveys – Lists the results of individual surveys. 
 

 ·        Customer Survey Analysis – Summarizes responses (number and percentage of respondents) to each question in a designated survey. 
 

 ·        Customer Remarks – Lists customer remarks in a given survey.
