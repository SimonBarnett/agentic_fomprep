# To define a default reconciliation method:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financial Constants form. 
 

 2 .  Move to the AutoRecon constant and change its value, as needed.
 Note: This constant also affects reconciliation of payments to vendors with purchasing invoices. When the constant receives a value of 1 (By Order), purchasing invoices are reconciled automatically by method 2 (By Invoice). Use 1 only if all (or most) of your customers are walk-ins. 
 
## To define a reconciliation method for a specific customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts Receivable form and retrieve the customer`s account. 
 

 2 .  In the Receipt Recon Method column, choose the reconciliation method to be used for this customer. When the column is empty, transactions are reconciled according to the assigned default method.
