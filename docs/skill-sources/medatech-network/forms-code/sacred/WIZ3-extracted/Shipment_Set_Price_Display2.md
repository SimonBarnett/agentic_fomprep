Customer Shipment/Return - Setting the Price Display.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 To round the total prices of shipping documents as a system default: 
 

 ·        Enter the Financial Constants form, and change the value of the RoundOrd constant, if desired.
 Note: The constant also affects total prices in price quotations and in sales orders. 
 
## To set a rounding value for a specific customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  Click the Invoices tab and select the desired value in the Round Prices column.
 Note: This definition also affects total prices in price quotations, sales orders, customer invoices and customer receipts. 
 
## To hide prices on printouts to a specific customer:

 

 1 .  Enter the Financ. Parameters for Customers form and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  Click the Invoices tab and flag the Hide Prices in Shipping Doc column.
