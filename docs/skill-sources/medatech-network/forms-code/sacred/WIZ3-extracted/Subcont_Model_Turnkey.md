# Modeling Production for Turnkey Subcontractors

 

Turnkey subcontractors are defined in the system as regular vendors. That is, you open a vendor record for each one, and you do not flag them as subcontractors (since you do not provide them with raw materials or manage the inventory on their plant floor). Unlike a regular vendor, however, you do model production of the parts purchased from turnkeys. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Model a part that is always produced by a turnkey subcontractor 
 

 Model a part that is sometimes produced by a turnkey and sometimes manufactured in the factory
