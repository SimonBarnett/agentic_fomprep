 3 .  Enter the Routing for the Work Order sub-level form. After the work order is released, all of the operations recorded in the part's routing are displayed automatically in this form.

 Note: If the original operation in the routing has an alternate operation that is carried out in the subcontractor's warehouse, that alternate operation will appear instead. 
 

 5 .  Make sure that all of the operations that are meant to be performed at the subcontractor are flagged with either a T or L in the Inventory/Costing Control column.
 Note: The L setting is only used for intermediary operations that are sometimes performed by a subcontractor. Explanation. 
 

 6 .  Move to the Kit List sub-level form. If you have defined a special BOM for this part at the designated subcontractor, the required issues are listed accordingly. Otherwise, issues are listed according to the part's standard BOM. Make sure that the listed issues are the parts needed for production at the designated subcontractor.
