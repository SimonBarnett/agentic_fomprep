# To finalize a shipment to subcontractor document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipmt to Subcontr/Issue of Kit form, and retrieve the desired shipping document. 
 

 2 .  Change the Status (in the General tab) to Final . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Before you can make changes in a final shipping document, you need to reopen it by selecting Reopen Document from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 ·        Click to print the shipping document . 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
