# Opening a Shipment to Subcontractor Document

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipmt to Subcontr/Issue of Kit form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the General tab). 
 

 2 .  Record the intended Date of shipment. The Document Number appears automatically. 
 

 3 .  In the Subcontractor Number column, specify the subcontractor to which you are shipping the parts. The warehouse linked to the subcontractor appears automatically in the Vendor Warehouse column ( General tab). 
 

 4 .  The kit warehouse linked to the subcontractor (in the Warehouses form) appears automatically in the From Warehouse column (in the General tab). If you want to open a document and itemize it manually, specify the warehouse from which the parts are being shipped. This warehouse appears automatically in each line item in the Shipped Items sub-level form, but may be revised for specific items. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to itemize the document with parts issued to kits. 
 

 ·        Click to itemize the document with parts issued in bulk. 
 

 ·        Click to itemize the document with parent parts in progress. 
 

 ·        If you are working with the Fashion module, and need to itemize the shipment document manually, run the Fashion Wizard to learn how to record style assortments.
