# To revise or cancel a shipment to subcontractor document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Shipmt to Subcontr/Issue of Kit form, and retrieve the desired shipping document. 
 

 2 .  If the document is final, select Reopen Document from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 3 .  Make the desired changes. To cancel the document, change the Status (in the General tab) to Canceled .
 Note: You cannot revise or reopen a canceled document.
