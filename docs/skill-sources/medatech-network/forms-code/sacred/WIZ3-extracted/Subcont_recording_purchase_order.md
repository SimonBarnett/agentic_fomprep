# Recording a Purchase Order to a Subcontractor

 

MRP calculates the required quantities of the manufactured part according to orders received and work orders for parent parts, and opens work orders accordingly. 
 

Since the parts need to be ordered from the subcontractor, purchase planning treats the work orders as raw material issues, and creates purchase requisitions for them. After the purchase requisitions are authorized and the work orders are released, you can open purchase orders on the basis of the relevant purchase demand document, and link them to the work order for which the demand document was opened. For assistance, run the Purchase Order Wizard . 
 

If the part is always manufactured by the same subcontractor, the subcontractor should be defined as the part's preferred vendor in the Part Catalogue form. Consequently, purchase demands will be opened automatically for that subcontractor. 
 

You can also open purchase orders to a subcontractor manually, but the following points must be considered: 
 

 ·        A purchase order opened manually for a part whose standard production routing does not include a subcontractor operation will not be taken into account by MRP and purchase planning. 
 

 ·        Even if you link a manual purchase order to a work order in the appropriate column, this does not ensure that purchase planning will use the purchase order for the work order in question.
