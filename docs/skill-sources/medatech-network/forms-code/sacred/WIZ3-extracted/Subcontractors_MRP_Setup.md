# Setting Up MRP to Select Which Subcontractor to Use

 

You have the option of defining the monthly production capacity of each subcontractor for each subcontracted part, as well as your own monthly floor capacity. Moreover, you can rank your subcontractors (together with your own floor) to create an order of precedence. MRP will use this order to determine which subcontractor (if any) to assign to each new work order it opens. 
 
## To define production capacity:

 

 1 .  Enter the Projected Prod Capacity-Subcontr form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the year and the part in question. 
 

 3 .  Indicate the subcontractor's name. Or, to specify capacity on your own floor, use the predefined “Internal” subcontractor. 
 

 4 .  Fill in monthly projections of the quantity that the subcontractor in question (or your own plant floor) is capable of providing. 
 

 5 .  Rank all subcontractors for the same part, using the Priority column. The lower the number, the higher the priority.
