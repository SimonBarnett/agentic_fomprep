# To check the inventory status:

 

 1 .  Make a note of the status of the child parts in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Inventory Statuses form. 
 

 3 .  Move to the line of the status being checked. 
 

 4 .  Ensure that it is flagged as Available Inventory .
