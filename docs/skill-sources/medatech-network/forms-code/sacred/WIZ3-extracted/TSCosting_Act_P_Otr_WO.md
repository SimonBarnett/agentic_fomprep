# Solution – Checking the Work Order Data

 

Under costing option W , the cost of a manufactured part for a specific costing date is determined by the actual quantities and costs of raw materials and production as recorded for work orders that were closed between the previous past balance date and the costing date. Open work orders are not taken into account. 
 
## To check work order data:

 

 1 .  Enter the Product Costing form and retrieve the part in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Product Cost Components sub-level form and check the data on each work order line.
