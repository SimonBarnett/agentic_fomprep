# To check the BOM of the manufactured part: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Catalogue form and retrieve the part. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Child Parts sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  The manufactured part’s standard cost consists of the sum of the standard costs of the purchased (Type R ) child parts listed in the BOM. Make sure that the BOM is correctly defined. Explanation 
 

 4 .  Make sure that the relevant child parts are defined in the BOM, and are not flagged as Info Only in the Child Parts form. 
 

 5 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Click if you define standard part costs in terms of Std Material Cost . 
 

 ·      Click if you define standard part costs in terms of Actual Material Cost . 
 

 Tip: If you don’t know what the defined method is, run the View Costing Parameters program.
