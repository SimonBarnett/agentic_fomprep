# To check subcontractor, non-standard and supply costs:

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts - Std Costs form and retrieve the part. 
 

 2 .  Check the values in the Subcontract Costs , Non-std Costs and Supply Costs columns. Revise them as necessary.
