# Analyzing Demand Resulting from Scrap

 

When type P parts have defined scrap, this adds to the demand and affects the work order quantity. When type R parts have defined scrap, this adds to the demand and affects the issue quantity. In both cases, demand is calculated as: 
 

 ·        the original demand quantity divided by the scrap percentage. Example. 
 

Scrap can be defined both for the part itself or for when it is joined with a child part. 
 
## To check if scrap is defined:

 

 1 .   Enter the Parts form and retrieve the part in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Additional Details tab and check the amount of scrap defined in the Scrap column. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Child Parts sub-level form and check the Scrap (%) column for each child part.
