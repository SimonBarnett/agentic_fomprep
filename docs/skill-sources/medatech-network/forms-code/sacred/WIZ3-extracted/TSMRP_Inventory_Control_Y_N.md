# Is The Part Subject to Inventory Control?

 

If the part in question is ignored by MRP, this may be a result of part definitions. In calculating demand, MRP only takes into account parts that are subject to inventory control. 
 
## To check if the part is subject to inventory control:

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Catalogue form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Click the Additional Details tab and check if the Inventory Control column is flagged. If it is not, flag it and rerun MRP.
