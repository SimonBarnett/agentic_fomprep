- 
 
 
 
## To view the work orders of parents:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase/MRP Parameters for Part form and retrieve the parent part in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the MRP Analysis - Processed Parts sub-level form. The form displays the open, released work orders and planned work orders that were used by MRP. The form also displays work orders that have been identified as surplus. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Understand how MRP treats released work orders 
 

 Understand how MRP treats planned work orders 
 

 Understand how MRP treats surplus work orders 
 

 Understand why work orders were not opened 
 

 Understand why work orders were opened unexpectedly (without obvious demand)
