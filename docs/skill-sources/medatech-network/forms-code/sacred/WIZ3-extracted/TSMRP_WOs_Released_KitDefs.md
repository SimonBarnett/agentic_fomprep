Troubleshooting MRP Wizard - Released Work Orders, Kit Definitions 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
## To check whether the child part is in the kit list:

 

 1 .  Enter the Work Orders form, and retrieve the work order in question. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Kit List sub-level form and check whether the child part appears. 
 
## To check the child part's issue type in the kit list:

 

 1 .  Enter the Parts form and retrieve the child part in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Additional Details tab. Check the following: 
 

 ·                If the Issued to Kits column is flagged, the part is issued to kits. 
 

 ·                If neither the Issued to Kits column nor the Manual Issue column are flagged, the part is issued in bulk.
