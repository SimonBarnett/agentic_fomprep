# Analyzing Demand Quantities

 

One of the factors in the calculation of demand for a part is the part’s demand range, that is, the period of time for which part demand is unified into a single work order. 
 
## To see if a demand range has been defined:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase/MRP Parameters for Part form, and retrieve the part in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Planning Quantities tab and check if a Demand Range is indicated in the relevant column.
