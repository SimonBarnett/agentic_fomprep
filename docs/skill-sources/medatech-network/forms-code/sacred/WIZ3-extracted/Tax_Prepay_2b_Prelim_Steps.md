# To indicate which income accounts are exempt from tax prepayments (and/or VAT):

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and retrieve the desired account. 
 

 2 .  Move to Full-Record Display Mode , click the Report tab and choose one of the following options in the VAT Report Item column: 
 

 ·      Taxable Income (option 5) 
 

 ·      Exempt from VAT (option 6) 
 

 ·      Exempt from Prepaymt (option 7) 
 

 ·      Exempt-Prepaymt & VAT (option 8) 
 
## Note: Income recorded for accounts in the first two categories is used to calculate prepayments.
