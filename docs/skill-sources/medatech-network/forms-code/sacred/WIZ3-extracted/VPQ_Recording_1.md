# Recording a Vendor Price Quotation

 

Price quotations received from vendors are recorded into the system, in order to be able to compare them with quotes from other vendors, and to generate purchase orders at a later stage. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Record a price quotation based on an RFQ 
 

 Record a price quotation based on a purchase demand document 
 

 Record a price quotation manually 
 

 Print a price quotation
