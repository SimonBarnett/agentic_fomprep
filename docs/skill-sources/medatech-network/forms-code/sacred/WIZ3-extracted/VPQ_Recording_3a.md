# To assign quoted prices to items:

 

 1 .  Enter the Itemized Quotation sub-level form. All line items from the RFQ are displayed. 
 

 2 .  Make sure that the part descriptions are suitable.
 Tip: You can change the description of any part that is flagged in the Change Desc. column of the Part Catalogue form. 
 

 3 .  Specify the minimum quantity of each item for which the quoted price is valid.
 Note: A vendor price quotation can be graded so that a different price is offered for different minimum quantities of the same part. Example. 
 

 4 .  For each line item, specify the Unit Price , Currency and Discount . 
 

 5 .  In the Due Date column, specify the date committed to by the vendor.
 Note: The Due Date is only the date on which the goods will be leaving the vendor's premises; it should be early enough to allow time for shipment and laboratory testing before the actual date on which the goods are required for use. 
 

 6 .  If the RFQ was based on a purchase demand document, the original PR Number of the quoted item appears automatically. Otherwise, you can link the item to a purchase requisition manually. 
 

 7 .  If this item is being purchased for a project, click to link it to a project or project activity. 
 

 8 .  Enter the Itemized Request - Remarks sub-level form and check the remarks recorded for this part. Revise them if necessary.
 Notes: 
 

 ·      The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable). 
 

 ·      To include these remarks in the printout of the RFQ, select a suitable print format . 
 

 9 .  Return to the Vendor Price Quotations form and check the quote's Total Price in the Price tab. 
 

 10 .  Click the References tab and finalize the quote by adjusting its Status .
