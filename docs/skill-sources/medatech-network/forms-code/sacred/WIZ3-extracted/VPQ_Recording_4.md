 6 .  In the Due Date column, specify the date committed to by the vendor.
 Note: The Due Date is only the date on which the goods will be leaving the vendor's premises; it should be early enough to allow time for shipment and laboratory testing before the actual date on which the goods are required for use. 
 

 7 .  If desirable, link the quoted item to a specific purchase requisition in the PR Number column. 
 

 8 .  If this item is being purchased for a project, click to link it to a project or project activity. 
 

 9 .  Enter the Itemized Request - Remarks sub-level form and check the remarks recorded for this part. Revise them if necessary.
 Notes: 
 

 ·      Any part remarks recorded in the part catalogue appear in this form automatically if Copy Part Remarks has been flagged for price quotations (in the Copy Part Remarks to Document form). 
 

 ·      The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable). 
 

 ·      To include these remarks in the printout of the quote, select a suitable print format . 
 

 10 .  Return to the Vendor Price Quotations form and check the quote's Total Price in the Price tab. 
 

 11 .  Click the References tab and finalize the quote by adjusting its Status .
