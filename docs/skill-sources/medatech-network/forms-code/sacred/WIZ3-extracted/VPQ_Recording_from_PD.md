# To record a vendor price quotation based on a purchase demand document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Vendor Price Quotations form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the References tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the Vendor Number . The system will automatically assign a Quote Number , and the Quote Date will be filled in with the present date, which can be revised.
 Note: In the case of a one-time vendor, specify the one-time vendor number. Then revise the name in the Vendor Name column, and fill in the address and other details in the Price Quote - Vendor Details sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Specify the Contact at the vendor for this price quote.
 Tip: To define new contacts for this vendor, use the Vendor Contacts form, a sub-level of the Vendors form. 
 

 4 .  Click the References tab. In the Purch Demand column, select the desired purchase demand document from the Choose list. The Itemized Quotation sub-level form will automatically list all line items in the demand document.
 Note: Only demand documents with a status that allows a price quote to be opened from it (e.g., Approved ) will appear in the Choose list. 
 

 5 .  Any Details recorded in the demand document appear automatically. Revise if necessary.
