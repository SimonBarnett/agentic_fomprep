Vendor Price Quotes - Updating Purchase Requisitions Automatically 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To update purchase requisitions automatically:

 

 1 .  Enter the Vendor Price Quotations form and retrieve the desired price quote. 
 

 2 .  Select Update PRs With Quote Data from the list of Direct Activations . Data from the price quote will be used to update the data in the corresponding PRs (e.g., vendor, manufacturer, vendor/manufacturer part number, price, discount, currency). 
 
## Note:

 

All PRs linked to a quoted item (in the Itemized Quotation sub-level form) will be affected.
