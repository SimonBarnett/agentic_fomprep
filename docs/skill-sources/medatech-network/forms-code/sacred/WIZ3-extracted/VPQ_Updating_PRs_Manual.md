Vendor Price Quotes - Updating Purchase Requisitions Manually 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To update purchase requisitions manually:

 

 1 .  Do one of the following: 
 

 ·      Enter the Purchase Demands form, retrieve the desired purchase demand document and enter the Itemized Purchase Requisitions sub-level form. 
 

 ·      Enter the Purchase Requisitions form and retrieve the relevant purchase requisitions. 
 

 2 .  Move to the Quote Number and Quote Line columns and fill them in for each part. Data from the price quote will be used to update the data in the PR (e.g., vendor, manufacturer, vendor/manufacturer part number, price, discount, currency).
 Tip: Use the Price Quotes for PR sub-level form to view more information regarding the various quotes received for each requisition. 
 
## Note:

 

To view the various PRs linked to a specific quoted item, retrieve the quote in question in the Vendor Price Quotations form, move to the relevant line in the Itemized Quotation sub-level form, and enter the next sub-level form, Purchase Requisitions for Item .
