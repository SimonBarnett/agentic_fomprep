- 
 
 
 
## To define a part's preferred vendor:

 

 1 .  Enter the Purchase/MRP Parameters for Part form and retrieve the part in question. 
 

 2 .  In the Preferred Vendor column (under the General tab), designate the appropriate vendor number. 
 
## To assign vendor rankings:

 

 1 .  Enter the sub-level form, Vendors per Part . All vendors that sell this part appear (provided their vendor part lists have been recorded). 
 

 2 .  In the Ranking of Vendor column, assign a number to each vendor. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        The preferred vendor appears with a ranking of 1 . 
 

 ·        The lower the number, the higher the ranking. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen .
