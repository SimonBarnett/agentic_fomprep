# Assigning Quality Levels for Purchases

 

 Priority offers a mechanism for maintaining quality levels for purchased parts (e.g., electronic components) and restricting purchases to those vendors that meet the designated criteria. The higher the quality level, the more demanding the quality conditions. 
 

To use this mechanism: 
 

 ·        assign a quality level to all appropriate purchased parts 
 

 ·        assign an overall quality level to all vendors selling those parts. 
 

 ·        assign special quality levels to vendors for specific parts (that override the vendor's overall quality level). 
 

 Priority allows you to purchase the part in question only from vendors whose quality level (overall or specific) equals or exceeds the part's required quality level. 
 
## To assign a quality level to a part:

 

 ·        Enter the Purchase/MRP Parameters for Part form and retrieve the part in question. Assign a Quality Code to the part (under the General tab). 
 
## To assign an overall quality level to a vendor:

 

 ·        Enter the Vendors form and retrieve the vendor in question. Click the Details tab and assign an Overall Quality Code to the vendor. 
 
## To assign a special quality level to a vendor for a specific part:

 

 ·        Enter the Parts per Vendor form (sub-level of the Vendors form) and move to the part in question. Specify the Special Quality Code for this vendor and part.
