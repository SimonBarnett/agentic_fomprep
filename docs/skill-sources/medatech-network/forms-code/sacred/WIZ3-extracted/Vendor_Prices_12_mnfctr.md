# Recording Manufacturers and Their Parts

 

It is useful to maintain a list of manufacturers and their parts when: 
 

 ·        vendors use the manufacturer's part number 
 

 ·        vendors maintain separate price lists per manufacturer. 
 
## To define a list of manufacturers and their parts:

 

 1 .  Enter the List of Manufacturers form and record a number and name for each manufacturer. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Parts per Manufacturer sub-level form and create a list of parts made by the manufacturer. For each part, specify Priority ' s internal part number and the manufacturer's part number and description.
