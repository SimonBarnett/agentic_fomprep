# Determining How You Work With Vendor Prices

 

Record a single purchase price per part when you buy each part from a single vendor and are not interested in maintaining a history of purchase prices. 
 

Record vendor price lists when: 
 

 ·        you buy the same part from different vendors 
 

 ·        you work with base products 
 

 ·        you wish to maintain a history of purchase prices 
 

 ·        vendors offer different prices for different minimum quantities of the same part. 
 

The PPriceByList constant determines whether the prices of the parts that you buy are maintained in price lists or each part is assigned a single purchase price. The constant is pre-set for work with vendor price lists. 
 
## To work with single purchase prices:

 

 1 .  Enter the Logistic Constants form. 
 

 2 .  Change the value of the PPriceByList constant to 0 .
