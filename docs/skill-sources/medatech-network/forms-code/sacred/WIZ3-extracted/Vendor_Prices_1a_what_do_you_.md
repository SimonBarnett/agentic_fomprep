# Recording Vendor Prices

 

Vendor prices in Priority serve as the source of part prices in purchasing documents (e.g., purchase orders). You can maintain vendor prices in one of two ways: recording one purchase price per part or working with vendor price lists. 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Record single purchase prices 
 

 Record vendor price lists 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Make sure the PPriceByList constant has been correctly set. Click for details . 
 

 ·        Single purchase prices are recorded manually in the Part Catalogue form. If you work with vendor price lists, the price offered by the part's preferred vendor appears in that form instead.
