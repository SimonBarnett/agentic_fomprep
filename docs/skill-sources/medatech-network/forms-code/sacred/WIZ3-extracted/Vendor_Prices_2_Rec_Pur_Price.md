# Recording a Purchase Price for Each Part

 

If you do not work with vendor price lists, you need to record a purchase price for each part. 
 
## To record a part's purchase price:

 

 1 .  In the Part Catalogue form, retrieve the part in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Prices & Costs tab and record the part's Purch Price .
