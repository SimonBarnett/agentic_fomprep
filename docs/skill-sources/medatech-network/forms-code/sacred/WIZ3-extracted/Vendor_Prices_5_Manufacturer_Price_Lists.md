# Recording a Manufacturer Price List

 

Before you can record a manufacturer price list, the manufacturer and its parts must be defined in Priority . Click to create a manufacturer part list. 
 
## To record a manufacturer price list:

 

 1 .  Enter the Vendor Price Lists form. 
 

 2 .  Assign a code to the price list and record its description. 
 

 3 .  Choose the vendor number and record the date on which the price list goes into effect. 
 

 4 .  Specify the currency of the prices. 
 

 5 .  Record the manufacturer number. 
 

 6 .  If you want the prices in this price list to serve as the source for commission sales involving this vendor, flag the Commission Prices column. For more details, run the Commission Sales Wizard .
