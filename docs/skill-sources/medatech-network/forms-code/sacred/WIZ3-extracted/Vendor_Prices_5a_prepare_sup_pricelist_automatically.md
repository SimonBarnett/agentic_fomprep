# Preparing a Vendor Price List Automatically

 

 Priority offers the option of automatically preparing vendor price lists with the items in each vendor's part list. Once the price list is prepared, you need to record the part prices manually. 
 
## Before You Begin

 

 ·        Make sure that the vendor's list of parts is up-to-date, using the Parts per Vendor form, sub-level of the Vendors form. 
 
## To prepare a vendor price list:

 

 ·        Run the Prepare Vendor Price List program. 
 
## To check the price list and record part prices:

 

 1 .  Enter the Vendor Price Lists form and retrieve the price list you have created. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Part Prices sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Check the list you have created. You can add or delete parts. 
 

 4 .  Record the price of each part.
