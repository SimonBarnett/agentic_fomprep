Vendor Parts/Prices - Updating Prices by a Fixed Sum or Percentage 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To update prices by a fixed sum or percentage:

 

 ·        Run the Raise/Lower Vendor Prices program. 
 

 OR 
 

 1 .  Enter the Vendor Price Lists form and retrieve the desired price list. 
 

 2 .  Select Raise/Lower Vendor Prices from the list of Direct Activations .
