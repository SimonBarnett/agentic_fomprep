# Updating a Price List from the Part Catalogue

 

You can update part prices and discounts in a vendor price list directly from the part catalogue. 
 
# To update vendor prices from the part catalogue: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Part Catalogue form and retrieve the desired part. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Part Price in Vendor Price Lists sub-level form and move to the desired price list. 
 

 3 .  Update the Quantity , Unit P rice and/or Discount% .
