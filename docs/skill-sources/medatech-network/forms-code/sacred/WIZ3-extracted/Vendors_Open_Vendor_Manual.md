# To open a vendor record and A/P account with different numbers:

 

 1 .  Open the Financial Constants form and make sure the IdentCustAcc constant is set to 0 . 
 

 2 .  Enter the Vendors form and record a Vendor Number and Vendor Name . Fill in all the vendor's details. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts Payable form and record an Account No. and Account Description for the vendor. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Financial Parameters for Vendors form and retrieve the new vendor. 
 

 5 .  In the Account No. column, specify the A/P account you opened for the vendor. Fill in any other financial parameters. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the vendor to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
