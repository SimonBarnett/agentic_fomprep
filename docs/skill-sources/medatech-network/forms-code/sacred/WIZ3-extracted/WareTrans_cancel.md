# Canceling a Pending Transfer Document

 

As long as a transfer document is not final, it may be revised or canceled. 
 
## To cancel a pending transfer document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouse Transfer form and retrieve the desired document. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Transferred Items sub-level form and make sure that the quantities of all items are 0 . 
 

 3 .  Return to the upper-level form, and change the status to Canceled (in the General tab). 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Cancellation cannot be undone; instead, open a new document. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
