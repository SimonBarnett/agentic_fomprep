# To record the packed crates:

 

 1 .  Enter the Packing Slips in Document sub-level form and specify the desired crates (by their packing slip numbers).
 Notes: 
 

 ·      You can only choose crates, recorded in final packing slips, that are located in the sending warehouse and bin designated in the upper-level form, and that are not linked to another document. 
 

 ·      If the packing slip is for an order, the order will be copied to the transfer document and order items will be closed automatically once each line is approved. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Transferred Items form (another sub-level). The transfer document is itemized with all parts in the designated crates. 
 

 3 .  For each item, approve the transferred quantities: 
 

 ·      If the SCratesQuant constant is set at 1 , the Approve column is flagged automatically, and the quantity is filled in automatically. 
 

 ·      Otherwise, flag the Approve column manually, and the quantity will appear automatically. 
 

 Tip: To change the value of the SCratesQuant constant, which will affect future warehouse transfers, enter the Logistic Constants form. 
 

 4 .  If you work with projects, click to link any transferred item to a project or project activity. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to finalize the document. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
