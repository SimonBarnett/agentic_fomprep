# Finalizing a Transfer Document

 

When you finalize a transfer document, all the line items with quantities of 0 are deleted. You cannot revise a final document; however, you can reopen it, make the necessary changes and finalize it again. 
 
## To finalize a transfer document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouse Transfer form and retrieve the desired document. 
 

 2 .  Move to the General tab. 
 

 3 .  Change the status to Final . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to print the document.
