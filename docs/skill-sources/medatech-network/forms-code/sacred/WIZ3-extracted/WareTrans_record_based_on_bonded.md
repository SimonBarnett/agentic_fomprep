# To record the transfer of goods which have been released from a bonded warehouse:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouse Transfer form.
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the General tab). 
 

 2 .  Specify the Date of the transfer. An internal Document number is filled in automatically. 
 

 3 .  Click the References tab, and specify the number of the release document in the Source Document column. 
 

 4 .  If the transfer has already been recorded in an external document, fill in the document number in the External Document Number column. 
 

 5 .  Record brief remarks pertaining to the transfer in the Details column. 
 

 6 .  Click the General tab, and specify the Receiving Warehouse (and Bin , where relevant). 
 Note: The warehouse into which the goods were originally released appears automatically in the Sending Warehouse (and Bin , where relevant). 
 

 7 .  Click if the warehouse transfer is for a project. 
 

 8 .  To record remarks pertaining to this transfer that will appear in the printout, use the Remarks sub-level form.
