# Recording a Warehouse Transfer Document

 
## A warehouse transfer document can be prepared automatically or recorded manually:

 

 ·        You can itemize the document automatically on the basis of internal orders from a given branch. 
 

 ·        You can itemize the document automatically on the basis of packed crates recorded in a packing slip. 
 

 ·        You can itemize the document manually. 
 

 ·        Dual-currency users: You can itemize the document on the basis of a Release from Bonded Warehouse document (when transferring released goods from their original receiving warehouse into a different warehouse). 
 
## Important:

 

If you are filling a branch order by transferring packed crates listed in a packing slip, make sure the packing slip is linked to the order and itemize the transfer on the basis of the packed crates . 
 
## What do you want to do?

 

 Record a warehouse transfer that fills an internal branch order 
 

 Record the transfer of packed crates 
 

 Record a warehouse transfer manually 
 

 Dual-currency users: Record the transfer of goods released from a bonded warehouse
