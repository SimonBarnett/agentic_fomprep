# Reopening a Final Transfer Document

 

Final transfer documents cannot be revised or canceled. However you can reopen the document to make any necessary changes. 
 
## To reopen a final transfer document:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouse Transfer form and retrieve the desired document. 
 

 2 .  Select Reopen Document from the list of Direct Activations . The document will return to its initial status. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        Click to cancel the document. 
 

 ·        Click to return to the main screen.
