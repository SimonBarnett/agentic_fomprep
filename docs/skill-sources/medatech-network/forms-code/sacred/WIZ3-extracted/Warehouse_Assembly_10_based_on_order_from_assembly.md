# To manually open an assembly document for an order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouse Assembly form. 
 

 2 .  Record the number of the customer who placed the order. 
 

 3 .  Click the References tab. Choose the order on which the current assembly is based. The order items are filled in automatically in the Assembly Reporting sub-level form, and their component parts are filled in automatically in its sub-level, the Assembly Components form. 
 Note: The Choose List for the Order column includes all the customer's open orders.
