# To open an assembly document from within a sales order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Sales Orders form and retrieve the order in question. 
 

 2 .  Select Open Assembly Based on Order from the list of Direct Activation s . The program opens a new assembly document for the date of the order. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Warehouse Assembly form. 
 

 4 .  Click the References tab and retrieve the new assembly document (by the order number).
