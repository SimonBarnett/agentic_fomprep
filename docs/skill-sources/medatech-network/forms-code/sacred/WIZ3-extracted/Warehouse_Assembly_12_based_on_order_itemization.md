Warehouse Assemblies - Viewing Parts to be Assembled 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To view parts to be assembled:

 

 1 .  Enter the Assembly Reporting sub-level form. The form displays the assembled parts which were ordered. 
 

 2 .  Make sure that the parts and the required quantities are correct. 
 

 3 .  If serial numbers are maintained for any of the ordered parts and you know the serial numbers in advance, specify them in the Serial Number column.
 Note: If a serial number was already opened for the part, select the number from the Choose list. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Line Items - Remarks sub-level form and check the recorded remarks. Revise them as needed.
 Note: The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable).
