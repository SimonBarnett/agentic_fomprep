# To report assembly:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouse Assembly form and retrieve the desired assembly document. 
 

 2 .  If the date that appears in the document is not the date on which the assembly took place, revise it. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Assembly Reporting sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  For each assembled part, record the quantity assembled. If a check mark appears in the Auto Record Child column, the assembled quantities of its child parts are automatically calculated according to the quantity you record here. 
 

 5 .  Record a Serial Number for the part, if necessary, or check the one that already appears. 
 Note: If a serial number was already opened for this part, select the number from the Choose list. 
 

 6 .  Enter the Assembly Components sub-level form. The form displays the child parts of the assembled part in the upper-level form. 
 

 7 .  If you are working with the standard BOM or the BOM created for an order, the assembled quantities of child parts are calculated automatically. Check that these are correct. Otherwise, record the quantity of each child part that was used. 
 

 8 .  For each of the child parts, ensure that the issuing warehouse is the one appearing here. Revise, if necessary. 
 

 9 .  Record a Serial Number for the child part, where necessary. 
 Note: If a serial number was already opened for the part, select the number from the Choose list.
