Warehouse Assemblies - Reporting Assembly Time 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To report assembly time:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouse Assembly form and retrieve the desired assembly document. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Assembly Reporting sub-level form. Move to the part for which you want to report assembly time. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Reported Hours sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Specify the Date of the assembly. Your username appears automatically in the Employee column. Change it if you are reporting work hours for another employee. 
 

 5 . The employee's default work hour part appears automatically. If no work hour part has been defined for the employee, specify the appropriate work hour part manually. 
 

 6 . Use either the From Hour and To Hour columns, or the Total Hours column, to report the time spent on the assembly.
