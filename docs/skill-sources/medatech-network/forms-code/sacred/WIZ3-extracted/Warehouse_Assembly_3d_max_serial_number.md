Warehouse Assemblies - Setup: Max Quantity of Serial Numbers 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To set the maximum quantity of serial numbers that can be automatically opened at one time:

 

 1 . Enter the Logistic Constants form. 
 

 2 . Move to the CMaxSernOpen constant and specify the desired quantity. 
 
## Note:

 

Serial numbers can only be opened for parts flagged in the Maintain Serial Nos column of the Part Catalogue form (in the Additional Details tab).
