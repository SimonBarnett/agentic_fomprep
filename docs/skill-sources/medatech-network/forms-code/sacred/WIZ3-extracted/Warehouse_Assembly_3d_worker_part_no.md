Warehouse Assemblies - Setup: Employee Work Hour Part 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 To define a work hour part: 
 

 1 .  Enter the Part Catalogue form. 
 

 2 .  Open a new line and record a Part Number and Description . 
 

 3 .  Click the Additional Details tab and select R in the Type column. 
 

 4 .  Remove the check mark from the Inv. Control column. 
 

 5 .  In the Unit column, select work hour . 
 

 6 .  Click the Details tab. In the Family column, select a work hour family (e.g., the predefined Work Hour family). 
 

 7 .  Click the Prices & Costs tab. In the List Price column, record the currency and price of one hour of this type.
 Note: To assign different prices for this work hour part to different customers, leave this column empty and record the part in the customer price lists. 
 

 8 .  In the Std. Cost column, record the standard cost of one hour of this type.
 Note: This value affects assembly cost calculations. 
 
## To assign default work hour parts to employees:

 

 1 . Enter the Personnel File form and retrieve the desired employee. 
 

 2 . Enter the Company-Specific Information sub-level form. 
 

 3 . Specify the work hour part.
