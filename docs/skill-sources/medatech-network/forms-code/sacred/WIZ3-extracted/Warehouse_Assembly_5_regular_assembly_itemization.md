Warehouse Assemblies - Recording Parts to be Assembled 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record parts to be assembled:

 

 1 .  Enter the Assembly Reporting sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Specify the part number of the part that is to be assembled. 
 

 3 .  Record a Serial Number for the part, if necessary. 
 Notes: 
 

 ·      If a serial number was already opened for this part, select the number from the Choose list. 
 

 ·      Once a serial number has been recorded for the part, you can attach serial numbers to its child parts as well. 
 

 4 .  Record the Required Quantity of the part that needs to be assembled. 
 Note: If this is a part with serial numbers, record each unit on a separate line, with a quantity of 1 . 
 

 5 .  If you are not using the standard bill of materials, but rather want to record a custom BOM (different components and/or different quantities), remove the check mark from the Auto Record Child . 
 

 6 .  Enter the Line Items - Remarks sub-level form and check the recorded remarks. Revise them as needed.
 Note s : 
 

 ·      Any part remarks recorded in the part catalogue appear in this form automatically if Copy Part Remarks has been flagged for assembly documents (in the Copy Part Remarks to Document form). 
 

 ·      The text may not be open to revision (if the IChgPartText constant is set to 1 and the part description is not changeable).
