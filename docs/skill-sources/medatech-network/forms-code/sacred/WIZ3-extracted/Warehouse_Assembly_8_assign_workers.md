# To schedule workers for an assembly:

 

 1 .  Enter the Warehouse Assembly form and retrieve the assembly document in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Scheduling tab and record the date and time on which the assembly is to begin and end. 
 

 3 .  You can schedule up to three workers to perform the assembly (in the Assigned to , Additional Worker , and Third Worker columns). This will be automatically recorded both as a task assigned to all the workers involved and as an appointment in each worker's calendar.
 Tip : To view current appointments for all users, enter the Calendar (All Users) sub-level form. 
 
## Note:

 

A task will be opened automatically for all scheduled employees, and (if the end time is later than the start time) an appointment will be opened in their calendars. For information on handling tasks and using the calendar, run the CRM Wizard .
