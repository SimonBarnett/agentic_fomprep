- 
 
 
 
# To assign warranties to serialized parts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. form. Retrieve the desired part and serial number. 
 

 2 .  Record the installation date of the serialized part. This can be identical to the shipping date, or it can be later.
 Note: If warranties are assigned when the part is shipped, the installation date is recorded automatically when you finalize the shipping document. 
 

 3 .  Click the Warranty tab and make any desired changes to the warranty data that was filled in automatically. 
 

 4 .  If the warranty lasts until a certain meter reading (e.g., the first 35,000 miles driven in a car) rather than until a certain date, click the Servicing tab and specify the reading in the Paid to Metered Qty column. 
 
## Note:

 

Click to extend warranties.
