# Recording Payments When Tax is Withheld

 

When a check payment or bank transfer to a vendor is recorded, taxes are withheld automatically from any vendor flagged to report income. The amount deducted is based on the withholding tax information recorded for the vendor in the Financial Parameters for Vendors form, but may be revised directly in the payment document. 
 

 Notes : 
 

 ·        Click to set the vendor ' s withholding tax information . 
 

 ·        When recording the invoice, you can assign a different Withholding Code to any invoice item, which will override the vendor's defined Max. Tax Code in determining progressive withholding tax. 
 

 ·        For details on recording payments, run the Check Payments Wizard or Bank Transfers Wizard . 
 

 ·        If any portion of the payment is not subject to withholding tax, flag it in the Vendor ' s Cust Debt column of the Payment Details sub-level form. This occurs automatically when the vendor is also a customer and amounts owed to you (customer invoices) are deducted from amounts that you owe (purchase invoices). Details. 
 

 ·        When withholding tax is progressive, you can view calculations of the sum and percentage of tax to be withheld for the payment in the Withheld Tax by Bracket sub-level form.
