# Defining the Withholding Tax Account

 

If the Account Initialization-Intl program has been run, the Withheld Tax-Vendors account has already been set up. All you need to do is record the maximum amount of tax to be withheld from vendors that lack an exemption. 
 
## To open an account for withholding tax:

 

 1 .  Enter the Chart of Accounts form and move to Full-Record Display Mode . 
 

 2 .  Define a new account.
 Tip : Run the Open GL Accounts Wizard . 
 

 3 .  Click the Report tab and select Withheld Tax-Vendors (value 9 ) in the VAT Report Item column. 
 
## To define the maximum withholding tax percentage:

 

 1 .  Enter the Withholding Tax Definitions form. 
 

 2 .  In the line for Withheld Tax-Vendors , record the maximum percentage to be deducted in the W. Tax% column. This amount will be deducted from vendors that do not have a full or partial exemption from the tax authorities. 
 

 3 .  Specify the account defined for withholding tax in the Account No. column.
