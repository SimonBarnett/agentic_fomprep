# Closing a Work Order

 

A work order is closed when production of the part is completed or discontinued. It is also closed automatically when assigned a status that is flagged to close the work order. In all other cases, you need to close the work order manually. 
 
## To close a work order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Work Orders form and retrieve the desired work order. 
 

 2 .  In the Details tab, flag the Closed column. The work order is automatically assigned the status defined as the Manual Close Status (see the Statuses for Work Orders form). 
 
## To reopen a closed work order:

 

 1 .  Enter the Work Orders form and retrieve the desired work order. 
 

 2 .  In the Details tab, remove the check mark from the Closed column. The work order status will be updated automatically.
