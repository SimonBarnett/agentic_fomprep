# To view and/or update the kit list:

 

 1 .  Enter the Work Orders form and retrieve the desired work order. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Kit List sub-level form. All child parts defined for the part to be produced are displayed. 
 

 3 .  Check that all of the required parts appear, and only these. Delete or add lines as required. 
 

 4 .  In the For Operation column, check that each child part displays the operation for which it is needed. 
 

 5 .  If part revisions are maintained for a particular child part, make sure that the correct Part Revision appears. 
 

 6 .  The quantity of each child part is calculated automatically according to the quantity of parent parts to be produced and the parent-child ratio defined for each part. Make sure that the quantities are correct for the current work order.
 Note: Unlike parts issued in bulk, issues to a kit do not automatically include the part's defined percentage of scrap, although they are taken into account by MRP and purchase planning. If scrap is defined for a part that was issued to the kit, you need to revise its quantity manually.
