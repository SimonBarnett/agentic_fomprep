# Clearing the Worksheet

 

Pending reconciliations are the result of unfinished reconciliation processes. They remain in the worksheet until they are finalized or canceled. Therefore, to clear the worksheet, you must either finalize (i.e., accept) or cancel each of the pending reconciliations. Explanation . 
 
## To clear the worksheet:

 

 1 .  Enter the Reconciliation Worksheet . Retrieve all records having a number greater than 0 in the Reconciliation Num . column. 
 

 2 .  Review each pending reconciliation and decide whether to cancel it or finalize it. 
 

 3 .  To cancel undesired reconciliations, do one of the following: 
 

 ·              Run the Cancel Pending Reconcil'ns program, using the appropriate reconciliation numbers as input, OR 
 

 ·              Cancel reconciliations manually in the Reconciliation Worksheet by replacing the reconciliation number with 0 in each of the relevant lines. 
 

 4 .  Finalize the remaining reconciliations.
