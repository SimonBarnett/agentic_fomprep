# Handling the Reconciliations Created by the Program

 

 1 .  To review the resulting reconciliations, run the Pending Reconciliations report. 
 

 2 .  Click if this is the first time you are reconciling a certain account . 
 

 3 .  If most of the reconciliations are incorrect, you can cancel them in a batch . 
 

 4 .  If only some of the reconciliations are incorrect, enter the Reconciliation Worksheet and cancel the problematic ones by replacing their reconciliation numbers with 0 . 
 

 5 .  If the Reconciliation Worksheet includes unreconciled items, click to reconcile those items manually . 
 

 6 .  Click to finalize the reconciliations in the worksheet .
