# To manually reconcile items:

 

 1 .  Enter the Reconciliation Worksheet . Move to the item you want to reconcile. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Entries for Reconciliation sub-level form. The items appearing here are potential matches for the item selected in the upper-level form. 
 

 3 .  Find the matching item or items. In the Reconciliation Num. column, assign them all the same number. Use a number that has not yet been assigned to a reconciliation; for example, if the number assigned to the last reconciliation was 30 , use 31 . As a result, the same number is filled in automatically in the upper-level form. 
 

 4 .  Click to reconcile matching items with variance (using a reconciliation adjustment account) .
