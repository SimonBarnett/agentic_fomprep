# Dealing With Variance

 

When a variance exists between the amounts of linked transactions, you can still reconcile them by creating an adjustment entry to cover the difference. When the reconciliation is finalized, a journal entry is automatically recorded, that transfers the difference to the designated reconciliation adjustments account. The final reconciliation will be between the original entries and the adjustment entry. There are two ways to create adjustment entries: automatically while finalizing reconciliation or manually during the reconciliation process. 
 
## Creating an Adjustment Entry While Finalizing Reconciliation

 

 1 .  Enter the Reconciliation Worksheet . 
 

 2 .  If you have not yet assigned reconciliation numbers to the matching items with variance, assign them now. 
 

 3 .  Select the Finalize Reconciliation program from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 4 .  Specify the account(s) and the amount(s) for the adjustment entry. 
 

 Notes: 
 

 ·        The total amount for adjustment may be divided among up to three separate accounts. 
 

 ·        When the program is completed, the adjustment entry is posted and reconciled with all the items in question. 
 
## Manually Creating the Adjustment Entry

 

 1 .  Enter the Reconciliation Worksheet . 
 

 2 .  If you have not yet assigned reconciliation numbers to the matching items with variance, assign them now. 
 

 3 .  Make sure you are on a line containing one of the items with variance. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Adjustment Entry Definitions sub-level form. 
 

 5 .  Select one or more account numbers for the adjustment entry. Record a transaction date and/or a due date and an amount for each selected account.
 Note:  You can use the Reconciliation Adjs. account or any other account. 
 

 6 .  Dual-currency users: To create a foreign-currency entry for a local-currency account (e.g., for the purchase of foreign currency), fill in the Foreign Currency , Exch. Rate and Sum in Foreign Curr. columns ( References tab).
 Note: The adjustment account must be in the local currency 
 

 7 .  Select Finalize Reconciliation from the list of Direct Activations of the Reconciliation Worksheet . The adjustment entry is posted and reconciled with all the items in question.
