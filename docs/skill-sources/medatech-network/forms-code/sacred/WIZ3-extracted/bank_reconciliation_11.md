# Dealing With Service Fees (and Similar Bank Entries)

 

Certain bank statement items (e.g., for service fees) do not have corresponding journal entries. Such transactions are recorded as an adjustment entry. There are two ways to create adjustment entries: automatically while finalizing reconciliation or manually during the reconciliation process. 
 
## Creating an Adjustment Entry While Finalizing Reconciliation

 

 1 .  If the Reconciliation Worksheet is not already open, open it now. 
 

 2 .  Assign the same reconciliation number to all relevant bank statement items. 
 

 3 .  Select the Finalize Reconciliation program from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 4 .  Specify the account(s) and the amount(s) for the adjustment entry. 
 

 Note s: 
 

 ·        The total amount for adjustment may be divided among up to three separate accounts. 
 

 ·        When the program is completed, the adjustment entry is posted and reconciled with all the items in question. 
 
## Manually Creating the Adjustment Entry

 

 1 .  If the Reconciliation Worksheet is not already open, open it now. 
 

 2 .  Assign the same reconciliation number to all relevant bank statement items. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Adjustment Entry Definitions sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Select one or more account numbers for the adjustment entry. Record a transaction date and/or a due date and an amount for each selected account. 
 

 5 .   Dual-currency users: To create a foreign-currency entry for a local-currency account (e.g., for the purchase of foreign currency), fill in the Foreign Currency , Exch. Rate and Sum in Foreign Curr. columns ( References tab).
 Note: The adjustment account must be in the local currency. 
 

 6 .  Select Finalize Reconciliation from the list of Direct Activations of the Reconciliation Worksheet . The adjustment entry is posted and reconciled with all the items in question.
