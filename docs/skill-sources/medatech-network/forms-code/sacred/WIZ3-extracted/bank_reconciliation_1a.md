# To set up bank reconciliations:

 

 1 .  If the bank account is not yet defined, define it now in the Banks and Bank Accounts forms. Be sure to open a GL account for the bank account in the Chart of Accounts and to assign this GL account to the bank account in the Bank Accounts form. 
 

 2 .  To avoid inadvertent cancelation of large batches of final reconciliations, you can define a maximum batch size. To do so, open the Financial Constants form and record the desired value for the CancelFinalRecons constant.
