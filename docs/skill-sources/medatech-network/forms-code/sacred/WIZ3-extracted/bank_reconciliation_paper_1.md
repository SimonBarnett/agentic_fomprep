# To flag journal entries for reconciliation:

 

 1 .  Enter the Reconcil Work Area-Paper Statemt and make sure that the amounts in the Opening Bal in Bank and Closing Bal in Bank columns match the corresponding amounts appearing on the bank statement to be reconciled. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Entries to Reconcile sub-level form. The entries appearing here are potential matches for the items in the current bank statement. 
 

 3 .  Flag the journal entries to be reconciled with the current bank statement (in the Reconcile column). As a result, the amounts in the Flagged for Reconcil and ClosingBal in Ledger columns in the upper-level form are updated automatically.
 Tip: To view all journal entries that have already been reconciled with the current bank statement, enter the parallel Reconciled Entries-Paper Statemt sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Do not close the Reconcil Work Area-Paper Statemt.
