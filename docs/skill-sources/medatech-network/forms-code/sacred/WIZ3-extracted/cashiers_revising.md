# Revising the Details of Checks and Credit Card Vouchers

 

Certain details of a check or credit card voucher can be revised if they are found to be inaccurate. 
 
## To revise check details:

 

 1 .  Enter the Revision of Checks form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired check and make the necessary revisions. 
 
## To revise credit card payment details:

 

 1 .  Enter the Revision of Credit Card Slips form. 
 

 2 .  Retrieve the desired voucher and make the necessary revisions. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        You can display all the changes made to payments by running the History of Payment Changes report. 
 

 ·        You can view all the changes made to a specific check or credit card slip in the History of Changes form, a sub-level of the relevant revisions form.
