# Confirming a Receipt

 

In certain circumstances, particularly when cashiers are physically removed from one another, you are required to manually record the receipt of checks and credit card slips. 
 
## To confirm a receipt:

 

 1 .  Enter the Deposit Slips form and retrieve the original deposit slip (the one recorded by the cashier who sent the funds). 
 

 2 .  Enter the Deposited Items sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Check the sums in the deposit slip items to see that they match what you received. 
 

 4 .  Return to the upper-level form and select Confirm Receipt Into Cashier from the list of Direct Activations . The receipt of all items in the deposit slip is confirmed. 
 
## Notes: 

 

 ·        You can also confirm a receipt, line by line, by using the Cashiers form and its sub-level, Unconfirmed Cashier Contents . 
 

 ·        You can view all the checks and credit slips whose receipt has been confirmed (whether via the deposit slip or the cashier) in the Cashier Balance form (another sub-level of Cashiers ).
