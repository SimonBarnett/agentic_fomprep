Working With Cashiers - Viewing the Cashier Balance 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To view the balance of a cashier:

 

 1 .  Enter the Cashiers form and move to the desired cashier. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Cashier Balance sub-level form to view all items at the cashier. Each check/credit card slip appears on a separate line.
 Note: Checks and credit card slips recorded in a pending deposit slip are included in the cashier balance. The (temporary) document number appears in the Pending Deposit column. 
 

 3 .  To delay the deposit of a given check or credit card slip, flag the Checking column.
