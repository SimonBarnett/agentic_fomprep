# Restricting Customers

 

Customers are defined as restricted or under warning on the basis of their status. 
 

When a customer is defined as restricted , the assigned status can prevent users from performing certain actions (e.g., opening orders, shipments, invoices). 
 

When a customer is under warning (i.e., about to be restricted), users receive a warning message whenever they open an order, shipment or invoice for that customer. The document can nevertheless be recorded. 
 
## To assign the status to a customer that is restricted or under warning:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  Select the relevant Status . 
 Notes: 
 

 ·      The date as of which the customer is restricted appears automatically in the Aging tab of the Customer Credit sub-level of the Financ. Parameters for Customers form. 
 

 ·      To view all customer statuses and their attributes, press F6 twice from the Status column and retrieve all records. 
 

 ·      Any customer for which a returned payment document is opened is automatically assigned whatever status is flagged as the one For Returned Payment .
