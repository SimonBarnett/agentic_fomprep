# Viewing the Customer's Status History

 

You can view a detailed history of changes to the customer's status, including warnings and restrictions. The company employee assigned to each status change is also displayed, as well as the date and time. 
 
## To view the status history:

 

 1 .  Enter the Customers form and retrieve the desired customer. 
 

 2 .  Enter the History of Statuses sub-level form.
