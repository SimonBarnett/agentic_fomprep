# Tips for using the Technician Scheduling Calendar:

 

 ·        Use the options in the Top Bar or Tool Tray to change how data are displayed. For instance, you can select which technicians to display, change the range of dates, and fine-tune the level of detail (number of days displayed and their division into hours or minutes). 
 

 ·        To select multiple technicians, hold down the Shift or Ctrl key while highlighting the users in question with the cursor. 
 

 ·        To schedule a new service call, double click in the appropriate time slot and fill in the required details. 
 

 ·        To postpone the service call to a later day or hour, drag it to the desired time. To change the technicians assigned to a service call, drag it to the line for the desired employee. 
 

 ·        To quickly locate a specific service call (or other appointment), search by key word (or string) in the subject (in a service call, this usually includes the customer name and the call number). That is, press Ctrl+F3 and record the desired text. Press Ctrl+F to perform additional searches for the same key word.
 Windows users: Press F3 instead of Ctrl+F to perform additional searches for the same key word. 
 

 ·        To view the details of a service call, press F11 . To change those details, double click on it or press F6 .
