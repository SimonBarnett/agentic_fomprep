# Opening/Updating a Service Call from an Incoming E-mail Message

 

If your company provides an e-mail address for technical support, you can easily convert an e-mail message into a service call from your Priority mailbox. Furthermore, you can update an existing service call by adding the text of a new message into the service call remarks. 
 
## Note:

 

You can also open and update service calls from e-mail messages received in MS-Outlook, via the interface between Outlook and Priority . For details, run the CRM Wizard . 
 
## To open or update a service call from an e-mail:

 

 1 .  Open the Mailbox form and retrieve your messages. 
 

 2 .  Click the From Cust tab. For each message that you want to convert, make sure a Customer Number is specified.
 Note: If the sender of the message is recorded in the system as a customer contact, the customer number appears here automatically. 
 

 3 .  If you are updating an existing service call, specify the Service Call Number . 
 

 4 .  Select Open/Update Service Call from the list of Direct Activations . 
 

 5 .  Enter the new/updated service call by pressing F6 twice from the Service Call Number column. The text of the e-mail message appears in the Details of Malfunction sub-level form.
