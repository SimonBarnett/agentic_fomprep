# To open a service call: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Calls form. 
 

 2 .  Choose the customer number and the serial number of the part.
 Notes: 
 

 ·      In the case of a walk-in customer, record the walk-in customer number. Then revise the name in the Cust. Name column, and fill in the address and other details in the Customer Details sub-level form. 
 

 ·      If payment for this service call will be made by another customer (that is not defined as a billing customer for the current customer in the Financ. Parameters for Customers form), specify the customer's number in the Billing Customer column in the Customer Details sub-level form. This customer's account will appear automatically in the journal entry that will open when an invoice is finalized for this service call. 
 

 ·      If this is not a serialized part, choose the part number.
