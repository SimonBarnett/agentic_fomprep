# Opening Service Calls Based on Orders 

 

In certain organizations and under certain circumstances, service calls are opened on the basis of orders for spare parts, replacement parts and/or technician work hours for installing those parts. 
 
## Before You Begin

 

It is strongly recommended that you record order tolerances for all relevant spare parts, replacement parts and work hours. This prevents supplying too many parts or work hours beyond what was ordered. 
 
## To record order tolerances:

 

 1 .  Enter the Inventory Parameters for Parts form. Retrieve the relevant parts and work hours. 
 

 2 .  Click the Sales tab. In the Order Tolerance (%) column, record a percentage for each part and work hour.
