# To open the service call:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Calls form. 
 

 2 .  Click the References tab and specify the Sales Order number. If desired, specify the customer's purchase order number in the Cust ' s Reference column. 
 Note: If payment for this service call will be made by another customer (that is not defined as a billing customer for the current customer in the Financ. Parameters for Customers form), specify the customer's number in the Billing Customer column in the Customer Details sub-level form. This customer's account will appear automatically in the journal entry that will open when an invoice is finalized for this service call.
