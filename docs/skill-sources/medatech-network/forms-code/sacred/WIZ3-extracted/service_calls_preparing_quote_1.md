# Price Quotes for Service Calls

 

If the customer wants to receive a quote before authorizing a repair, you can prepare one. 
 
## To prepare the quote:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the service call. Click the Financial Data tab and make sure the tax code is correct. 
 

 2 .  If you want the prices of work hours or replacement parts to be taken from a price list other than the basic price list or the customer's regular price list, specify the Price List in the References tab. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Service Report-Parts sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  Record each part (e.g., replacement parts) you expect to be used in the repair. 
 

 5 .  In the Billable column, either a check mark appears or does not appear automatically. 
 

 ·      The column is blank if the part is covered by a warranty or service contract. If necessary, flag the column. 
 

 ·      If a check mark appears, you can remove the check. Specify a billing exemption type.
 Tip: To add a new exemption type, use the Types of Billing Exemptions form. 
 

 6 .  Change prices and discounts if necessary.
