Service Calls - Recording an FAQ from a Service Call 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record a question from within a service call:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the desired service call. 
 

 2 .  Enter the FAQ sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Record the Question . When you leave the column: 
 

 ·      the FAQ Number is assigned automatically, and 
 

 ·      the contents of the parallel Details of Malfunction and Description of Repair forms are copied into the Answer sub-level form; revise the answer text as needed. 
 

 4 .  The initial Status appears automatically; revise as necessary. In the Assigned to column, make sure the correct username is specified.
 Tip: You can define work flow rules so that the assigned user changes automatically when a given status is assigned. 
 

 5 .  When the question is authorized to appear in the FAQ report, return to the upper-level form and assign a status that is defined as Approved . The Approved column is flagged automatically. 
 
## To add the question to a menu:

 

 1 .  Enter the Menu Link sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  In the Menu Number column, select the number of the desired menu. The Menu Title will appear automatically. 
 

 3 .  Specify the Position you want this question to occupy in the menu. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        To view the full contents of the menu, use the FAQ Menu form. 
 

 ·        Click to link the menu to the Main Menu , for display in the FAQ report.
