Service Calls - Reporting Part Usage.2 
 
 
- 
 
 
 

 2 .  Enter the Service Report-Parts sub-level form. Record each part and the date it was used. Fill in the Actual Quantity column. 
 Note s: 
 

 ·      If a price quote was prepared for this service call, or if the call is based on an order or servicing package, parts are already listed with planned quantities. Record the actual quantities used, any additional parts, and delete any parts that were not used. 
 

 ·      If Copy Part Remarks has been flagged for service calls (in the Copy Part Remarks to Document form), any part remarks recorded in the part catalogue are copied automatically to the Line Items - Remarks sub-level form for each part listed. 
 

 3 .  To report the use of a serialized part, choose the Serial Number . This serialized part will automatically be attached to the parent part (appearing in the upper-level form), and will update the Serialized Components of Part form, sub-level of the Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. form. 
 

 4 .  In the Billable column, either a check mark appears or does not appear automatically. 
 

 ·      The column is blank if the part is covered by a warranty, service contract or servicing package. If necessary, flag the column. 
 

 ·      If a check mark appears, you can remove it. If you do, choose a billing exemption type.
 Tip: To add a new exemption type, use the Types of Billing Exemptions form. 
 

 5 .  If you want the prices of work hours or replacement parts to be taken from a particular price list (other than the customer's regular price list), specify the Price List in the References tab. 
 

 6 .  Change any of the following if necessary: prices and discounts, the part status, the warehouse and/or bin. 
 

 7 .  If the part belongs to a work order or lot, record it. 
 

 8 .  If you work with projects, click to link the part to a project or project activity. 
 

 9 .  Click if any component parts were returned (e.g., replacement of a defective component).
