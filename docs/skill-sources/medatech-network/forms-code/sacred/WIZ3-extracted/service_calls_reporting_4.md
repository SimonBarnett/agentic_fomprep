- 
 
 
 
# To report the work hours invested in the service call: 

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Report-Labor form (another sub - level of the Service Calls form). Record the date, technician, work hour part, and start and end times of the call. 
 Note: If a price quote was prepared for this service call, or if the call is based on an order or servicing package, work hours are already listed with planned hours. You need to fill in the Actual Hours , technician, start and end times, and delete any work hour parts not used. 
 

 2 .  In the Billable column, either a check mark appears or does not appear automatically. 
 

 ·      The column is blank if the work hours are covered by a warranty, service contract or servicing package. If necessary, flag the column. 
 

 ·      If a check mark appears, you can remove the check. Specify a billing exemption type.
 Tip: To add a new exemption type, use the Types of Billing Exemptions form. 
 

 3 .  Change prices and discounts if necessary. 
 

 4 .  Remain in the Service Report-Labor form.
