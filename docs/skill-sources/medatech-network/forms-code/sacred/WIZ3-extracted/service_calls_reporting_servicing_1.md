# Reporting Routine Servicing

 

Once a routine servicing has been completed, you need to report the details of the repair that was carried out, including: 
 

 ·        The parts used for the call (including returns) 
 

 ·        The labor invested in the call (including travel time) 
 

 ·        Purchase invoices from subcontractors (when appropriate). 
 
## To record general information for the routine servicing:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the call for routine servicing. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Service Report-Parts sub-level form. The Servicing Paymt item appears on the first line. Its unit price is the price assigned to this servicing package, and it is flagged as billable. 
 

 3 .  To charge the customer for the servicing, specify 1 in the Actual Quantity column. If desired, change the price and/or discount.
