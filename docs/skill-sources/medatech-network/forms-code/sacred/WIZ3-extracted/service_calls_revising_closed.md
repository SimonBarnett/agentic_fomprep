# Revising a Closed Service Call

 

After a service call is closed, you can still change its details, service contract or billing status (i.e., whether or not the call will be billed). You can also change the price, discount, billing status and billing exemption type of individual parts and work hours reported for the service call, which will affect its total price. In addition, you can flag a part or work hour report for checking, to prevent its accidental billing, or delete an existing checking flag. 
 

 Note: You can change the status of a closed service call (to another final status), as well as the name of the user responsible for handling it, in the Service Calls form. 
 
## To revise a service call:

 

 ·        Use the Revision of Closed Service Calls form, and its sub-level, Revision of Details .
