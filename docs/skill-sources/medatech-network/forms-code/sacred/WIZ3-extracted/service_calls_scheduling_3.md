Service Calls - Scheduling a Call Manually 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To schedule a call manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the call in question. 
 

 2 .  Click the Dates tab and change the Agreed Start Date , if necessary. In the Authorized By column, specify the person at the customer site who authorized a late start date.
 Note: This person must be one of the customer's contacts. 
 

 3 .  Click the Scheduling tab and fill in the times work is scheduled to begin and end. 
 

 4 .  Record the main technician assigned to the call in the Assigned to column. 
 

 5 .  If there are additional technicians scheduled to handle the call, do any of the following: 
 

 ·      To schedule up to two additional technicians for the same times and dates, fill in the 2 nd Technician and 3 rd Technician columns. 
 

 ·      To schedule larger groups of technicians and/or to schedule work on additional dates, enter the Tasks for Document sub-level form. Record the names of individual users and/or entire groups, indicating start and end dates and times.
 Note: You must be authorized to access the designated group. If you are not, contact your system manager.
