# Canceling a Service Call

 

You cannot delete service calls, but you can flag them as canceled. 
 
## To cancel a service call:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the desired service call. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Service Report-Parts sub-level form and change all reported part quantities to zero. 
 

 3 .  Enter the parallel Service Report-Labor sub-level form and change all reported labor quantities to zero. 
 

 4 .  Return to the upper-level form and change the Status of Call to Canceled (in the General tab).
