# Analyzing Malfunctions

 

The many reports in the Malfunction Analysis Reports menu provide summary data to help you evaluate your customer service and product quality. For instance: 
 

 ·        Mean Time to Repair (MTTR) 
 

 ·        Mean Time to Assist (MTTA) 
 

 ·        Mean Time Betw. Failures (MTBF) 
 

 ·        Frequency of Resolution Usage 
 

 ·        Frequency of Malfuncts by Type 
 

 ·        No. Malfuncts per Serial Number 
 

 ·        Number of Malfunctions per Part
