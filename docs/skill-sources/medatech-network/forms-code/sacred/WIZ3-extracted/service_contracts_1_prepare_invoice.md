# Preparing an Invoice for a Service Contract

 

 Priority allows you to prepare a multi-shipment invoice for a service contract automatically. The program bills for all recorded contract payments that have not yet been billed. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·      The InvContPart constant determines the part number appearing in service contract invoices. For more details, run the Service Setup Wizard . 
 

 ·      If you work on a cash basis, you can automatically prepare a pro forma invoice for contract payments. For more details, run the Cash-Based Accounting Wizard . 
 

 ·      You can only open an invoice for a service contract with a status flagged with the In Effect property. 
 
## To prepare a multi-shipment invoice:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Contracts form and retrieve the desired service contract. 
 

 2 .  Select Prepare Invoice from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 
## To finalize the invoice:

 

 1 .  Select Multi-Shipment Invoices from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 

 2 .  Check all details recorded in the form and all its sub-levels. 
 

 3 .  Select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 
## To print the invoice:

 

 ·        Select Print Invoice/Credit Memo from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        You can also prepare an invoice by running the Prepare Invoices program. This program opens multi-shipment invoices based on various documents, including service contracts. It is recommended to use this program when working with projects, or when you want to prepare a batch of invoices together. 
 

 ·        For a detailed explanation of customer invoices, including setups, run the Customer Invoices Wizard .
