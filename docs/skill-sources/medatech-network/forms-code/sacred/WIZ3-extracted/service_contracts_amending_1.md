# Adding Items to an Active Contract

 

Sometimes it is necessary to add parts to an active service contract which did not previously include them. For example, if your customer signed a five-year service contract for twenty-five computers and later purchased an additional five, you can add the latter to the original service contract (for an additional cost, naturally). 
 
## To add new items to an active service contract:

 

 1 .  Retrieve the desired contract by its Customer No. and Contract No.
Note: You may have to change the contract status to one that allows revision. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Items in Contract sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Open a new line and record the Part Number and Serial Number of the new item. 
 

 4 .  Fill in the Installation Date and End of Warranty columns.
 Note: Billing for the new parts does not begin until their warranty expires. If you do not indicate an end date for the warranty, the system will bill the customer for the additional parts retroactively from the date the contract went into effect.
