# Determining Charges for the New Items

 

You can charge either by prorate or by flat rate. 
 
## To charge by prorate: 

 

 1 .  If the part has a prorate charge, that percentage appears in the Items in Contract sub-level form. Otherwise, you can record a percentage manually. 
 Notes: 
 

 ·      You can set up prorate percentages for parts in the Service Parameters for Part form. 
 

 ·      If the contract was opened for a project, rates are taken from there. 
 

 2 .  Make sure that the Unit Price column is filled in and that the Discount% for the part is correct. 
 

 3 .  In the upper-level form, make sure the percentage of overall discount and the tax code are correct. 
 
## To charge a flat rate:

 

 1 .  In the Items in Contract sub-level form, fill in the Flat Rate Charge/Yr column. 
 

 2 .  In the upper-level form, make sure the percentage of overall discount and the tax code are correct.
 Note: If the contract was opened for a project, rates are taken from there.
