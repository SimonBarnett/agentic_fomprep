# Automatic Calculation of Payments for Additional Content

 

If you have already calculated the service contract payment for the current or following month, and afterwards additional items were added to the contract, you can calculate the additional cost automatically. 
 
## To calculate an exceptional payment for goods added to a service contract:

 

 1 .  Select Calculate Paymt for Additions from the list of Direct Activation s . 
 

 2 .  Enter the Payment Installments sub-level form and make sure that the information is correct. 
 

 3 .  If you need to check the calculated payment (e.g., with the customer), flag the Checking column. When you want to bill the customer, remove the check mark from the column. 
 
## Notes:

 

 ·        By default, payments are calculated over the same period as the last recorded payment. For example, if the original payment for the goods in the contract was calculated until the end of the following month, the payment for the additional goods will also be calculated until the end of the following month. 
 

 ·        As of the next payment, calculation of contract payments will automatically include payment for the new items, so there will be no need to handle them separately. 
 

 ·        Click to prepare the invoice .
