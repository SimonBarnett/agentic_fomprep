# Checking Contract Data

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Contracts form and retrieve the problematic contracts. 
 

 2 .  For each contract, click the Dates in Effect tab and make sure that the contract has a Status that permits you to record payments.
 Note: Payment installments can only be previewed and calculated when the contract has a status flagged as Can Record Payments in the Statuses for Service Contracts form (e.g., Active , Approved ). 
 

 3 .  Click the Terms tab and make sure the payment frequency ( Paymt Freq ) is correct. 
 

 4 .  Check the Overall Discount , Tax Code and Final Price . Revise as necessary. 
 

 5 .  Enter the Items in Contract sub-level form and check all part prices. Revise as necessary.
