Service Contracts - Recording Payment Installments Manually 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To record payment installments manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Contracts form. Retrieve the desired contract. 
 

 2 .  Click the Dates in Effect tab and make sure that the contract has a Status that permits you to record payments.
 Note: Payment installments can only be previewed and calculated when the contract has a status flagged as Can Record Payments in the Statuses for Service Contracts form (e.g., Active , Approved ). 
 

 3 .  Enter the Payment Installments sub-level form. 
 

 4 .  In the Date column, record the date payment is due. 
 

 5 .  In the Covers From and Covers Until columns, record the first and last dates of the period covered by this installment. 
 

 6 .  If desired, change the description of the installment. 
 

 7 .  The balance due for the entire contract appears automatically in the Payment Amount column. To charge for part of the contract, change this value. 
 

 8 .  The serviced part that will appear in the invoice for the contract payment appears automatically in the Part to Charge column, according to the value of the InvContPart constant. You can override the constant and specify a different part, as needed. 
 

 9 .  To exempt the customer from part (or all) of the installment, add a line to the Payment Installments form. Record the sum to write off and flag the Exempt from Payment column. 
 

 10 .  If the installment still needs to be cleared (e.g., with the customer), flag the Checking column. When you are ready to bill the customer, remove the flag.
