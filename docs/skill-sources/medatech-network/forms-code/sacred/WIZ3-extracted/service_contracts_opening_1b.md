# Preparing the Contract

 
## To open the contract:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Contracts form. 
 Note: At any point in the following process, you can transfer handling of the document to another employee, by specifying his/her username in the Assigned to column (in the Dates in Effect tab). 
 

 2 .  Choose the desired customer.
 Note: If payment for this contract will be made by a customer other than the usual billing customer (as defined for the current customer in the Financ. Parameters for Customers form), specify the appropriate customer number in the Billing Customer column ( References tab). This customer's account will then appear automatically in the journal entry that opens when an invoice for this service contract is finalized. 
 

 3 .  Enter the effect and expiration dates 
 

 4 .  Click the References tab. If necessary, choose different service terms. 
 

 5 .  If the customer maintains a number for the service contract in an external system, enter it in the External Document Number column. 
 
## Note:

 

Remain in the Service Contracts form.
