# Itemizing the Contract

 

You can itemize the contract either automatically or manually. 
 
## To itemize the contract automatically:

 

 1 .  Click the Contract Source tab. 
 

 2 .  To itemize the contract on the basis of: 
 

 ·      A sales invoice: Fill in the Sales Invoice No. column. 
 

 ·      A customer shipment: Fill in the Shipment Number column. 
 

 ·      An earlier contract: Fill in the Previous Contract column. 
 

 ·      A project: Click here . 
 

 3 .  In the Items in Contract sub-level form, make any desired changes and additions to the information (e.g., delete a part; record a discount for a part). 
 
## To itemize the contract manually:

 

 1 .  Enter the Items in Contract sub-level form. 
 

 2 .  Record the serial number and quantity (generally 1 ) of each part. 
 Tip: Remain in the Items in Contract sub-level form.
