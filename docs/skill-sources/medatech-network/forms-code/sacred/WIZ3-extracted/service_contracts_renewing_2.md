# Renewing Service Contracts

 

You can renew contracts individually or automatically in a batch. 
 
## To renew one contract:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Contracts form. Retrieve the desired contract. 
 

 2 .  Change the expiration date. 
 
## To renew a batch of contracts:

 

 1 .  Enter the Service Contracts form. Retrieve the desired contracts. 
 

 2 .  For each contract, make sure that a value appears in the Renewal Option (Years) column. 
 

 3 .  Run the Renew Contracts program. You can view the new expiration dates in the Service Contracts form.
