Service Contracts - Calculating Contract Cost 
 
 
- 
 
 
 
# To calculate the total cost of a contract: 

 

 1 .  Retrieve the contract in question in the Service Contracts form. 
 

 2 .  Select Update Service Contract Cost from the list of Direct Activation s . This program uses the costs defined in the Service Cost per Period form to calculate the contract cost for each defined service period. 
 

 3 .  Enter the Contract Costs sub-level form to view the calculated costs. To view the cost breakdown per part, enter the Itemized Contract Costs sub-level.
 Notes: 
 

 ·      The calculated cost is added to the cost of the project designated in the upper-level form. You can also use the Projects/Accounts sub-level form to link the cost to a project or activity. 
 

 ·      You can also define costs manually in the Contract Costs form, but if you do so, the cost source will change to "Manual" and the cost will not be affected by subsequent runs of the Update Service Contract Cost program. 
 

 ·      To allow a manually recorded cost to be updated automatically, move to the desired line and select Update Cost Using Sub-level Data from the list of Direct Activation s . The cost will be updated immediately by the data in the sub-level form, and will be updated by subsequent runs of the Update Service Contract Cost program.
