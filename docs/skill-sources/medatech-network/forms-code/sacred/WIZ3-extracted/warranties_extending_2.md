# To extend the warranty free of charge:

 

 1 .  In the Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. form, retrieve the desired part and serial number. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Extend Warranty sub-level form. 
 

 3 .  Fill in the Extend Warr. Until column. If the end of the warranty period is determined by the meter reading, fill in the Extend Metered Qty column instead.
