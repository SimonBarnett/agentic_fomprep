# Charging for Warranty Extension

 

 1 .  Enter the Over-the-Counter Invoices form and open an invoice.

 Tip: If you already have an open over-the-counter invoice to this customer, you can use that invoice. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Invoice Items sub-level form and choose part number 000 (undefined part). For the part description, record “Warranty Extension”. 
 

 3 .  Record a quantity of one. In the Unit Price column, record the amount charged for extending the warranty. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Extend Warranty form (another sub-level of the Over-the-Counter Invoices form). 
 

 5 .  Select the desired serial number. 
 

 6 .  Change the Extend Warr. Until date. Or , If the warranty lasts until a certain meter reading rather than until a certain date, fill in the Extend Metered Qty column.
