# Charging for Warranty Extension

 

 1 .  Enter the Receipts form and open a receipt.

 Tip: If you already have an open receipt to this customer, you can use that receipt. 
 

 2 .  Enter the Invoice Amounts Being Paid sub-level form and record the sum charged for the warranty extension. 
 

 3 .  In the Details column, record “Warranty Extension”. 
 

 4 .  Enter the Extend Warranty form (another sub-level of the Receipts form). 
 

 5 .  Select the desired serial number. 
 

 6 .  Change the Extend Warr. Until date. Or , If the warranty lasts until a certain meter reading rather than until a certain date, fill in the Extend Metered Qty column.
