# Prevent Identical Cust & Supplier No Rich

- **Source:** `Prevent Identical Cust & Supplier No Rich.docx`

Prevent Identical Customer and Supplier Numbers

R:\INSTALLED AS STANDARD\22 - PREVENT IDENTICAL CUSTOMER & SUPPLIER NUMBERS\ZEMG_CUSTNONOTSUPNO

Installs triggers to prevent creation of Customers and Suppliers with the same number, this also applies to Chart of Accounts Receivable and Payable.

CRM > Customers > Customers

Purchasing > Vendors > Vendors

Financials > Nominal Ledger > Chart of Accounts > Chart of Accounts Receivable

Financials > Nominal Ledger > Chart of Accounts > Chart of Accounts Payable
