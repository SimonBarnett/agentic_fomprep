# ERP Implementer Developer

- **Source:** `ERP Implementer Developer.docx`

ERP Implementer/Developer 

Background 

Emerge Information Technology Ltd is a supplier of Enterprise Resource Planning software solutions to the small-to-medium sized business community. The solution we provide is an extremely flexible, easy-to-use and highly adaptable ERP system. As well as the software solution, Emerge also provide business consultancy to assist our customers through the entire lifecycle of the system, training, data migration services and extensive programming and customisation services.

The role

Is multi-dimensional and requires a dynamic individual. From first analysing the customer’s business requirements through to programming, training, implementation and phone support, this individual will be expected to take ownership of the entire project lifecycle. These responsibilities may be undertaken on the customer site, requiring a degree of travel. The role provides an excellent opportunity to join a fast-growing, vibrant business and gain exposure to a leading ERP software package.

Key Responsibilities 

• Take ownership of the entire project lifecycle 
• Create and maintain project plans 
• Gather and analyse customer requirements 
• Produce documentation in accordance with standards 
• Communicate effectively to stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle 
• Design system modifications 
• Program changes to the standard system using extended SQL 
• Provide training to customers from end-users to management 
• Provide software support (face-to-face and telephone) for customers 

Person Specification: 

• Educated to degree level in I.T, business or related subject. Alternatively, significant experience in ERP projects. 
• Strongly customer focused 
• Programming experience. While developments to the system are coded in extended SQL, Emerge will consider      candidates with programming experience or understanding in any language 
• System design experience 
• Excellent communication skills 
• Ability to manage multiple tasks concurrently 
• Good team player 
• Able to function well without supervision 
• Capacity to think creatively with good problem solving skills 
• Capable of rapidly understanding business and system processes with customers 
• Ability to be flexible and thrive under pressure
