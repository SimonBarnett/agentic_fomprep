Introduction (00:00-00:42) 
Priority provides you with a comprehensive solution to all your sales and marketing 
needs, from handling leads, through opportunities, price quotations and sales orders; 
to customer shipments, and all the financial aspects of the sale: including billing, 
cash flow projections and customer debt. The software operates on a web-based 
platform, using HTML5 for convenient operation on smartphones and tablets, as 
demonstrated here. Our interface with Microsoft Office, including Outlook, Word and 
Excel, and with Gmail, makes for a smooth transition between your heavy-duty data 
management when at the office, and all your mobile needs when on the go.  
Creating Priority Leads from Outlook Contacts (00:43-01:36) 
To update leads in Priority with Outlook contacts, all you need to do is click a couple 
of buttons, after an initial set-up process. Moving to MS-Outlook, in your Contacts 
pane, simply right-click the contact name and select the "Sync" category to mark that 
contact for synchronization with Priority. Depending on how you define the contact 
in Outlook, he or she will be recorded in Priority either as a contact for an existing 
customer, or as a new lead, the next time synchronization is performed. The 
synchronization program is run automatically at intervals that you decide upon, but 
we can run it manually too.  
Let's move to the Leads form and take a closer look. In the Source of Lead column, 
you can see that the source of this lead is "Outlook", meaning it was automatically 
created by synchronization.  
Viewing Leads in Priority (01:37-02:14) 
The form opens in single-record mode, meaning a sing
le lead is presented in the 
form. Switch to multi-record mode to see a list of all the leads, and then to Query 
mode  to find the lead you need. Retrieving the lead you are looking for can be as 
simple as searching by Name or Source of Lead. For more refined searches, you 
can use several columns together, or if need be, narrow your search even further 
using the Query Generator, available in all Priority forms, and retrieve by a range of 
values, or use operators to further limit the search.  
In-Depth Lead Information (02:15-03:23) 
Once you find the lead that you want, switch back to single-record mode, more 
suited to entering data and handling a lead in depth.  
Note that contact information and the name of the company were copied here, and 
you can elaborate further as needed. New leads automatically receive the New 
status, and other statuses reflect that an opportunity has been opened for this lead, 
or that the lead was converted into an actual customer. You can change statuses,

Sales Opportunities (03:24-04:48) 
To view the opportunity in greater detail, zoom to it directly from the lead.  
The opportunity operates within a limited time frame, as reflected by the Projected 
Closing Date and Estim. Days to Close columns. The Status determines the Odds 
of Closing. Opportunities with a good chance of succeeding are taken into account 
in sales forecasts, together with quotations and orders. Once you change the Status, 
the Odds of Closing change accordingly.  
Note Priority's To Do List, containing documents of any type that are assigned to 
you. Automatic mail messages and alerts ensure that documents do not get lost on 
the way, so customers are not kept waiting.  
In addition, in the Customer Dashboard, you can see a list of all the opportunities 
open for a certain customer. Click the opportunity number to see it in greater detail in 
Priority, or access contact data directly from here.   
Back in the sales opportunity, you can list the sales items included in the opportunity, 
together with prices and discounts, which can be based on default prices defined 
system-wide, or customized individually for this opportunity. Just like in the Leads 
form, you can move this opportunity further along the pipeline by opening a price 
quotation directly from the form.  
Sales – Price Quotes (04:49-06:25) 
Next in line are the pri
ce quotes and sales orders. Price quotations and sales orders 
are structured in Priority in a similar manner to the forms we've seen, with an overall 
view in the upper-level form that includes contact details, total prices, follow-up 
details and statuses; and detailed itemization in the sub-levels. If we focus on the 
Price Quotations form you can see additional features related to closing the actual 
sale. For example, for each line item  you can see the inventory available in the 
system, taking into account not only actual inventory, but also open purchase and 
sales orders. Layered information helps you precisely tailor the quote: for example, 
you can see past prices recorded for this part in the system; and view Additional 
Quotes for Part issued to this customer and others. These and other data help you 
refine the prices in the quote, commit to supply dates, and improve the service you 
provide your customers.  
Here and in virtually any other form, you can attach files such as part specifications, 
the customer's request for a price quote, an image of the part or anything else.  
Back in the Customer Dashboard, you can see that orders and quotes are shown 
here, as well as links to tasks for this customer. Use the Documents tab to access 
files linked to any of the customer documents in the system: orders, tasks, projects 
and the like.

Sales – Orders (06:26-07:36) 
Let's click on one of the customer's orders to access the Orders form, and highlight 
a few of Priority's capabilities there. Of course, when you base an order on a quote, 
all the items and their price details are copied. Let's give an example of a long-
standing customer who has accumulated substantial debts at our company. When 
trying to record an item for that customer, you receive a warning message notifying 
you of those debts. Without leaving the record, simply access the Customer Credit 
sub-level to see the details of those debts. In this example, quite a few postdated 
checks have been received from the customer. Use the Postdated Checks form to 
view their details. You may decide to go ahead with the order as planned, despite 
the debt. In the Follow-Up tab, simply change the status of the order to "Approved" 
to move it along the sales pipeline. Or you might decide to move to the Tasks for 
Document form, while still in the same order record, and open a new billing task for 
the relevant team member.  
Customer Shipments (07:37-08:35) 
Our Inventor
y module enables you to define parts and inventory in the system for 
businesses of all sizes, ranging from small stores that hold inventory locally, to 
manufacturers that maintain several warehouses, factories and other facilities. 
Customer shipping documents are linked to the sales orders from which they 
stemmed, and to invoices for billing purposes, and may include bills of lading, crate 
details, and a record of interactions with the customer.  
For enterprises that require a more advanced level of warehouse control, we offer 
our WMS module: Warehouse Management System.  
When customers pick up goods directly from your store or warehouse, you can issue 
an over-the-counter invoice, which records an inventory transaction as well as a 
financial one, without having to record a customer shipment separately.  
Invoices and Aging (08:36-10:05) 
Once a deal is closed and the order has been shipped, the sale moves on to the 
care of your accounting department, and invoices are linked to the relevant sales or 
shipping document. Priority supports three types of invoices: regular sales invoices, 
over-the-counter invoic
es and multi-shipment invoices, used for charging a customer 
periodically after a number of shipments have been issued. The various Aging and 
Open Invoices reports help you keep track of customer debts, and the Aging Work 
Area not only displays a list of unbilled documents and a summary of customer 
debts, but also enables you to open a billing task immediately.  
Returning to the Customer Dashboard, a summary of these debts are conveniently 
grouped in the left-hand part of the screen. The Invoices to Customer web part 
shows you the  cumulative sum of all the invoices from the beginning of the year, or 
divided by the months of the year so that you can see the peaks and declines in 
sales to this customer. 
In many dashboards in Priority, such as the CFO Dashboard, you can build your 
own indicator to measure change between periods: for example, to compare the 
sums of customer invoices between this month and the previous one, with the 
growth represented in the Change (%) column. Of course, the dashboard provides 
you with an overview of invoices, profit & loss, cash flow forecasts and more.

Follow-Up (10:06-11:38) 
Several tools are available for follow-up on sales data in Priority. We've seen some 
of the dashboards, but perhaps an even more fundamental tool is the To Do List, 
where you can not only keep track of tasks and documents assigned to you, but also 
retrieve those assigned to other users, to see the tasks other team members are 
working on – provided you have the proper privileges to do so.  
In addition, a large variety of built-in Priority reports provide you with valuable data 
concerning CRM, sales, inventory and financials. These include reports which show 
you the make-up of your profit, such as this report, which displays profit per sold part; 
or a BI report that measures the performance of your sales department, comparing 
actual sales to the targets you set. Clicking on any of the sums in the report displays 
the documents these sums are taken from, with the option to drill down to the 
resolution of a single record.  
In addition, you can build your own customized report using any of our report 
generators, which will then be available for your own use or for all users, as you 
choose.  
This concludes our survey of the tools available in Priority for following a sale 
through the sales pipeline, from a lead to actual income. Additional videos that 
demonstrate how to work with various sales documents, and how to manage sales 
and sales reps, are available on our Learn Priority page.
