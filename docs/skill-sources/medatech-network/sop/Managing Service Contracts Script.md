Introduction (00:00-01:14) 
This video addresses Priority's Service Management software, an all-in-one solution 
for companies that sell integrated systems – such as security systems, HVAC 
(Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning) systems, elevator systems and the like – 
and provide ongoing service for these systems. This solution enables you to manage 
all pre-sale and post-sale activities relating to the integrated systems you provide – 
including warranty and maintenance of these systems – all within a single software 
package.  
Priority's Service Management software helps companies oversee customer 
relations across the sales process; organize the various costs associated with the 
sales process within a single sales project; work with service contracts, and handle 
service calls: whether by help desk staff, or by field technicians. Moreover, a variety 
of financial tools are integrated into Priority's customer service solution, and various 
executive reports are available to help you manage the entire process. An overview 
of the entire solution is presented in a separate video clip. 
In this video, we will demonstrate one aspect of the solution: managing service 
contracts in Priority. 
Sales Projects (01:15-02:10) 
All sales related activities can be linked to a single project, beginning with the initial 
price quote, enabling y
ou to keep track of all relevant expenses from within the sales 
project. Once you succeed in closing the sale, you can open a service contract for 
the project by running the Open Service Contract For Proj program directly from 
the upper-level Projects form. Service contracts can also be opened independently 
of the sales project, or any project for that matter. 
Let's proceed to review the service contract itself and see what options are available. 
You can open the Service Contracts form from the main menu, but at this time 
we're going to access the contract via a sub-level of the Projects form: Service 
Contracts for Projects, from which we can continue to the contract itself.

Service Terms (03:26-04:27) 
Under the References tab, note the value of the Service Terms column. This 
column has been filled in automatically with the default value '000', indicating full 
payment. Let's change this to "Service for New Systems".  
Press F6 twice from this column to proceed to the Service Terms form. In the 
Conditions of Service Terms sub-level form, you can specify which part families 
are covered by the service terms and which are not. For example, under the current 
service terms, you'll notice that work hours are covered by the overall contract fee, 
as indicated by the fact that they are not flagged as Billable. Similarly, servicing for 
the part families associated with new system components is exempt from payment. 
However, if the client requests service for consumable components such as batteries, 
the provided services will be billed separately. Let's return to the service contract. 
Itemizing the Service Contract (04:28-06:52) 
In the Items in Contract sub-level form, you can see a list of components included 
in this contract and the charges for each. In cases where service is offered only for a 
specific serialized part, such as an electrical component, a column is provided in 
which to indicate the relevant Serial Number. This definition is crucial, as it helps 
ensure that company technicians provide services only for the actual parts that you 
provided, or – if you sell a servicing package for an existing system – that the part in 
question has passed inspection by an authorized technician. Note that all information 
pertaining to the serialized part can be accessed from the Catalogue of Parts 
w/Serial Nos., including the date of installation at the customer site. Moreover, you 
can use the Serialized Components of Part sub-level form to list any components 
that make up the device, some of which may also be assigned a serial number. In 
summary, detailed identifying information regarding the specific part you supplied 
can be linked to the service contract, service calls and many other system 
documents, enabling company technicians to verify that repairs and replacements 
are provided only for the original components that were purchased from your 
company. 
For items that are not serialized, you can record the installation date directly in the 
service contract, as well as the date on which the warranty expires. Use the 
Quantity column to indicate the number of items covered by the contract. Note that 
for serialized parts, the recorded quantity should always be '1', and a separate line 
should be recorded for each serial number.

The Unit Price indicates the price at which a single unit of the part is sold to the 
customer, according to the defined Price Source. In other words, this amount 
corresponds to the price of the part itself, rather than its service. 
The annual service fee for each part can be defined either as a percentage of the 
part price or as a flat fee in either the Prorate Charge (%)or the Flat Rate 
Charge/Yr column, respectively. The amounts recorded in this sub-level form are 
used to calculate the total cost of the service contract. 
Billing & Financials (06:53-08:41) 
To enable billing for the service contract, return to the upper-level form and update 
the Status to "Approved". In the Terms tab, you can choose the desired payment 
frequency – in this case, monthly. Next, we'll activate a program that calculates the 
amount of this month's payment installment. We'll confirm the displayed notification, 
and then move to the Payment Installments sub-level form. As you can see, the 
system has automatically filled in the payment amount that covers the first month of 
the contract, from the effect date through the end of the current month. Additionally, 
a Description has been filled in for this installment, as well as the Payment Amount 
and other details.  
Returning to the upper-level form, notice that you can activate a program from this 
form to automatically prepare an invoice billing the customer for the calculated 
payment amount. Moreover, note that the Paid Until column indicates the date up 
until which the customer has paid for the contract.  
We recommend that you also run periodic reports to ensure that all billable items and 
service calls linked to a current service contract have in fact been billed. In this way, 
you can ensure accurate and timely billing for service contracts, and keep track of all 
related invoices and receipts.  
You may have also noticed that a number of informative sub-level forms are linked to 
the Payment Installments form: Details of any invoices issued for the current 
payment installment and actual payments received, data pertaining to linkage to 
foreign currencies, where relevant, calls to the customer for clarification, and a form 
in which to record additional Remarks regarding the current payment installment.
