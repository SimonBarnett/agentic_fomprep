# SOP Aged Receivables

- **Source:** `SOP Aged Receivables.pdf`

Cat.No. LB110525 1 Ver.1 – Aug. 22, ’11 
 
SOP: Aged Receivables* 
Customer:  Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To track Aged Receivable reports. 
Working Assumptions 
 Payment terms for customers have been defined in the Financ. Parameters for 
Customers form. 
 Credit terms have been defined in the Financ. Parameters for Customers form. 
 Reconciliations have been performed in A/R accounts. Receipts that are not 
reconciled prior to the running of aging reports will not be taken into account in the 
calculation of customer debts. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Working with the Aging Work Area 
1. Run the Load Aging Work Area program. In the Account No. column, choose the 
desired customer number and in the As of Date field specify the date for aging 
calculations. It is possible to perform a retroactive calculation. 
2. You can load customer accounts accord ing to the company employee responsible 
for billing by selecting this employee in the Billing Follow-up By column. 
3. Specify a To Document Date and/or To Report Date to restrict the load to those 
documents/reports that were unbilled as of the specified date. 
4. Enter the Aging Work Area form. The following information appears: 
 Account number and description 
 Customer status 
 Aging contact and phone number 
 Distribution of customer debt: current, 1-30 days, 31-60 days, 61-90 days, and 
over 90 days 
                                                 
* Standard Operating Procedures should be used as guidelines for customers (and their consultants) to 
develop their own operating procedures. As you will note, the following procedures are very specific 
and customers are strongly advised not to use them without prior testing to make sure that they fit their own 
requirements and work practices.

 The total amount owed for documents that were not billed as of the To Document 
Date and the date as of which this total was calculated 
 The total amount owed for reported hours that were not billed as of the To Report 
Date and the date as of which this total was calculated 
 The company employee responsible for billing this customer. 
5. Enter the Unbilled Documents and Unbilled Reports sub-level forms to view 
details of all documents and reports that affect the amounts owed. 
6. Enter the Ledger Transactions - YTD, Ledger Transactions - Last Year, and 
Ledger Transactions - Multi-Year sub-level forms to view details of transactions 
recorded against the account during the current fiscal year or during previous fiscal 
years. Enter the Unreconciled Transactions sub-level forms to view details of all 
transactions recorded against the account that have not yet been reconciled. 
7. Enter the parallel Customer Credit sub-level form to view credit data (e.g., debit 
balances, open orders, unbilled shipments). 
8. Enter the next sub-level form to view details of all documents and transactions which 
affect each portion of the credit data. 
9. Enter the Tasks form (another sub-level of the work area) to record customer 
communications related to debt collection. 
 In the Start Date column, indicate the date the task was carried out. 
 In the Assigned to column, record the party responsible for carrying out the task. 
 In the Task Type column, specify “Billing”. 
 Record the Customer Contact. 
 In the Task Code column, select the method of communication with the customer. 
 Record the Status of the task. 
 Enter the next sub-level form ( Explanation of Task) to record a brief remark 
regarding the task. 
Stage II: Running Aging Reports 
Aging By Customer 
 Run the Daily Aged Receivables report to view amounts owing from individual 
customers on a daily basis, together with the number of days elapsed since the 
payment date. 
 Run the Weekly Aged Receivables report to view amounts owing from individual 
customers on a weekly basis, together with the number of days elapsed since the 
payment date. 
 Run the Monthly Aging by Account report to review amounts owing from individual 
customers by month, based on payment date. 
 Run the Aging by 30-Day Period report to view amounts owing from individual 
customers by 30-day aging periods (in other words, current debt, 1-30 days, 31-60 
days, 61-90 days, and 90+ days), beginning from a designated date.

 Run the Aging by 30-Day Period-Detailed report to view amounts owing from 
individual customers, broken down by invoice and by 30-day aging periods (i.e., 
current debt, 1-30 days, 31-60 days, 61-90 days, and 90+ days), beginning from a 
designated date. 
 Run the Monthly Aging by Trial Bal. Item report to display monthly amounts owing of 
related customer account groups (those which appear in the same line in trial balance 
reports). 
Aging By Billing Contact 
 Run the Outstand Debts-Bill Follow (No.) report to display outstanding customer debts 
for specified billing contacts, broken down by invoice and sorted by A/R account 
number. 
 Run the Outstand Debts-Bill Follow(Desc) report to display outstanding customer 
debts for specified billing contacts, broken down by invoice and sorted by A/R 
account name. 
 Run the Aging by Period-Billing Followup report to display the amount owed by 
individual customers per 30-day aging period (i.e., current debt, 1-30 days, 31-60 
days, 61-90, and 90+), sorted by the employee responsible for billing. 
Aging By Sales Representative 
 Run the Outstanding Debts per Rep (No.) report to display outstanding customer 
debts for specified sales representatives, broken down by invoice and sorted by A/R 
account number. 
 Run the Outstanding Debts per Rep (Desc) report to display outstanding customer 
debts for specified sales representatives, broken down by invoice and sorted by A/R 
account name. 
 Run the Aging by 30-Day Period per Rep report to display the amount owed by 
individual customers per 30-day aging period (i.e., current debt, 1-30 days, 31-60 
days, 61-90, and 90+), sorted by sales representative. 
Detailed Aging (By Invoice) 
 Run the Aged Receivables by Invoice report to view the details of unpaid invoices, as 
well as advance payments (indicated by a minus sign). 
 Run the Aging by Invoice (Account Curr) report to view all overdue invoices to select 
customers, sorted by payment date. 
 Run the Detailed Aged Receivables report to view all unpaid invoices, receipts for 
partial payments of these invoices, and advance payments (indicated by a minus 
sign). 
 Run the Outstanding Debts by Orders report for walk-in customers, to view unpaid 
invoices for specific customer accounts, by order, as well as the current payment 
balance for the order.

Consolidated (Multi-company) Aging 
 Run the Consolidated Aging by Invoice report to display details of outstanding 
invoices recorded in all the companies, as well as early payments (identified by a 
minus sign). 
 Run the Consolidated Monthly Aging report to display the monthly amounts owed by 
individual customers in all the companies in a multi-company setting. 
Open Transactions 
 Run the Open Transacts as of Date-Detail report to display all invoices that have not 
been paid as of a designated date. 
 Run the Open Trans.- Monthly Summaries report to display monthly summaries of 
specified A/R accounts (e.g., for each of the four months that just passed). For each 
month, the report displays the total owed by the customer on open invoices dated in 
that month.
