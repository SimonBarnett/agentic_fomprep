SOP: Analyzing Projects* 
 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To present tools that enable users to critique the outcome of a project, in terms of 
planning vs execution, and in terms of project revenue and profit. 
Working Assumptions 
 A project was opened and project planning was defined. 
 Financial documents in the system are linked to the project. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Reviewing Planning and Execution 
Aim: To compare planning to execution in terms of project hours. 
1. Enter the Projects form and retrieve the desired project. 
2. Enter the Planning and Execution sub-level form. You can compare project 
execution to what was defined in the project plan. The form allows you to view 
planned costs versus actual and projected costs. To view details of the different 
components affecting costs, enter the various sub-level forms. 
3. Move to the parallel Project Planning sub-level form, and from there, for each 
planned activity, to the Planning and Execution sub-level form. The form enables 
you to compare planned costs to actual and projected costs for each planned 
activity. 
Stage II: Reviewing Project Revenue and Profit 
Aim: To review revenue from the project based on recorded or projected income and 
expenses. This information will assist in pricing similar projects in the future. 
1. Enter the Projects form and retrieve the desired project.
