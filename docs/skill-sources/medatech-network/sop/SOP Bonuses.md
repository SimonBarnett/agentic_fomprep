# SOP Bonuses

- **Source:** `SOP Bonuses.pdf`

Cat.No.A8000924 1 Ver.1 – Dec.7,’08 
 
SOP: Bonuses* 
 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To define a part as a bonus, to be awarded to customers upon the purchase of a 
given part, or upon the purchase of any part(s) belonging to a given part family. 
There are two types of bonuses: 
 A regular bonus is given to all customers (i.e., that are eligible to receive them), for 
free or at a reduced price, in exchange for purchasing a given part. 
 A special bonus is only given to a specific customer. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Defining a Regular Bonus for a Part or Part Family 
1. Enter the Bonuses for Ordering Parts form or the Bonuses for Ordering from Family 
form. 
2. Select the Part Number or Family that must be ordered to receive the bonus. 
3. Record the start and end dates of the bonus offer. 
4. Record the minimum quantity of this part or part family that customers must order to 
be eligible for the bonus. 
5. Choose the part number of the bonus item, and record the quantity of parts offered 
as the bonus.  
6. If the bonus item is not free, record its price and the currency. 
7. Enter the Customers form, and retrieve the desired customer(s). 
8. Click the Details tab and flag the Gets Reg. Bonus column. 
Stage II: Defining a Special Bonus for a Part or Part Family 
1. Enter the Customers form, and retrieve the desired customer.
