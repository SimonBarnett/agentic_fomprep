# SOP Courses of Study

- **Source:** `SOP Courses of Study.pdf`

Cat.No.A8000047 1 Ver.1 – Dec.11,’08 
 
SOP: Courses of Study* 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aims 
 To monitor all aspects of courses run by  the company for customers and employees. 
 To monitor courses attended by employees outside of the company. 
Setups 
 For external courses: 
 Make sure the course provider is defined in the Vendors form. 
 Make sure that the instructor is recorded as a vendor contact in the Vendor 
Contacts sub-level of the Vendors form. 
 For courses that include participants from other companies: Make sure that the other 
companies are recorded as customers in the Customers form. 
 Define academic Degrees/Certificates. 
 Define certifications in the Certification  form. 
 Define Certification Levels. 
 Define courses in the Course Credits form, recording a code for each different 
number of credits awarded for the same course. 
 Define Topics of Study. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Recording General Course Details 
1. Enter the Courses of Study form. 
2. Record the Course Name. The Course No. and From Date appear automatically, 
but may be revised. 
3. In the To Date column, record the date the course ends. 
4. Select the course's Topic from the Choose list. 
                                                 
* Standard Operating Procedures should be used as guidelines for customers (and their consultants) to 
develop their own operating procedures. As you will note, the following procedures are very specific 
and customers are strongly advised not to use them without prior testing to make sure that they fit their own 
requirements and work practices.

5. Do one of the following: 
 If the course is run by your company, flag the Internal Course column in the 
Instruction Info tab, and select the Instructor's Employee ID.  
Note: You can only select an employee flagged as an instructor in the Personnel 
File form. 
 If the course is external, click the Add'l Details tab. Specify the company running 
the course in the Training Center No. column, and the instructor in the Training 
Ctr Teacher column. 
6. Specify the Cost of Course in the Add'l Details tab. 
7. If the course comprises a number of sessions, enter the Course Sessions sub-level 
form and record the dates and times planned for each session. 
8. To include documents for the course, enter the parallel Documents for the Course 
sub-level form and attach each document.  
9. Use the parallel Remarks sub-level form to record general text pertaining to the 
course (e.g., the course syllabus). 
10. Enter the parallel Positions Requiring the Course sub-level form and select the 
relevant position codes. 
11. To print the course details, return to the upper-level form and select Print Details of 
Course from the list of Direct Activations. 
Stage II: Recording Course Participants 
1. Enter the Participants in Course sub-level of the Courses of Study form. 
2. Do one of the following: 
 If the participant is a company employee, select the Employee ID. 
 If the participant is from an external company, record the Name of Participant and 
select the Company No. If the participant has a contact, fill in the Contact column. 
3. In the Degree/Certif. Code column, select the code of the highest academic degree 
or certificate received by the participant, or for which the course is offered. 
4. If participation in the course automatic ally bestows a certification (within the 
company), enter the Certification for Participant sub-level form and specify the 
Certification Code and Level of Certification. 
5. Run the Participants in Course report to prepare a list of the participants. 
6. To view the courses in which a given employee is participating, enter the Courses of 
Study sub-level of the Personnel File. 
Stage III: Recording Information After the Course is Completed 
1. Enter the Courses of Study form and retrieve the desired course. 
2. In the Instruction Info tab, record the general Grade for Course. 
3. Enter the Participants in Course sub-level form and flag the Passed and Feedback 
Received columns, as needed. Select the appropriate number of course credits in 
the Credits (Code) column.
