Cat.No.LB09000176 1 Ver.1 – May 3, '09 
 
SOP: Creating Accounting Families* 
 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To group products by accounting families for varied financial needs (e.g., products in 
income/expense groups, tax-exempt products). 
Working Assumptions 
 Parts are defined in the Part Catalogue form. 
 The relevant income and expense accounts are defined in the Chart of Accounts 
form. 
 Family types are defined in the Family Types form. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Creating an Accounting Family 
1. Enter the Accounting Families form. 
2. Define a family code in the Family column and its description in the Family 
Description column. 
3. Assign the accounting family to  a family type, if one exists. 
Stage II: Assigning Accounts to an Accounting Family 
1. Enter the Accounting Families form. 
2. With the cursor in the Family column, select Accounts for Families from the list of 
Direct Activations. 
3. Move to the Income & Purchasing Accounts tab and assign each relevant column 
the designated account, for example, Income, Tax Exempt Income, 
Purchases/Inventory.

Stage III: Assigning a Single Part to an Accounting Family 
1. Enter the Financial Parameters for Parts form. 
2. Retrieve the Part Number to be assigned. 
3. Move to the General tab and record the accounting family in the Accounting Family 
column. 
Stage IV: Assigning Multiple Parts to an Accounting Family 
1. Run the Add Parts to Accounting Family program. 
2. In the dialogue box, choose the accounting family to which parts will be added, and 
the part numbers in question. You can access the Part Catalogue form by pressing 
F6, and retrieve parts by any criterion appearing in the form. 
Stage IV: Removing Parts from an Accounting Family 
1. Run the Remove Parts from Acc't Family program. 
2. In the dialogue box, choose the accounting family from which parts will be removed, 
and the part numbers in question. You can access the Part Catalogue form by 
pressing F6, and retrieve parts by any criterion appearing in the form. 
Result 
 You can run financial reports by accounting families or family types and add this 
parameter to report generators. Any product assigned to an accounting family will 
appear in these reports under the relevant grouping.
