# SOP Defining Product Bundles

- **Source:** `SOP Defining Product Bundles.pdf`

Cat.No.LB09000180 1 Ver.3 – June 15,’10 
 
SOP: Defining Product Bundles* 
Customer:  Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To define product bundles in the system. A product bundle is a part that is sold as 
a single unit but actually comprises a variety of items. 
Procedure 
1. Enter the Product Bundles (Quotes/Orders) form. 
2. In the Product Bundle column, record the name or number of the new product 
bundle. 
3. In the Bundle Description column, record a short description of the product 
bundle. 
4. To set the order in which product bundles appear in Choose lists, assign a 
number in the Display Order column. 
5. Enter the Product Components sub-level form. 
6. For each component included in the product bundle, record the Part Number 
serving as the default in this bundle. 
7. Indicate the Quantity of this component in the bundle. 
8. Determine the price of the part in the bundle, as it will be displayed in price 
quotes and orders: 
 To use the part price from the regular price hierarchy, flag the Calc. Price in 
Doc column, and leave the Special Unit Price column blank. 
 To use a price that differs from the part’s usual price, record a Special Unit 
Price and leave the Calc. Price in Doc column empty. 
 To display the part without any price, leave both columns blank. 
 To set a discount for the part in the current bundle, record the desired 
percentage in the Discount % column. 
9. For any component which may have substitute parts in the bundle, enter the 
Substitutes for Component sub-level form. For each possible substitute part, 
record a Part Number, Quantity and price details. 
Notes: 
 For any product bundle no longer in use, enter the Product Bundles 
(Quotes/Orders) form and remove the check mark from the In Use column.
