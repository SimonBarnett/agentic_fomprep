SOP: Defining and Opening Fiscal Periods* 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
Priority functions in a multi-year environment. At the beginning of each year, a new 
fiscal year should be opened, and its fiscal periods defined. In most cases, fiscal periods 
are defined according to the months of the year (i.e., 12 periods); nonetheless, Priority 
allows you to define up to 15 fiscal periods. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Defining the Fiscal Year and Fiscal Periods  
1. Enter the Define Fiscal Years form. 
2. Record the Start Date and End Date of the fiscal year. 
3. The Prefix for Entry No. column automatically displays two digits that will represent 
the fiscal year in financial documents.  
4. Enter the  Fiscal Periods sub-level form. 
5. Twelve periods appear automatically, one for each month. You can change the start 
and end dates of each period in the From Date and To Date columns, add periods 
and delete them as needed. 
6. The Opened, Closed (Trans Date) and Closed (Ref Date) columns display the 
current status of each period in the ledger. Fiscal periods are opened and closed via 
a program external to this form. 
Stage II: Opening a Fiscal Period 
 Run the Open Fiscal Period program. The program opens the next unopened period 
in the Fiscal Periods form. 
Stage III: Closing a Fiscal Period
