# SOP Managing Events

- **Source:** `SOP Managing Events.pdf`

Cat.No. LB10000128 1 Ver.1 – June 3,’10 
 
SOP: Managing Events* 
Customer:  Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To maintain the list of marketing events organized and managed by your 
company. 
Procedure 
1. Enter the Marketing Events & Activities form. 
2. For each event, record a code in the Marketing Event column and a 
Description. 
3. Record the Date of the event. 
Note: Dating the event helps you distinguish between different events of the 
same type. 
4. To link participating customers to the event, do one of the following: 
 Enter the  Customers Who Participated sub-level form and record the 
customers and customer contacts that attended the event. 
 Load the relevant customers/contacts into the Customer Work Area and run the 
Record Marketing Event for Custs program to record the event for many 
participants at the same time. 
Results 
 You have a list of marketing events that  can help you track the participation of 
your customers at such events. 
 You can run the Load by Marketing Event/Activity  program to load all 
customers/contacts that participated in the designated event(s) into the 
Customer Work Area. You can use this data to determine the customer groups 
or market sectors on which to focus your marketing efforts at future events.
