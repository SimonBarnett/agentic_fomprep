SOP: Managing Leads* 
 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To update and manage leads following such  marketing events as exhibitions, 
conferences and the like. 
Setups 
 Create a list of lead sources and sales opportunities sources (e.g., Internet, direct 
mailing, word of mouth). Record each source on a separate line in the Sources of 
Leads/Sales Opps form. 
Note: You can add additional sources later, when recording new leads. 
 Define types for leads recorded in the system. Record each type on a separate line in 
the Types of Leads form. 
Note: You can add additional types later, when recording new leads. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Adding a Lead  
1. Enter the Leads form. 
2. Specify the lead’s first and last name in the Name column. 
3. In the Company column, designate the name of the company. 
4. Click the General tab, and record the lead’s telephone number and e-mail address. 
5. Indicate the Source of Lead and Type of Lead. 
6. Click the Position/Team tab, and indicate the lead’s position in the Job Title 
column. 
7. Enter the Address sub-level form, and record the lead’s address. 
8. Enter the Contacts - Text sub-level form, and record remarks regarding the lead.

Stage II: Turning Leads Into Potential Customers 
1. Enter the Leads form, and retrieve the desired lead. 
2. Select Open Potential Customer from the list of Direct Activations. The new 
customer number will appear in the Customer column (in the General tab). 
Note: For continued handling of potential customers, see the standard operating 
procedures for Opening a Customer. 
Stage III: Viewing Records in the Customer Work Area 
1. Run the Load by Business Classification program in order to load customers linked 
to specific business classifications to the work area. 
2. Enter the Customer Work Area to view all loaded records. 
Results 
 Leads are managed and documented in the system. 
 Immediately after a given exhibition, y ou can send mailings to potential customers 
and leads according to their areas of interest.
