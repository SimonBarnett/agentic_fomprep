Aim 
 To plan and manage marketing campaigns (e.g., ad campaigns). 
Procedure 
Stage I: Marketing Campaign Definitions 
Recording Sales Opportunity Sources 
 Enter the Leads form and record each lead on a separate line. 
Recording Sales Opportunity Sources 
1. Enter the Task Codes form. 
2. Retrieve the desired task code or record a new one. 
3. In the Opp Status When Done column, indicate the status that will be automatically 
assigned to sales opportunities after completion of a task with that code. 
Creating Dependence Between Price Quotes and Sales Opportunity Status 
1. Enter the Statuses for Price Quotations form. 
2. Move to the desired status and enter the Sales Opportunity Status sub-level form. 
3. Select the Sales Opp Status that will be automatically assigned to sales 
opportunities when a price quote linked to the opportunity reaches the status in the 
upper-level form. 
Creating Dependence Between Orders and Sales Opportunity Status 
1. Enter the Statuses for Orders form. 
2. Move to the desired status and enter the Sales Opportunity Status sub-level form.

Defining Probability to Close 
1. Enter the Statuses for Sales Opportunities form. 
2. For each status, enter the More Definitions sub-level form and indicate, in 
percentages, the probability that sales opportunities with the current status will be 
successful. 
Stage II: Opening a Marketing Campaign 
1. Enter the Marketing Campaigns form. 
2. Indicate the Campaign Code and its description. 
3. Indicate the campaign Start Date and End Date. 
4. Enter the Employees Involved in Campaign sub-level form, and select the user 
name of each employee that will work on this campaign. Besides its informative 
value, this will make it easier to link tasks recorded by these users to the campaign. 
Stage III: Defining Tasks for a Marketing Campaign 
Aim: To define tasks that are linked to the progress of sales opportunities in the 
marketing campaign. 
1. Enter the Marketing Campaigns form and retrieve the campaign in question. 
2. Move to the Check List for Sales Opps sub-level form. 
3. Indicate the Sales Opp Status that triggers this task. A new task will be opened 
automatically for any sales opportunities that are linked to the current marketing 
campaign and assigned this status. 
4. Indicate the Subject, Task Code, Task Type and initial Task Status to be assigned 
to the new task.  
Stage IV: Opening Sales Opportunities in a Marketing Campaign 
Opening a Sales Opportunity for a Single Customer, Including a Task 
1. Enter the Tasks form and indicate a task Subject and Task Code. 
2. Click the Definitions tab, and indicate the Customer No. for which the task and 
sales opportunity are being opened. 
3. Ensure that the correct date and time appear in the  Start Date, Start Time, End 
Date, and End Time columns. 
4. Click the Campaign tab and select a Campaign Code. If a sales opportunity is 
already open for the customer in this campaign, the Sales Opportunity No. column 
is filled in automatically, and the sales opportunity is linked to the current task. 
Otherwise, a new sales opportunity will be created automatically when you exit the 
form, and linked to this task. 
5. Enter the Sales Opportunities form, and retrieve the relevant sales opportunity.

Adjusted Revenue columns in the upper-level form (Prices tab) are filled in

Opening a Sales Opportunity for a Group of Customers or Contacts 
1. Run the Open Tasks for Custs in W. Area program. 
2. Retrieve the desired customers or contacts in the work area, and indicate a 
Campaign Code in the dialogue box. The program will create a sales opportunity for 
each of the selected customers, and simultaneously create a task for each of the 
selected customers/contacts. 
3. Enter the Sales Opportunities form, and retrieve the relevant sales opportunity. 
4. Indicate the Estim. Days to Close. The Projected Close Date is automatically 
calculated accordingly. 
5. If relevant, indicate the Source of Opportunity. 
6. Enter the Part in Sales Opportunity sub-level form, and indicate the Part Number, 
Unit Price, and Qty of each part being offered for sale. The Projected Revenue and 
Adjusted Revenue columns in the upper-level form (Prices tab) are filled in 
automatically. 
Note: The Adjusted Revenue is calculated based on the probability of success of 
the sales opportunity, as defined for its current status. 
Stage V: Opening Activities for a Sales Opportunity 
Opening a Task Linked to a Sales Opportunity 
1. Enter the Tasks form and create a task. 
2. Click the Campaign tab and indicate a Campaign Code. 
3. Record additional details related to the task. 
Note: If the task code is properly defined, completion of the task will advance the status 
of the sales opportunity. 
Opening a Price Quote Linked to a Sales Opportunity 
1. Enter the Price Quotations for Customers form. 
2. Specify the Customer No. for which the quote is being opened. 
3. In the References tab, select the Sales Opp No. from the list of sales opportunities 
created for this customer in different campaigns. 
4. Complete the price quote details, as usual. 
Note: The sales opportunity will be advanced by any change of status in the price quote, 
according to the business rules defined for price quote statuses. 
Opening an Order Linked to a Sales Opportunity 
1. Enter the Sales Orders form. 
2. Indicate the Customer No. for which the order is being opened. 
3. In the References tab, select the Sales Opp No. from the list of sales opportunities 
created for this customer in different campaigns. 
4. Complete the order details, as usual.

Stage VI: Marketing Campaign Analysis 
Tracking Customer Progress for the Campaign 
1. Enter the Sales Opportunities form. 
2. Retrieve the desired customer or campaign. 
Notes: 
 The Status column reflects the customer status in the current campaign. 
 The value in the Adjusted Revenue  column is a function of the projected 
revenue resulting from the sale to this customer, and the odds of closing the deal 
at this stage of the campaign. 
3. To print a document of the sales opportunity, select Print Sales Opportunity Info 
from the list of Direct Activations. 
Viewing Sales Opportunity Progress for the Campaign 
1. Enter the Marketing Campaigns form, retrieve the relevant marketing campaign, and 
enter the Campaign Progress sub-level form. 
2. Run the Campaign Progress Analysis report, or run the Analyze Progress (Work 
Area) program. 
Running BI Reports 
1. Run the Prep for Sales Opp Analysis-BI program. 
2. Run the Sales Opportunity Analysis (BI) report. 
Stage VII: Closing a Marketing Campaign 
1. Enter the Marketing Campaigns form, and retrieve the desired marketing campaign. 
2. Indicate the campaign end date in the End Date column, and flag the Done column. 
Result 
Marketing campaigns are created from sales opportunities opened for specific potential 
customers. Activities (tasks, price quotes, sales orders) are linked to each sales 
opportunity, and their success or failure affects the status and estimated monetary value 
of the sales opportunity. Based on the cumulative monetary value of these sales 
opportunities, it is possible to track the progress of the entire campaign.
