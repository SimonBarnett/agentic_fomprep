# SOP Service Contracts

- **Source:** `SOP Service Contracts.pdf`

SOP: Service Contracts* 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To prepare service contracts, calculate their costs and bill customers. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Opening a Service Contract 
1. Enter the Service Contracts form and specify the relevant Customer No. 
2. Record the contract’s Effect Date and Expiration Date (in the Dates in Effect tab). 
3. To enable automatic renewal of the contract, specify the number of years for which 
the contract will be extended in the Renewal Option (Years) column. 
4. Click the References tab and select the Service Terms. 
5. Click the Terms tab. In the Paymt Freq. column, specify the billing frequency 
(monthly, bi-monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, annually, manual). This value affects 
the manner in which the contract is billed, based on its annual price. 
6. If you want the billing dates and sums to be determined according to the contract’s 
effect date and payment frequency, remove the checkmark from the Set Pay Date 
column. Otherwise, the contract will be billed at regular intervals according to the 
calendar year. 
7. The Paymt Terms column displays the terms agreed upon with the customer. 
Revise as needed. 
8. Enter the Items in Contract sub-level form and record the Serial Number and 
Quantity of each part. 
9. If a prorate percentage was defined for the part, it appears automatically in the 
Prorate Charge (%) column; revise as necessary. Or record the charge in the Flat 
Rate Charge/Yr column.  
10. Return to the upper-level form and click the Dates in Effect tab. Change the Status 
to one that allows payments to be opened.

Stage II: Calculating Payments for the Contract 
Calculating payments automatically for a group of contracts 
1. Run the Calc. Paymt Installments-Preview report and check the results. 
2. Run the Calculate Payment Installments program. This program automatically opens 
a single payment for each contract. 
3. Enter the Service Contracts form and retrieve the contracts in question. 
4. For each contract, enter the Payment Installments  sub-level form and make sure all 
the details are correct. 
Calculating payments automatically for a single contract 
1. Enter 
the Service Contracts form and retrieve the desired contract. 
2. Select either Calc. Installmts for This Month or Calc. Installmts for Next Month 
from the list of Direct Activations. 
3. Enter the Payment Installments sub-level form and make sure all the details are 
correct. 
Calculating payments manually 
1. Enter 
the Service Contracts form and retrieve the desired contract. 
2. Enter the Payment Installments sub-level form. 
3. Specify the Date of the payment. 
4. In the Covers From and Covers Until columns, specify the start and end dates of 
the period covered by the payment. 
Stage III: Preparing an Invoice 
1. Select Prepare Invoice from the list of Direct Activations of the service contract. 
2. Select Multi-Shipment Invoices from the list of Direct Activations. 
3. Check the details in the invoice that was opened. 
4. Select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations of the invoice. 
Stage IV: Renewing the Contract 
 To renew a single contract, retrieve it in the Service Contracts form and change the 
Expiration Date (in the Dates in Effect tab). 
 To renew a group of contracts, run the Renew Contracts program.
