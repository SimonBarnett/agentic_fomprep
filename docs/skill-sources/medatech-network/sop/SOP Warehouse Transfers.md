# SOP Warehouse Transfers

- **Source:** `SOP Warehouse Transfers.pdf`

Cat.No. A8000203 1 Ver.2 – Jun.29,’08 
 
SOP: Warehouse Transfers* 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
This document describes the process of transferring inventory from one warehouse or 
bin to another. 
Opening a Warehouse Transfer Document 
1. Enter the Warehouse Transfers form. 
2. Specify the Date. The document Number appears automatically. 
3. In the General tab, check the default Sending Warehouse and Bin. Revise as 
needed. 
4. Specify the desired Receiving Warehouse and Bin. 
5. Enter the Transferred Items sub-level form. 
6. Record the Part Number of each transferred item. 
7. Warehouse and bin details are filled in automatically according to the values recorded 
in the upper-level form. Revise as needed. 
8. Check the To Status column. Revise as needed. 
9. Specify the Quantity of parts that you want to transfer. 
10. Return to the upper-level form. 
11. Change the Status column to the next status defined in the BPM workflow, until the 
transfer is finalized. 
12. To make revisions to the document after it is closed, click Reopen Document from 
the list of Direct Activations. The program will return the document's status to 
"Draft". After making the necessary revisions, move the document to the next status. 
13. To print the document, click Print Warehouse Transfer Doc. from the list of Direct 
Activations. 
Results 
• The balance in the sending warehouse and bin is reduced by the transferred quantity. 
• The balance in the receiving warehouse and bin is increased by the transferred 
quantity.
