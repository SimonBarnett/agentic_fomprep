# SOP Warranties for Serialized Parts

- **Source:** `SOP Warranties for Serialized Parts.pdf`

Cat.No.A8000097 1 Ver.1 – Jan.8,’09 
 
SOP: Warranties for Serialized Parts* 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To define warranties for serialized parts. 
Setups 
 The CAutoWarrant constant in the Logistic Constants form has been defined, to 
determine the starting point for warranties given to serialized parts. 
 The CMaxSernOpen constant has been defined (in the same form), to determine the 
maximum number of serial numbers that can be defined automatically at the same 
time for any given part. 
 Types of warranties have been defined in the Warranty Codes form. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Recording a Warranty for a Serialized Part 
1. Enter the Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. form and retrieve the desired serial 
number. 
2. Specify the Installation Date in the Customer Details tab.  
Note: If the warranty period begins when the part is shipped to the customer, this 
column is filled in automatically when the shipping document is finalized. 
3. Click the  Warranty tab and revise the data as needed. 
Stage II: Extending the Warranty 
Extending the warranty without charging the customer 
1. Enter the Catalogue of Parts w/Serial Nos. form and retrieve the desired serialized 
part. 
2. Enter the Extend Warranty sub-level form. 
3. Fill in the Extend Warr. Until column. 
                                                 
* Standard Operating Procedures should be used as guidelines for customers (and their consultants) to 
develop their own operating procedures. As you will note, the following procedures are very specific 
and customers are strongly advised not to use them without prior testing to make sure that they fit their own 
requirements and work practices.

Cat.No.A8000097 2 Ver.1 – Jan.8,’09 
Extending the warranty and charging the customer (invoice + receipt) 
1. Enter the Over-The-Counter Invoices form and open a new invoice. 
2. Enter the Invoice Items sub-level form. 
3. In the Part Number column, specify "000" (undefined part). 
4. In the Part Description column, record "Warranty Extension." 
5. Specify a Quantity of 1. 
6. In the Unit Price  column, specify the sum to be charged for the extension of the 
warranty. 
7. Enter the parallel Extend Warranty  sub-level form. 
8. Choose the appropriate Serial Number. 
9. Change the date in the Extend Warr. Until column. 
Extending the warranty and charging the customer (receipt only) 
1. Enter 
the Receipts form and open a new receipt. 
2. Enter the Invoice Amounts Being Paid sub-level form. 
3. Record the sum charged for the extension of the warranty. 
4. In the Remarks column, record "Warranty Extension." 
5. Enter the parallel Extend Warranty  sub-level form. 
6. Choose the appropriate Serial Number. 
7. Change the date in the Extend Warr. Until column.
