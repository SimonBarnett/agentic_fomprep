Introduction (00:00-00:26) 
This clip demonstrates the tools available in Priority for setting up vendors in the 
system and for keeping track of d
ocuments and data related to vendors. The main 
tool for managing all the vendors in the system is the Vendors form, which 
provides a comprehensive view of the vendor. In this clip, we'll be using the Dual-
Currency package, so certain columns shown here may not appear, or appear 
differently, in the U.S. package.  
Recording a Vendor (00:27-01:04) 
Begin by opening the Vendors form from the Purchasing > Vendors menu. In 
the Vendor Name column, record the name of the vendor. In this demonstration 
we'll use "Joe's Transportation Inc."  
The system automatically assigns a vendor number according to the definitions in 
the Cust & Vend Numeration Templates form. When you leave the record, the 
system automatically opens an A/P account for the vendor with a number identical 
to the Vendor Number, as the automatic message indicates.  
BPM for Vendors (01:05-01:41) 
Returning to the record, note that the default Status for new vendors appears 
automatically, and the name of the user who opened the vendor is displayed in 
the Assigned to column.  
You can create additional statuses for vendors by selecting BPM Flow Chart-
Vendors from the list of Direct Activations. In the BPM chart, right-click any empty 
space to create a new status or define status rules. Right-click a status to view or 
change the status properties.  
Vendor Details (01:42-04:08) 
Let's go back to the Vendors form.  
In the Address and Phone tab, fill in the vendor's address, phone number and 
the like.  
In the Details tab:  
 Flag the Change Name column to allow users to revise the vendor's name 
in various documents. This option is primarily used for one-time vendors.  
 You can select a Vendor Group Code  if your company classifies vendors 
into groups.  
Let's move on to the Vendor Contacts sub-level form. Press F4 to switch to 
single-record mode, which makes it easier to record new contacts. Let's quickly fill

Financial Parameters for Vendors (04:09-07:06) 
Return to the upper-level form by pressing Esc or by clicking the name of the form 
in the Form Navigation pane.   
Place the cursor on the Vendor Number column and select Financial 
Parameters for Vendors from the list of Direct Activations, which moves you to 
the form of that name. The details of the new vendor are filled in automatically.  
If check payments are to be sent to an address other than the one defined in the 
Vendors form, fill in the Remit to Address (Checks) sub-level form.   
In the Currency column, record the currency of the vendor's account. The 
currency is also defined in the Vendors form, and changes you make here will 
appear there, and vice versa. Note that you will also be able to record payments 
in a different currency. If, for example, you record a payment in Euros, then the 
journal entry for the payment will be recorded both in Euros and in U.S. Dollars.  
Record the vendor's Co. Registration No.. You can also record the number of the 
company as recognized by tax authorities in the Vendor's VAT Number column 
and revise the VAT Code as needed.  
If you classify your vendors by their type of business, you can select a 
Classification.  
You can also define the Payment Terms that will appear by default in the 
vendor's documents. These may later be revised in individual documents. Default 
payment terms can also be defined in the Vendors form, in the Payments tab.  
Let's move to the Withholding Tax tab. If the vendor has a full or partial tax 
exemption, record the percentage of withholding tax that is to be deducted from 
this vendor's payments. If you leave the column empty, the percentage appearing 
in the % Maximum Tax column will be deducted. Make sure to also define the 
Expiration Date  of the exemption.

In the Registr No.-W. Tax, record the vendor's number, as registered with income 
tax authorities, and the Type of Tax File. If relevant, also fill in the date that the 
vendor's tax authorization expires in the Authorized Until column.  
In the Invoice Data tab, record the expense account for transactions with the 
vendor in the Purchases Account column. When you record vendor invoices in 
the system, this expense account will be used automatically.  
Once you finish recording data in this form, press Esc to return to the Vendors 
form. You can revise these definitions as needed at any later date.  
Following Up on Vendors (07:07-08:45) 
After learning how to open a new vendor and define financial parameters , let's 
see how to use additional functions in the Vendors form and various reports to 
follow up on existing vendors.  
In the Vendors form, switch to query mode by pressing F11 and retrieve the 
vendor, for instance, by Vendor Number.  
Enter the To Do List sub-level form to view a list of documents related to the 
vendor that are in effect, such as invoices, purchase orders, and GRVs, as well as 
the user responsible for each.  
In the Tasks for Vendor form, you can see tasks that are related to the vendor, 
such as phone calls, price comparisons and the like.  
The Open Purchase Orders form displays a list of all the purchase orders 
recorded for the vendor. In the Vendor Price Lists form you can view the price 
lists offered by the vendor. 
From the Vendor Reports menu, you can run several reports related to vendors. 
You can also use the Vendor Data Report Generator to define custom reports 
suited to your needs. The various Purchasing menus offer access to a variety of 
related reports, such as Purchase Order Reports, as well as dynamic and 
interactive analytical tools.  
This concludes our demonstration of opening a new vendor and performing 
follow-ups. For additional details, run any of the Vendor Wizards.
