 SOP: Initial Survey 
 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
The initial survey is an effective means of recording information collected during initial contact with 
the customer, usually through a telemarketing questionnaire. The initial survey is composed of 
questions and multiple-choice answers, created on the basis of the business parameters you 
choose to include in it. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Defining Business Classifications and their Parameters 
1. Enter the Business Classific. Parameters form and record a Parameter Code and Parameter 
Description for each business classification parameter. 
2. In the Incl in Init Survey column, flag those business classification parameters to be included in 
the initial survey. 
3. Enter the Possible Classific per Parameter sub-level form and record a description for each 
appropriate classification. You can designate a Classification Code manually, or allow Priority 
to assign a code automatically after you exit the line. 
4. To assign business classifications: 
 For customers, use the Business Classifications form, a sub-level of the Customers form. 
 For customer contacts, enter the Customer Contacts form, a sub-level of the Customers 
form, and then enter its sub-level form, Business Classifications. 
 To assign a batch of classifications automatically to customers and/or customer contacts, 
use the Customer Work Area. 
 For vendors, use the Business Classifications form, a sub-level of the Vendors form. 
Stage II: Completing the Initial Survey 
1. Enter the Customers form and retrieve the new customer. 
2. In the Initial Survey Results sub-level form, for each question (parameter description) record 
the customer’s response in the Classification Desc column. 
Note: The Business Classifications form (another sub-level of the Customers form) will be 
filled in automatically with the responses to this survey.

Results 
 The initial survey responses provide a quick and simple method for automatically assigning 
business classifications to a new customer. 
 When new customers are recorded in Priority, an initial survey is created for them that includes 
the questions (business classification parameters) you have chosen.
