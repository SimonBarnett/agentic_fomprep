 SOP: Part Conversion 
 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To record conversion of one part to another part, which then updates the inventory of both. 
Setups 
 Define warehouses (see Standard Operating Procedures for opening warehouses). 
 Define parts. 
Procedure 
1. Enter the Inventory Conversions form. 
2. Indicate the Date of conversion. A document number is assigned automatically and the 
document receives the “Draft” status. 
3. In the General tab, indicate the name of the Issuing Warehouse where the conversion is being 
performed. 
4. Enter the Inventory After Conversion sub-level form, and define the part(s) created as a result 
of the conversion: 
 Indicate a Part Number. The Part Description appears automatically. 
 Specify the Qty (Factory Units) of the part that is received as a result of conversion. 
5. With the cursor on the line for the part resulting from the conversion, move to the Original Part 
sub-level form. 
6. In the Part Number column, indicate the part undergoing conversion. 
7. If you are changing the status or work order/lot number of the part, record the new Status or 
Work Order/Lot No.  
8. In the Qty (Factory Units) column, specify the quantity being converted.  
9. Return to the Inventory Conversion form, and change the document Status to “Final”. 
10. To print the document, select Print Inventory Conversion Doc. from the list of Direct 
Activations. 
11. To make changes to the document after closing it, select Reopen Document from the list of 
Direct Activations. The program will change the document status back to “Draft.” After making 
the changes, change the document to the next status.

Results 
 The inventory for the new part will increase based on the designated quantity, status, lot number 
or work order recorded in the Inventory After Conversion sub-level form.  
 The inventory of the original part that was converted will be reduced based on the recorded 
quantity/status/work order/lot number.
