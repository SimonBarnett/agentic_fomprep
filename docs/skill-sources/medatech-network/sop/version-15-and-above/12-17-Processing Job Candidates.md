 SOP: Processing Job Candidates 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To record the personal details of job applicants, indicate the results of interviews and tests, 
analyze evaluations and record details of salary offers. 
Setups 
 Make sure jobs have been opened in the Jobs form. See the Standard Operating Procedure for 
opening a new job. 
 Make sure qualifications are listed in the Qualifications form and that possible values have been 
defined for each. 
 Make sure Reasons for Rejecting Job Offers have been defined. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Recording a Candidate's General Details 
1. Enter the Job Candidates form. 
2. Record the candidate's Full Name, Social Security No., (in the dual-currency package: ID 
Number), Title and Gender. 
3. Record the relevant contact data in the Address & Phone tab. 
4. Enter the Personal Details sub-level form and fill in the relevant details.  
Note: You can attach the candidate's resume In the Resume File column (in the Personal 
Details tab) or in the parallel sub-level form, Attachments. 
5. Enter the parallel Jobs Applied For sub-level form and specify the job(s) for which the 
candidate has applied. 
6. Enter the parallel Qualifications sub-level form and specify the Qualification Code. A 
description of the qualification appears automatically. 
7. Select the most appropriate Value for each of the candidate's qualifications. 
8. Record additional details in the parallel References, Professional Experience, Education, 
Foreign Languages, Medical Tests and Remarks sub-level forms. 
Stage II: Interviewing the Candidate 
1. To set up an interview, enter the Job Candidates form and its Job Interviews sub-level form. 
Record the Date of Interview, Job Number and Code (Interview), and change the Status of 
Interview (e.g., to "Scheduled").

2. To record the results of the interview, reenter the Job Interviews sub-level form and: 
 Specify the Interviewer-Empl ID.  
 If the interview was successful, flag the Passed column. 
 Record a brief Remark, as needed. 
 Change the Status of Interview (e.g., to "Completed"). 
 Enter the Summary of Interview sub-level form to record an extended account of the 
interview. 
Stage III: Performing an Evaluation 
Defining an evaluation questionnaire 
1. Enter the Evaluation Formats form, and record a Format Code and Questionnaire Title. 
2. Enter the Questions in Questionnaire sub-level form. Record the number of each question in 
the No. (Question) column, and record the text of the Question. 
3. For each question, enter the Possible Responses sub-level form and record a number and text 
for each possible response. 
4. Run the Print Evaluation Formats report. 
Recording the performance of an evaluation 
1. Enter the Evaluation Recording form. 
2. Record the Date the evaluation was performed, the Questionnaire No. and the relevant Format 
Code. Also record the candidate's Employ/Cand SocSec# (in the dual-currency package: 
Employ/Cand. ID) and the Evaluator (Empl. ID).  
3. Enter the Responses to Questions sub-level form. All of the questions defined in the 
designated questionnaire appear automatically. 
4. For each question, select the appropriate pre-defined response in the No. Response column. 
5. Record any additional remarks for the questionnaire in the parallel Respondent's Remarks 
sub-level form. 
Analyzing the evaluation 
 To view the contents of a particular evaluation questionnaire, run the Print Evaluations report. 
 To analyze the distribution of answers given to the questions in a particular questionnaire, run 
the Evaluation Analysis report.  
 To view remarks recorded by the evaluator when filling in a particular questionnaire, run the 
Evaluator's Remarks report.  
Stage IV: Recording Test Results 
1. Enter the Job Candidates form and retrieve the candidate in question. 
2. Enter the Tests Taken by Job Applicant sub-level form. 
3. Record the Date of Test and the relevant Job Number. 
4. Record the candidate's Grade. To indicate a passing grade, specify Y in the Passed column. 
5. Attach any relevant test documents in the Documentation (File) column.

Stage V: Recording the Details of Salary Offers 
1. Enter the Job Candidates form and retrieve the candidate in question. 
2. Enter the Salary Offers sub-level form. 
3. Record the Date the offer was made, and the relevant Job Number. 
4. Select the employee that made the offer in the Offered by (Empl ID) column. 
5. In the Reply Date column, record the date the candidate agreed to accept or reject the offer.  
6. If the candidate accepted the offer, flag the Offer Accepted column. If not, record the reason in 
the Why Offer Rejected column. 
7. Return to the upper-level form and change the candidate's Status, as needed. 
Stage VI: Converting the Candidate to an Employee 
1. Enter the Job Candidates form and retrieve the candidate in question. 
2. Select Convert Candidate to Employee from the list of Direct Activations. The candidate's 
details are copied automatically into a new Personnel File and its sub-levels. 
Tip: See the Standard Operating Procedure for recording employee details to complete the 
process.
