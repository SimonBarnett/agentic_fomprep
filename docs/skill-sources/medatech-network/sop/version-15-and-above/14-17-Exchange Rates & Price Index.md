 SOP: Recording Exchange Rates & the Price Index 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To manually record the exchange rates of the currencies in use, as well as the Consumer Price 
Index. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Manually Recording Exchange Rates 
1. Enter the Exchange Rates form. 
2. In the Date column, record the date for which the exchange rates are valid. 
3. Enter the Itemized Rates sub-level form. In the Exchange Rate column, record the appropriate 
exchange rate for each currency. 
Stage II: Loading Past Exchange Rates 
1. Enter the Currencies form. 
2. Retrieve the desired currency and move to the Exchange Rates Load Table sub-level form. 
3. Move to an empty line and record the Date and the Exchange Rate valid for that date. 
Tip: You can also copy exchange rates for an entire range of dates from an external source, 
such as an MS-Excel spreadsheet, and paste them directly into this form. 
4. Return to the upper-level form and select Update Exch Rates by Load Table from the list of 
Direct Activations to load the exchange rates into the Past Exchange Rates sub-level form for 
this currency. 
Stage III: Recording the Consumer Price Index 
1. Enter the Exchange Rates form. 
2. To update the index for a given month (e.g., January) with the CPI published the following 
month: 
 In the Date column, record the dates of the first and last days of the month for which the index 
is being recorded (e.g., January 1 and January 31). 
 For each of these dates, enter the Itemized Rates sub-level form, select the "Price Index" 
currency and record the appropriate index in the Exchange Rate column. 
Result 
 Exchange rates are recorded for the currencies, and the Consumer Price Index is updated.
