 SOP: Service Calls 
Customer:   Company Inc. 
Assigned to: Dept./Position 
Aim 
 To open a service call (regular and follow-up), prepare a price quotation, report services/parts 
provided and issue an invoice for the call. 
Procedure 
Stage I: Opening a New Service Call 
1. Enter the Service Calls form and specify the relevant Customer No.  
2. Click the General tab. If the call is covered by a service contract, specify the Contract No. 
3. Indicate the Priority given to this service call, as needed. 
4. Click the Service Info tab and select the Type of Call, as needed. 
5. In the Cust’s Problem column, select the code representing the customer’s reason for opening 
the service call. 
Note: You can record a more detailed description of the problem in the Details of Malfunction 
sub-level form. 
6. If relevant, specify the Serial Number of the part for which the call was opened (in the General 
tab): 
 If there is an open service contract for the part, the End Warranty date appears automatically. 
 If the part is due for periodic maintenance, a warning will appear notifying you of the 
approaching maintenance date. 
7. Enter the Internal Dialogue sub-level form to record any internal remarks related to this call. 
Stage II: Opening a Follow-up Service Call 
1. Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the service call for which you want to open a follow-up 
call. 
2. Select Open Follow-up Call from the list of Direct Activations. 
3. Retrieve the opened follow-up call by its number. The number of the original service call appears 
in the Follow-up of Call column (in the References tab), and the following details are filled in 
automatically from the original call: 
 Customer No., Site Code and Contact 
 Call Taker 
 Serial Number, Part Number, Part Description and Part Family

 Warranty and service contract details, including Service Terms 
4. Fill in any additional details, as needed. 
5. Change the Status of Call, according to the defined workflow for service calls. 
6. Enter the Internal Dialogue sub-level form to record any internal remarks related to this call. 
Stage III: Preparing a Price Quote 
1. Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the service call for which you want to create a price 
quote. 
2. Enter the Service Report-Parts sub-level form. 
3. Record each part (e.g., replacement parts) that you anticipate will be needed for the repair. 
4. Revise the part prices and discounts as needed. 
5. Enter the parallel Service Report-Labor sub-level form. 
6. In the Work Hr Part No. column, specify the appropriate work hour type and fill in the Planned 
Hours column. 
7. Return to the upper-level form and select Print Service Quote from the list of Direct Activations. 
Stage IV: Scheduling the Service Call 
1. Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the service call you wish to schedule. 
2. Click the Dates tab and revise the Agreed Start Date, as needed. 
3. Click the Scheduling tab and record the planned start and end times. 
4. In the Assigned to column, specify the main technician responsible for handling the call. 
5. If additional employees are scheduled to handle the service call, fill in the 2nd Technician and 
3rd Technician columns. 
6. To schedule a larger group of technician on additional dates, enter the Tasks sub-level form and 
fill in the users or groups of employees and their start and end dates. 
Stage V: Reporting Service and Closing the Service Call 
1. Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the service call in question. 
2. Click the Service Info tab. 
3. Select the appropriate Malfunction code. 
4. Select the appropriate Resolution code. 
5. Add remarks regarding the services provided in the Description of Repair sub-level form. 
6. Check the value in the Spare Parts Wareh column (in the Scheduling tab). 
7. Enter the Service Report-Parts sub-level form. Record the parts used and the dates they were 
used. Fill in the Actual Quantity column. 
8. To report the use of a serialized part, fill in the Serial Number column. 
9. To report the return of parts, record each part and the date it was returned. 
10. In order to charge for travel time, enter the parallel Service Report-Labor sub-level form and 
flag the Charge Travel Time column. The default travel time defined for the customer appears 
automatically in the Travel Time column.

11. Return to the upper-level form and change the Status of Call (in the General tab) to "Final." 
Stage VI: Preparing an Invoice for a Service Call 
1. Select Prepare Invoice/Credit Memo from the list of Direct Activations of the service call. 
2. Enter the Multi-Shipment Invoices form using the list of Direct Activations and check the 
details of the invoice that was opened. 
3. Select Finalize Invoice/Memo from the list of Direct Activations of the invoice.
