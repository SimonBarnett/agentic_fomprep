INTRODUCTION  
The Support Desk role is to ensure that customer’s queries/problems are captured and answer ed as soon as 
possible. This service is delivered in a simple and effective mann er to our customers. System users can raise 
service calls in a number of different ways such as phone calls, e-mails to the support team or through Medatech 
UK website.  
The Support desk team will record the query into Priority via the service call form. Service Calls from customers 
are prioritised according to the severity classification, each of which has a service level commitment and 
escalation procedure. 
This document provides the support technicians with instructions to follow on how to manage and respond to 
service calls in compliance with the Service Level Agreements (SLAs) between Medatech UK and the customer.  
If the response times identified in the SLA are violated the technician must immediately inform the Head of 
Customer Service. At this point the Head of Customer Service will call the Customer to discuss the situation and 
propose a solution or assign it to a project manager.  
The support desk also tracks the history o f calls, creates documentation, records FAQ’s and offers general 
assistance to all of the Medatech team.

OPENING SERVICE  CALLS:  
Note: all service or assistance provided to the customer should be recorded in the system, including the issue, 
the provided solution and the time spent by each technician. 
MANUAL FORM ENTRY  
When a customer raises a service call over the phone, the technician should record a service call in our system.  
Open Service > Service Calls > Service Call and select the relevant Customer, Contact, Severity, Contract, Project 
& Type of Call.  
Type in a meaningful description (this will be the reference for the customer). 
The Assigned to will populate with your username. You can change this to a different technician to investigate 
the call, or to a screener if it still needs screening.  
The Malfunction will populate with “Not Identified” and should be changed when the malfunction is known. 
The Call Source will populate with Email but should be changed in this instance to Telephone. 
If you are opening a service call on behalf of someone at Medatech, you can  assign them as the “raised by” so 
that they will be informed of any progress. 
Now fill in the external dialogue with details of the issue raised. You should then change the call status from 
New to Helpdesk. An email will now send to the contact with the c all number, details and  the external call 
dialogue.  
OUTLOOK SYNC  
Customers can email their issues to support@medatechuk.com. We synchronise their emails into Priority, which 
will raise a service call for the contact. 
In Outlook, right-click on the email you wish to synchronise. Click on the option to Synchronise with Priority. In 
the dialogue box which appears, choose the customer and select synchronise with Priority and open service call. 
You can also select a technician here otherwise the user who synchronises the emails will be assigned the call. 
To synchronise any emails you have prepared for sync, in Priority navigate to Mail > Mail Options > Outlook > 
Synchronise with Outlook and then select “sync now”. 
To retrieve calls opened through sync, query for any calls which are in New status.  
Fill out the remaining details and set the call to Helpdesk status. To meet SLAs, you should change the call to 
Helpdesk and review it, within one hour of the email reaching the support inbox. 
Note: Priority will open a new call providing the email does not include an existing service call nu mber in the 
subject. Otherwise the email will synchronise with this existing call.

WEB SERVICE CALL  
Customers can login to our service call portal, https://support.medatechuk.com/servicenew/, to raise and 
update service calls. These are automatically raised in our system with call source Web. We receive a notification 
by email that there is a new web call. 
We can find this by querying for calls in New status. We should then fill out the mandatory details and assign it 
to a technician. The call can then be set to Helpdesk status.

ACTIONS ON AC TIVE SERVICE CALL S 
 You should click the Take Service Call the action button on the right hand side of the SC form, this will 
record that you have started working at the given time and will display a warning message to anyone 
wanting to work on the same service call.  
 Go to the Internal Dialogue and start recording investigation made or record a brief comment about 
what were the issue and the solution to it.  
 Phone the customer to discuss the issue if necessary.  
 When you have completed working on it (at this point). The last things to do are:  
o Record final text to be shown to the customer in the External Call Dialogue. This may be an 
update asking them for information, or an update to advise what the next steps will be on 
our side.  
o Optional: Internal Dialogue for only internal use.  
o Change the status from Helpdesk to the appropriate status. (When you change the status of 
the service call, the In Progress is removed. ) 
o Record the time you have now spent on it.  
o Move onto the next call. 
 NOTE: It is important to note that the final action when working on any call should be to update the 
customer. To record dialogue in the external remarks and to use this to update the customer with the 
status or to ask them a question, this is how they know that the call is being progressed and being 
actioned.  
CRITICAL SERVICE CALLS  
Critical service calls should be reviewed when received. If the call is estimated to take more 
than 2 hours to resolve the customer should be updated of this and then once every hour. 
When a service call is critical someone should be allocated to work solely to resolve the 
issue until it is resolved.  
INTERNAL ESCALATION  
When you reach a point of escalating the service call to a developer or project manager, write your 
communication to the project manager in the internal dialogue, add them as the Escalated To user and change 
the status to Esc to PM. Add a task in their calendar for when you want them to look at the call.   
To save time, do any investigation you can to help the project manager and detail this in the internal dialogue.  
Communication with the customer, should be done through support. Before escalating the call, make sure you 
notify the customer that their call is being looked at by a developer. When the developer has finished with the 
call, they should assign it back to helpdesk with details in the Internal Dialogue. Support will then communicate 
with the customer.

EXTERNAL ESCALATION  
After investigation, you might decide that a call should be escalated to Priority Software. You should document 
the issue including details about the customer’s current version and get this approved internally by the Head of 
Customer Service or the Operations Manager before raising a call on the Priority software website. 
You will be assigned a service call number, which you should paste in to the Priority Service Call field on the 
internal service call. Paste any communication with Priority in the Internal Dialogue, add any attachments to the 
Attachments sub level and let the customer know you have escalated the call.  
To open a service call to Priority you need to go to the Priority Xpert Site https://support.priority-
software.com/#/ and do the following: 
 Sign in as support@medatechuk.com (You will be provided the password. 
 Click on the Ticket List menu 
 Select New to open a new call. 
 Type the subject of the call (This will be the summary when it is shown in the preview list so try to make 
it as relevant as possible).  
 Select the Customer 
 Select the Classification 
 Then you can write the content of the call.  
 It is very important to provide all the relevant details which Priority will need to ensure the most 
efficient resolution of the issue.  
o The Priority version (both Priority, bin, and HTML) – Can be found in Help -> About.  
o Whether the issue happens for all users or just one.  
o Whether the issue happens on all machines or just one.  
o Whether the problem is replicated in Client, Web or Both 
o Whether the customer recently had any changes to hardware or software.  
o Any relevant screen shots or videos to show the issue.  
o Be sure to ask the right direct question to get the answers you need. 
 Select the Priority of the call. In most cases this will be Normal.  
o Only select High when it is causing many users to be unable to work  
o Only select Critical when no users can work.  
 Once Priority respond, change the call to Helpdesk so that it is back on your to-do list. 
 If the  call is particularly urgent and response is not being received, Head of Customer Service can 
contact the Head of Customer Service at Priority to push the service call through. This should only 
happen in very special circumstances.

CLOSING AND CANCELLI NG SERVICE CALL:  
CLOSING CLOSED REVIE W CALLS  
Once a service call has been in Answered for 1 day, or Client Action for 1 month, the call will change to Closed 
Review. This gives the customer a chance to dispute the call before it closes. 
Support should then review any Closed Review calls and change them to Closed. Once closed, the call details 
cannot be changed, and t he customer will not be able to update this service call. It is therefore important to 
ensure all details are correct before closing a call. 
THINGS TO CHECK WHEN  CLOSING CLOSED REVI EWS:  
 Details should reflect the problem in the service call. Consider someone searching for the service call 
in the future. 
 Malfunction and Resolution should be accurate. The more specific you can be about the problem, the 
better. Use no response from cust when there is no resolution because the customer failed to respond. 
 Type of call should be specified if possible.  
 Verify Call source is correct to the best of your knowledge. 
 In External Call Dialogue check that we actioned anything we promised to do. Look for any images with 
text and type out the message in the Internal Dialogue. This is particularly useful when searching for 
error messages. 
 In External Call Dialogue, remove any duplicate/unnecessary email text. 
 Ensure the resolution is detailed in the External or Internal Call Dialogue. 
 Check there is time recorded in Service Report - Labour, which accurately represents the time spent on 
the service call. Follow-up on any time ticked as billable. 
 In Task Log check tasks are ticked as completed.

DESCRIPTION OF EACH STATUS AND SEVERITY CODE  
CALL STATUS ES: 
Each status describes current action on the service call, it is defined along with attributes, rules and conditions 
in the BPM flow chart-service calls program. Below is the meaning of each status: 
 New: is an initial and default status assigned to new service calls raised that have not been looked at 
yet. 
 Helpdesk: call will be in this status when it is assigned to a technician for investigation. 
 ClientAct: Notify customer that there is an update on a SC and requires his action. 
 Re-ClientAct: If the call was previously in ClientAct and you want to send a further update to the 
customer. 
 Cust Updated: This status is assigned to a call when the contact updates the call. 
 Update: This status is used to send an update to customer without requiring any action from them 
the status will change to helpdesk after a minute. 
 Quote req’d: This status means that the customer requires a quote, this should be changed to 
Answered after creating a quote. No message sent. 
 Monitoring: This is used when we can’t replicate an issue or an issue has been solved but we are 
waiting to see if it will replicate again or the customer has asked to leave it open.  
 Answered: This is the status a call is assigned to when it is deemed as answered.  
 Closed review: This is assigned to a service call when the customer has not responded to any action 
on the call for 1 month (i.e. it was in Client Act). or when it was Answered 1 day ago. These calls are 
deemed as closed as far as the customer is aware, and the act of moving to Closed status is an 
administrative issue, rather than one that will concern the customer. 
 Closed: This is the final status of the call when all actions and checks were completed, the call is 
closed and will not be reopened.

SEVERITY CODE S:  
It is important that the severity of each query is determined accurately and those which are assigned Critical 
or Major must be given the highest Priority and monitored closely. Severity is assigned to the service call based 
on these criteria. 
Critical: Server is offline or nobody can access the system to complete their work 
Major: An issue stopping a few employees from doing their job but the majority of the company can still 
continue to work. 
Normal: Issue with the system but this is not preventing anybody from doing their daily duties. Most service 
calls will have this severity. 
Minor: Issue which has very little impact on the business and does not need to be immediately resolved. 
Enquiry: Enquiry from customer about adding extra functionality to Priority, normally calls of this severity 
result in a price quotation.

FAQ’S  
MAINTAINING FAQS FOR  SERVICE CALLS  
Customers’ most frequently asked questions (FAQs) can be recorded and displayed in an Explorer-style 
window. It is also possible to create an FAQ from within a service call, by copying the call’s malfunction details 
directly into an FAQ answer field. 
The FAQ should be created when the service call is being changed to ClosedReview. At this point you should 
consider if the resolution to this call may come up again for other customers. If so then an FAQ should be 
recorded as follows.   
To record a question manually: 
 Enter the FAQ form. 
 Record the Question. When you leave the column, the Item Number is assigned automatically. 
 Enter the Answer sub-level form and record the answer. 
 When the question is authorized to appear in the FAQ report, flag the Approved column in the upper-
level form. 
 
To record a question from within a service call: 
 Enter the Service Calls form and retrieve the desired service call. 
 Enter the FAQ sub-level form. 
 Record the Question. When you leave the column: 
 The Item Number is assigned automatically, and the contents of the parallel Details of  Malfunction 
and Description of Repair forms are copied into the Answer sub-level form;  You should revise the answer 
text as needed. 
To add the question to a menu: 
 Enter the Menu Link sub-level form. 
 In the Item Number column, select the number of the desired menu. The Menu Title will appear 
automatically. 
 Specify the Position you want this question to occupy in the menu. 
FAQ authorisation 
When you have populated all the information about the FAQ you should change the status to ManagerReview, 
this will assign it to the Support manager who can review it and make any necessary changes before it is shown 
in searches for customers or staff.
