Support Desk Manual

The Support Desk role is to ensure that customer’s queries are captured and answered as soon as possible. This service is delivered in a simple and effective manner to our customers. System users can raise Service Calls by:

Telephoning the Support Desk

Emailing the Support Desk

Through the Medatech UK Support Portal: https://priority.medatecherp.co.uk/customer_portal/#/login

The Support Desk team will record the query into Priority via the Service Calls form: Service > Service Calls > Service Calls. Service Calls are prioritised according to the severity classification.  Each Service Call has a service level commitment and escalation procedure.

This document provides the Support Technicians with instructions on how to manage and respond to Service Calls in compliance with the Service Level Agreements (SLAs) between Medatech UK and the customer.

If the response times identified in the SLAs are violated the Support Technician must immediately inform the Support Desk Manager.  At this point the Support Desk Manager will call the customer to discuss the situation and propose a solution or assign it to a Project Manager.

The Support Desk also tracks the history of Service Calls, creates documentation, records FAQ’s and offers general assistance to all the Medatech team.

The Support Desk also manages:

Priority Software upgrades

Installations

Server migrations

Installing SSL certificates

SSL installations are managed as Service Calls. They should be raised as follows:

Malfunction - No Fault Found

Resolution - No Fault Found

Type - SSL

Call Source - Internal

Assign to the Service Call to the Upgrades Manager and leave in Call Status Monitoring.

The Service Call is then managed by the Upgrades Manager.

Guides for installing SSL certificates, upgrades, installation and server migrations can be found in our internal library: Office > Library > Library Catalogue

Daily communications are done in Microsoft Teams. There is a group for the Support Desk called “Support Beans” that includes all members of the Support Desk team. Reminders are also automatically sent in this Teams chat.

Every Thursday the Support Desk have a meeting to go over current Active Service Calls. Each team member should come to the meeting prepared to summarise each Service Call currently in their action with any planned actions to take. This meeting is led by the Support Desk Manager.

On the last Friday of every month, the Support Desk have a Monthly Meeting. This is led by the Support Desk Manager who updates the team with news about any sensitive customers, upcoming and recently completed upgrades, and any news the team needs to be aware of. The agenda is made up of cards from Monthly Meeting on the Support Tasks Trello board: https://trello.com/b/4QBRbwA2/support-tasks

All service or assistance that is provided to the customer must be recorded into the system:

The issue

The solution

The time spent by each Support Technician

All communication with the customer

Open Service > Service Calls > Service Calls and select the relevant Customer, Severity, Contract, Project & Type of Call.

Choose the Contact you spoke to on the phone. If the contact doesn’t exist against the customer, add their details to the Contacts form and choose them as the contact. Note: the choose list of contacts on the Service Calls form is all the “approved” contacts. To generate a full list of contacts, select Ctrl+F6 on the field. If you are opening a Service Call on behalf of someone at Medatech, you can assign them as the Raised by so that they will be notified when the Service Call is opened and can track it if required.

When answering the phone, you should be polite and professional. You must mention the company name and your name. For example:

For Service Calls updated through sync, you should check each Service Call to make sure the update went in successfully.

Link an FAQ If one was sent to the customer

Move onto the next Service Call.

Note: It is important to note that the final action when working on any Service Call should be to update the customer. To record dialogue in the external remarks and to use this to update the customer with the status change or to ask them a question, this is how they know that the Service Call is being progressed and being actioned.

As soon as possible it should be identified whether more information is needed to assist with a Service Call and this sent to the customer. At 4pm every day, review your list and check whether there are any Service Calls we need to ask the customer for more information, review each call and make sure you understand what is being asked, update any customers that have not had an update recently, send any quotes that have been approved and not sent, add any internal remarks to note where you are in the investigation. The Support Desk refer to this internally as “Hammer Time”.

At 4pm every day a report called Calls Coming Back In Our Action is sent to the Support Desk team. Each Technician should handle any Service Call where they are the Assigned User on the report.

Critical Service Calls should be reviewed when received.  If the Service Call is estimated to take more than two hours to resolve the customer should be updated of this and then once every hour.

When a Service Call is critical someone should be allocated to work solely to resolve the issue until it is resolved.

In the case when the customer is required to approve billable time to investigate a Service Call (for example when it is unclear if the blame lies with Priority or user), a Purchase Order or confirmation of time to be charged should be received from the customer.

When received it should be attached to the Service Call and recorded in the Customer’s PO field.

Actions depending on data received:

When you reach a point of escalating the Service Call to a developer or project manager outside of the Support Desk team, write your communication to the developer or project manager in the internal dialogue, add them as the Escalated To user and change the status to Esc to PM. Contact the project manager to discuss when they can look at the Service Call and add a task in their calendar according to their availability. First check with senior members of the Support Desk team to see if it can be handled within the team first.

To save time, do any investigation you can to help the project manager and detail this in the internal dialogue.

Note:  This only applies for assigning to a project manager.  If escalating within the Support Desk team, including to a developer, assign the Service Call to them in Call Updated status.

Communication with the customer, should be done through the Support Desk. Before escalating the Service Call, make sure you notify the customer that their Service Call is being looked at by a developer or project manager (you do not need to name the person). When the developer or project manager has finished with the Service Call, they should update the Internal Dialogue, and by change the status to Call Updated. The Support Desk will then communicate with the customer.

Supporting Developments:

Developments done by Medatech are supported by the Support Desk. Whereas developments done by an external developer are not.

As soon as an issue is found to be caused be a development done by an external developer, the Support Desk should ask the customer to approve billable time to continue the investigation.

If the development is done by Medatech, look for the sales order to find when the work was completed. If the sales order was closed more than two months ago, support should continue investigation with the help of developers on the support team if needed. If the sales order was closed more recently or is still open, the sales order should be reopened if necessary and the Service Call closed. The developer on the sales order will be assigned to pick up the issue, report their hours against the sales order and communicate with the customer.

After investigation, you might decide that a Service Call should be escalated to Priority Software. You should document the issue including details about the customer’s current version and get this approved internally by a Senior member of the team before raising a Service Call on Priority Xpert. Service Calls raised to Priority are appropriate and cannot be answered within the Medatech team

Duplicate escalations are avoided

Related Service Calls across different customers are linked where appropriate

Billable time:

If Priority ask for billable time, we must get a quote from them (the price per hour). You should check with the Support Desk Manager if we can approve the time and whether we will pass the charge onto the customer.

If we are passing the charge onto the customer, forward the quote from Priority to the Accounts team and ask them to create a quote for two hours to send the customer. Once the quote is approved, send the quote to the customer. Once the customer has sent back a purchase order to confirm the charge, forward this back to Accounts to create a purchase order to send to Priority. The Support desk will send the Purchase order to Priority.

If Medatech are covering the charge, forward the quote to the Accounts team to create a purchase order for two hours to send to Priority. Send the Accounts team the Medatech Service Call number, Priority Service Call number and a brief description of what the quote is for. Mention that we are covering the charge (on the condition that if the fault is found in the standard software package, we will not be charged). Once the purchase order is made, send it to Priority via the service call.

Escalation to Priority and Closing Service Calls:

Only bugs should be raised to Priority. Bugs should be reproducible across multiple systems and on the latest Priority version.

If the issue does not occur on the latest version, we can ask for a backport. If no backport exists:

For versions more than three releases behind (including point releases), the customer must be instructed to upgrade.

For versions fewer than three releases behind, the Service Call may remain open for a maximum of six months while awaiting a fix. After six months, the Service Call must be closed.

Where a bug is confirmed in the latest version and Priority agrees to fix it, the following must occur:

Confirm whether a target version or timeframe is available.

Close the Service Call and record it under Service > Service Calls > Service Calls Awaiting Next Fix.

Inform the customer that the issue has been added to Priority’s development backlog and that no ETA is guaranteed.

Resolution codes must be applied as follows:

P14 – Priority Future Fix: Target version unknown.

P01 – Next Version Fix: Target version confirmed.

A response to queries is expected within three months. If this timeframe is exceeded, the Support Desk Manager must be consulted to determine whether an alternative approach is required.

Customer-specific issues must have a target resolution timeframe of six months. These Service Calls must not be added to Service Calls Awaiting Next Fix. If the six-month timeframe is exceeded, the Support Desk Manager must be consulted to determine whether an alternative approach is required.

If Priority confirms that an issue has been added to their list of future developments for consideration in a future release:

The Service Call must be closed

Resolution P03 – Added to Future Developments must be applied

The Service Call must be recorded under Service > Service Calls > Priority Future Developments

there is a workaround

Check there is time recorded in Service Report - Labour, which accurately represents the time spent on the Service Call. Follow-up on any time ticked as billable.

Check that any contacts on the call have had a response.

If Priority have sent us a fix and it is in a link, make sure that you click on the link and download the fix and add it to an attachment to the Service Call.

You would need to look in the Service Call dialog to confirm the Debug has been removed.  If not a developer would need to check in the customers systems to ensure that the Debug has been commented out or removed.

Each status describes current action on the Service Call, it is defined along with attributes, rules and conditions in the BPM Flow Chart-Service Calls program. Below is the meaning of each status:

Open:  Status new Service Calls go into to trigger email to customer before reaching Helpdesk.

New: Initial and default status assigned to new Service Calls raised that have not been looked at yet.

Helpdesk: Service Call will be in this status when it is assigned to a technician for investigation.

Client Act: Notify customer that there is an update on a Service Call and requires their action.

Re-ClientAct: If the Service Call was previously in Client Act and you want to send a further update to the customer.

Call Updated: This status is assigned to a Service Call when the contact updates the Service Call, or if someone internal amends the internal dialog and needs the assigned user to review the Service Call again.

Web Update: When a customer updates a Service Call on our portal, the Service Call changes to this status, then to Call Updated.

Update: This status is used to send an update to customer without requiring any action from them the status will change to Helpdesk after a minute.

Monitoring: This is used when we can’t replicate an issue, or an issue has been solved but we are waiting to see if it will replicate again or the customer has asked to leave it open.

Client Monit: Puts the Service Call in the client’s action, however it reverts to Helpdesk in 1 week.

ReClientMont: Sends and email to the contact, then returns to Client Monit status.

With Client: Puts the Service Call in the client’s action but does not send them an email.

Esc to Prio: Escalated to Priority Software.

Prio w/ upd.: Sends and email to the contact, then returns to Esc to Prio status.

Answered: This is the status a Service Call is assigned to when it is deemed as answered.

Closed: This is the final status of the Service Call when all actions and checks were completed, the Service Call is closed and will not be reopened.

Active: This is the status a Service Call goes to if the Service Call is reopened from Closed. Should be done only if necessary.

Cancelled: Cancels the Service Call. This emails the contact. This is usually used when a Service Call is opened in error.

Quote reqd: This status means that the customer requires a quote, this should be changed to Q-Auth-Reqd once quote is sent for authorisation and Answered after the quote is sent to the customer.

Q-Auth-Reqd:  When a quote is made but waiting for authorisation.

Esc to Exter: Escalated to an external company.

Exter w/ upd: Sends and email to the contact, then returns to Esc to Exter status.

Esc to PM: Escalated to a Project Manager.

PM w/ update: Sends and email to the contact, then returns to Esc to PM status.

Scheduled: Service Call scheduled in the calendar with next action.

Sched w/ upd: Sends and email to the contact, then returns to Scheduled status.

Client Monit: Puts the Service Call in the client’s action, however it reverts to Helpdesk in 1 week.

ReClientMont: Sends and email to the contact, then returns to Client Monit status.

With Client: Puts the Service Call in the client’s action but does not send them an email.

It is important that the severity of each query is determined accurately and those which are assigned Critical or Major must be given the highest priority and monitored closely. Severity is assigned to the Service Call based on these criteria:

Critical - The entire Priority system is inaccessible to all users.

Major - A specific function within Priority is not working, affecting some users' ability to work.

Minor - The system is not performing as expected, but users are still able to work.

Enquiry - The system is functioning correctly, but a question or change request has been raised.

When an order is assigned to someone, they are supposed to be contacting the customer to confirm but there may be cases where this does not happen in which case follow the above process.
