# SERVICE CALL ACCOUNTS

- **Source:** `SERVICE CALL ACCOUNTS.docx`

Make sure that you use Internet Explorer

Make sure that Emerge IT has enabled you as a customer contact (must use email address from customer contact form) that can raise service calls (Emerge SC allowed check box on customer contact)and has internet privileges set to Marketgate-service. (sub form of customer contacts-internet privilidges)

Clear your browsing history before you attempt to sign up, if you don’t do this then the login boxes may not appear.

(Settings wheel>Internet options>browsing history>delete>then tick the following and delete:

Go to http://www.emerge-it.co.uk and then click on support

Click on new service call

Enter your email address in the email field (use your work email) and leave the password field blank, then click the new user link.

It should come up saying that it has emailed you your password and then you should be able to go support> new service call and then log in with the password that was emailed to you. This login should also work for logging in to update a service call.

If you click new service call and there are no boxes to log in, clear your browsing history and then try again.
