# New Starter Checklist

- **Source:** `New Starter Checklist.docx`

Before returning documentation to Step please ensure you have completed and included the following forms

(Please tick to indicate that this has been included):

Signed contract

Bank details

P45 or P46

Equal opportunities monitoring form

A copy of your passport and right to work in the UK

Also included:

Your Step Briefing, with advice on how to prepare for your first day, absence and holiday procedures and forms and advice of maintaining a Step Journal.
