# Problem w

- **Source:** `Problem w.docx`

Problem w/the "Opening Bal in Ledger" in the Reconcil Work Area-Paper Statemt.

Elaboration of the Problem

After running the Prep for Recon. w/Paper Statemt program (menu path: Financials > General Ledger > Reconciliations > Bank/Credit Card Reconciliations > Bank Reconcils-Paper Statement), the balance in the Opening Bal in Ledger column of the Reconcil Work Area-Paper Statemt form (same menu path) differs form the balance in the ledger (or in the sub-level of the Chart of Accounts) for that date.

Solution

In the Chart of Accounts form (menu path: Financials > General Ledger > Chart of Accounts), check that the balance in the Ledger Transactions -Multi-Year sub-level form equals the balance in the upper-level form. If it does not, follow the instructions here.
