Sales Workshop 2

CRM

You are attending a new marketing event. The marketing manager would like this recorded in Prioirty so that they can monitor how much revenue is going to be potential generated. Create a new marketing event called ‘Crisp conference’.

Our current customer ‘David & Sons LTD’ have approached us at the event and we believe there is a chance that we could sell them 1000 of our new ‘Salt and Pepper Crisps’. Create a sales opportunity for this customer based on the below information. And then change the status of the Sales Opportunities to be ‘In Progress’

We expect the sale of 200,000 packets of crisp to be made with a unit price of £0.15  to be made by 01/06/19. The Marketing manager has estimated that this sale could be worth £30,000. The customer seemed very interested so we will assign a 90% chance of closing the sale.

A new lead has just has approached you and shown slight interest in the products, you have retained his name, ‘Lee Davis’ and Company name, ‘LCN construction’ Raise a new lead ion the system and reference this marketing campaign in the lead record so we can keep track of how we generated this lead.

We now need to convert LCN Construction into a temporary customer. Once we have done this we need to open a sales opportunity linked to the marketing campaign based on the below information.

We expect the sale of 50,000 packets of crisp to be made with a unit price of £0.30  to be made by 01/06/19. The Marketing manager has estimated that this sale could be worth £16,000. The customer was just enquiring so we will assign a 30% chance of closing the sale.

Run the ‘Campaign Value’ report to see the value of the Crisp Conference marketing campaign.
