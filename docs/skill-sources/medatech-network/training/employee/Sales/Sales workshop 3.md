Sales Workshop 3

Quotations and Sales Orders

‘Williams PLC’ have asked for a price quotation for 1000 packets of ‘Salt and Pepper Crisps’ we will quote them a price of £0.25 per packet of crisp. Raise the quotation and send the price quotation to the customer (this should already be set to be your email address).

David Williams has responded to your quotation. He would like to know how much 5000 packets would cost. Save the current version of the quotation and then make the change as required. The cost per unit will now be£ 0.22 due to the change in quantity. Resend the quotation out to the customer.

David Williams confirms that the second quote is the one they would like to go with. He has sent a purchase order PO1900021 for the sales order. Open a new sales order using the direct activation on the price quotation

David has asked that a note be put on the order to say that the delivery needs to be made between the hours of 9 and 5. Add this note to the sales order and then send an order conformation out to David.

David & Sons LTD have confirmed they would like to place the order for the items discussed during the Crisp conference. Raise a sales order for this and make sure you reference the sales opportunities. Check that prices and quantities have pulled from the sales opportunity

David & Son LTD have asked for the items to be supplied on pallets. Per the company process any remarks such as this need to be recorded on the line level of the sales order as it may not apply to the whole order. Record a comment against the line to notify the packers that this needs to go on a pallet.

Display the order conformation to the screen and make sure the remarks display against the line and not the whole sales order.
