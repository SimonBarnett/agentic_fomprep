MRB from GRV

Raising a Malfunction Document

Find the Malfunction Document form under Inventory > Quality Assurance > Handling of Malfunctions (MRB) > Malfunction Document.

The form looks like this:

Under pert No., define which part is rejected.

Under quantity, record how many parts are rejected.

You can also record the severity and where the malfunction was detected.

Print MRB Form can then be selected from the list of direct activation to Print/View the Malfunction Document.

Which looks like this:

This Malfunction Document can be linked to the relevant Purchase Order or Goods Receiving Voucher as shown below.

Severity codes can be defined here:

Inventory > Quality Assurance > Handling of Malfunctions (MRB) > Malf. Handling Definitions > Levels of Malfunction Severity

Malfunction locations can be defined here:

Inventory > Quality Assurance > Handling of Malfunctions (MRB) > Malf. Handling Definitions > Places of Malfunction Detection
